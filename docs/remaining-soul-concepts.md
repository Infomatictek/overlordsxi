# OverlordsXI — Remaining Soul Concept Bible
**Concept pass:** 2026-11-19 · **Not deployed** · Levels **10–75**

## Design guardrails
Every Soul must offer a different **combat decision**, not just different numbers or a reskinned builder/spender bar. Be viable from Lv10 with ordinary gear; add depth at Lv30/50/70. Preserve hard-boss single-target practicality, FFXI mechanical/lore inspiration, role trade-offs, and existing playable Souls. This is a concept reference, **not** canonical FFXI lore; full lore sessions will happen later.

**Important correction:** Defender is the canonical PLD tank identity already associated with the Aegis Templar legacy registration. **Do not implement a second Defender.** Of the previous 14 entries, 13 need concept treatments and one needs registry/name reconciliation.

## Compact player-facing overview

| Soul | Role | How it plays | Why bring it instead of another Soul in that role? | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Oathbreaker (PLD)** | DPS | Great Sword combat with voluntary vows: commit to an offensive technique and accept a brief defensive vulnerability; use well-timed divine/dark-edged punishment on one target. **Commitment/risk**, not a meter. | A disciplined melee damage dealer with particularly reliable damage during predictable boss openings; different from Berserker's sustained aggression or DRK's HP-cost offense. | Commit at the wrong time and survival collapses; no Defender interception. |
| **Reaper (DRK)** | DPS | Scythe execution specialist: assess enemy HP, time Souleater/Drain-like HP costs, and capitalize on marked finishing windows. **Execute planning**, not repetitive stacking. | Strong boss-finishing pressure and clutch high-risk burst rather than Black Belt's continuous rapid attacks or Oathbreaker's commitment windows. | Less effective before execute windows and vulnerable if healing/support can't cover the HP risk. |
| **Scourge (DRK)** | Support | Curser who chooses **one important affliction** to maintain: hinder target offense, weaken defenses, or interfere with casting, adapting to each boss. **Debuff triage**. | Specialist at exploiting a dangerous boss's particular weakness; unlike Royal Tactician's ally/enemy spell sequencing or Trickster's positional enmity disruption. | Resistant bosses limit curse effects; must offer a small fallback party benefit, not unresistable control. |
| **Sniper (RNG)** | DPS | Long-range rifle/crossbow precision: hold position, judge the safe shot window, deliver heavy single-target hits; reposition resets ideal firing setup. **Preparation and execution**. | Burst on distant or dangerous targets with deliberate shots; the opposite of Flintlock's mobile, sustained dual-pistol bullet barrage. | Movement, missed shots, and bad timing hurt output; no pet-tanking. |
| **Arcane Archer (RNG)** | Support | Bow-powered elemental arrow attunement: pick which enemy resistance or party skillchain interaction needs help, and re-attune for the next mechanic. **Elemental adaptation**. | Ranged elemental utility for a skillchain/magic-burst team; not Sniper precision DPS or Huntsman's beast command. | Limited physical damage, dependence on relevant elemental interactions; don't copy Bard songs or GEO auras. |
| **Nightblade (NIN)** | Support | Ninjutsu infiltrator: alternate suppression, repositioning, and short targeted sabotage, exploiting boss telegraphs without relying on clones. **Disruption/positioning**. | Can suppress or expose specific boss mechanics with fast reactions; Shinobi is offensive clone management and Kagemusha is decoy/shadow tanking. | Unavoidable boss mechanics and resistances constrain sabotage; little stand-alone damage. |
| **Warmage (BLU)** | DPS | Sword/Club and Blue Magic **adaptive chaining**: select a monster ability according to range, weakness, and physical-versus-magical opportunity; adapt rather than fill a gauge. | Highly flexible damage against changing enemy defenses; unlike Spellblade's rune conduction or Scarlet Weaver's RDM magic/melee weaving. | More complex ability setup, less peak specialization than pure melee or elemental nukers. |
| **Archivist (SCH)** | Support | Field scholar who **prepares an answer**: identify incoming mechanic, select a protective or tactical grimoire entry, and deploy it before the window; limited active preparations. | Powerful foresight and planned prevention for scripted fights, not Bard's continuous song coverage or Royal Tactician's reactive magic control. | Poorer emergency recovery when surprised; not Medic's Eos healing or Sage's Titania/Helix offensive play. |
| **Beastspeaker (BST)** | Support | Direct animal **behavior and instincts**: give real pet-driven tactical commands that mark an enemy, protect a teammate briefly, or exploit beast-type knowledge. No guardian pet tanking. | Adaptive beast-mediated support that moves with the pet, instead of Alpha Warden's Soul-Link tank or Untamed's two-predator DPS. | Pet must stay alive and act; beast unavailable means reduced utility. Can't duplicate Huntsman's warhorn/defensive pet orders. |
| **Primalist (SMN)** | DPS | Choose the right avatar for the target's element/phase, commit to offensive Blood Pact timing, and swap when the encounter demands it. **Elemental roster mastery**, not stack accumulation. | Element- and phase-responsive avatar burst rather than Necromancer's undead pet or Astromancer's planned personal nukes. | Summoning transitions and pet vulnerability cost uptime; not Celestial's healing Ward loop. |
| **Herald (SMN)** | Support | Coordinate **avatar manifestations** and timed beneficial wards based on upcoming danger or offense windows; avoid passive all-purpose stat inflation. | Specific strategic avatar utility timed to boss events, not Musician's magical song aggression or Diviner's terrain-based zones. | Limited adaptability when avatar is missing; only a few support windows, not permanent best buffs. |
| **Diviner (GEO)** | Support | Place **spatial fields** strategically and decide when to relocate their effects as boss positions change. Position-first support, not a resource cycle. | Spatial control and battlefield-shaping support rather than Herald's timed avatar wards or Bard's portable songs. | Movement-heavy fights disrupt positioning; not Verdant's luopan healing sanctuary. |
| **Terramancer (GEO)** | DPS | Weaponized geological **fault lines**: anticipate where a boss will stand and time earth/elemental eruptions to hit one priority enemy. **Position + prediction**, no stacks. | Damage rewards encounter knowledge and field placement rather than Astromancer's elemental spell schedule or Primalist's avatar rotation. | Unpredictable movement and displacement undermine damage; avoid broad accidental AoE pulls. |

## Level progression contract (each of the 13)
- **Lv10:** One complete and understandable action loop plus a relevant weakness. No rare item or Lv50 JA requirement for the basic fantasy.
- **Lv30:** A tactical choice, either positioning, opponent adaptation, or an additional timing interaction. Not another generic gain-and-spend meter.
- **Lv50:** One meaningful boss-facing upgrade that retains single-target control and avoids unconditional raid buffs.
- **Lv70–75:** Mastery expression and viable scaling, not a new mandatory rotation that invalidates levels 10–69.

## FFXI lore/ability seeds (inspiration, not invented canon)
- **Oathbreaker:** Paladin oaths, Divine Magic, shield-versus-Great Sword thematic inversion.
- **Reaper:** Dark Knight Souleater, Drain/Aspir, Scythe, costly darkness.
- **Scourge:** Dark Magic Absorb spells, Bio, ancient hexes.
- **Sniper:** Ranger marksmanship, weapon delay, Barrage/Eagle Eye Shot as precision references (do not clone Flintlock's sustained barrage).
- **Arcane Archer:** Ranger bow skills, elemental arrows, skillchain/magic-burst interactions.
- **Nightblade:** Ninja tools, enfeebling ninjutsu and stealth.
- **Warmage:** Blue Mage monster spell acquisition, physical/magical Blue Magic, Chain/Burst Affinity.
- **Archivist:** Scholar stratagems, grimoire preparation, battlefield analysis.
- **Beastspeaker:** Beastmaster pet families, Charm and pet commands.
- **Primalist:** Avatar elements, Blood Pact: Rage, Astral Flow.
- **Herald:** Avatar wards, Blood Pact: Ward, elemental protection.
- **Diviner:** Geomancer Indi-/Geo- placement and luopan range.
- **Terramancer:** Geomancer geomancy, earth currents, earth-element magic.

## Cross-role boundaries that must survive implementation
1. **No why-bring-X-over-Y:** every Soul has a fight type it favors and a situation it struggles with, including resistant bosses.
2. **No default resource systems:** only add a meter if it offers a new decision unavailable from standard TP, MP, recasts, pet commands, positioning or status conditions.
3. **No new unnecessary AoE:** hard bosses are dangerous; avoid accidental pulls and free splash damage.
4. **No encroachment on finished Souls:** Flintlock's dual pistols are a separate friend's work; Shinobi remains an offensive clone manager; Chi Weaver and Black Belt are already in game; Huntsman is a beast-commander melee tank *design goal* but its currently implemented RNG module remains ranged/modal and requires its own later reconciliation.
5. **Defender alias warning:** existing `aegistemplar` uses club+shield interception and Zeal; a second Defender implementation would conflict with it. Reconcile display naming/aliases instead.
6. **Siblings and rivals must not be strictly dominated:** record one comparative benchmark, role advantage, weakness and actual Lv10/75 tests for every eventual Soul release.
