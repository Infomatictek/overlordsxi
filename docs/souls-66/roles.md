# Choosing between Souls in the same role

Each row identifies a decision the player makes and an encounter that rewards it. HybridSupport Souls also appear in the damage comparison where their personal contribution matters. Their existing offense budget remains HybridSupport. Necromancer and current-main Huntsman enter the support comparison through their alternate pets. The Huntsman redesign's Saurian has a tank role. Combat healers share the healing comparison. Read these as matchup hypotheses for playtesting.

## Damage

| Soul | Player decision | Choose it when | Closest alternatives and the price of that choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Berserker](jobs/war.md#berserker) | Overstrain | Short physical burn with healing available | Pick it for a short melee burn with healer cover. Black Belt rewards uninterrupted fist contact, while Oathbreaker commits to one vow over an encounter. |
| [Black Belt](jobs/mnk.md#black-belt) | Chain fist WS | Sustained uninterrupted melee with fast TP recovery | Choose fast personal WS recovery without managing a pet, tank counter or HP sacrifice. Berserker has a stronger reason to wait for an offensive buff window. Forced disengagement breaks Black Belt's cadence. |
| [Zealot](jobs/whm.md#zealot) | Earn casting MP with a staff | A long fight with alternating melee and divine-cast windows | Pick sustained divine damage against one boss when approaching it is feasible. Bishop uses Smites to support healing. Astromancer can deliver its peak hit from range but waits out saturation. |
| [Astromancer](jobs/blm.md#astromancer) | Commit Ancient Magic | One brief elemental burst check on an unsaturated boss | Pick the strongest deliberate spell for a short damage check. Sage's Titania and Helix keep dealing damage through movement. A second Astromancer on the same target gets sharply diminishing bonus damage. |
| [Channeler](jobs/blm.md#channeler) | Channel for one caster | One ally whose cast time or MP is limiting | Choose for a specific ally's MP and cast-time bottleneck. Arcane Archer marks an enemy and Herald coordinates several casters. Channeler assists even if the ally targets a friend. |
| [Scarlet Weaver](jobs/rdm.md#scarlet-weaver) | Hold Yin or Yang | A boss alternating melee and magic vulnerabilities | Choose a RDM who can switch between resistant phases. Spellblade builds Conduction to pay for a cast. Scarlet Weaver can stay in one aspect indefinitely and pays a real opportunity cost to switch. |
| [Bandit](jobs/thf.md#bandit) | Exploit Defense Down | Another party member or gear reliably exposes defense | Choose to capitalize on allies or equipment exposing an enemy. Reaper follows the boss's recovery and Black Belt gets an unconditional H2H refund. Bandit relies on an external opening. |
| [Oathbreaker](jobs/pld.md#oathbreaker) | Choose an encounter vow | A predictable fight with a stable offensive or reactive assignment | Choose predictable damage under a chosen vow. Reaper times its hit after the enemy's action regardless of whom it targeted. A bad pre-pull vow costs Oathbreaker a phase. |
| [Reaper](jobs/drk.md#reaper) | Strike during recovery | A boss with frequent readable action completions | Choose a finisher when the boss repeatedly exposes itself. Oathbreaker chooses a vow, Bandit exploits Defense Down. Unpredictable recovery and lost melee time reduce Reaper's peak. |
| [Adventurer](jobs/brd.md#adventurer) | Turn melee cadence into sonic WS | A melee slot must keep songs up during sustained contact | Choose melee DPS that maintains songs while fighting. Musician spends its windows actually casting offensive songs. Losing melee access stalls Cadence. |
| [Musician](jobs/brd.md#musician) | Burst with hostile songs | Frequent allied skillchains and time to sing | Bring offensive song bursts to a magic-burst party. Channeler lends one ally a casting opportunity. Musician contributes damage directly and needs cast time. |
| [Sniper](jobs/rng.md#sniper) | Hold a sightline | A clear firing lane and few mandatory movements | Pick predictable one-shot precision with low ammo consumption. Flintlock floods a target with independent pistol fire. Frequent knockbacks punish Sniper's aim. |
| [Warlord](jobs/sam.md#warlord) | Close chains for an aura | Melee allies can exploit a closer's sustained aura | Choose a closer who generates group benefit while doing melee damage. Commander can call a ward on demand. Warlord loses uptime when it cannot reach the target. |
| [Shinobi](jobs/nin.md#shinobi) | Maintain a living clone squad | Rebuild windows between boss area attacks | Pick an offensive squad whose survival and replacement you personally manage. Kagemusha consumes defensive images instead. Boss spikes and movement cost damage. |
| [Lancer](jobs/drg.md#lancer) | Choose a launch lane | Melee burst after a safe approach from distance | Choose offensive repositioning when a fight allows run-up space. Skyborn times defense against a hit; Wyvernheart's jumps heal. Lancer commits its own position for damage. |
| [Warmage](jobs/blu.md#warmage) | Set monster answers | A prepared Blue Mage can exploit changing damage resistances | Choose flexible damage on a fight that changes its defenses. Azure Bastion uses physical Blue spells for Chitin. A Warmage who equips the wrong answers has lower peak damage than a specialist. |
| [Flintlock](jobs/cor.md#flintlock) | Sustain successive pistol volleys | A single target allows long firing uptime | Choose repeated single-target fire with two independently acting pistols. Sniper trades volume for one deliberate shot. Movement and interrupted firing cost Flintlock sustained output. |
| [Bladewheel](jobs/dnc.md#bladewheel) | Dance at chakram range | Melee is hazardous but Dazes and Sambas need upkeep | Choose a ranged physical skirmisher who continues dancing outside melee. Sniper waits still for one shot. Bladewheel needs the correct ranged slot and dance timing. |
| [Sage](jobs/sch.md#sage) | Maintain Helix with Titania | Long fights with forced movement and durable targets | Choose long-fight elemental pressure that keeps ticking through movement. Astromancer wins a brief opening with a huge impact and saturates that target. Short-lived enemies waste Sage's Helix duration. |
| [Untamed](jobs/bst.md#untamed) | Synchronize beast and master | Both can stay on one target and land actual Ready attacks | Choose coordinated master and beast DPS with two meaningful damage sources. Pugilist prepares a frame's WS for a chain. Huntsman defends with a pet and Alpha Warden shares incoming damage. |
| [Primalist](jobs/smn.md#primalist) | Commit an avatar's Rage | Elemental vulnerabilities rotate between boss phases | Pick adaptable elemental pet burst when vulnerability changes across phases. Astromancer commits its own long cast and Celestial uses Wards for recovery. A bad avatar choice wastes the Rage window. |
| [Pugilist](jobs/pup.md#pugilist) | Prepare the automaton's next WS | A party needs deliberate master-to-frame skillchain timing | Choose a configurable machine as a skillchain partner. Untamed needs a living beast landing real Ready attacks on the same target and returns tempo in both directions. |
| [Spellblade](jobs/run.md#spellblade) | Conduct a runic spell | Great-sword uptime can prepare a personal magic burst | Choose a melee caster who turns rune selection and contact into a prepared offensive cast. Scarlet Weaver switches damage mode as defenses change. Lost contact delays Conduction. |
| [Terramancer](jobs/geo.md#terramancer) | Predict a fault impact | The boss will stay still for a delayed single-target detonation | Pick positional burst on a boss whose movement you understand. Sage's Helix travels with its victim, while Diviner maintains a corridor for allies. A bad prediction can waste an entire cast. |

## Collisions requiring explicit tests

| Pair or group | Different player decisions | Test that could disprove the distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Alpha Warden / Huntsman / Vanguard / Necromancer | Master holds and splits; fragile master directs living beast; maintain Valoredge and Strobe; select tank thrall or caster. | Record actual hate owner before/after a cure and after pet death. If all four function by parking an invulnerable pet without action, review the failing kit. |
| Untamed / Pugilist / Shinobi | Match beast and master targets for reciprocal real actions; feed a configurable frame for a chain; replace and command five fragile copies. | Move the target or kill the pet/one clone. Compare lost decisions as well as lost DPS. |
| Flintlock / Sniper / Bladewheel / Imperial Archer | Independent volume; uninterrupted aim; ranged dance upkeep; two-weapon WS sequence. | Run stationary and forced-movement trials. Measure shots per window, claims, lost casts and time outside melee. |
| Zealot / Astromancer / Sage / Primalist / Terramancer | Sustain divine MP by staff contact; schedule a shared Ancient impact; maintain Titania and Helix; commit an avatar Rage; predict a location. | Compare a 10s break check, 5min boss and forced relocation. Log direct damage, ticks, pet damage and saturation separately. |
| Scarlet Weaver / Spellblade / Warmage | Hold a damage aspect until resistance changes; generate Conduction and cast; set and select the appropriate monster technique. | On fixed resistances check Scarlet does not need to switch. On rotating resistances check Warmage gains from its set choice. |
| Oathbreaker / Berserker / Reaper / Bandit | Preselect a vow; accept personal recoil; react after a boss action; exploit external Defense Down. | Use one boss without debuffs, one with forced recovery and one scripted retaliation. Keep TP and equipment equivalent. |
| Kagemusha / Sensei / Phantom / Mirage Sentinel / Skyborn | Lose and restore images; heal on staff parry/miss; retain hate on real misses; choose a parry/Flourish expenditure; guard an incoming sequence. | Test unavoidable magic and a rapid multi-hit attack. Record the event that produced each mitigation and enmity change. |
| Defender / Templar / Valkyrie | Intercept HP damage; use beacon and splash cures; rescue an interrupted cast. | Land damage without interrupting a cast, then interrupt a low-damage cast. Payoff must attach to a different event for each. |

| Templar / Magus / Vampire / Wyvernheart / Lifebinder | Lowest-HP beacon; Blue spell and maneuver-independent catalyst wave; damage-derived healing; jump/breath triage; preselect rune-dependent Pulse. | Remove contact, mute casting and remove a pet in separate trials. Log who can still save the endangered ally and how. |
| Medic / Animist / Celestial / Verdant | Delegate cleanse to Eos; command a specific maneuver; keep an avatar Ward cycle; renew a placed luopan. | Move only half the party. Compare ally coverage, manual responses and pet loss. |
| Arcane Archer / Channeler / Herald / Musician | One enemy mark for one hit; hold a link to a caster; coordinate several elemental actions; personally burst with a song. | Swap an ally's target and spell element mid-window. Each support's reward must change according to its own condition. |

| Bishop / Zealot | Divine offense pays for ward-healing on Bishop; successful regular staff hits pay for divine damage on Zealot. | Same equipment budget; measure ward absorption, divine DPS and net MP outside melee. |
