# OverlordsXI · 66-Soul design and implementation handoff

**22 jobs, 66 active Souls, levels 10–75.** Start with the [audit and implementation classifications](audit.md). The cards preserve the 41 existing server kits, including two whose target identities are under separate development. Four feature-branch prototypes and 21 concepts have explicit target contracts. Freelancer retains its historical storage reservation outside the active roster.

## Player selection guide

The short descriptions below describe the **target roster**. Open a Soul for its current implementation class, full rotation, equipment, approved actions, unlock schedule, opportunity cost and boss test. Huntsman and Flintlock include their distinct main-branch baselines.

| Job | Soul | How you play | Why pick it |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAR | [Commander](jobs/war.md#commander) | Read the party's next action. Share one WAR stance through Warcry, mark the enemy before a chain, or spend the same animation window on a defensive formation. | A planned group chain or knockback. |
| WAR | [Berserker](jobs/war.md#berserker) | Fit a string of physical WS into enhanced WAR buffs. Choose when the defense loss and Savage Cleave recoil are affordable. | Short physical burn with healing available. |
| WAR | [Spartan](jobs/war.md#spartan) | Brace into a telegraphed impact with polearm and buckler. Build Grit from pressure and Impaling Thrust, then time a WS to renew Iron Resolve. | A telegraphed heavy hit with a buckler. |
| MNK | [Sensei](jobs/mnk.md#sensei) | Let a staff parry or evade an attack to riposte, recover HP and gain threat. Choose Counterstance or Dodge for the incoming string. | Repeated parryable or evasible melee. |
| MNK | [Chi Weaver](jobs/mnk.md#chi-weaver) | Leave both hands empty. Land Light Chi bolts on the engaged enemy to pulse healing around it. Decide whether to spend Boost early on Chi Blast or keep building while smaller pulses suffice. | Allies clustered around an engaged enemy. |
| MNK | [Black Belt](jobs/mnk.md#black-belt) | Stay in H2H range, line Boost up with a WS and use kicks, crits and double attacks to get back to the next WS. Use Chakra to regain tempo after a disabling effect. | Sustained uninterrupted melee with fast TP recovery. |
| WHM | [Bishop](jobs/whm.md#bishop) | Pre-cure a threatened ally under Solace. Work a Smite or eligible WS between cures to recover MP and build Sacred Grace for group sustain. | One ally about to take a lethal hit. |
| WHM | [Zealot](jobs/whm.md#zealot) | Alternate between fighting in staff reach for MP and stepping clear to land a heavy single-target Holy cast. A miss, WS, additional effect or offhand strike never earns the melee refund. | A long fight with alternating melee and divine-cast windows. |
| WHM | [Valkyrie](jobs/whm.md#valkyrie) | Name an ally as your intervention target. When a boss interrupts that ally's cast, decide whether to escort the retry immediately or save the intervention for a dangerous later cast. | A vulnerable caster repeatedly interrupted by enemy hits. |
| BLM | [Astromancer](jobs/blm.md#astromancer) | Probe elemental resistance, move into position, then commit to one very large Ancient Magic impact. Read the target's shared Astral Saturation before starting the cast. | One brief elemental burst check on an unsaturated boss. |
| BLM | [Channeler](jobs/blm.md#channeler) | Choose one caster and lend it a short, interruptible casting conduit. The Channeler gives up its own casting opportunity while the ally lines up one expensive spell. | One ally whose cast time or MP is limiting. |
| BLM | [Necromancer](jobs/blm.md#necromancer) | Select an undead warrior to hold hate or a caster to pressure a target. Use scythe WS to sustain the thrall and order its appropriate action for the phase. | One slot must alternate between a proxy tank and caster pet. |
| RDM | [Crimson Duelist](jobs/rdm.md#crimson-duelist) | Fence with an empty offhand. Maintain different enfeebles for Hex-Mitigation and parry. Spend a sword WS's Crimson Flow on the most urgent ward or enfeeble. | A boss susceptible to several different enfeebles. |
| RDM | [Royal Tactician](jobs/rdm.md#royal-tactician) | Read the next enemy melee or spell action and choose who receives your response. A landed Slow or Paralyze opens a short order to one frontliner; a landed Silence or Blind opens a different order for a caster. | A mixed party needing different individual buffs mid-fight. |
| RDM | [Scarlet Weaver](jobs/rdm.md#scarlet-weaver) | Choose a Yin or Yang aspect as the boss changes defenses. Yin exploits magical access from range. Yang commits your sword to normal physical contact. Stay in one aspect while the encounter favors it. | A boss alternating melee and magic vulnerabilities. |
| THF | [Trickster](jobs/thf.md#trickster) | Set up behind the right ally for a native Trick Attack transfer, then time a dagger/sword WS for the enemy's heavy attack window. | Planned physical mitigation and tight hate ordering. |
| THF | [Bandit](jobs/thf.md#bandit) | Exploit an existing Defense Down with a dagger or sword WS. Decide whether to take the current opportunity or delay for a party chain without wasting TP. | Another party member or gear reliably exposes defense. |
| THF | [Phantom](jobs/thf.md#phantom) | Open with a targeted challenge and frustrate physical swings. Each actual evasion reinforces the mob's focus. Choose when to use Perfect Dodge versus trusting natural evasion. | Fast enemy attack rounds and little unavoidable magic. |
| PLD | [Templar](jobs/pld.md#templar) | Keep Majesty active. Land club strikes to beacon the lowest ally, then decide whether splash cures or a shield-assisted emergency JA suit the next incoming hit. | Frontline triage and clustered Majesty cures. |
| PLD | [Defender](jobs/pld.md#defender) | Stand in reach of allies with club and shield. Interception and blocks build Zeal. Spend Zeal on the next Flash, Shield Bash, qualifying club WS or Judgment Gavel. | Exposed party members within interception range. |
| PLD | [Oathbreaker](jobs/pld.md#oathbreaker) | Before the pull choose Condemn for sustained great-sword punishment or Absolve for a riposte after you personally take a meaningful hit. Commit to the choice through that encounter. | A predictable fight with a stable offensive or reactive assignment. |
| DRK | [Reaper](jobs/drk.md#reaper) | Watch the boss complete a damaging spell or TP move. Put a deliberate scythe execution into the short recovery window. Save it for low HP only if the next opening is close. | A boss with frequent readable action completions. |
| DRK | [Dreadnought](jobs/drk.md#dreadnought) | Use scythe, Drain II and timed Absorbs to overcap HP and build Blood Tithe. Put up Dread Spikes before the physical string so the buffer and returns carry the tank. | Sustained physical hits from a drainable boss. |
| DRK | [Scourge](jobs/drk.md#scourge) | Choose a single curse for the boss behavior that matters now. Maintain it through a phase or overwrite it to answer a different threat. | One recurring source of boss damage or healing dominates. |
| BRD | [Adventurer](jobs/brd.md#adventurer) | Keep useful songs rolling, dual-wield dagger/sword to build Cadence and release the sonic follow-up and Encore with a WS. | A melee slot must keep songs up during sustained contact. |
| BRD | [Musician](jobs/brd.md#musician) | Protect the party's buff songs, then time a Threnody, Requiem or Elegy to burst. Choose when to detonate three Resonance stacks with Grand Finale. | Frequent allied skillchains and time to sing. |
| BRD | [Minstrel](jobs/brd.md#minstrel) | Cycle hostile songs for hate and defensive songs for personal mitigation. Use Dissonant Chord when a healer pulls attention and Iron Aria before heavy damage. | Ranged threat while sustaining personal defensive songs. |
| RNG | [Sniper](jobs/rng.md#sniper) | Plant at a safe firing lane and aim at one claimed target. Move to escape a telegraph or hold still for a single accurate shot. Normal ranged shots cover downtime. | A clear firing lane and few mandatory movements. |
| RNG | [Huntsman](jobs/rng.md#huntsman) | On main a ranged WS feeds the pet. The redesign moves the master into axe range. Direct a Tusker to hold a punishing string or a Saurian to snap threat, then stay close enough for Beastguard. | A pet must hold hate while its fragile master directs it. |
| RNG | [Arcane Archer](jobs/rng.md#arcane-archer) | Choose an element, land one arrow and tell a party member to follow with that element. A mismatched hit leaves the mark available for the intended ally. | One ally can land a specific elemental follow-up. |
| SAM | [Ronin](jobs/sam.md#ronin) | Keep Seigan and Third Eye up. Read deferred Mushin Stagger, then purify with a Tachi WS, Meditate or Blade Bash before the next blow. | Large hits that leave time to clear deferred damage. |
| SAM | [Imperial Archer](jobs/sam.md#imperial-archer) | Open a Tachi-to-bow sequence, then use Drawn Oisoya and Cosmic Brand to feed the return Tachi WS. Match your two WS timings to allies. | A chain needs both archery and Tachi steps. |
| SAM | [Warlord](jobs/sam.md#warlord) | Support the party with Hasso and Ashura's Edge while closing chains. Choose when Hagakure's draw-slash or Onimaru Tithe will maintain the aura through the next window. | Melee allies can exploit a closer's sustained aura. |
| NIN | [Shinobi](jobs/nin.md#shinobi) | Time Ichi and Ni to maintain up to five short-lived attackers. Save Shadow Legion for living clones on a stable target and Phantom Assault for clones with time to swing. | Rebuild windows between boss area attacks. |
| NIN | [Kagemusha](jobs/nin.md#kagemusha) | Refresh defensive Utsusemi and use broken images to accumulate Ash and spread Shadow Smoke. Time a katana WS to restore shadows and HP before a dangerous hit. | Absorbable single hits with time to restore images. |
| NIN | [Nightblade](jobs/nin.md#nightblade) | Watch the enemy's telegraph and choose whether to interrupt a spell or weaken a physical technique. A premature ninjutsu leaves no protection for the real cast. | Slow, readable enemy casts or techniques. |
| DRG | [Lancer](jobs/drg.md#lancer) | Choose a safe launch lane, then leap toward one locked target for a heavy polearm impact from the correct approach distance. In cramped fights stay close and use ordinary polearm WS. | Melee burst after a safe approach from distance. |
| DRG | [Skyborn](jobs/drg.md#skyborn) | Hold hate on the ground, then time a short aerial guard to soften one telegraphed hit and bring the boss back onto you at landing. | One predictable incoming technique while the tank retains hate. |
| DRG | [Wyvernheart](jobs/drg.md#wyvernheart) | Jump and use Spirit Link to manage Wyrmscale. A polearm WS triages the lowest ally. Time Restoring Breath while the wyvern is alive for group recovery. | Emergency spot healing with a living wyvern. |
| BLU | [Warmage](jobs/blu.md#warmage) | Select and set a compact Blue Magic loadout, then match blunt, slashing or magical attacks to the boss's changing resistance. Knowing when to change your answer matters more than alternating casts. | A prepared Blue Mage can exploit changing damage resistances. |
| BLU | [Magus](jobs/blu.md#magus) | Strike with two weapons to build Azure Catalyst. Decide between a healing Blue spell, a WS-triggered wave, or Osmotic Conduit during uninterrupted contact. | Repeated group damage with opportunities to dual-wield. |
| BLU | [Azure Bastion](jobs/blu.md#azure-bastion) | Cast tagged physical Blue Magic to layer Chitin. Decide whether a sword/club WS's free instant Blue spell should restore, defend or take pressure off the group. | Tanking demands flexible Blue Magic responses. |
| COR | [Flintlock](jobs/cor.md#flintlock) | Main uses Quick Draw to mark a target, a gun WS to detonate it, and a self-only Deadeye Chamber. The separate twin-pistol target is sustained waves of lighter shots on one enemy. | A single target allows long firing uptime. |
| COR | [Gambler](jobs/cor.md#gambler) | Keep three reliable rolls active. Stand on the safe floor or ante 10% current HP and 150 TP on Double-Up for a Lucky/XI reward, with personal debt on bust. | Reliable party rolls plus a voluntary cooldown gamble. |
| COR | [Buccaneer](jobs/cor.md#buccaneer) | Use a sword WS and pistol order at close range to build Grapeshot. Choose the Quick Draw element for Salt-Spray utility and turn stacks into a Stoneskin buffer. | Close-range gun control and timed personal shielding. |
| DNC | [Vampire](jobs/dnc.md#vampire) | Use Drain Samba, hits and Steps for group sustain, converting overheal to Crimson Veil. Spend Finishing Moves with dagger/throwing WS when someone needs a burst or cleanse. | A long damageable target supports MP-free group sustain. |
| DNC | [Bladewheel](jobs/dnc.md#bladewheel) | Set Throwing Stance to fight with a chakram. Keep Dazes and Samba active at range, then decide when to spend Finishing Moves on a throwing finisher. | Melee is hazardous but Dazes and Sambas need upkeep. |
| DNC | [Mirage Sentinel](jobs/dnc.md#mirage-sentinel) | Maintain Fan Dance with dual daggers. Parry, Step, then choose the Flourish that pulls, interrupts or converts moves into an Aegis Waltz shield. | Frequent single-target hate snaps and short interrupts. |
| SCH | [Medic](jobs/sch.md#medic) | Summon Eos, maintain Light Arts and spend fast, safe Sublimation between group Cure/Regen casts. Let Eos clean minor statuses while you triage the next hit. | Repeated group wounds and removable status effects. |
| SCH | [Sage](jobs/sch.md#sage) | Keep Titania active, lay down the right enhanced Helix under Dark Arts, then use Ebullience-driven elemental spells while the DoT works. | Long fights with forced movement and durable targets. |
| SCH | [Archivist](jobs/sch.md#archivist) | Observe an enemy action before writing a countermeasure for its next occurrence. Predict which party member will need it, then spend the prepared page before impact. | A known enemy repeats a specific move on a predictable victim. |
| BST | [Untamed](jobs/bst.md#untamed) | Fight beside an offensive jug beast on the same target. Land an axe WS to feed its TP, then time the beast's real Ready move to return TP to the master. Either can act first. | Both can stay on one target and land actual Ready attacks. |
| BST | [Alpha Warden](jobs/bst.md#alpha-warden) | Keep a guardian alive while the master holds the boss. Let Soul-Link split incoming master damage and route pet threat back to the master. Time Soul Bond and Reward before linked damage endangers the beast. | The master must hold hate while the beast shares damage. |
| BST | [Beastspeaker](jobs/bst.md#beastspeaker) | Read a monster's family and watch its behavior. Send the beast to expose the appropriate physical opening, then call the damage type your melee allies should exploit. | Physical allies can coordinate their swings to an observed weakness. |
| SMN | [Primalist](jobs/smn.md#primalist) | Choose an avatar for a coming elemental phase, commit its next Rage and hold the pact until resistance favors it. A swap changes the damage option but costs preparation time. | Elemental vulnerabilities rotate between boss phases. |
| SMN | [Celestial](jobs/smn.md#celestial) | Keep an avatar deployed to maintain Sanctuary, choose the Ward for the incoming pressure and use Siphon or eligible WS for Starlight Mend. | Grouped allies need a Ward response and continuous sustain. |
| SMN | [Herald](jobs/smn.md#herald) | Select an avatar, announce its accord before your party acts, and coordinate several allies to follow that element during one short window. | Several allies can follow an announced element together. |
| PUP | [Vanguard](jobs/pup.md#vanguard) | Deploy Valoredge as the actual tank. Direct Strobe, shield responses and Hydraulic Slam, then use H2H WS and Repair to keep the automaton standing. | A controllable shield automaton must absorb the encounter. |
| PUP | [Animist](jobs/pup.md#animist) | Choose Light, Water or Earth Maneuver for a direct heal, cleanse or ward. Attack with H2H to pulse the maneuver and feed Aether Cells, then decide when to Overhaul. | The healer needs a pet-delivered cure, cleanse or ward on demand. |
| PUP | [Pugilist](jobs/pup.md#pugilist) | Use a master H2H WS to feed TP and create a Tandem Opening. Prepare the frame and maneuvers so its next real WS catches the opening and extends the team's chain. | A party needs deliberate master-to-frame skillchain timing. |
| RUN | [Spellblade](jobs/run.md#spellblade) | Choose a rune element, attack with a great sword and use Effusion Slash to charge Conduction. Spend that preparation on Runic Bolt or Sever during a burst window. | Great-sword uptime can prepare a personal magic burst. |
| RUN | [Lifebinder](jobs/run.md#lifebinder) | Choose three runes for the next Vivacious Pulse. Swing for Runic Lifetap, cover ongoing harm with party Regen, and hold a rune combination that answers the next status or damage event. | A frontline party needs different healing or cleanse outcomes. |
| RUN | [Inquisitor](jobs/run.md#inquisitor) | Read whether the incoming string is physical or magical, hold the matching runes and switch between Iron Battuta and Runic Aegis. Use Convergence at the right transition. | Physical and elemental boss phases alternate. |
| GEO | [Diviner](jobs/geo.md#diviner) | Place one convergence anchor and stand at the other end of a corridor. Move to preserve the line between yourself and the anchor as the fight shifts. | The party can hold a line whose endpoints you control. |
| GEO | [Verdant](jobs/geo.md#verdant) | Position a luopan for a group that can stay in its sanctuary. Renew the luopan with Cure, Indi/Geo magic or a qualifying WS to trigger a group surge. Move when the encounter forces the party out. | A group can stand within a healing sanctuary. |
| GEO | [Terramancer](jobs/geo.md#terramancer) | Mark the predicted position of one boss, then keep it inside the fault until the eruption. Decide between a quick shallow fault and a delayed deeper strike. | The boss will stay still for a delayed single-target detonation. |
