# Scourge (DRK) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Single-curse boss suppressor.** Scourge chooses one curse at a time to blunt the most dangerous part of an enemy's kit.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Scourge over broad party buffers when one enemy behavior is particularly dangerous and needs surgical suppression.

## Setup & Gear
- Magic Accuracy and safe time to cast one focused Dark Magic suppression.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Use Withering against dangerous TP attacks or Ash against threatening spell output.
2. **Act:** At Lv30+, choose Famine for bosses whose ordinary self-healing is the problem.
3. **Adapt:** Renew or change the curse to match the boss phase; don't treat it as a universal debuff stack.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- A target can hold one global Scourge curse; curses share a 12s recast and last 24s.
- Damage curses suppress their category by 8/8/12/15%; Famine suppresses non-scripted self-heals by 20/25/30%. Hard immunity offers a limited one-hit half-strength fallback.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't stack three curses or expect scripted heals and mechanics to be invalidated.
- Only the actions actually granted to this Soul should be treated as part of its kit.
