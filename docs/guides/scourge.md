# Scourge (DRK) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Scourge is a Tier 3 **single-target curse support** Soul. Rather than stacking every possible debuff, you choose **one curse** that blunts the enemy action currently threatening the group. **Withering** reduces physical enemy techniques; **Ash** reduces damaging spells; and **Famine** reduces enemy self-healing once unlocked. A fully dark-immune target may instead receive a brief one-use **immunity ward**, so even an explicit immunity can create a narrower defensive opportunity.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Scourge Soul Crystal or use `!spec set scourge` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapon:** Scythe is the eligible weapon-skill family. Scourge's curse kit works independently of weapon-skill talent investment and does not require a specific subjob.
- **Stats:** Prioritize Magic Accuracy and sustainable MP to apply curses; the Soul grants growing Magic Accuracy, Max MP and Max HP through level progression.
- **Party coordination:** Determine whether the dangerous enemy relies on direct physical TP moves, damaging spells or self-healing. You can only keep one active curse type on a given target at once.
- **Spell economy:** Curses cost 10 MP each, have a one-second cast and share a 12-second recast. Avoid changing types without a reason.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Identify the incoming threat:** If a boss's physical technique is the major danger, select **Withering**. Against a dangerous damaging spell, choose **Ash**.
2. **Apply the chosen curse:** Cast the chosen curse on a living enemy. A full landing lasts up to 24 seconds; a partial landing lasts 12 seconds. An outright resist leaves any prior curse in place.
3. **Sustain the defense:** Keep the curse active on the enemy whose dangerous action you need to dampen. The curse protects eligible matches, not ordinary attacks or unrelated spell categories.
4. **Recover MP with Rite of Malice:** If your current enemy bears one of your active curses or wards and you are missing MP, activate **Rite of Malice** for a level-scaled MP recovery. It has a 24-second recast.
5. **Unlock Famine at Lv. 30:** Use **Famine** instead when an enemy repeatedly heals itself through normal combat healing. It weakens the enemy's own eligible recovery rather than blocking healing from other actors or scripted phase resets.
6. **Handle explicit dark immunity:** A fully immune target can receive an **8-second ward** at half the normal curse potency if the matching curse is not already active and your ward cooldown is ready. The next matching qualifying hit or heal can consume the ward.
7. **Change curses as phases change:** You can replace a curse with a different type when the boss starts using a new threatening action. Because curses share a 12-second recast, plan before the shift.
8. **Use your HUD:** Monitor the current target's curse type and duration, any immunity ward type and time, shared curse cooldown, and Rite of Malice cooldown.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Withering and Ash at Lv. 10–29:** Reduce matching eligible physical techniques or damaging spells by **8%**.
- **Level 30–49:** Withering/Ash remain at **8%**; Famine unlocks and reduces eligible enemy self-healing by **20%**.
- **Level 50–69:** Withering/Ash increase to **12%**; Famine to **25%**.
- **Level 70–75:** Withering/Ash reach **15%**; Famine reaches **30%**.
- **Curse duration and resistance:** Full landing lasts **24 seconds**, partial resistance can result in **12 seconds**, and a full resist does not replace an existing curse.
- **Shared recast and cost:** Withering, Ash and Famine each cost **10 MP** and share a **12-second recast**.
- **Scope of curses:** Withering affects qualifying physical/ranged enemy special techniques, Ash affects qualifying damaging magical spell actions, and Famine affects the enemy's own qualifying combat heal.
- **Immunity ward:** When explicitly dark-immune, a target can receive an **8-second**, one-use matching ward at half potency. Wards have their own **24-second internal cooldown** and require that an equivalent active curse is absent.
- **Rite of Malice:** Restores **14/18/22/28 MP** at Lv. 10/30/50/70, up to missing MP, with a 24-second recast and a live owned curse/ward on your current enemy.
- **Passive progression:** Magic Accuracy +10/18/28/38, Max MP +6/10/14/18% and Max HP +4/6/8/10% at the same progression brackets.
- **Soul HUD:** Shows current curse category, duration, any ward category/duration, shared curse recast and Rite cooldown.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Trying to stack all three curses:** A successful replacement changes the active curse type; choose the threat that currently matters most.
- **Using Withering on basic swings:** It is tuned for eligible enemy physical special moves, not every ordinary melee attack.
- **Expecting Ash to stop all magic:** It reduces matching direct damaging spell events; it does not silence or universally block the enemy.
- **Using Famine for scripted healing:** Phase resets and healing supplied by another actor do not satisfy its self-heal restriction.
- **Assuming dark immunity gives a full-strength permanent curse:** The immunity ward is weaker, lasts only eight seconds and is consumed by one matching event.
- **Using Rite without an owned effect:** It requires your current enemy to carry your curse or ward and you must be missing MP.
