# Scourge (DRK) - Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Scourge is a Tier 3 support Soul. Pick the enemy action that endangers your party most and keep one curse on that enemy.

## Setup & Gear

- Equip the Scourge crystal or use `!spec set scourge` at Lv. 10. A Scythe is the eligible weapon for weapon skills. Curses function with ordinary gear and zero weapon skill talent points.
- Prioritize Magic Accuracy and MP. Passives scale at Lv. 10/30/50/70+.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Cast **Withering** at Lv. 10 to reduce physical TP move and weaponskill damage. Cast **Ash** instead when damaging spells are the bigger threat.
2. Use **Rite of Malice** while engaged with an enemy you cursed or warded to recover MP.
3. At Lv. 30, use **Famine** against enemies that heal themselves with standard Cures or healing moves.
4. Watch the HUD. Refresh the curse as it expires, or replace it when the boss changes which attack it relies on.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- Each curse costs 10 MP, has a one-second base cast and shares a 12-second recast. A full landing lasts 24 seconds, a partial landing lasts 12 seconds, and a full resist leaves the existing curse in place.
- Withering and Ash reduce their matching damage by 8/8/12/15% at Lv. 10/30/50/70+. Famine reduces matching heals by 20/25/30% at Lv. 30/50/70+.
- Explicit dark immunity can provide an eight-second ward at half potency for the next matching hit on your party, or the next matching enemy self-heal. Wards have a 24-second personal cooldown and require that the same matching curse is absent.
- Rite of Malice recovers 14/18/22/28 MP at Lv. 10/30/50/70+ on a 24-second recast. A live enemy must carry your effect.

## Common Mistakes

- Coordinate with other Scourges. A successful curse replaces the enemy's current curse.
- Match Withering to physical special moves. Ordinary attacks and damage-over-time effects are outside its scope.
- Use Famine against combat healing. Scripted resets, phase transitions and healing from other actors remain outside its scope.
