# Spellblade (RUN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Spellblade transforms Rune Fencer into a **Great Sword + Elemental Spellweaving DPS** (`Damage` tier: 0% damage penalty, 1.00x WS scaling, A-rank Elemental Magic, A+ Greatsword). Your auto-attacks and `Effusion Slash` fire elemental **Rune Edge** strikes and build **Conduction stacks (0–3)**, which reduce the cast time and MP cost of **`Runic Bolt` & `Runic Sever`** to unleash **Magic Bursts** and ignite **Edgeflare**!

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon:** **Great Sword in Main Hand.** Strictly required for `Rune Edge`, `Conduction` stacks, `Effusion Slash`, `Runic Bolt`, and `Runic Sever`.
- **Single-Cast Auto-Fill:** Casting a single Rune spell automatically fills **all unlocked Rune slots** (`1` @ Lv.5, `2` @ Lv.35, `3` @ Lv.65).

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Harbor Runes & Overcharge:** Cast 1 Rune matching the element you want to nuke/burst with. Pop `Overcharge Runes` (30s CD, 12s: `+15–40 MAB`, `+10–24% Attack%`, `+15–30 MB Bonus`, **2x Rune Edge damage**, refreshes Runes).
2. **Build `Conduction` (Max 3):** Swing with your Great Sword (`1st landed hit/round` fires elemental **Rune Edge** damage + restores MP + builds **+1 Conduction** — `+2` under `Elemental Sforzo`!) and weave `Effusion Slash` (20s CD, 4y: non-consuming GS arcane hit that can **Magic Burst**, gives `+100–250 TP`, **+1 Conduction**, and ignites **Edgeflare**).
3. **Spend `Conduction` on `Runic Bolt` / `Runic Sever`:** At **2–3 Conduction stacks**, cast **Runic Bolt** (Lv.10+, 8s CD) or **Runic Sever** (Lv.55+, 30s CD — best timed to **Magic Burst** off a Skillchain!). Consuming Conduction stacks cuts **-0.5s cast time & -15% MP cost per stack** while boosting damage (`+10%/+12%` per stack) AND refreshing **Edgeflare** (12s, **+25% Rune Edge damage**)!
4. **2-Hour & Gambit / Vallation:** Use **Swipe/Lunge** freely (they do NOT consume Runes and grant Edgeflare). At Lv.70+, `Gambit` (180s CD) consumes Conduction (without consuming Runes!) for +10% alliance elemental/SC damage (`10–22s`), and `Elemental Sforzo` enters **Runic Ascendance** (30s: +50% Rune Edge & 2 Conduction/round — note **no elemental immunity**!).

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Conduction (0–3 stacks):** Built by GS melee rounds (+1/+2) and `Effusion Slash` (+1). Consumed by `Runic Bolt`, `Runic Sever`, `Gambit`, and `Vallation`.
- **Edgeflare (12s):** Triggered by Bolt, Sever, Effusion Slash (Lv.30+), Swipe, or Lunge (+25% Rune Edge damage for that Rune element).

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **NEVER swap weapons or grips mid-fight / while holding Conduction:** Changing `Main` or `Sub` immediately wipes all Conduction stacks and cancels/fails `Runic Bolt` and `Runic Sever`!
- **Don't expect `Elemental Sforzo` to make you immune to magic:** On Spellblade, it is an offensive steroid (`Runic Ascendance`), NOT magic immunity.
- Disengaging for **30+ continuous seconds** clears Conduction, and switching Rune elements clears active Edgeflare.
