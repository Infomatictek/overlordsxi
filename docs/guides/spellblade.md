# Spellblade (RUN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Spellblade is a **Great Sword elemental damage Soul** built around the interaction of enchanted melee attacks, **Conduction**, and prepared offensive casting. Your chosen Rune determines the element of **Rune Edge**, while Great Sword strikes and **Effusion Slash** build Conduction for faster, more efficient **Runic Bolt** or **Runic Sever** casts. Spending those stacks creates **Edgeflare** and empowers the next series of enchanted swings. Unlike a conventional Rune Fencer tank, this specialization is built for damage and Magic Burst opportunities.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Spellblade Soul Crystal or use `!spec set spellblade` from Lv. 10.
- **Required weapon:** Main-hand a **Great Sword**. Switching Main or Sub equipment can erase Conduction and interrupt the active spellweaving setup.
- **Runes:** Choose a Rune matching your intended elemental offense. One Rune enchantment fills the currently available slots: one at early levels, two from Lv. 35, three from Lv. 65.
- **Stats:** Great Sword skill, Accuracy, INT, STR, Elemental and Enhancing Magic skill, Magic Attack Bonus and Magic Accuracy all contribute to a hybrid physical/magical loop.
- **Position:** Remain in melee reach to generate Conduction, but be ready to cast Runic Bolt or Sever in a safe window. Coordinate Magic Bursts with the party's skillchain sequence.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose your Rune element:** Set the Rune whose element you want Rune Edge and your next arcane cast to use. Reconsider the choice when the boss's resistance changes.
2. **Begin Great Sword attacks:** Your first qualifying landed hit each round adds elemental **Rune Edge** damage and MP recovery and generates **one Conduction stack**, to a maximum of three.
3. **Use Effusion Slash:** Strike within 4 yalms for a special arcane hit, TP return and another Conduction stack without consuming your current Rune enchantments. Its recast is 20 seconds.
4. **Choose the spender:** Cast **Runic Bolt** from Lv. 10 for a frequent shorter-recast attack, or **Runic Sever** from Lv. 55 for a larger committed hit. Both benefit from spending accumulated Conduction.
5. **Time the cast for a skillchain:** Runic Sever is especially valuable when you can land a **Magic Burst**. Spending Conduction shortens cast time, reduces MP cost and increases damage.
6. **Continue under Edgeflare:** A successful eligible arcane strike can trigger **Edgeflare**, increasing Rune Edge damage for 12 seconds. Immediately resume Great Sword attacks to use the increased elemental swing damage.
7. **Use Overcharge Runes for a burst:** Activate **Overcharge Runes** before a sustained burst window to enhance Rune Edge, offensive magic and Rune duration. It lasts 12 seconds and has a 30-second recast.
8. **Use specialized cooldowns knowingly:** **Swipe** and **Lunge** support elemental damage without consuming Runes in this kit. Later, **Gambit** can spend Conduction for a group elemental/skillchain payoff, and **Runic Ascendance** improves the damage loop in place of a conventional immunity effect.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Conduction:** Up to **three stacks**; generally one per qualifying landed Great Sword attack round, or two during Runic Ascendance. Effusion Slash also adds a stack.
- **Runic Bolt:** Lv. 10, **2-second base cast**, **8-second recast**, approximately 8–24 MP by level, and a 10-yalm cast range.
- **Runic Sever:** Lv. 55, **3-second base cast**, **30-second recast**, 40 MP and a 10-yalm range. Stronger single-target damage and Magic Burst synergy.
- **Stack spending:** Conduction can reduce eligible spell cast time by **0.5 second per stack**, MP cost by **15% per stack**, and improve Bolt/Sever damage. Bolt gains about **10%** and Sever **12%** damage per spent stack.
- **Rune Edge:** An added elemental strike on the first landed Great Sword attack in a round, scaling with weapon delay, skill and Rune count. Overcharge doubles its special damage.
- **Effusion Slash:** **20-second recast**, 4-yalm Great Sword strike with element matching the newest Rune; it can Magic Burst and returns **100/150/200/250 TP** with progression.
- **Edgeflare:** A short **12-second** effect increasing Rune Edge damage by **25%**. Eligible arcane hits, Swipe and Lunge can trigger or refresh it.
- **Overcharge Runes:** **30-second recast**, **12-second** self-buff with scaling Magic Attack, Attack and Magic Burst bonuses, plus refreshed Rune durations.
- **Resource loss:** Weapon swaps can remove Conduction; long disengagement can also clear it. Element swaps can terminate active Edgeflare.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Changing Main/Sub gear during setup:** This can erase saved Conduction and invalidate the prepared Bolt/Sever cast.
- **Casting without stacks when melee is available:** You miss the damage, cast-time and MP advantages of Conduction.
- **Letting Edgeflare idle:** Its value depends on returning to enchanted melee attacks during the short window.
- **Choosing the wrong Rune for an enemy's resistance:** The enhanced physical/magic loop still depends on elemental matchup.
- **Assuming Runic Ascendance grants magic immunity:** Spellblade's specialized ultimate is an offensive mode, not the usual protective effect.
- **Waiting too long between exchanges:** Extended disengagement can clear your accumulated Conduction.
