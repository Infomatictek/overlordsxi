# Reaper (DRK) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Reaper is a Tier 1 **single-target Scythe damage** Soul that punishes an enemy immediately after it finishes an attack. A damaging enemy spell or TP move, or every sixth completed enemy melee round, opens a five-second **Recovery** window. You use that window for a heavy **Finality** strike and can add **Grave Toll** dark magic without consuming your personal Finality opportunity. This Soul rewards tracking enemy actions and responding quickly, rather than simply using every offensive ability on cooldown.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Reaper Soul Crystal or use `!spec set reaper` from Lv. 10.
- **Weapon:** Equip a **Scythe**. Finality requires this weapon and targets an enemy within 4 yalms; Scythe is also its eligible weapon-skill family.
- **Stats:** Accuracy and STR help Finality land and hit hard. Attack and Scythe skill become stronger through passive progression; appropriate dark-magic accuracy also supports Grave Toll.
- **Battle awareness:** Keep track of your enemy's finished actions. Reaper gains the most from a predictable enemy attack cycle and frequent qualifying recovery openings.
- **Talent independence:** Finality and Grave Toll unlock at Lv. 10 and work with standard gear before investing in weapon-skill talents.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Engage with your Scythe:** Stand in melee range of one enemy while the tank controls its position.
2. **Watch for Recovery:** When the enemy finishes a damaging spell or TP move, or completes its sixth regular melee round, it opens a five-second Recovery window. Watch the Soul HUD rather than guessing.
3. **Use Finality:** While the window remains open, use **Finality** within 4 yalms for a much stronger physical Scythe strike. Finality has a 16-second recast.
4. **Cast Grave Toll opportunistically:** **Grave Toll** is a single-target dark spell with a 1.5-second cast, 12 MP cost and 12-second recast. It deals 50% more damage during Recovery but does not spend Finality's personal opening.
5. **Know when to use a weaker Finality:** Outside Recovery, Finality still deals baseline damage. If the next opening is unlikely before a recast would have passed, using it without the Recovery bonus can be reasonable.
6. **Finish weakened enemies:** Starting at Lv. 50, a Recovery Finality against an enemy below 25% HP gains another damage bonus; this grows at Lv. 70.
7. **Respect misses and mitigation:** Finality still goes on recast after a miss. A qualifying positive-damage hit consumes your personal opportunity for that particular Recovery window.
8. **Keep target discipline:** Recovery is associated with an enemy. A window on one quarry does not empower a strike against another.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Recovery trigger:** A qualifying damaging enemy spell/TP move or the sixth completed ordinary melee round opens five seconds of Recovery.
- **Finality at Lv. 10–29:** 1.3× normalized Scythe damage outside Recovery, or **2.3×** during Recovery.
- **Lv. 30–49:** Recovery Finality scales to **2.6×**, while the ordinary non-Recovery version remains at 1.3×.
- **Lv. 50–69:** Recovery Finality remains **2.6×**, gaining **15% extra damage** when the quarry is below 25% HP.
- **Lv. 70–75:** Recovery Finality improves to **3.0×**, with a **20% extra** below-25%-HP finishing bonus.
- **Finality activation:** A 4-yalm Scythe ability with a 16-second recast. It follows hit and damage-mitigation checks; a successful Recovery hit uses your personal opportunity for that enemy's current window.
- **Grave Toll:** Ranged single-target dark magic, 12 MP, 1.5-second cast and 12-second recast. During Recovery it gains 50% damage. It **does not heal** and **does not consume** a Finality opening.
- **Individual opportunity:** More than one Reaper can benefit from the same enemy's Recovery, but each Reaper's successful Finality may spend that Reaper's own opportunity.
- **Level-scaled passives:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, Reaper receives Scythe skill +12/22/34/46, STR +6/12/18/25, Accuracy +10/18/28/40 and Attack +12/24/38/54.
- **Soul HUD:** Tracks remaining Recovery time if Finality is still available, enemy melee-round progress and Finality's remaining cooldown.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Swinging Finality before a Recovery window:** The same button deals substantially less damage outside the opening.
- **Assuming any enemy action qualifies:** Interrupted spells, non-damaging actions and periodic damage do not create a qualifying Recovery window.
- **Using Finality from outside 4 yalms:** It is a close-range Scythe action.
- **Confusing Grave Toll with a health-drain spell:** It deals dark damage and does not restore HP.
- **Treating a miss as a free retry:** A missed Finality still spends its 16-second ability recast.
- **Swapping targets just as an opening appears:** Recovery and each personal spent opportunity are tracked against the relevant enemy.
