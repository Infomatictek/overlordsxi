# Reaper (DRK) - Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Reaper is a Tier 1 Scythe damage Soul. Watch the enemy's attacks and land **Finality** during the five seconds when it is recovering.

## Setup & Gear

- Select Reaper at Lv. 10 with `!spec set reaper` or equip its Soul Crystal. Bring a Scythe and prioritize Accuracy and STR. Only Scythe weapon skills are eligible.
- **Finality** and **Grave Toll** both unlock at Lv. 10. Use them with ordinary gear even before you invest in weapon skill talents.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Engage with the group and watch the HUD for enemy melee rounds. An enemy spell or TP move that deals damage, or the sixth completed regular round, opens five seconds of recovery.
2. Use **Finality** within four yalms during recovery. It deals baseline damage outside recovery, so spend it if an opening is unlikely before the next use.
3. Cast **Grave Toll** for ranged dark damage. It gains 50% damage during recovery and leaves the opening available for Finality.
4. At Lv. 50, a recovery Finality gains additional damage against enemies below 25% HP. The finishing bonus increases at Lv. 70.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- Finality has a 16-second recast and scales from 1.3× baseline damage to 2.3/2.6/2.6/3.0× in recovery at Lv. 10/30/50/70+. The sub-25% bonus is 15% at Lv. 50 and 20% at Lv. 70.
- A miss starts Finality's recast. A hit that costs the enemy HP uses your opening. Each Reaper has their own opportunity against the same enemy.
- Grave Toll has a base 1.5-second cast, 12-second recast and a 12 MP cost. It does not restore HP.

## Common Mistakes

- Wait for a damaging enemy action to finish. An interrupted spell or periodic poison tick does not open recovery.
- Track the enemy you plan to strike. An opening on one enemy does not empower Finality against another.
