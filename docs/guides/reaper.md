# Reaper (DRK) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Scythe recovery executioner.** Reaper punishes enemy recovery windows with a dedicated Scythe strike; execution damage grows late in the fight.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Reaper when scripted boss recovery offers opportunities to finish phases decisively.

## Setup & Gear
- Scythe, Accuracy, safe melee access; no Dreadnought Blood-Pool tanking engine.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Keep fighting normally while observing the enemy's recovery openings.
2. **Act:** Use Finality during the brief five-second recovery opportunity to gain its large single-target bonus.
3. **Adapt:** At low target HP, execute harder; missing still triggers personal recovery, so timing and accuracy matter.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Finality is a 4y, 16s action: 1.3x by default and 2.3/2.6/2.6/3.0x during recovery.
- Below 25% target HP, correctly timed landed recovery strikes gain an additional 15% at Lv50 or 20% at Lv70; periodic ticks do not create openings.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't use Finality blindly into recovery downtime or mistake this for Dreadnought's HP-sustain tank.
- Base-job access does not guarantee every spell or ability is approved for this Soul.
