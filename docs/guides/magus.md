# Magus (BLU) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Dual-Wield Azure Combat Healer.** Magus uses two weapons and healing Blue Magic to sustain nearby allies. Melee attacks and weapon skills feed Azure Catalyst, while healing Blue Magic spreads across the party.

**Why play Magus?** It rewards staying engaged and weaving Blue Magic into melee, unlike Chi Weaver's ranged Chi pulse healing, Templar's Club/Shield Cure splashes or Azure Bastion's Blue Magic tanking.

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapons:** Dual-wield compatible Sword or Club setup, starting at Lv10.
- **Priority:** MND, Cure Potency, Blue Magic and weapon accuracy, with sufficient MP for emergency healing.
- **Positioning:** Keep wounded allies within 15y for healing Blue Magic diffusion.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Engage safely:** Use a dual-wield weapon setup and keep attacking while maintaining adequate MP.
2. **Heal with Blue Magic:** Healing Blue Magic splashes to nearby party members at 85% potency.
3. **Build Catalyst:** Land dual-wield strikes to generate Azure Catalyst stacks.
4. **Release healing pressure:** At five stacks, or through an eligible Sword/Club weaponskill, activate an Azure Mending Wave party heal and MP refund.
5. **Cover emergencies:** Use White Wind Surge for strong area healing and cleansing, or Osmotic Conduit to reduce magic damage while delivering HP/MP support through attacks.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Azure Catalyst (max 5):** Melee-driven healing cadence; an eligible WS can also trigger Azure Mending Wave, which heals 12/16/20/24% maximum HP and restores 6% maximum MP.
- **Azure Mending:** Healing Blue Magic spreads in a 15y party area at 85% potency.
- **White Wind Surge:** 20s cooldown, 28% HP area heal, two-debuff cleanse and 15% Stoneskin.
- **Osmotic Conduit:** 25s cooldown, 12s of -10% magic damage taken and party HP/MP siphon benefits on successful attacks.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Do not expect the Magus's melee damage to match Warmage or another dedicated damage Soul.
- Do not sacrifice emergency casting for one more melee hit when the party is in danger.
- Do not confuse this healer's Catalyst with Azure Bastion's defensive Chitin Carapace.
