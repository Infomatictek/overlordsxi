# Commander (WAR) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Commander is a **frontline Warrior support Soul** whose main job is increasing the party's damage and keeping allies alive at critical moments. It selects **one** Warrior stance and uses **Warcry** to share a specialized version of that stance, marks a target with **Tactical Breach** to reward skillchains and Magic Bursts, and cycles targeted party defense, TP management and emergency cleansing. Commander contributes damage when possible, but its value comes from helping the entire group coordinate.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Commander Soul Crystal or use `!spec set commander` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** No exclusive weapon lock in the Commander support definition; use accessible Warrior weapons compatible with your preferred weapon skills and the party's skillchain plan.
- **Stats:** Accuracy, Haste and TP generation support active melee participation. HP, Defense and status resistance improve survival while operating on the frontline.
- **Party briefing:** Before the pull, decide which stance the group needs and who will close skillchains. Coordinate defensive abilities and Rallying Horn with the healer.
- **Position:** Several party support abilities affect a limited nearby radius; keep your relevant group within range rather than assuming they are global.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose one stance:** Activate **Berserk**, **Defender**, **Aggressor**, **Retaliation** or **Restraint** according to the group's current needs. Commander permits only one active Warrior stance at a time.
2. **Share the stance with Warcry:** Use **Warcry** after selecting the stance. It grants the party a specialized version of your current stance rather than stacking every stance simultaneously.
3. **Mark the priority quarry:** Apply **Tactical Breach** to the target that will receive coordinated weapon skills, skillchains and Magic Bursts. Its effect lasts 10 seconds with a 20-second recast.
4. **Call the damage window:** Encourage allies to complete their skillchains during Tactical Breach. The skillchain closer can earn extra TP, while skillchain, Magic Burst and critical damage receive the relevant bonuses.
5. **Layer group protection:** Use **Formation: Bulwark** when the party needs Phalanx-style mitigation, magic defense, status resistance and knockback protection.
6. **Support TP and healing flow:** Apply **Battle Cadence** to help the nearby party manage Subtle Blow, Conserve TP and healing received during a sustained fight.
7. **Save Rallying Horn for a real problem:** **Rallying Horn** removes qualifying crowd-control and stat debuffs, restores some HP and TP and grants temporary movement speed.
8. **Reassess by boss phase:** Change the selected stance between opportunities if the group shifts from damage to survival. Reapply Warcry as available and align Tactical Breach with the next skillchain.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Single stance:** Commander cannot keep multiple Warrior stances simultaneously. Warcry shares a stance-dependent special benefit to nearby allies.
- **Warcry combinations:** Berserk emphasizes Attack; Defender emphasizes Defense and mitigation; Aggressor helps Accuracy and Double Attack; Retaliation helps Counter and Store TP; Restraint supports weapon-skill damage and TP Bonus.
- **Tactical Breach:** **20-second recast**, **10-second target window**; scales skillchain damage by **12/18/24/30%**, Magic Burst and critical damage by **8/12/16/20%**, and can reward the skillchain closer with **100/150/200/250 TP** at Lv. 10/30/50/70.
- **Formation: Bulwark:** **25-second recast**, **15-second** nearby-party ward that improves Phalanx, Magic Defense, status resistance and knockback resistance.
- **Battle Cadence:** **20-second recast**, **12-second** group buff improving Subtle Blow, Conserve TP and Cure recovery.
- **Rallying Horn:** **45-second recast**, **12-second** group rescue. Removes Slow/Bind at Lv. 10, adds Weight/Paralysis at Lv. 30, Defense/Magic Defense Down at Lv. 50 and Attack/Accuracy Down at Lv. 70.
- **Rallying Horn restoration:** Heals **5/7/9/12% of party HP** and grants **75/125/175/250 TP** at Lv. 10/30/50/70, plus temporary movement speed.
- **Passive progression:** At Lv. 10/30/50+, Commander gains Max HP +5/8/12%, Attack +8/16/28, Accuracy +6/12/20 and Store TP +4/8/12.
- **Support tradeoff:** Commander has reduced personal damage compared with a pure DPS Soul. Choose actions that increase total party output rather than overvaluing personal hits.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Trying to stack all Warrior stances:** Activating a second stance cancels the first. Choose the effect your party actually needs.
- **Using Warcry before selecting a stance:** The shared effect depends on your current stance; coordinate the sequence.
- **Applying Tactical Breach long before a skillchain:** The important bonus window lasts only ten seconds.
- **Firing Rallying Horn only for minor damage:** Preserve it for meaningful group debuffs or a planned rescue.
- **Ignoring nearby party range:** Defensive buffs and Rallying Horn cannot help teammates who are outside the action's effective radius.
- **Treating Commander like a pure DPS Warrior:** Its support toolkit is the reason to bring the Soul.
