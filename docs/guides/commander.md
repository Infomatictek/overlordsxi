# Commander (WAR) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Commander turns Warrior into an active frontline **party buffer and Skillchain multiplier** (`Support` tier: -40% personal damage dealt, 0.35x WS scaling). Instead of dealing top personal DPS, you pick **one** Warrior stance (`Berserk`, `Defender`, `Aggressor`, `Retaliation`, or `Restraint`) to broadcast to your party with `Warcry` while cycling short-cooldown party defensive wards, Subtle Blow, cleanses, and Vulnerability debuffs.

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapon / Armor:** Any Warrior weapon or armor works — no weapon restrictions.
- **Role / Playstyle:** Focus on Haste, TP gain, and survivability so you can keep `Tactical Breach` and party auras rolling while feeding Skillchains.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Pick One Stance & Shout:** Activate **one** WAR stance matching what your party needs (`Aggressor` for +25 ACC/RACC & +8% DA, `Berserk` for +15% Attack, `Defender` for +20% DEF & -8% DT, `Retaliation` for +12% Counter & +10 Store TP, or `Restraint` for +12% WSD & +200 TP Bonus), then press `Warcry` (Lv.10+, 60s CD) to share it with your party.
2. **Mark Priority Targets:** Keep `Tactical Breach` (20s CD, 10s duration) on your kill target so your party deals +12–30% Skillchain damage, +8–20% Magic Burst & Crit damage, and refunds +100–250 TP to whoever closes a Skillchain.
3. **Rotate Party Buffs:** Weave `Formation: Bulwark` (25s CD, 15s party Phalanx + MDEF + Knockback/Status Resist) and `Battle Cadence` (20s CD, 12s party Subtle Blow + Conserve TP + Cure Potency Received) on cooldown.
4. **Cleanse & Rescue:** Save `Rallying Horn` (45s CD) for nasty AoE debuffs — it cleanses Slow/Bind (and Weight/Para @ 30+, DEF/MDEF Down @ 50+, Atk/Acc Down @ 70+), heals 5–12% party HP, and grants +75–250 TP + Move Speed.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Single-Stance Sharing:** Your `Warcry` reads whichever single WAR stance you currently have active and shares its Commander version with allies within 10y.
- **Reduced Self-Penalties:** Under Commander, `Defender` only lowers Attack by -10% (instead of -25%), `Aggressor` only lowers Evasion by -10, and `Retaliation` has no movement speed penalty.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't try to stack two stances:** Turning on a second WAR stance (`Berserk` + `Aggressor`, etc.) automatically cancels the first one. Pick one stance per pull/fight before using `Warcry`.
- **Don't ignore `Tactical Breach`:** A huge share of Commander's value comes from timing `Tactical Breach` (10s window) right before allies fire Weaponskills and Magic Bursts.
