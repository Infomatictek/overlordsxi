# Archivist (SCH) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Exact-action countermeasure scholar.** Archivist learns a specific enemy action and files a protective answer for the next occurrence of that exact action.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Archivist for rehearsed, repeatable boss mechanics; Bishop and Medic are better at general recovery.

## Setup & Gear
- MP, defensive positioning and close observation of a recurring boss action.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Use Record on a dangerous enemy ability to memorize its exact action ID.
2. **Act:** File Countermeasure onto an appropriate ally before the enemy repeats that attack.
3. **Adapt:** When the exact action returns, the matching page provides a one-time reduction; retain it if the enemy uses something else.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Record has a 6s cooldown and 60s memory; one recorded slot at Lv10–49 and two at Lv50+.
- File Countermeasure has a 15s cooldown and 20–25s window at 15–20y, reducing the next exact matching hit by 20/25/25/30%. Scripted HP-setting events bypass it.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't mistake a similar-looking attack for the identical recorded action.
- Confirm available commands and casting rules in the active Soul's menu before building a rotation.
