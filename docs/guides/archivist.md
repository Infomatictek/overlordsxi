# Archivist (SCH) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Archivist is a Tier 3 **countermeasure support** Soul. It observes a specific hostile spell or enemy technique, saves the exact action as a **Record**, and files a one-use **Countermeasure** on an ally expected to be hit by that same action again. This is precise encounter preparation, not blanket damage reduction. With good knowledge of recurring boss attacks, Archivist can protect the right ally at the right moment.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Archivist Soul Crystal or use `!spec set archivist` from Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Staff and Club are the eligible weapon-skill families. The observation and filing loop works with ordinary equipment, any subjob and zero weapon-skill talent points.
- **Stats and survival:** Max MP, Magic Accuracy and Max HP scale through the Soul's passives. Your primary value is successful observation and page placement rather than personal damage.
- **Position:** **Record** requires the enemy within 20 yalms. **File Countermeasure** can target yourself or a valid ally within 15 yalms initially, expanding to 20 yalms from Lv. 30.
- **Preparation:** Identify boss attacks that are likely to repeat and decide who is most at risk before placing your one active page.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Watch the dangerous enemy:** Target a living enemy within 20 yalms and activate **Record**. It watches for the next **completed hostile spell or technique** over the following 12 seconds.
2. **Confirm the recorded action:** When the enemy completes a qualifying action, Archivist stores its exact identity for up to 60 seconds. The HUD displays the selected record and expiration time.
3. **Choose the vulnerable ally:** Think about who will be hit by the same action next. A tank may need protection from a direct strike, while a caster might need it for a recurring targeted spell.
4. **File the page:** Cast **File Countermeasure** on yourself or one valid party member in range. It costs no MP and, on a successful filing, has a 15-second recast.
5. **Wait for the exact repeat:** The page activates when that same enemy and spawn resolves the recorded direct damaging action against your recipient. The protection then reduces that action's qualifying damage.
6. **Use Margin Note as filler:** **Margin Note** is a Lv. 10 single-target earth spell costing 4 MP with a 0.5-second cast and 6-second recast. Use it when no critical observation or filing is pending.
7. **At Lv. 50, manage two records:** Archivist can hold two different live actions. Use **Index Pages** (1-second recast) to switch which record will be filed next.
8. **Refill and adapt:** After a page is spent or expires, record and file another likely repeating action. A new page from you replaces your prior owned page on another recipient.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Record:** 20-yalm target range, 12-second observation window and 6-second recast. A saved record lasts 60 seconds and is tied to the exact enemy, spawn and action identity.
- **Level 10–29:** One record slot; 15-yalm filing range; page lasts **20 seconds** and reduces matching damage by **20%**.
- **Level 30–49:** One record slot; filing range rises to **20 yalms**; page remains 20 seconds and reduces matching damage by **25%**.
- **Level 50–69:** Two record slots and **Index Pages** unlocks; page reduction remains **25%** for 20 seconds.
- **Level 70–75:** Two record slots; page lifetime rises to **25 seconds** and reduction to **30%**.
- **Exact-match rule:** A page activates only against the recorded category/action from the recorded living enemy and spawn. Similar moves, another enemy or a later respawn are not equivalent.
- **Damage scope:** Pages reduce eligible direct physical, ranged, magical or breath damage. Normal melee swings, interrupted abilities, periodic effects and scripted HP changes are outside the recorded-action system.
- **Multiple Archivists:** When several eligible pages match the same damage event, the strongest percentage is applied. Weaker pages need not be consumed for that event.
- **One active page per owner:** Filing a different countermeasure replaces your previous owned page even if the first was placed on another ally.
- **Soul HUD:** Displays used record slots, current record name/time, live observation window, page duration, Record recast and File Countermeasure recast.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Recording a random harmless action:** Choose a move likely to recur and threaten an ally during the page's limited lifetime.
- **Assuming a page covers all attacks from that enemy:** It protects only the exact recorded action identity and valid direct damage.
- **Filing the page on the wrong teammate:** Target selection matters; another ally's matching hit will not use your page.
- **Expecting two records before Lv. 50:** The second slot and Index Pages are level-gated.
- **Ignoring record expiry:** A 60-second record cannot be filed indefinitely.
- **Assuming a new page stacks freely with your old one:** Archivist owns one currently filed page; the strongest matching page from different Archivists wins.
