# Archivist (SCH) - Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Archivist is a Tier 3 support Soul. Watch an enemy's move, record its exact identity, and write a countermeasure for the party member who needs it.

## Setup & Gear

- Equip the crystal or use `!spec set archivist` at Lv. 10. Staff and Club are the eligible weapon-skill families.
- **Record**, **File Countermeasure**, and **Margin Note** are available at Lv. 10. The support loop works with any subjob and zero weapon skill talent points.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Use **Record** on an enemy within 20 yalms. It watches the next completed hostile spell or technique for up to 12 seconds.
2. When the record appears in your HUD, cast **File Countermeasure** on yourself or a party member likely to face that attack again.
3. Refile after the page is spent or expires. Use **Margin Note** for single-target earth damage while observing.
4. From Lv. 50, keep two distinct records and use **Index Pages** to switch which one you will file. A new record selects itself.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

| Level | Record slots | Countermeasure range | Page duration | Reduction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | 1 | 15y | 20 s | 20% |
| 30 | 1 | 20y | 20 s | 25% |
| 50 | 2 | 20y | 20 s | 25% |
| 70–75 | 2 | 20y | 25 s | 30% |

- Record has a six-second recast. Records last 60 seconds. A repeat refreshes its entry.
- File Countermeasure costs no MP and has a 15-second recast on successful filing. One page per Archivist stays active.
- A page covers one matching action from the same enemy. If multiple pages match, the strongest protects the action and the others remain available.

## Common Mistakes

- Match the exact entry. Similar spells, different TP moves, a different enemy and a new spawn leave the page intact.
- Record recurring damage actions before committing the party to a plan. Normal swings, interrupted casts and scripted HP changes are outside the page's scope.
