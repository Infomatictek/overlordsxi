# Pugilist (PUP) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Pugilist is Puppetmaster's pure **Master + Automaton combo DPS** crystal (`Damage` tier: 0% damage penalty for both Master and Pet; Soulsoother healing AI disabled). You use **Hand-to-Hand Weaponskills** to feed **+350 TP** directly to your Valoredge or Sharpshot Automaton and paint the boss with **Tandem Opening**, amplifying your Automaton's next WS by **+25%** and extending Skillchain windows by +3s!

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon:** **Hand-to-Hand.** Needed for all Master & Pet Attack/R.Atk, Martial Arts/DA/Pet Haste, Crit Rate, -Enmity mods, and `Tandem Barrage`.
- **Forced Frame/Head:** Forces **Valoredge** (melee) or **Sharpshot** (ranged) Frame & Head (`Harlequin` / `Stormwaker` are switched automatically; Soulsoother Cures are disabled).

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Deploy DPS Automaton & Burst:** Summon **Valoredge** or **Sharpshot** (`15s CD`, 100% Overload Immunity) and `Deploy`. Pop `Limiter Release` (35s CD, 12s: **+22% ATK/RATK** & **+15% Double Attack / Rapid Shot** on BOTH you and your Automaton, `-15% DEF`).
2. **Synchronize TP with `Tandem Barrage`:** Hit `Tandem Barrage` (25s CD, 6y, H2H required) for a physical strike that gives **+300 TP to BOTH Master & Pet** and applies `Breach` (`-15% DEF` for 10s).
3. **Create `Tandem Opening` Before Pet WS:** Whenever you fire an **H2H Weaponskill**, your Automaton gains **+350 TP** AND the target receives an 8s **Tandem Opening** mark. When your Automaton consumes that mark with **String Shredder, Armor Shatterer, Arcuballista, or Bone Crusher**, it deals **+25% bonus damage** (`1.25x`) and extends active Skillchain windows by +3s!
4. **Manuever Spam in `Overdrive`:** Maintain Fire/Wind/Thunder Maneuvers (10s CD normally — **0s cooldown during `Overdrive`** with `+50 Pet Store TP` for 30s). Use `Repair` (30s CD, 0 oil) if Pet takes splash damage, and `Ventriloquy` (25s CD) to shed Master threat.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Tandem Opening (8s debuff on target):** Planted by Master's H2H Weaponskills (+350 Pet TP); consumed by your Automaton's String Shredder / Armor Shatterer / Arcuballista / Bone Crusher for **1.25x damage** + Skillchain extension.
- **0s Maneuver Overdrive:** During `Overdrive`, spam Fire/Wind/Thunder Maneuvers back-to-back with zero recast.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't unequip Hand-to-Hand:** You lose all Master/Pet DPS mods and `Tandem Barrage` immediately.
- **Don't expect Automaton Cures:** Pugilist blocks Cure I–VI on your Automaton so it stays focused purely on physical/ranged WS damage.
- **Time H2H WSs right before your Pet hits 1000 TP** so `Tandem Opening` (8s) is active when your Automaton fires its WS.
