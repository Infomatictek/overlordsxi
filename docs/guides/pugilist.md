# Pugilist (PUP) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Pugilist is a Tier 1 **Hand-to-Hand master-and-automaton damage** Soul. Both master and pet contribute sustained attacks, but your strongest opportunity comes from **Tandem Opening**: a master weapon skill feeds the automaton TP and marks an enemy for its next qualifying weapon skill. **Tandem Barrage** and **Limiter Release** add resource acceleration and burst pressure. This Soul relies on offensive Valoredge or Sharpshot behavior rather than automaton healing.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Pugilist Soul Crystal or use `!spec set pugilist` at Lv. 10.
- **Required weapon:** Use **Hand-to-Hand**. Much of the Soul's master and pet offense, its custom actions and special bonuses require this weapon family.
- **Automaton choice:** **Valoredge** is the close-range physical frame; **Sharpshot** is the ranged offensive frame. Other frames may be switched to an eligible offensive setup by the Soul.
- **Stats:** Accuracy, Attack, Martial Arts, Double Attack, critical rate, Pet Accuracy/Attack and Pet Haste support the synchronized loop.
- **Party role:** Coordinate with the tank so both you and your automaton can attack one stable target without being forced into repeated pet repositioning.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Summon and deploy:** Activate the automaton in its valid Pugilist setup, then **Deploy** it against the party's current quarry.
2. **Maintain maneuvers:** Use Fire, Wind or Thunder Maneuvers to support the offensive frame's behavior, as appropriate for its attachments and combat situation.
3. **Build TP on both combatants:** Use ordinary Hand-to-Hand attacks while the automaton builds its own TP. Avoid letting the pet idle on a different target.
4. **Apply Tandem Opening:** Land a **Hand-to-Hand weapon skill** on the current enemy. The special interaction gives **350 TP** to your automaton and opens an **8-second Tandem Opening** on that quarry.
5. **Have the pet capitalize:** During Tandem Opening, the automaton can consume the mark with **String Shredder, Armor Shatterer, Arcuballista or Bone Crusher** for **25% extra damage** and a longer applicable skillchain window.
6. **Use Tandem Barrage:** Activate **Tandem Barrage** on a close enemy when you and the pet are ready. It deals a physical hit, gives **300 TP each** and applies short **Defense Down**.
7. **Plan the Limiter Release burst:** **Limiter Release** grants a 12-second master/pet offense window, with increased Attack or Ranged Attack and attack frequency at the cost of Defense.
8. **Manage uptime and threat:** Use **Repair** for pet survival and **Ventriloquy** when the master needs to shed enmity. During **Overdrive**, exploit reduced Maneuver recasts and increased Pet Store TP.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Tandem Opening:** An **8-second** target-specific mark created by eligible master Hand-to-Hand weapon skills. The listed eligible automaton weapon skills consume it for **1.25× damage** and can extend an active skillchain window by **3 seconds**.
- **Pet TP injection:** Master Hand-to-Hand weapon skills add **350 TP** to the automaton, accelerating its next qualifying weapon skill.
- **Tandem Barrage:** **25-second recast**, requires Hand-to-Hand and a living automaton; damages one enemy within **6 yalms**, grants **300 TP** to master and pet, and applies **15% Defense Down for 10 seconds**.
- **Limiter Release:** **35-second recast**, **12-second duration**; grants both combatants **22% Attack/Ranged Attack** and extra Double Attack/Rapid Shot, with **15% Defense reduction** as the tradeoff.
- **Overdrive:** Special Pugilist synergy grants **50 Pet Store TP** and removes Maneuver recasts while active, allowing rapid maneuver changes.
- **Frame restrictions:** Pugilist favors **Valoredge** and **Sharpshot**. It is not designed to use Soulsoother Cure magic as a reliable healing engine.
- **Equipment and passive scaling:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, master/pet Attack and Ranged Attack bonuses rise approximately **15/22/30/38%**, Martial Arts/Double Attack/Pet Haste **10/15/20/25%**, and critical rate **8/12/16/20%**, provided Hand-to-Hand is equipped.
- **Overload:** The Pugilist implementation grants strong overload prevention, so offensive Maneuver use is less constrained than ordinary Puppetmaster play.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Using the wrong main weapon:** The special offense and Tandem Barrage are conditional on Hand-to-Hand equipment.
- **Letting the pet target a different enemy:** Tandem Opening belongs to the current quarry and must be spent by your automaton's eligible action on it.
- **Using the master weapon skill too early:** Try to create Tandem Opening when the automaton has enough TP to spend it within eight seconds.
- **Expecting Soulsoother healing:** Pugilist is a damage specialization with offensive frame/head restrictions.
- **Forgetting Repair:** The pet is a significant portion of your output; a defeated automaton breaks the loop.
- **Using Limiter Release before a damage spike:** Its reduced Defense raises risk to both combatants during concentrated incoming damage.
