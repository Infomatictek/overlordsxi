# Lancer (DRG) - Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Lancer is a Tier 1 polearm damage Soul. Open from a clear lane with **Spineshatter Vault**, then keep pressure with normal polearm attacks and **Piercing Jump**.

## Setup & Gear

- Equip its Soul Crystal or use `!spec set lancer` from Lv. 10. A regular polearm works, and Polearm is the eligible weapon skill family.
- The core rotation uses no weapon-skill talents or support subjob. Lancer fights independently of a wyvern.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Start between four and twelve yalms from your target. **Spineshatter Vault** moves you to a safe landing near it and attacks once.
2. Aim for more than seven yalms when the area gives you a clear lane. A longer lane improves the opening hit.
3. Continue with **Piercing Jump** and polearm attacks. At Lv. 50, a long Vault that takes enemy HP enables an empowered Jump against that same enemy for five seconds.
4. When terrain blocks you, reposition and try again after three seconds.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

| Level | Short Vault | Long Vault | Jump follow-up |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | 1.6× | 2.2× | — |
| 30 | 1.6× | 2.5× | — |
| 50 | 1.6× | 2.5× | +15% |
| 70–75 | 1.6× | 2.8× | +20% |

- Vault's resolved cooldown is 18 seconds, including on a miss. Its blocked-lane retry is three seconds, with no movement or attack.
- Piercing Jump uses its native attack and base recast. A landed hit spends the target-matched follow-up.
- The HUD displays short and long launch lanes, Vault recast, and the five-second follow-up.

## Common Mistakes

- Check the route and finish point. Walls, sharp elevation changes, unavailable navigation data and obstructed ground can block the attempt.
- Leave enough room to launch. Vault needs at least four yalms at activation.
- Keep the same enemy for the follow-through. A Jump against someone else leaves the stored bonus on its original target.
