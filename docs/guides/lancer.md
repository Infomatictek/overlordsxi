# Lancer (DRG) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Polearm distance attacker.** Lancer uses a long approach lane and jump timing to deliver a forceful committed polearm hit.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Lancer when careful spacing and polearm mobility outperform stationary melee damage.

## Setup & Gear
- Polearm, Accuracy and safe movement space; the long lane is 7–12y.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Stand far enough away to take advantage of the stronger long-distance Spineshatter Vault.
2. **Act:** Launch Vault along a clear path, then return to safe melee engagement if terrain permits.
3. **Adapt:** At Lv50+, follow a successful long-lane Vault promptly with native Jump to exploit its brief bonus.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Spineshatter Vault is a 4–12y, 18s action; 4–7y grants 1.6x and >7–12y grants 2.2/2.5/2.5/2.8x.
- Blocked terrain cancels displacement and damage with a three-second retry; Lv50+ long-lane impact primes Jump for +15/20% once.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't launch into obstructed geometry or expect tank threat removal or immunity from the maneuver.
- Base-job access does not guarantee every spell or ability is approved for this Soul.
