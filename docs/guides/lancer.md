# Lancer (DRG) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Lancer is a Tier 1 **Polearm damage** Soul that turns a clear launch lane into a heavy opening attack. **Spineshatter Vault** jumps toward an enemy 4–12 yalms away; longer unobstructed lanes hit harder. From Lv. 50, a successful long Vault opens a five-second follow-up for **Piercing Jump** against that same quarry. Your skill is choosing launch positions and recognizing when a safer short Vault is better than risking a blocked route.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Lancer Soul Crystal or use `!spec set lancer` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapon:** Main-hand a **Polearm**. Vault and the special follow-up require it; Polearm is the eligible weapon-skill family.
- **Stats:** Accuracy, STR, Attack and Polearm skill all contribute to the Soul's physical damage. Basic Polearm equipment is enough for the starting loop, without weapon-skill talent investment.
- **Positioning:** Start **at least 4 and at most 12 yalms** from the target. A launch lane longer than 7 yalms yields the stronger Vault hit, if the landing is valid.
- **Team role:** Lancer is a damage Soul, not a tank. Coordinate with the tank before vaulting into an unsafe area. Its distinctive combat loop does not depend on a wyvern.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Find a clear lane:** Approach a living enemy to 4–12 yalms, checking for walls, bad elevation and obstructed landing spots.
2. **Launch with Spineshatter Vault:** Activate **Spineshatter Vault**. If a valid landing exists, you relocate near the enemy and make one Polearm strike; a launch farther than 7 yalms deals more damage.
3. **Respond to a blocked route:** If terrain blocks landing, Vault neither moves nor damages you, and gives a short **3-second retry** rather than its full 18-second recast. Reposition before retrying.
4. **Continue in melee:** Use Polearm attacks and eligible weapon skills after the landing. **Piercing Jump** supplies an additional strike on its own 60-second base recast.
5. **At Lv. 50+, link the two abilities:** A successful damaging long Vault on a still-living target grants a **5-second** window to follow with Piercing Jump against **that same enemy**. Jump's damage is enhanced during the window.
6. **At Lv. 70+, exploit stronger long Vaults:** Long-lane Vault damage and the Jump follow-up bonus increase. Still prioritize a valid landing over maximal theoretical distance.
7. **React to missed Vaults:** Vault's 18-second recast applies if the move and attack resolve but miss; only terrain-blocked launches get the 3-second recovery.
8. **Use the HUD:** The Soul HUD distinguishes no lane, short lane and long lane, along with Vault cooldown and any active target-matched Jump follow-up.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Level 10–29:** Short Vault (4–7 yalms) deals **1.6×** normalized physical damage; long Vault (more than 7–12 yalms) deals **2.2×**.
- **Level 30–49:** Short Vault remains **1.6×**; long Vault improves to **2.5×**.
- **Level 50–69:** Short Vault **1.6×**, long Vault **2.5×**. A successful long Vault against a surviving target empowers Piercing Jump by **15%** for 5 seconds.
- **Level 70–75:** Short Vault **1.6×**, long Vault **2.8×**, with a **20%** Piercing Jump follow-up bonus.
- **Vault geometry:** Only enemies 4–12 yalms away qualify at activation. Landing requires valid reachable terrain; blocked geometry produces a 3-second retry without moving or dealing damage.
- **Ability recasts:** Spineshatter Vault ordinarily has an **18-second recast**; Piercing Jump has a **60-second recast**. Plan around both when choosing a long-lane Vault at high levels.
- **Target binding:** The five-second follow-up is bound to the enemy damaged by the long Vault. Jumping on another target does not receive the stored bonus.
- **Passive scaling:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, Lancer gains Polearm skill +12/22/34/46, STR +6/12/18/25, Accuracy +10/18/28/40 and Attack +12/24/38/54.
- **Soul HUD:** Reports lane category, Vault recast and remaining five-second follow-up for the current quarry.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Trying to Vault from point-blank range:** You need at least 4 yalms of horizontal distance at activation.
- **Ignoring terrain:** A blocked landing wastes the attempt and forces repositioning, even if the enemy appears to be in range.
- **Forcing the longest Vault into an unsafe position:** Lancer still needs allies to manage threat and incoming damage.
- **Using Piercing Jump on a different quarry:** The Lv. 50+ follow-up only boosts the enemy you hit with the long Vault.
- **Letting the five-second window expire:** Be ready to Jump after landing when the ability is available.
- **Assuming a miss grants the short retry:** A valid Vault whose hit misses has the normal 18-second recast; only invalid terrain uses the 3-second retry.
