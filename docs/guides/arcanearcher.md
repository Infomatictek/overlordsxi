# Arcane Archer (RNG) - Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Arcane Archer is a Tier 3 bow support Soul. Pick the element your party plans to use, mark an enemy, and coordinate the next spell or skillchain.

## Setup & Gear

- Equip the Arcane Archer crystal or use `!spec set arcanearcher` at Lv. 10. Bring a bow and arrows. Only Archery weapon skills are eligible.
- The team loop works with ordinary equipment, any subjob and zero weapon skill talents.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Choose Fire, Ice, Wind, Earth, Lightning or Water with **Next Attunement** and **Previous Attunement**.
2. Use **Attunement Arrow** within 20 yalms. A landed arrow marks the target for the selected element. A miss still costs one arrow and starts the 16-second recast.
3. Call out the mark so a party member can land a matching direct elemental spell or skillchain. The first hit that deals damage claims the bonus.
4. When solo, a matching elemental bow weapon skill can spend the mark at half potency. Fire and Flaming Arrow are an early pairing if you invest in the corresponding weapon skill talent.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

| Level | Mark duration | Party bonus | Solo weapon skill bonus |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | 10 s | 12% | 6% |
| 30 | 12 s | 15% | 7% |
| 50 | 12 s | 18% | 9% |
| 70–75 | 14 s | 20% | 10% |

- You hold one live mark. Landing a mark on another enemy replaces yours. An enemy carries only its most recently landed mark.
- The HUD displays the selected element, the engaged target's mark, and the arrow's recast.

## Common Mistakes

- The element of the hit must match the mark. Hits from outside your party and periodic damage cannot claim it.
- Plan attunement around actual skillchain damage. A multi-element skillchain uses the element that the server chooses against that target.
- Changing your selected element only affects the next arrow.
