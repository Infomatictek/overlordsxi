# Arcane Archer (RNG) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Elemental arrow coordinator.** Arcane Archer uses a Bow to mark an enemy for one matching elemental ally follow-up, not personal bow-and-katana weaving.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Arcane Archer for targeted elemental team setup, rather than Imperial Archer's personal Bow/Great Katana rotation.

## Setup & Gear
- Bow, arrows, Ranged Accuracy and an ally willing to exploit your chosen element.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Select an element the team is about to use and fire an Attunement Arrow at the intended target.
2. **Act:** Have an allied matching elemental spell or eligible skillchain land while the mark remains active.
3. **Adapt:** In solo play, use an eligible elemental Bow weapon skill to claim a smaller follow-up bonus.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Attunement Arrow reaches 20y and has a 16s shared recast; its 10–14s mark amplifies the first matching party elemental hit by 12/15/18/20%.
- One mark per target and archer; DoTs and wrong-element hits don't claim it. A missed arrow spends ammo and recast but doesn't overwrite existing marks.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't assume an unmatched or uncoordinated team will realize the mark's value.
- Only the actions actually granted to this Soul should be treated as part of its kit.
