# Valkyrie (WHM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Valkyrie is a Tier-3 polearm support Soul. Pick the party caster most likely to take damage mid-cast and watch for an interruption. Valkyrie's Escort protects one retry so an important spell can finish.

## Setup & Gear

- Unlock at Lv.10 with `!spec set valkyrie` or Crystal Socket. Equip a two-handed polearm.
- Bronze Spear is a Lv.7 starter weapon. Valkyrie can also use Spear, Halberd, Partisan and Golden Spear as it levels. Polearm weapon skills use standard TP and talent rules.
- You can name yourself as Wingmate and practice with Vigil Lance at Lv.10.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Use **Wingmate** on yourself or one party member within 15 yalms.
2. When enemy damage interrupts their spell, the HUD shows a six-second opening. Use **Valkyrie's Escort** on that Wingmate.
3. The Wingmate has six seconds to start the same spell. That retry resists damage-triggered interruption. Resolve any movement, Silence or crowd control as usual.
4. At Lv.30 an ally who finishes the retry recovers 15% of its MP cost. At Lv.50 Escort reaches 20 yalms and self-retries gain half the normal refund. At Lv.70 the ally refund rises to 25%.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- One Wingmate per Valkyrie, and one Valkyrie can claim an interruption. Wingmate has a five-second recast and Escort has a 25-second recast.
- Both windows use the Soul HUD. Vigil Lance costs 15 MP and has a 15-second base recast.

## Common Mistakes

- Save Escort for damage interruptions. Movement, manual cancellation, Silence and incapacitation do not create an opening.
- Choose the most important caster. The rescue covers a single ally and a single retry.
