# Valkyrie (WHM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Valkyrie is a Tier 3 **Polearm support** Soul specializing in saving one party caster after enemy damage interrupts an important spell. **Wingmate** designates your chosen ally (or yourself); **Valkyrie's Escort** then opens a protected retry of the exact interrupted spell. This is a reactive rescue mechanic, not permanent interruption immunity or a blanket party buff. Success depends on choosing the right caster, noticing the qualifying interruption and responding within a short window.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Valkyrie Soul Crystal or use `!spec set valkyrie` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapon:** Equip a **Polearm** in the main slot. Wingmate, Valkyrie's Escort and the Soul's special support interactions require it.
- **Starter option:** Bronze Spear can serve as an early Polearm. Later Spear, Halberd, Partisan and Golden Spear options can fit as levels and equipment permit; weapon skills retain ordinary TP and talent requirements.
- **Choose your caster:** Assign Wingmate to a party member whose cast would be especially painful to lose, or choose yourself to practice with **Vigil Lance**.
- **Position:** Stay within 15 yalms of your Wingmate for selection and low-level Escort; at Lv. 50 the Escort range increases to 20 yalms, but Wingmate selection remains limited to 15 yalms.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Designate Wingmate:** Use **Wingmate** on yourself or one living party member within 15 yalms. It has a 5-second recast. Only one person can be your active Wingmate at a time.
2. **Watch for hostile interruption:** If enemy damage interrupts that Wingmate's spell, a short actionable window opens. Read the HUD's interruption timer rather than guessing.
3. **React with Valkyrie's Escort:** Target the same Wingmate and activate **Valkyrie's Escort** within the interruption window. The ability has a 25-second recast and requires Polearm.
4. **Have the caster retry promptly:** Escort opens a **6-second window to start the same spell**. The protection applies to that one eligible retry against damage-triggered interruption.
5. **Continue normal encounter mechanics:** Escort does not negate movement, Silence, incapacity or other unrelated casting restrictions; the Wingmate still needs valid conditions to recast.
6. **Use Vigil Lance when no rescue is needed:** **Vigil Lance** is a Lv. 10 single-target light attack costing 15 MP, with a 2.5-second cast and 15-second recast.
7. **Benefit from progression:** At Lv. 30, an ally's completed retry refunds 15% of its spell MP cost. At Lv. 50, Escort reaches 20 yalms and self-retries gain a reduced refund. At Lv. 70, the ally refund rises to 25%.
8. **Monitor the HUD:** Confirm Polearm qualification, Wingmate validity, interrupted-spell timer, retry timer and Escort recast before committing.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Wingmate:** Choose yourself or a party member within 15 yalms; 5-second recast. Changing the designated Wingmate clears the previous association's pending retry from you.
- **Escort trigger:** The selected Wingmate must have a **hostile damage interruption**, not a voluntary cancellation or movement-triggered failure. It protects one repeat of the same recorded spell.
- **Retry timing:** Escort must be activated during the interruption opportunity, and the protected same-spell retry must begin within 6 seconds of Escort. Only one Valkyrie can claim an interruption.
- **Level 10–29:** Escort has 15-yalm range and no MP refund. Wingmate selection remains at 15 yalms.
- **Level 30–49:** A completed ally retry recovers 15% of the original spell's MP cost.
- **Level 50–69:** Escort's range grows to 20 yalms. An eligible self-retry receives half the normal refund (rounded down), while ally refunds remain 15%.
- **Level 70–75:** Ally refund increases to 25%; an eligible self-retry receives half of that amount (rounded down).
- **Soul passives:** Polearm skill, HP and Magic Evasion increase with level. These support your positioning but do not replace the reactive escort timing.
- **Soul HUD:** Displays current Polearm eligibility, valid Wingmate, remaining interruption opportunity, remaining protected retry and Escort recast.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Trying to protect everyone at once:** Valkyrie selects one Wingmate; choose the most critical caster for the encounter.
- **Using Escort after a non-hostile interruption:** Movement, voluntary cancellation, Silence or incapacity do not grant the special retry opportunity.
- **Confusing Wingmate and Escort ranges:** Wingmate is chosen within 15 yalms even though Escort expands to 20 yalms at Lv. 50.
- **Repeating a different spell:** The protected retry is bound to the exact spell that enemy damage interrupted.
- **Waiting too long to retry:** A successful Escort opens only 6 seconds to begin the protected spell.
- **Assuming protection makes you immune to all mechanics:** Escort addresses damage-triggered casting interruption, not the encounter's other restrictions.
