# Valkyrie (WHM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Polearm spell escort.** Valkyrie protects an ally's second attempt at a spell that enemy damage interrupted; it is not a second primary healer.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Valkyrie when reliable completion of important allied casts matters more than broad healing or raw buffs.

## Setup & Gear
- Polearm and supportive positioning near the chosen ally; up to 15y for Wingmate.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Designate a Wingmate ally (or yourself when solo) before the enemy's spell-interrupting attack.
2. **Act:** If enemy damage actually interrupts their cast, a short Escort opportunity appears.
3. **Adapt:** Use Valkyrie's Escort to protect a retry of that same spell, not to buff unrelated casts.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Wingmate has a 5s cooldown; an enemy-damage interruption opens a 6s Escort window.
- Escort is a 25s action protecting one retry with 100% interruption resistance and up to 25% MP refund by level. Manual cancellation, movement, Silence or death do not qualify.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't confuse it with Defender's mitigation or Bishop's curing; never expect a movement-canceled spell to trigger Escort.
- Only the actions actually granted to this Soul should be treated as part of its kit.
