# Huntsman (RNG) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Huntsman is a **Ranger with two distinct pet-driven roles**. Summoning a **Glacial Tusker** makes your beast the group's single-target tank; summoning a **Volcanic Saurian** turns you into a ranged pack-support specialist. You are not supposed to run both roles simultaneously. The current pet controls the Soul's active mode, damage penalties, threat behavior and support effects. Pick the beast for the encounter and treat its health, target and shared summon cooldown as central resources.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Huntsman Soul Crystal or use `!spec set huntsman` from Lv. 10.
- **Ranged loadout:** Bring a **Bow or Crossbow and matching arrows/bolts**. A starter Light Crossbow and bolts can be supplied when eligible slots are empty. The Soul has strong ranged skill, ammo Recycle, and no ordinary ranged distance damage penalty in its special attack setup.
- **Pet decision:** **Wild Raaz** (Glacial Tusker) is the Lv. 10 tank option; **Stalker Raptor** (Volcanic Saurian) is the Lv. 10 support option. **Cehuetzi** and **Gabbrath** are their advanced Lv. 75 alternatives.
- **Resources and positioning:** Watch the beast's HP and TP, your ranged weapon-skill TP and the party's position. Saurian buffs generally cover allies within **15 yalms**.
- **Before a pull:** Choose whether the party needs a pet tank or extra offensive support. The two pet families share a **60-second summon recast**, so swapping in the middle of danger is a meaningful commitment.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Select your role before combat:** Summon **Wild Raaz** if your beast will hold the boss. Summon **Stalker Raptor** if another tank is handling threat and your team wants offensive pack buffs.
2. **Direct the pet:** Engage the chosen single enemy and send your beast after the party's quarry. Huntsman's companion attacks and commands are intentionally single-target.
3. **Tusker — establish a stable tank:** Attack your quarry with ranged or melee actions while the Glacial Tusker is present. The special single-target threat transfer moves your threat to the Tusker, helping it hold attention even when you attack.
4. **Tusker — use Rimetusk Gore:** Activate **Rimetusk Gore** for a high-threat pet strike, an immediate pet heal and a temporary pet defense boost. At Lv. 75, Cehuetzi adds another enemy-offense reduction.
5. **Saurian — maintain the pack aura:** Stand so the intended allies remain within the pet's party support range, increasing their physical/ranged accuracy and attack. Use **Saurian Rend** for an offensive party burst with TP support.
6. **Feed your beast through ranged weapon skills:** Land an eligible **Archery or Marksmanship weapon skill** to trigger **Warden's Ration**, restoring pet HP and supplying **250 pet TP**. Ordinary melee weapon skills do not give this benefit.
7. **Use Primeval Takedown for a mode-specific payoff:** In Tusker mode it reinforces threat and adds stun and pet protection; in Saurian mode it provides a strong single-target attack and **300 TP** to Huntsman.
8. **Adapt to a loss or mode change:** If a beast falls, your special tank or pack behavior disappears. Respect the shared summon recast before attempting to replace it; do not expect a pet change to preserve the previous role's benefits.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Two modes:** **Glacial Tusker** is the tank specialization; **Volcanic Saurian** is the hybrid support specialization. No pet uses the non-Tusker mode rather than retaining tank benefits.
- **Summon access:** Wild Raaz and Stalker Raptor at Lv. 10; **Cehuetzi** and **Gabbrath** at Lv. 75. Summons share a **60-second** cooldown and only one beast can be active.
- **Tusker defensive scaling:** Pet maximum HP improves by **40/50/60/70%** and pet Enmity by **60/80/100/120** at Lv. 10/30/50/70, along with extra pet damage reduction and qualifying boss AoE/conal defenses.
- **Tusker threat and cures:** Eligible single-target threat from the Ranger is forwarded to the Tusker. Healing the master can forward **80%** of the direct recovery and eligible overhealing to the active Tusker.
- **Saurian pack aura:** Allies within roughly **15 yalms** receive **+8/12/16/20 Accuracy and Ranged Accuracy** and **+8/10/12/15% Attack and Ranged Attack** at Lv. 10/30/50/70.
- **Warden's Ration:** Eligible ranged weapon skills restore the active pet for **15/18/20/22% of pet maximum HP** at Lv. 10/30/50/70 and grant **250 pet TP**.
- **Mode commands:** **Rimetusk Gore** provides pet threat, about **20% pet HP** recovery and short extra damage reduction; **Saurian Rend** heals the beast, grants nearby allies **150 TP** and temporary offensive buffs. Both are single-target pet actions.
- **Primeval Takedown:** **45-second recast**, target up to **20 yalms**, requires a living beast. Tusker adds threat, short stun and protective Stoneskin; Saurian rewards the master with **300 TP**.
- **Ranged quality of life:** Huntsman has strong ammo recycling and an extended ranged combat setup, but still needs a compatible ranged weapon and ammunition for ranged actions.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Treating both beasts as the same pet:** Tusker is the tank; Saurian is offensive support. The active companion changes your role.
- **Trying to swap repeatedly:** Both summon families share the same 60-second recovery. Decide before a boss pull when possible.
- **Using melee weapon skills to heal the beast:** Warden's Ration requires eligible **Archery or Marksmanship** weapon skills.
- **Assuming threat transfer works with the Saurian:** The special transfer is tied to Tusker tank mode.
- **Assuming Tusker's special protections cover everyone:** Pet-specific AoE and magic protection does not make the Ranger or other party members immune.
- **Fighting outside the support group:** Saurian's pack aura has a limited range; keep your allies close enough to benefit.
- **Forgetting the pet's health:** Your primary role disappears if the pet dies. Use the pet's defensive tools and Warden's Ration before it falls.
