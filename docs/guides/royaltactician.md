# Royaltactician (RDM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Enfeeble-to-order strategist.** Royal Tactician turns meaningful enfeebling casts into a targeted ally's next tactical benefit.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Royal Tactician when the party needs targeted tactical effects derived from active enfeebling.

## Setup & Gear
- Magic Accuracy, MP and the appropriate Slow, Paralyze, Blind, Silence and later Dispel access.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Open with Slow or Paralyze when you want an Advance order to help physical allies.
2. **Act:** Use Blind or Silence when a caster needs Recompose to save MP on its next eligible spell.
3. **Adapt:** From Lv30, Dispel can repeat the last order; choose and manage the recipient instead of spraying generic group buffs.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Advance grants 10/15% Accuracy and 5/8 Store TP; Recompose discounts the next spell by 15/20/25% MP.
- One recipient's most recent order supersedes previous pending orders. At Lv50+, a full resist can grant a half-strength order with a cooldown.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't expect orders to stack indefinitely or replace Scourge's enemy suppression.
- Only the actions actually granted to this Soul should be treated as part of its kit.
