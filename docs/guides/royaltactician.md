# Royal Tactician (RDM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Royal Tactician is a Tier 3 **enfeebling support** Soul. Successfully applying specific enfeebles prepares a short-lived **Advance** or **Recompose** order; you then choose the single teammate who will benefit most and issue it before the opportunity expires. The Soul rewards selecting the correct enfeeble, knowing which ally is about to act and coordinating brief eight-second boosts. Its orders are targeted tactical windows, not permanent party auras.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Royal Tactician Soul Crystal or use `!spec set royaltactician` from Lv. 10.
- **Weapon and stats:** Sword is a practical melee option; Magic Accuracy is especially important because full enfeeble landings open your strongest orders. The Soul's native progression also grants Max MP.
- **Party planning:** Pick the next recipient before you cast the enfeeble. **Advance** favors physical attackers who need Accuracy and TP flow; **Recompose** favors a caster about to spend MP on a meaningful spell.
- **Range and solo:** **Issue Order** can target yourself or one valid party member within **15 yalms**, so the core loop is usable when solo.
- **Talent independence:** All initial enfeeble/order interactions work at Lv. 10 with ordinary gear and zero weapon-skill talent investment.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose the desired order:** Use **Break Stride** (enemy attack speed reduction) or **Seize Initiative** (enemy action interference) to prepare an **Advance** order. Use **Obscure Aim** (enemy accuracy reduction) or **Quiet Front** (enemy spell suppression) to prepare **Recompose**.
2. **Land the enfeeble:** A successful application opens a **6-second pending order**. Check the HUD's order icon and remaining time.
3. **Pick the best ally:** For Advance, target a melee ally whose attacks need Accuracy; for Recompose, select a caster prepared to finish an MP-consuming spell.
4. **Issue Order before expiry:** Use **Issue Order** on the intended recipient within 15 yalms. It has an **18-second recast**, grants its effect for **8 seconds** and consumes the pending order.
5. **Keep the recipient active:** Advance gives short-term Accuracy and, from Lv. 30, Store TP. Recompose refunds a portion of MP actually spent on an eligible completed cast; coordinate for completion inside the window.
6. **At Lv. 30, reuse your last plan:** **Unravel Defense** can remove an enemy enhancement and reopen the last order you successfully issued. If none has been issued yet, the fallback order is Advance.
7. **At Lv. 50, handle full resists:** A fully resisted qualifying enfeeble can open a **half-potency order** once every **24 seconds**. This helps maintain some support under difficult resistance conditions, but does not make a resisted enfeeble equal to a successful one.
8. **Use the HUD before the next cycle:** Track pending Advance/Recompose, time remaining, half-strength status, Issue Order recast and resisted-spell fallback cooldown.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Advance source spells:** Break Stride and Seize Initiative, both available from Lv. 10. They prepare an Advance order on successful enfeeble resolution.
- **Recompose source spells:** Obscure Aim and Quiet Front, both available from Lv. 10. They prepare Recompose.
- **Lv. 10–29:** Advance gives **10% Accuracy** (relative to recipient's current Accuracy) with no Store TP. Recompose's MP-return potency is **15%**.
- **Lv. 30–49:** Advance gives **10% Accuracy and +5 Store TP**; Recompose's potency improves to **20%**. Unravel Defense unlocks.
- **Lv. 50–69:** Full-strength order potency remains as above; a qualifying full resist can produce a **half-potency** pending order at most once per 24 seconds.
- **Lv. 70–75:** Advance improves to **15% Accuracy and +8 Store TP**; Recompose to **25%** potency.
- **Pending order:** A qualifying enfeeble grants **6 seconds** to Issue Order. A new enfeeble replaces the previous pending choice.
- **Order lifetime and recast:** Issue Order grants a single ally an **8-second effect** and has an **18-second recast**. It cannot replace a stronger still-active order of the same kind.
- **Order ownership:** A Royal Tactician maintains one owned recipient order at a time; issuing another replaces its previous granted order.
- **Soul HUD:** Displays the pending order type, duration, half-potency indicator, Issue Order recast and full-resist fallback cooldown.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Casting a second enfeeble before using your pending order:** The newer enfeeble can replace the order type you intended.
- **Issuing Recompose to a caster who cannot finish:** The refund requires an eligible completed MP-consuming cast; instant, item or failed attempts do not provide the expected payoff.
- **Expecting orders to last throughout the fight:** The recipient gets eight seconds, so use it when they are ready.
- **Assuming every resisted spell creates a full-strength order:** The Lv. 50 fallback is half potency and has its own 24-second recovery.
- **Trying to overwrite a stronger same-kind order:** The ability protects the currently stronger active order rather than downgrading it.
- **Giving Advance to an ally who does not need to attack:** Match the bonus to what the recipient will actually do in the next eight seconds.
