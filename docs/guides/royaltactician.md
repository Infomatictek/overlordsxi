# Royal Tactician (RDM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Royal Tactician is a Tier-3 support Soul. Land an enfeeble that fits the fight, then issue its order to the party member who can use it best.

## Setup & Gear

- Select at Lv.10 with `!spec set royaltactician` or Crystal Socket. Use a Sword for melee and prioritize magic accuracy for enfeebles.
- The complete order loop is available at Lv.10 with zero talents. You can issue orders to yourself in solo play.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Land **Break Stride** or **Seize Initiative** to open **Advance**. Issue it to a melee ally who needs accuracy.
2. Land **Obscure Aim** or **Quiet Front** to open **Recompose**. Issue it to a caster preparing a costly spell.
3. Use **Issue Order** within six seconds on one party member or yourself within 15 yalms.
4. At Lv.30, a successful **Unravel Defense** reopens the last order you issued. It defaults to Advance before you have issued an order.
5. At Lv.50 and above, a full resist can open a half-strength order once per 24 seconds. Lv.70 increases the full-strength order bonuses.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- Advance lasts eight seconds and grants accuracy, plus Store TP at Lv.30 and above.
- Recompose lasts up to eight seconds and refunds a portion of MP paid when its recipient completes an eligible cast.
- Issue Order has an 18-second recast. Only the latest pending order can be issued. A weaker order of the same kind cannot replace an active stronger one.

## Common Mistakes

- Issue a pending order before opening another. Even a useful new enfeeble can replace the order you were holding.
- Coordinate Recompose around a spell the caster can finish. Instant and item casts do not spend the order.
- Reapply enfeebles when they can land. A spell that has no effect does not create another order.
