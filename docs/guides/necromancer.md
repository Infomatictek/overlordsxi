# Necromancer (BLM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Necromancer turns Black Mage into a **Scythe-wielding Undead Commander with two distinct modes** controlled by whichever thrall you summon:
- **Tank Mode** (*Undead Warrior* Lv.10 / *Dullahan* Lv.75): `-60%` personal damage dealt; 100% of your single-target threat transfers to your thrall, which is immune to boss AoEs/Breaths/Magic.
- **Caster / Solo Mode** (*Undead Mage* Lv.10 / *Corse* Lv.75 — or no pet): `-25%` damage dealt; your thrall kites at range while projecting party DPS/Haste/MP/TP buffs.

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapon:** **Scythe in Main Hand** (unlocks **A+ Scythe skill** and `Harbinger's Drain` WS healing).
- **Stat Conversion (`Grim Conversion`):** Your mage stats automatically convert into melee stats while Necromancer is socketed (`MACC -> ACC`, `MAB -> Attack`, `Fast Cast -> Haste`, `Magic Burst Bonus -> WSD`). Stacking mage/dark gear still empowers you in combat AND powers your Caster thrall's Dark magic!

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Pick Your Thrall (60s shared CD):**
   - Summon **Undead Warrior** (405, Lv.10) or **Dullahan** (411, Lv.75) when you want a **boss-anchoring pet tank**.
   - Summon **Undead Mage** (406, Lv.10) or **Corse** (410, Lv.75) when you already have a tank and want **ranged Dark magic & party offensive buffs**.
2. **Command via Pet Sub-Menu (25s shared CD per menu):**
   - *Tank Thralls (`Grim Reaping` 408 menu):* Open with `Grave Provoke` (Lv.10 taunt + Atk Down), protect pet with `Bone Aegis` (Lv.35 Stoneskin + DEF + Dread Spikes), debuff with `Black Cloud` (Lv.20 Blind) or `Aegis Schism` (Lv.75 -25% DEF/MDB), and heal pet with `Blood Saber` (Lv.50 100% drain slash).
   - *Caster Thralls (`Grim Incantations` 409 menu):* Rotate `Covenant of Ash` (Lv.20, 90s party +Atk/MAB/Haste), `Necrotic Bolt` (Lv.10, -DEF/MDB), `Curse of the Fallen` (Lv.35, Slow/Eva Down + party ACC), `Soul Harvest` (Lv.50, party HP/MP/TP), and `Danse Macabre` (Lv.75, party Crit + Regain).
3. **Melee with Scythe to Sustain Pet (`Harbinger's Drain`):** Stand in melee with your Scythe (powered by `Dark Pact` +DA/+Store TP/+ACC while any thrall is alive) and fire **Scythe Weaponskills** — every WS heals your Tank thrall for **16–22% Max HP** (or Caster thrall for 25–50% of WS damage).
4. **Emergency Finisher:** `Corpse Explosion` (407, 45s CD) detonates your active thrall onto a single engaged mob for burst Dark damage + Bio.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Cure Forwarding (Tank Mode):** Any `Cure` or `Curaga` cast on you forwards **80% of the heal + 100% of overheal** straight to your Tank thrall.
- **Grim Conversion & Elemental Celerity:** `Grim Conversion` converts raw `Fast Cast` and `MAB/MACC` into melee stats; `Elemental Celerity` stays intact if you ever cast elemental magic.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't let your pet die (or use `Corpse Explosion` carelessly):** Without a live thrall, `Dark Pact` (+DA, +Store TP, +ACC) shuts off immediately, and Tank Mode drops back to HybridSupport (losing enmity transfer and pet AoE immunity).
- **Don't try to nuke as a normal BLM:** Because `Grim Conversion` drains your `MACC`, `MAB`, and `Fast Cast` to fuel your Scythe melee stats, your personal nukes are weak — let your Scythe WSs and your Undead Mage/Corse deal the dark magic.
- **Note Shared Sub-Menu Cooldowns:** Abilities 419–423 inside *Grim Reaping* share one 25s timer, and 424–428 inside *Grim Incantations* share one 25s timer.
