# Necromancer (BLM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Necromancer is a **Scythe-wielding undead commander** with two pet-defined roles. **Undead Warrior** or **Dullahan** provides a threat-absorbing tank thrall; **Undead Mage** or **Corse** supplies dark magic and party offense. Necromancer's personal equipment and **Grim Conversion** turn conventional Black Mage magic stats into Scythe combat strength. Your playstyle depends on choosing, controlling and sustaining a single thrall, not playing like a normal backline nuker.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Necromancer Soul Crystal or use `!spec set necromancer` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapon:** Main-hand a **Scythe** to use the special weapon-skill recovery and combat stat support.
- **Thrall choice:** **Undead Warrior** and **Undead Mage** unlock at Lv. 10. **Dullahan** and **Corse** are their Lv. 75 counterparts.
- **Equipment:** Accuracy, Attack, Store TP, Dark Magic and traditional magic stats can all matter because **Grim Conversion** transforms some mage-oriented bonuses into melee performance.
- **Party role:** Tell your team which thrall you brought. Tank-mode threat transfer and caster-mode buffs are fundamentally different.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose the thrall role:** Use **Undead Warrior** to let the pet anchor a boss, or **Undead Mage** when the group already has a tank and wants offensive magic and support.
2. **Keep the thrall engaged:** Summon the chosen undead pet, designate the party's single kill target and keep its health visible. The summon families share a **60-second recast**.
3. **Tank mode — establish threat:** The warrior thrall benefits from the special single-target enmity transfer. Use its **Grim Reaping** menu and **Grave Provoke** to reinforce control.
4. **Tank mode — protect the pet:** Use **Bone Aegis**, **Black Cloud**, **Blood Saber** or later **Aegis Schism** as available for defense, status control and pet recovery.
5. **Caster mode — choose support spells:** Use **Grim Incantations** for **Necrotic Bolt**, **Covenant of Ash**, **Curse of the Fallen**, **Soul Harvest** and later **Danse Macabre** as their level requirements permit.
6. **Sustain through Scythe weapon skills:** Eligible Scythe weapon skills activate **Harbinger's Drain**, healing the tank thrall by a percentage of maximum HP or restoring the caster thrall according to the damage dealt.
7. **Plan Corpse Explosion carefully:** It consumes the active thrall for a single-target dark finisher and Bio effect. Only use it when the burst outweighs losing the pet's role and bonuses.
8. **Adapt to lost pets:** Without a living thrall, your **Dark Pact** combat bonuses and pet-mode support cease. Respect the shared summon cooldown before rebuilding the setup.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Modal roles:** Warrior/Dullahan is the pet tank mode with specialized threat forwarding; Mage/Corse is the ranged caster and party support mode.
- **Summons:** Undead Warrior and Undead Mage at Lv. 10; stronger Dullahan and Corse options at Lv. 75, sharing a **60-second** recast.
- **Tank threat transfer:** Eligible single-target enmity generated by the Necromancer can be passed to the tank thrall. The pet receives special boss-area protections but still needs maintenance.
- **Caster support:** The Mage/Corse menu combines dark damage, target debuffs, party offensive buffs, and resource recovery according to the chosen action.
- **Grim Reaping and Grim Incantations:** Each command menu has approximately **25-second shared recast** across its own options. Do not assume every command has a separate immediate use.
- **Harbinger's Drain:** Eligible Scythe weapon skills heal the active tank thrall by approximately **16–22% maximum pet HP** or help the caster thrall recover based on weapon-skill damage.
- **Cure forwarding:** In tank mode, eligible recovery on the owner can forward a portion of effective healing and overheal to the tank thrall.
- **Grim Conversion:** Transfers parts of Magic Accuracy, Magic Attack Bonus, Fast Cast and Magic Burst Bonus into melee-oriented capabilities.
- **Corpse Explosion:** **45-second recast**, single-target dark damage and Bio at the cost of ending the active thrall.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Assuming either thrall always acts as a tank:** Caster thralls support offense rather than receiving the tank role's special threat transfer.
- **Using Corpse Explosion while the thrall is essential:** The burst removes a major part of your ongoing damage, defense or party support.
- **Fighting with a wrong main weapon:** The Scythe enables the key Harbinger's Drain sustain.
- **Expecting a conventional Black Mage nuking rotation:** Grim Conversion moves important mage stats toward melee performance; choose your actions accordingly.
- **Ignoring pet command shared recasts:** Each command menu has a shared timer, so choose the most useful response for the boss phase.
- **Forgetting the pet's health or summon delay:** A dead thrall can leave the entire party without its intended tank or support function.
