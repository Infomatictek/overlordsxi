# Beastspeaker (BST) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Beastspeaker is a Tier 3 support Soul that studies an enemy's physical weaknesses and commands a mandragora tracker to expose them. Your role is to identify the quarry, set up a short vulnerability window, and communicate which ordinary attack types your party should use. Unlike a general damage buff, exposure only rewards the matching eligible attacks.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Select the Beastspeaker Soul Crystal or use `!spec set beastspeaker` from Lv. 10.
- **Weapon:** Axe is the eligible weapon-skill family. The support loop works with ordinary gear, any subjob and zero weapon-skill talents.
- **Companion:** Use **Call Tracker** to summon your mandragora without a jug. Keep it available to land the confirming melee hit.
- **Party preparation:** Tell your teammates whether the quarry is most vulnerable to blunt, piercing or slashing physical attacks, and ask them to use eligible regular attacks during exposure.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Read the target:** Use **Read Quarry** on a chosen enemy within 15 yalms. Your read lasts 60 seconds and identifies the physical damage type to exploit. Known monster families use the least resisted type; tied or unknown cases begin with blunt.
2. **Summon and assign the tracker:** Use **Call Tracker** to bring out the mandragora, then **Track Quarry** to direct it at the read enemy.
3. **Open the weakness window:** Use **Expose Quarry**. Your tracker has 6 seconds to land a melee hit on the quarry and activate the exposure.
4. **Coordinate the party:** During the active exposure, have allies use ordinary melee attacks or eligible single ranged hits that match the indicated physical type. Each player can benefit twice, with no more than one benefit per attack round.
5. **Control the pet:** **Recall Tracker** brings it back when its position is unsafe or unhelpful. **Release Tracker** dismisses it when no longer needed.
6. **Lv. 30 — Learn from enemy actions:** After a read, observe the quarry's next qualifying hostile action; the HUD can show the recorded action.
7. **Lv. 50 — Change the weakness cycle:** Use **Choose Weakness** to cycle the type of exposure when the party composition calls for a different physical attack.
8. **Refresh deliberately:** Check the HUD's tracker order timer, exposed damage type and remaining exposure before committing to another setup.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Level 10–29:** Known-family exposure bonus 8%; unknown-family bonus 4%; duration 8 seconds.
- **Level 30–49:** Known-family bonus 10%; unknown-family bonus 4%; duration 8 seconds. Adds hostile-action recording after a read.
- **Level 50–69:** Known-family bonus 10%; unknown-family bonus 4%; duration 10 seconds. Adds Choose Weakness.
- **Level 70–75:** Known-family bonus 12%; unknown-family bonus 6%; duration 10 seconds.
- **Read rules:** Reads last 60 seconds. Unknown families and ties begin with blunt, which includes hand-to-hand.
- **Eligible damage:** Exposure strengthens matching normal melee attacks and single ranged hits; weapon skills, skillchains, counterattacks and multi-shot volleys do not benefit.
- **Multiple Beastspeakers:** A stronger exposure takes priority on the same quarry.
- **Soul HUD:** Displays family, target weakness, tracker order window and active exposure; from Lv. 30 it can also show the last observed qualifying hostile action.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Opening exposure before your tracker can hit:** Expose Quarry requires the mandragora to land a melee hit within the 6-second order window.
- **Expecting weapon skills to benefit:** Exposure is for eligible ordinary physical hits, not weapon skills or skillchains.
- **Ignoring the actual weakness type:** A strong attack of the wrong physical type does not receive the exposure bonus.
- **Failing to coordinate:** The exposure is brief; announce the vulnerability before it expires.
- **Assuming every monster has a known family:** Unknown or tied family information begins with blunt. Use the HUD and later options to adapt.
