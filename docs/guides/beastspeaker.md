# Beastspeaker (BST) - Playstyle Guide

Beastspeaker is a Tier 3 support Soul. Study one enemy, then send a mandragora to expose a physical weakness for your party.

## Level 10 opening

1. Equip the crystal or use `!spec set beastspeaker`. Axe is the eligible weapon-skill family.
2. Use **Call Tracker** to summon a mandragora without a jug. Use **Read Quarry** on an enemy within 15 yalms.
3. Send the tracker after that enemy with **Track Quarry**, then use **Expose Quarry**. The tracker has six seconds to land a melee hit.
4. Have your party use regular attacks of the weakness shown in the HUD. Each player can benefit twice, once per attack round.

Use **Recall Tracker** to bring the companion back or **Release Tracker** to dismiss it. The loop works with any subjob and zero weapon-skill talents.

| Level | Known family bonus | Unknown family bonus | Exposure duration | Progression |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 10–29 | 8% | 4% | 8s | Read, summon, assign and expose |
| 30–49 | 10% | 4% | 8s | Record the next hostile action the quarry completes |
| 50–69 | 10% | 4% | 10s | Choose Weakness cycles the exposure type |
| 70–75 | 12% | 6% | 10s | Final bonus scaling |

- For known families, Read Quarry selects the least resisted physical damage type. Ties and unknown families start with blunt. Blunt includes hand-to-hand. Reads last 60 seconds.
- An exposure affects matching melee attacks and single ranged hits. Weapon skills, skillchains, counterattacks, and multi-shot volleys are outside its scope. Stronger exposure takes priority on a quarry.
- The HUD shows the family, damage type, tracker order window, and active exposure. From Lv. 30, it can also show the most recent qualifying action observed after a read.
