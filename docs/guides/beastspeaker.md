# Beastspeaker (BST) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Bestiary exposure specialist.** Beastspeaker studies a target's physical weaknesses and directs its engaged pet to expose that opening for allies.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Beastspeaker when a physical team can exploit observed monster weaknesses rather than needing generic pet buffs.

## Setup & Gear
- Axe plus an engaged pet focused on the same enemy; physical allies ready to benefit.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Use Read Quarry to identify the target's weakest physical attack type.
2. **Act:** Command Expose Quarry while the pet engages that exact target to create a brief exposure.
3. **Adapt:** Coordinate allies' matching ordinary physical hits during the opening; at Lv50+ you can choose a manual physical type.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Read Quarry reaches 15y and has a 10s cooldown; it chooses among blunt, slashing and piercing (with stable tie order).
- Expose Quarry has a 25s cooldown, lasts 8–10s, and offers 8–12% damage on known exposure (smaller unknown fallback), limited to two claims per actor. WS and skillchains are excluded.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't treat the pet as Huntsman's main tank or Untamed's double-damage partner.
- Confirm available commands and casting rules in the active Soul's menu before building a rotation.
