# Medic (SCH) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Medic turns Scholar into a **faerie-assisted Light Arts party healer** (`Healer` tier: -60% personal damage dealt, 0.12x WS scaling). You summon **Eos** (a true server-side faerie companion who automatically cleanses debuffs and maintains **stackable Eos Regen**), gain **free 10y AoE on all Cure and Regen spells** without spending Stratagems, and charge **MP twice as fast with zero HP drain** via Sublimation.

## Setup & Gear
- **Recommended Companion Item:** Equip **Orphic Egg (#18256)** in your **Ammo** slot — it grants level-scaling **Refresh directly to Eos** so her MP stays full on long fights.
- **Weapon / Armor:** Standard Scholar Staff/Club + Healing/MND/Cure Potency/Regen/Fast Cast gear.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Summon Eos & Activate White Arts:** Cast `Call Eos` (Ability #231, 300s CD -> summons Light Pixie #128) and turn on `Addendum: White` (automatically extended to **2 hours**).
2. **Charge MP Safely (`Sublimation`):** Activate `Sublimation` — under Medic it charges **2x faster with ZERO HP loss** and completes for 2 hours. Keep `Light Arts` / `Addendum: White` active.
3. **Heal & Regen Party-Wide for Free:** Cast **any single-target `Cure` or `Regen` spell** — Medic automatically turns Cure and Regen spells into **free 10y Radial AoEs** without consuming an Accession Stratagem charge!
4. **Let Eos Handle Cleanse Triage & Bonus Regen:** Stay within **20 yalms** of Eos. Every 5 seconds she automatically prioritizes casting `-na` / `Cursna` / `Erase` on debuffed allies/Trusts/self AND maintains **Eos Regen (I–V)** across the party — which **stacks on top of your own Regen spells**! (`!eos heal` restores Eos's HP; `!eos dismiss` releases her.)

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Stackable Eos Regen (Status #789):** Runs independently alongside player `Regen` spells for double HoT coverage.
- **2x HP-Free `Sublimation`:** Free MP battery with zero HP drain penalty.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't waste Accession Stratagem charges on Cure/Regen:** Cure and Regen already gain free 10y AoE passively (save Stratagems for `Erase` or Status-na spells!).
- **Don't forget Orphic Egg in Ammo** if fighting long bosses where Eos casts non-stop `-na` and Regens.
- Swapping off SCH or switching Soul Crystals immediately dismisses Eos.
