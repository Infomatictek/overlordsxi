# Sniper (RNG) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Sniper is a Tier 1 **deliberate single-target ranged damage** Soul for Gun or Crossbow users. **Sightline Shot** trades a stationary aiming window for one powerful ranged hit. From Lv. 50, **Deep Sight** lets you choose a slower four-second aim for more damage, or return to the standard three-second shot when the fight demands movement. This specialization is about one committed projectile, not rapid-fire multi-hit barrages.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip Sniper's Soul Crystal or use `!spec set sniper` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapon and ammunition:** Use a **Gun with bullets** or **Crossbow with bolts**; bring sufficient matching ammunition. Marksmanship is the eligible weapon-skill family. An ordinary low-level Crossbow with bolts works for the starting loop.
- **Stats:** Ranged Accuracy, Ranged Attack, Marksmanship skill and AGI scale with your progression. Clear shooting lanes and reliable accuracy are essential.
- **Position:** Maintain a clear line of sight to a living target within 20 yalms, with enough room to stand almost completely still during the aim.
- **Talent independence:** Sightline Shot is available at Lv. 10 without weapon-skill talents. Ordinary ranged attacks and weapon skills remain tools for damage between special shots.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose an appropriate target:** Find a clear 20-yalm firing lane. You may start **Sightline Shot** while disengaged, provided you have a valid target and equipped ranged weapon and ammunition.
2. **Begin Sightline Shot:** Activate the ability to start a **3-second aim**. The ability ordinarily has a **12-second recast**.
3. **Hold your position:** Remain within 1 yalm of your starting point, maintain the required firing lane and avoid changing your engaged target. The aim can be canceled by excessive movement, losing line of sight, invalid equipment or incapacitation.
4. **Resolve the projectile:** A valid completed shot uses one matching projectile and inflicts a strong single-target ranged hit subject to normal accuracy and damage mitigation. A miss still consumes the ordinary 12-second recast.
5. **Recover from canceled aim:** A canceled aim spends **no ammunition** and provides a **3-second retry**. Reposition or address the canceled condition before trying again.
6. **Attack between shots:** Use ordinary ranged attacks and eligible Marksmanship weapon skills when appropriate while planning your next safe Sightline window.
7. **Unlock Deep Sight at Lv. 50:** Toggle **Deep Sight** to replace the 3-second aim with a **4-second aim** for higher damage. Toggle it off again when mechanics demand quicker reactions. The toggle has a 5-second recast.
8. **Use the HUD to plan:** Track remaining aim time, current Deep/standard mode and Sightline recast. Choose the stronger mode only when the extra second of immobility is safe.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Level 10–29:** Standard 3-second Sightline Shot deals **2.0×** normalized ranged damage with **+10 Ranged Accuracy** for the shot.
- **Level 30–49:** Standard Sightline Shot rises to **2.3×** with **+20 Ranged Accuracy**.
- **Level 50–69:** Standard mode remains **2.3×**, while 4-second Deep Sight offers **2.8×**; shot accuracy bonus remains +20.
- **Level 70–75:** Standard remains **2.3×**; Deep Sight becomes **3.2×**, with +30 shot Ranged Accuracy.
- **Aim break conditions:** Moving more than 1 yalm from the start, losing a valid firing lane, changing the engaged quarry, invalid weapon/ammunition or becoming incapacitated cancels aim.
- **Projectile economy:** Only a completed eligible shot consumes a projectile. Cancellation gives a 3-second retry rather than the full 12-second recast.
- **Deep Sight toggle:** Unlocks at Lv. 50 and has a 5-second recast. It changes the next Sightline Shot's aim duration and damage, not its role as one projectile attack.
- **Level-scaled passives:** Marksmanship skill +12/22/34/46, Ranged Accuracy +12/22/34/46, Ranged Attack +12/24/38/54 and AGI +6/12/18/25 at Lv. 10/30/50/70.
- **Soul HUD:** Displays aiming progress, selected mode and remaining shot recast.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Mismatching ammunition:** Guns use bullets; Crossbows use bolts. Bring appropriate projectiles for the equipped weapon.
- **Moving in the final second of an aim:** Movement beyond 1 yalm cancels the shot and forces another setup.
- **Selecting Deep Sight for mobile encounters:** Four seconds of commitment is stronger only when you can remain safe and uninterrupted.
- **Assuming every completed shot will hit:** Normal ranged accuracy and mitigation still apply; a miss uses the ordinary recast.
- **Losing the firing lane or changing targets:** An invalid lane, different engaged quarry or incapacity cancels the pending shot.
- **Confusing Sniper with rapid-fire styles:** Its strength is precision timing and large individual shots, not sustained multi-shot spam.
