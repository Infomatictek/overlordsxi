# Sniper (RNG) - Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Sniper is a Tier 1 single-target damage Soul. Pick a clear firing lane and time one carefully aimed shot. A safe firing window gives you room to trade aim time for damage as you level.

## Setup & Gear

- Equip the Sniper crystal or use `!spec set sniper` from Lv. 10. Bring a crossbow with bolts or a gun with bullets. An ordinary Lv. 10 crossbow and bolts give you the full starting loop.
- Marksmanship is the eligible weapon skill family. Use regular ranged attacks while Sightline Shot recharges. Neither requires weapon skill talent points.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Face an enemy within 20 yalms and leave yourself a clear sightline. Use **Sightline Shot** to start a three-second aim. You can begin while disengaged.
2. Stay within one yalm of your starting position and hold the same engaged target. If the shot resolves, it spends one projectile and has a 12-second recast, including on a miss.
3. Move when an encounter calls for it. A canceled aim spends no ammunition and lets you retry in three seconds.
4. At Lv. 50, toggle **Deep Sight** to aim for four seconds when the enemy gives you a longer window. Toggle it again for the faster shot.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

| Level | Three-second shot | Four-second shot | Added ranged accuracy |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | 2.0× | — | +10 |
| 30 | 2.3× | — | +20 |
| 50 | 2.3× | 2.8× | +20 |
| 70–75 | 2.3× | 3.2× | +30 |

The HUD shows aim duration, the current mode, and remaining recast. Native mitigation and ranged positioning still matter.

## Common Mistakes

- Match ammunition to the weapon subtype. Use bolts with crossbows and bullets with firearms.
- Wait for a real opening before selecting Deep Sight on mobile bosses.
- Aim only at the enemy you mean to shoot. Changing your engaged target, losing line of sight, equipment changes, and incapacitation can cancel aim.
