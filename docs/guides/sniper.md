# Sniper (RNG) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Long-range sightline marksman.** Sniper devotes time to a high-value, single-target shot, unlike Flintlock's sustained independent pistol fire.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Sniper for deliberate priority-target ranged pressure; choose Flintlock for mobile, sustained pistol combat.

## Setup & Gear
- Marksmanship firearm and ammunition, Ranged Accuracy and a clear firing lane.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Select a high-priority enemy at range and start Sightline Shot.
2. **Act:** Hold aim for its 3–4 second window without significant movement, losing line of sight or swapping targets.
3. **Adapt:** Commit the shot; if it resolves as a miss, ammo and cooldown are still consumed.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Sightline Shot reaches 20y and uses a 12s recast, with 2.0/2.3/2.8/3.2x single-shot power and tiered accuracy bonuses.
- Movement over 1y, target switching, obstruction or incapacitation cancels aiming with no ammo spent and a short retry. Eagle Eye Shot never inherits the multiplier.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't expect moving and firing constantly to match a clean prepared shot.
- Base-job access does not guarantee every spell or ability is approved for this Soul.
