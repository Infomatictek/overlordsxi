# Ronin (SAM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Ronin is a **Great Katana tank** combining Seigan, Mind's Eye anticipation and **Mushin Stagger**. A portion of incoming damage is temporarily deferred rather than prevented outright. If you do not remove that deferred pool, it returns as stagger damage over time. Timely Tachi weapon skills, Meditate and Blade Bash **purify** the pool, turning precise defensive action into survival and renewed threat. Ronin is about planning the next purge before the pool becomes dangerous.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Ronin Soul Crystal or use `!spec set ronin` from Lv. 10.
- **Essential weapon:** Main-hand a **Great Katana**. The special tank passives, Seigan bonuses, Mind's Eye follow-up and Stagger conversion are conditional on that weapon even though the equipment system may allow other weapons.
- **Stats:** HP, VIT, Defense, Damage Taken reduction, Parry, Counter and Enmity support the tank loop. Aim for enough Accuracy to land your Tachi weapon skills when you must purify stagger.
- **Party role:** Keep the enemy on you while pacing defensive abilities. Let healers know that deferred damage still exists even when an initial hit appears partly recovered.
- **Resource tracking:** Watch your pending stagger pool, its ticks, current TP, Seigan state and Mind's Eye charges.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Enter Seigan:** Activate **Seigan** with a Great Katana equipped for additional mitigation, Defense, Counter and enmity. Its specialized recast is 15 seconds.
2. **Prepare Third Eye:** Use **Third Eye** under Seigan to gain **Mind's Eye** charges. You receive two before Lv. 50 and three from Lv. 50 onward; eligible enemy attacks can trigger retaliation.
3. **Absorb and defer:** When qualifying damage lands, **Mushin Stagger** can defer **30%** while Seigan is active or **25%** without it. The stored amount becomes a nonlethal damage-over-time threat if it is not purified.
4. **Cleanse with Tachi weapon skills:** Land an eligible **Great Katana weapon skill** to purify about **half** the accumulated Stagger and gain healing/threat benefits. Hold enough TP to react when a large pool appears.
5. **Use Meditate to stabilize:** **Meditate** offers a full Stagger purge, personal healing and threat reinforcement on its modified cooldown; favor it when you cannot wait for another Tachi strike.
6. **Use Blade Bash during danger:** **Blade Bash** can purge all pending Stagger and apply a short stun/Attack Down while adding high threat, making it valuable in a dangerous boss phase.
7. **Prepare larger cooldowns:** **Warding Circle**, **Hamanoha** and **Meikyo Shisui** have specialized defensive, debuff or stagger-purging functions. Choose a button based on the actual incoming threat.
8. **Maintain stable target control:** Avoid dropping Seigan or swapping away from Great Katana. Your anti-burst engine and special enmity loop depend on both.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Mushin Stagger:** Defers up to **25% of qualifying damage** normally or **30% during Seigan**, with a pool capped around **50% maximum HP**. The accumulated pool resolves over **three ticks / nine seconds** unless purified.
- **Stagger purification:** Eligible Tachi weapon skills remove about **50%** of the current pool; **Meditate**, **Blade Bash** and **Meikyo Shisui** provide complete purges under their respective conditions.
- **Mind's Eye:** Third Eye gives **two charges** before Lv. 50 and **three charges** from Lv. 50, countering eligible incoming hits and generating TP/enmity when correctly triggered.
- **Seigan:** Modified tank stance with **15-second recast**, additional Damage Taken reduction, Defense/Counter support and enmity bonuses while correctly equipped.
- **Blade Bash:** Around **30-second recast**, plus targeted control and full Stagger purification.
- **Meditate:** Modified **60-second recast**, with full stagger purification, personal healing and additional party-area threat.
- **Emergency tools:** Warding Circle provides a party defensive effect; Meikyo Shisui offers a stronger personal survival window and refreshes Mind's Eye opportunities.
- **Level progression:** Passive HP, Defense, Damage Taken reduction, Magic Evasion, Enmity and retention bonuses grow through Lv. 10/30/50/70. The Mind's Eye charge count increases at Lv. 50.
- **Important tradeoff:** Deferral is **not true damage immunity**. A neglected Stagger pool still threatens your HP after the triggering attack.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Equipping an unrelated weapon:** Great Katana is required for Ronin's special tank engine despite the general equipment system's flexibility.
- **Treating deferred damage as prevented:** Mushin Stagger still hurts unless you purge it before its damage ticks finish.
- **Saving TP indefinitely while Stagger grows:** A well-timed Tachi weapon skill can protect you more effectively than another offensive swing.
- **Abandoning Seigan for Hasso during tanking:** You lose important stance-dependent defensive and anticipation benefits.
- **Using all full-purge cooldowns at once:** Preserve Meditate or Blade Bash for the next dangerous stagger spike.
- **Neglecting enmity:** Use your modified tank actions to hold the enemy instead of assuming damage alone will secure attention.
