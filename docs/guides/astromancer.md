# Astromancer (BLM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Astromancer is a Tier-1 damage Soul built for a decisive single-target cast. Probe an enemy to choose the element, then wait for an opening long enough to complete Ancient Magic. Once an Ancient strike lands, coordinate with other Astromancers before investing MP in the same target.

## Setup & Gear

- Select at Lv.10 with `!spec set astromancer` or Crystal Socket. Equip a staff and prioritize INT, magic accuracy, magic attack and MP.
- All six probes, Ancient Invocation and Gather Aether are available at Lv.10. No WS talent or specific support subjob is needed.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Use a low-cost probe from **Cinder**, **Rime**, **Gale**, **Bedrock**, **Static Spark** or **Tsunami**. When it deals damage, it sets Ancient Invocation's element.
2. Read the opening. **Ancient Invocation** has a 4.5-second base cast and costs 16% max MP. Its default alignment is Earth.
3. During Ancient recovery, keep using probes or reposition for the next opening. **Gather Aether** restores 10% max MP.
4. At Lv.30, **Elemental Survey** reports the target's lowest elemental resistance ranks. **Starway** returns you to your home point.
5. At Lv.50, the six advanced Ancient spells add fixed-element alternatives. At Lv.70, their burst rises and the extra damage against notorious monsters is capped.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- A completed Ancient cast starts 18 seconds of personal recovery across the Ancient kit.
- The first damaging Ancient hit starts 20 seconds of Astral Saturation on that target. Other Astromancers' follow-up Ancients keep their ordinary damage and receive one tenth of their additional Soul bonus.
- The HUD shows current alignment, Ancient recovery, last target's Saturation and Gather Aether recast.

## Common Mistakes

- Avoid committing several Ancients to the same target at once. Coordinate the opening and use cheap probes between commitments.
- Leave time for the cast to finish before a movement mechanic. Both tier-50 Ancient Magic and its Lv.70 upgrades have a seven-second base cast.
- Check resistance ranks again when a boss changes phase. A low rank can still coexist with separate immunity or absorption.
