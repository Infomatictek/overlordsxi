# Astromancer (BLM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Ancient Magic artillery.** A deliberate Black Mage who specializes in enormous Ancient Magic hits, not Sage-style Helix attrition.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Astromancer over Sage when a boss phase rewards a very large single targeted magical impact rather than ongoing Helix and Titania pressure.

## Setup & Gear
- Staff, MP and Magic Accuracy; choose an element suited to the enemy.
- Unlocks at Lv10; final specialization cap is Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Start:** At Lv10–49, use Ancient Invocation to probe the target; from Lv50, choose the appropriate named Ancient aspect.
2. **Core:** Commit your Ancient cast during an opening, then respect the 18s personal recovery before another empowered impact.
3. **Timing:** Check whether the enemy is already Astrally Saturated before committing another major cast.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Ancient Invocation is the Lv10–49 probe (3.0x); named aspects gain 2.0x at Lv50–69 and 2.5x at Lv70–75. Water is presented as Tsunami.
- Astral Saturation lasts 20s per enemy; concurrent Astromancers receive only 10% of the additional Ancient bonus while it is active. Lv70+ boss bonus is capped.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Do not chain multiple Astromancers' Ancient casts onto one Saturated enemy expecting each to receive full bonuses.
- Do not infer additional spells or job abilities from the base job; check the Soul's actual unlocked actions.
