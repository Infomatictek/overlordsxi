# Astromancer (BLM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Astromancer is a Tier 1 **single-target Ancient Magic** damage Soul built for deliberate, high-commitment spellcasting. Small elemental **probes** choose the next **Ancient Invocation** element, and stronger fixed-element Ancients become available later. You need to identify an enemy's weak element, finish a long cast and avoid wasting expensive follow-up Ancients into **Astral Saturation**. This is a patient burst caster, not a constant spell-spam specialization.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip Astromancer's Soul Crystal or use `!spec set astromancer` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapon and stats:** A Staff is a practical magic-focused choice. Prioritize INT, Magic Accuracy, Magic Attack Bonus and sustainable MP; none of the starting spell loop requires weapon-skill talent points or a particular subjob.
- **Target choice:** Choose an enemy that can remain targetable throughout a 4.5-second Ancient Invocation, or later 7-second advanced Ancient.
- **Party coordination:** Other Astromancers should avoid launching expensive Ancients simultaneously at the same boss once Saturation is likely. Separate targets or stagger major casts when possible.
- **Element planning:** When the enemy changes phase or resistance, re-probe to align your next Invocation. From Lv. 30, Elemental Survey can identify the weakest of the surveyed resistance ranks.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Probe the quarry:** Cast one of **Cinder** (Fire), **Rime** (Ice), **Gale** (Wind), **Bedrock** (Earth), **Static Spark** (Thunder) or **Tsunami** (Water). A probe that deals positive damage aligns Ancient Invocation to that element.
2. **Choose a safe cast window:** **Ancient Invocation** becomes available at Lv. 10. It takes **4.5 seconds** to cast, costs **16% maximum MP** and defaults to Earth if you have not established an alignment.
3. **Commit the Ancient:** Finish the cast on one target. The normal damage and mitigation rules apply, along with Astromancer's additional Ancient bonus. The kit enters an **18-second shared Ancient recovery** after commitment.
4. **Use lighter magic during recovery:** Continue with low-cost elemental probes, update alignment if needed and move into position for the next safe Ancient.
5. **Recover MP deliberately:** **Gather Aether** restores **10% of maximum MP** on a **90-second recast**, provided you are not already at full MP.
6. **Use Elemental Survey at Lv. 30:** **Elemental Survey** reads the enemy's lowest resistance ranks among the six probe elements; it costs **2% max MP** and has a **30-second recast**. **Starway** also unlocks for out-of-battle travel to your home point, costing 100 MP.
7. **At Lv. 50, choose a specialized Ancient:** **Solar Collapse**, **Rimefall**, **Skybreak**, **Worldsplitter**, **Thunderstar** and **Tidal Cataclysm** add fixed-element options with **7-second casts**, **22% maximum MP** cost and the same 18-second Ancient recovery.
8. **Account for Saturation:** A damaging Ancient can establish **20 seconds of Astral Saturation** on that target. During this time, follow-up Ancients from Astromancers gain only one tenth of their unique extra Soul damage, so avoid committing big MP just for diminished bonus returns.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Six starter probes:** Cinder (6 MP), Rime (8), Gale (6), Bedrock (4), Static Spark (9) and Tsunami (5); each has a 1.5-second cast and 3-second recast.
- **Ancient Invocation:** Lv. 10; 4.5-second cast, 16% max MP, default Earth unless aligned by a damaging probe. It uses a shared 18-second recovery with other Ancient spells.
- **Lv. 30 additions:** Elemental Survey (1-second cast, 30-second recast, 2% max MP) and Starway (4-second cast, 100 MP, 10-second recast).
- **Lv. 50 additions:** Six advanced Ancients, one per probe element. Each has a 7-second base cast, 22% max MP cost and shared 18-second recovery.
- **Lv. 70 scaling:** Advanced Ancients gain additional unique bonus damage. Against notorious monsters, the extra Soul damage is capped relative to the target's initial recorded encounter HP.
- **Astral Saturation:** The first damaging Ancient opens a **20-second** target state. Follow-up Ancient damage retains ordinary damage but only **10% of the Astromancer-specific added bonus** during Saturation.
- **Gather Aether:** Restores 10% maximum MP with a 90-second recast. It cannot be activated at full MP.
- **Passive scaling:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, Astromancer gains INT +8/15/22/30, Magic Attack Bonus +10/18/28/38, Magic Accuracy +8/15/24/34 and Max MP +6/10/14/18%.
- **Soul HUD:** Current Invocation element, personal Ancient recovery, current or last target's Saturation time and Gather Aether recast.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Launching an Ancient without aligning or checking resistance:** Default Earth is not always suitable; use a probe or Elemental Survey to inform your element.
- **Trying to rush advanced Ancient casts:** Seven seconds is a long commitment. Save them for uninterrupted openings rather than moving through every boss mechanic.
- **Stacking multiple Astromancer Ancients into one Saturation window:** The follow-ups lose most of the special added bonus, even though their ordinary damage still applies.
- **Overusing percent-MP spells without a recovery plan:** Invocation and advanced Ancients consume substantial MP; weave probes and use Gather Aether appropriately.
- **Treating a low elemental resistance as guaranteed damage:** Separate immunity, absorption and ordinary mitigation may still apply.
- **Ignoring the shared Ancient recovery:** Switching between different Ancient spell names does not eliminate the common 18-second constraint.
