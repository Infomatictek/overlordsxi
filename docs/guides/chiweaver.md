# Chi Weaver (MNK) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Chi Weaver converts Monk into a **0-MP unarmed ranged Light-Chi healer** (`Healer` tier: -60% damage dealt, -58% WS damage). Instead of weapons or spells, you stand up to **20 yalms away** completely bare-handed, firing Light Chi bolts that emit **12y AoE healing pulses around your target**, building **Boost stacks (0–100)**, and detonating huge party-wide `Chi Blast` heals every 5 seconds.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapons:** **Completely unarmed — BOTH Main and Sub must be empty.** Equipping any weapon automatically unequips it and disables your ranged Light Chi bolts & 20y ranged WSs.
- **Priority Stats:** MND, Cure Potency, Magic Atk. Bonus (MAB), Magic Accuracy (MACC), H2H Skill, Haste, HP. (**Zero MP/Refresh needed** — Chi Weaver uses no MP.)

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Stand & Shoot Bare-Handed:** Activate `Focus` (+25 MACC, +20 MAB, +15% Cure Potency) and `Dodge` (+30 EVA, -10% DT, -25 Enmity). Stand within 20y of the boss (and ideally within 12y of injured melee, or position for `Chi Blast`) and engage — your auto-attacks become Light Chi bolts that build **+1 Boost stack per hit** (max 100) and pulse a 12y AoE heal around the mob.
2. **Weave `Boost`, `Resonant Chi`, & WSs:** Tap `Boost` (+5 stacks, +50 TP, free 12y pulse) and `Resonant Chi` (20s CD, 20y: +125 TP + 1.75x AoE heal + 1 debuff cleanse around target). Fire ranged H2H Weaponskills to gain **+3/4/5 Boost stacks** (at 1000/1500/2000+ TP) + a 12y heal pulse (cleansing 1 debuff at 2000+ TP).
3. **Detonate Stacks with `Chi Blast`:** `Chi Blast` has a **5s cooldown** and deals 0 damage — instead it spends ALL current Boost stacks (0–100) to unleash a **20y AoE heal around both you and your target**, scaling sharply with stack count and cleansing **1/2/3 debuffs at 25/50/80+ stacks**.
4. **Heavy Healing Cooldowns:** Pop `Inner Harmony` (45s CD, 18s overdrive: +40% Cure Potency, +60 MAB/MACC, +15% Abil Haste, +40% Pulse potency, +25% Chi Blast) + `Hundred Fists` (2x Chi bolt firing speed) during intense raid damage, or use `Tranquil Lotus` (30s CD) for an instant 12–24% Max HP party heal + 15s Regen/Sanctuary.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Boost Stacks (0–100, 180s):** Every 5 stacks passively grant +1% Cure Potency, +1 MACC, and +1 MAB (up to +20 each at 100 stacks). `Chi Blast` cashes in all stacks for exponential AoE healing.
- **Dual Healing Ranges:** Auto-attack & WS pulses heal within **12y of the mob** (and only heal you for 35% if you stand farther than 12y away); `Chi Blast`, `Tranquil Lotus`, and `Chakra` reach Allies/Self within **20y / 12y around you**.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't equip any weapon:** Main & Sub must stay completely empty at all times.
- **Don't forget ranged healers take only 35% of mob-centered pulses if >12y away:** If you are hurt far from the boss, hit `Chi Blast`, `Tranquil Lotus`, or `Chakra` (cleanses Poison/Para/Blind/Silence + self/party AoE heal) instead of relying purely on auto-attack pulses.
- **Don't confuse `Chi Blast` with damage:** It deals 0 damage here — it is your signature AoE burst heal and debuff cleanse.
