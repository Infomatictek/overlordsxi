# Chi Weaver (MNK) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Chi Weaver is a **completely unarmed, ranged light-chi healer**. Its ordinary attacks become light bolts that heal allies near the quarry and build up to **100 Boost stacks**. **Chi Blast** no longer exists as a damage finisher: it consumes Boost for a large group heal and cleanse. You manage two different healing centers—the enemy for attack pulses and yourself/your target for emergency recovery—while staying safely within your 20-yalm combat range.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Chi Weaver Soul Crystal or use `!spec set chiweaver` at Lv. 10.
- **Empty hands required:** **Both Main and Sub must be empty.** Weapons disable its special light bolts; its equipment rules can remove invalid weapons.
- **Stats:** MND, Cure Potency, Magic Attack Bonus, Magic Accuracy, Hand-to-Hand skill, Haste and HP. **MP is not the operating resource** for the main Soul kit.
- **Position:** Stay within **20 yalms** of the intended enemy, ideally also near allies who require healing within **12 yalms of the target**.
- **Party role:** Watch the distribution of injured players, not just the boss. You can heal from range but must choose the appropriate target-centered or self-centered action.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Stay unarmed and engage:** Activate **Focus** for stronger magic/healing and **Dodge** for safety, then attack from a valid range so your ordinary swings become light-chi projectiles.
2. **Build Boost stacks:** Each successful light bolt adds **one Boost** and pulses recovery to allies within roughly **12 yalms of the quarry**.
3. **Use Boost as an active healer button:** **Boost** grants five stacks, 50 TP and another healing pulse. It accelerates preparation for a larger Chi Blast.
4. **Use Resonant Chi for the group:** **Resonant Chi** adds TP, strengthens the target-centered pulse and removes a qualifying debuff. Time it when nearby allies are actually hurt.
5. **Convert TP through Hand-to-Hand weapon skills:** Available ranged Hand-to-Hand weapon skills build additional Boost based on TP spent, produce healing pulses and can cleanse at higher TP thresholds.
6. **Spend stacks with Chi Blast:** The special **Chi Blast** has a **five-second recast** and **deals no damage**. It consumes the current Boost stack pool for a stronger heal over a wider area; higher stacks enable more cleansing.
7. **Prepare emergency windows:** **Inner Harmony** improves pulse strength, Chi Blast and magic-based support. **Hundred Fists** accelerates light-bolt output, while **Tranquil Lotus** provides a direct group heal and Regen.
8. **Use Chakra when appropriate:** Its specialized cleanse and healing can cover ailments or personal damage when target-centered pulses are insufficient.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Boost:** Up to **100 stacks**, retained for approximately **180 seconds**. Ordinary light bolts add one; Boost adds five; eligible weapon skills add more according to TP.
- **Passive stack reward:** Every five Boost stacks improve Cure Potency, Magic Accuracy and Magic Attack Bonus, up to the listed maximum at 100 stacks.
- **Chi Blast:** **Five-second recast**; consumes all available Boost and heals instead of dealing damage. Cleansing improves at **25, 50 and 80** stacks.
- **Two healing centers:** Light-chi bolts and ranged Hand-to-Hand weapon skills pulse around the enemy (about **12 yalms**). Other emergency heals can cover allies nearer the Chi Weaver (up to about **20 yalms**).
- **Distance penalty on self recovery:** When positioned too far from the enemy-centered pulse, your own recovery from those pulses is reduced; use a self-centered emergency action if needed.
- **Resonant Chi:** **20-second recast**, target up to 20 yalms, adds **125 TP**, improved pulse healing and one-debuff cleansing.
- **Inner Harmony:** **45-second recast**, **18-second** enhanced healing window. Tranquil Lotus is a separate group rescue with approximately **30-second recast**.
- **Role progression:** Healing pulse and Chi Blast capacity grow with level, equipment and available Hand-to-Hand skills; the unarmed requirement is unchanged.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Equipping a weapon or offhand item:** Either can disable the entire ranged light-chi attack engine.
- **Using Chi Blast as damage:** In Chi Weaver it is an area heal and debuff cleanse.
- **Standing too far from the fight:** Light bolts require a valid ranged quarry, and your own recovery from target pulses can be reduced at larger distances.
- **Spending 100 Boost stacks only after everyone is safe:** Build toward useful windows, but cash out in time to prevent deaths.
- **Ignoring where the injured players are:** Target-centered and self-centered healing pulses cover different areas.
- **Trying to solve an MP problem with gear:** The main Soul kit operates through Boost, TP and healing statistics instead of MP costs.
