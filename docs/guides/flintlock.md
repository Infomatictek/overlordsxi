# Flintlock (COR) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Flintlock is Corsair's **pure single-target Gun DPS sniper** (`Damage` tier: 0% damage penalty, 1.00x WS scaling, 100% Bullet & Card Recycle). Instead of rolling party buffs, `Phantom Roll` becomes a 15s self-only **Deadeye Chamber** (+15–30% WSD & +15% Ranged Crit DMG), while **Quick Draw** primes targets with **Powder Mark** so your Marksmanship Weaponskills detonate it for bonus Fire damage and **+150 TP**.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon:** **Gun (`Marksmanship`) in Ranged + Bullets in Ammo.** (If empty at Lv.10+, socketing auto-equips `Firefly` + `99x Bronze Bullet`, and both Bullets and Quick Draw Cards have 100% Recycle.)
- **Priority Stats:** AGI, Ranged Attack, Ranged Accuracy, Snapshot, Rapid Shot, Crit Rate/Damage, Marksmanship Skill.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Self-Buff with `Phantom Roll` (`Deadeye Chamber`):** Press `Phantom Roll` (15s CD — **self-only**, no Double-Up) to activate **Deadeye Chamber** (30s: +15–30% ALL WS Damage & +15% Ranged Crit Damage).
2. **Prime with `Quick Draw` (`Powder Mark`):** Shoot **any Quick Draw** (10s charge recast) to apply **Powder Mark** (10s) onto the target.
3. **Burst Window (`Hair Trigger` + Marksmanship WS):** Pop `Hair Trigger` (30s CD, 10s: +30% Triple Shot, +30% Snapshot, +25% WSD, -15% DEF) and unleash a **Marksmanship WS** to detonate `Powder Mark` for extra Fire damage AND immediately refund **+150 TP**!
4. **Reset Charges & Refill TP:** Use `Broadside Salvo` (22s CD, 20y) for a heavy ranged hit + **+250 TP** + **+1 Quick Draw charge**; use `Wild Card` (180s CD, self) for an instant **+1000 TP**, **+2 QD charges**, and full standard JA resets.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Powder Mark Loop:** `Quick Draw` -> Marksmanship WS detonation (+Fire damage +150 TP).
- **100% Ammo & Card Preservation:** Neither your bullets nor your Quick Draw Trump Cards are ever consumed.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't fire melee WSs expecting to pop `Powder Mark`:** You MUST use a **Marksmanship WS** with a Gun equipped.
- **Don't wait around for Double-Up or party rolls:** On Flintlock, `Phantom Roll` is an instant 15s self-buff.
- **Watch `-15% DEF` during `Hair Trigger`:** Use `Hair Trigger` when the tank holds solid hate since Flintlock has zero self-healing.
