# Verdant (GEO) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Verdant is a **Luopan-centered Geomancer healer**. Its active Luopan sustains **Verdant Bloom** pulses that heal nearby allies, restore MP and convert overhealing into protective barriers. Normal healing, Geomancy actions and eligible weapon skills can trigger **Nature's Surge** for extra group recovery and to restore Luopan HP. **Canopy of the Ancients**, **Verdant Renewal** and a modified **Life Cycle** help the party survive sudden danger. Verdant's personal damage is reduced in exchange for exceptional group sustain.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip Verdant's Soul Crystal or use `!spec set verdant` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** **Club with Shield** or **Staff**, plus a suitable **Handbell**. Eligible Club and Staff weapon skills can trigger additional healing.
- **Stats:** Max HP, MND, Cure Potency, Fast Cast, Geomancy, Handbell and Healing skill. Many pulses depend on the caster's HP scaling.
- **Essential pet:** Keep a **Luopan** deployed and alive when possible. Verdant Bloom cannot pulse without the active companion.
- **Position:** Most support effects reach **15 yalms**. Place the Luopan and stand where key party members can receive repeated healing while minimizing exposure.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Summon the Luopan early:** Use a suitable **Geo** spell to deploy it before the party takes sustained damage. Keep it available for Verdant Bloom.
2. **Maintain passive healing:** While the Luopan is present in combat, **Verdant Bloom** pulses every **3 seconds**, restoring a percentage of Verdant's maximum HP to nearby allies and **15 MP** to recipients.
3. **Turn overhealing into protection:** Excess Bloom recovery can become a short **Barkskin** barrier, capped by the recipient's maximum HP.
4. **Trigger Nature's Surge:** Cast appropriate **Cure, Indi or Geo** spells, or use an eligible Club/Staff weapon skill, to restore the Luopan's HP and pulse a larger party heal with a one-debuff cleanse.
5. **Prepare Canopy for incoming damage:** Use **Canopy of the Ancients** before an intensive boss phase. During its 12 seconds, nearby allies gain damage-reduction protection and knockback immunity; your applicable spells and weapon skills can trigger further healing.
6. **Use Verdant Renewal for group emergencies:** It delivers a major nearby-party heal and three-debuff cleanse, followed by Regen and Refresh support.
7. **Use Life Cycle to restore balance:** The modified **Life Cycle** heals the Luopan back to full and provides additional party HP recovery without the typical self-HP sacrifice.
8. **Coordinate Bolster:** When **Bolster** is active, Verdant Bloom's pulse potency increases significantly. Reserve it for periods of unusually high sustained damage.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Verdant Bloom:** A **3-second** combat pulse that heals **6/8/10/12% of Verdant maximum HP** at Lv. 10/30/50/70, restores **15 MP**, and can convert overhealing into a barrier capped at **25% of the recipient's maximum HP**.
- **Bolster interaction:** Doubles Verdant Bloom's healing percentage while active.
- **Nature's Surge:** Qualifying Cure/Indi/Geo spell use or Club/Staff weapon skill restores Luopan HP and triggers a **16% Verdant maximum-HP** group heal and one-debuff cleanse.
- **Canopy of the Ancients:** **25-second recast**, **12-second** 15-yalm protective window with knockback immunity, added damage protection and **10% extra group healing** on qualifying actions.
- **Verdant Renewal:** **20-second recast**, approximately **30% party healing**, cleansing of up to three debuffs, and 15 seconds of hybrid Regen and Refresh.
- **Life Cycle:** Modified **15-second recast**; restores the Luopan fully and provides **25% party healing**, without costing Verdant self-HP.
- **Level progression:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, Max HP/MP +14/18/22/28%, MND/VIT +12/18/24/32, Cure Potency +18/26/34/42 and Fast Cast +15/25/35/45, among other healer passives.
- **Luopan recovery:** It is the anchor for the passive pulse. If it disappears, you must restore the companion or rely on active recovery skills until it is back.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Forgetting to summon a Luopan:** Verdant Bloom does not operate without a living deployed companion.
- **Standing too far from the group:** Multiple benefits use a 15-yalm radius rather than entire-zone coverage.
- **Only waiting for passive pulses:** Large incoming hits require Nature's Surge, Renewal, Canopy or Life Cycle.
- **Wasting Bolster while no healing is needed:** Its amplified Bloom is most valuable during sustained boss pressure.
- **Allowing the Luopan to fall and staying in the same loop:** Resummon it or adjust to active healing when possible.
- **Using offensive weapon skills without considering survival:** The Soul's strongest use of Club/Staff weapon skills is additional party recovery, not damage rankings.
