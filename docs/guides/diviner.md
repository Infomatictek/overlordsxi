# Diviner (GEO) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Diviner is a Tier 3 support Soul that turns its own position into the endpoint of a moving accuracy corridor. Place a fixed meridian anchor, move a short distance away, and keep allies close to the line between the anchor and yourself. This grants both physical Accuracy and Magic Accuracy, making Diviner most effective when the party can coordinate its formation. Your challenge is maintaining useful corridor coverage without leaving the correct battle area.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Select Diviner at Lv. 10 using the Soul Crystal or `!spec set diviner`.
- **Weapons:** Club and Staff are its eligible weapon-skill and talent families. Its core corridor works without weapon-skill talent investment or a specific support job.
- **Positioning:** Choose an anchor location from which you can move 3–15 yalms while allies remain close to the segment. The corridor does not work if you stand on your anchor or move too far away.
- **Stat priorities:** The native Soul progression adds Geomancy skill and HP. Positioning and maintaining ally coverage are more important to its unique support mechanic than dealing damage.
- **Party coordination:** Discuss where the tank, melee attackers and casters will stand. A straight line that covers several allies is more useful than a longer line that misses them.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Place your anchor (Lv. 10+):** Cast **Lay Meridian** at your current position. It costs 0 MP, has an 18-second recast and creates a fixed anchor that normally lasts 30 seconds.
2. **Form the corridor:** Walk 3–15 yalms from the anchor on roughly the same floor. The line between that anchor and your current position becomes the active support corridor.
3. **Cover your allies:** Party members within the segment's width and vertical limit gain the accuracy bonuses. This includes allies near either endpoint, not just the middle. Stay in the same battle and instance as your party.
4. **Maintain coverage:** Watch the Soul HUD's anchor distance, covered member count, remaining duration and corridor validity. Move to adjust the line when your party repositions; moving yourself changes the second endpoint instantly.
5. **Attack without abandoning your role:** Use **Ley Spark** for a single-target earth cast when your positioning is safe. It unlocks at Lv. 10, costs 4 MP and has a 5-second recast.
6. **Reanchor at Lv. 30+:** Use **Meridian Shift** to move the fixed anchor to your current location, then step 3–15 yalms away again. Shift has a 20-second recast and does not refresh the corridor's original expiration time.
7. **Reset when necessary:** **Recall Meridian** ends your active corridor and its bonuses. Reposition and cast Lay Meridian again when it is available; do not leave a corridor placed where it helps nobody.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Level 10–29:** +12 physical Accuracy and +12 Magic Accuracy. Allies must stay within 2 yalms horizontally of the corridor; anchor duration is 30 seconds.
- **Level 30–49:** +20 Accuracy and Magic Accuracy, 2-yalm width and 30-second lifetime. Meridian Shift is now available.
- **Level 50–69:** +28 Accuracy and Magic Accuracy; the corridor grows to 2.5 yalms horizontally and lasts 30 seconds.
- **Level 70–75:** +35 Accuracy and Magic Accuracy with a 2.5-yalm width and a 40-second lifetime.
- **Valid corridor geometry:** Your distance from the anchor must be at least 3 and at most 15 horizontal yalms. The endpoints cannot differ by more than 3 yalms vertically, and each recipient must be within 3 vertical yalms of the line.
- **Party and battle limits:** The bonus applies only to valid party members in the same zone, battle and combat instance. Moving outside the corridor removes the benefit.
- **Overlapping Diviners:** When multiple valid corridors cover the same ally, only the strongest available bonus applies. A weaker corridor can resume applying if the stronger one no longer covers the ally.
- **Soul HUD:** Shows corridor duration, anchor distance, horizontal/vertical validity, covered party count and bonus strength.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Standing directly on your anchor:** A corridor shorter than 3 yalms does not provide its bonus. Move outward after laying or shifting it.
- **Stretching past 15 yalms:** Going too far temporarily disables the lane. Watch its distance indicator.
- **Ignoring elevation:** Both the anchor-to-you height change and each member's distance above or below the corridor can invalidate coverage.
- **Treating this like an unrestricted party aura:** Allies must actually be positioned near your line; they do not receive the benefit everywhere in the area.
- **Expecting Meridian Shift to renew duration:** It moves the anchor, but the original expiry remains. Plan to recast Lay Meridian when the lane runs out.
- **Repositioning for personal damage at the expense of support:** Ley Spark is a filler action. Prioritize maintaining coverage for allies who need Accuracy and Magic Accuracy.
