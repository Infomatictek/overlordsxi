# Diviner (GEO) - Playstyle Guide

Diviner is a Tier 3 support Soul. Place a fixed anchor and use your position to form an accuracy corridor for allies.

## Level 10 opening

1. Equip the crystal or use `!spec set diviner`. Club and Staff are the eligible weapon-skill families.
2. Cast **Lay Meridian** where you want one end of the lane. It costs no MP and has an 18-second recast.
3. Move 3–15 yalms away on the same floor. Your position forms the other end. Allies near the segment receive physical and magical accuracy.
4. Adjust your position as the group or enemy moves. The HUD shows anchor distance, covered party members and time remaining. **Ley Spark** gives you a single-target earth cast while holding position.
5. Use **Recall Meridian** to remove the anchor and its bonuses. From Lv. 30, **Meridian Shift** moves the anchor to your current position. Step away again to reactivate the line. Shifting keeps the original expiry.

The support loop works with any subjob and zero weapon-skill talents.

| Level | ACC and MACC | Radius from segment | Lifetime |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10–29 | +12 | 2y | 30s |
| 30–49 | +20 | 2y | 30s |
| 50–69 | +28 | 2.5y | 30s |
| 70–75 | +35 | 2.5y | 40s |

The radius includes the ends of the segment. Recipients must stay within three vertical yalms of the line and share your party and battle area. If two Diviners cover the same ally, only the strongest bonus applies. Leaving that corridor restores the weaker bonus when the ally is still inside it.

Keep the team close to the intended path. A line that stretches beyond 15 yalms, collapses below three, or crosses more than three vertical yalms temporarily loses power.
