# Diviner (GEO) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Positional geomancy corridor.** Diviner creates a narrow beneficial Meridian line that rewards party positioning instead of providing always-on ambient auras.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Diviner when holding a specific lane yields superior team accuracy, not when everyone needs portable buffs.

## Setup & Gear
- Geomancy and an encounter where the party can stand along a safe, defensible corridor.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Lay a Meridian between the field anchor and your player position at an appropriate distance.
2. **Act:** Ask allies who need Accuracy and Magic Accuracy to stay in the corridor while the enemy remains positioned.
3. **Adapt:** From Lv30, use Meridian Shift to change its position when the battle moves; Recall when the location is no longer useful.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Lay Meridian has an 18s cooldown and 30–40s lifetime. The beneficial corridor is 3–15y long and about 2–2.5y wide.
- Party members within its correct height and line gain 12/20/28/35 Accuracy and Magic Accuracy. Strongest overlapping Diviner segment takes precedence; Shift does not reset lifetime.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't expect an area-wide buff while the party is scattered or the boss constantly moves.
- Confirm available commands and casting rules in the active Soul's menu before building a rotation.
