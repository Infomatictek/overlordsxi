# Bandit (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Opportunistic melee striker.** Bandit delivers dagger-driven damage while exploiting Defense Down created by other party members.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Bandit over Black Belt for coordinated weakness-exploitation burst; Black Belt has a steadier natural WS rhythm.

## Setup & Gear
- Dagger or Sword with Accuracy, critical and attack-focused gearing.
- Unlocks at Lv10; final specialization cap is Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Start:** Use the Lv10 Sneak Attack loan for immediate openers.
2. **Core:** Watch the target for an existing Defense Down, then land a Dagger or Sword weapon skill.
3. **Timing:** Receive a conditional TP rebate and continue offensive pressure while waiting for another opportunity.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Landed qualifying weapon skills refund 75/100/125/150 TP once per 12s when Defense Down is present.
- Bandit does not apply Defense Down itself; misses do not consume its ready refund.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't wait indefinitely for Defense Down or expect evasion-tank threat tools.
- Do not infer additional spells or job abilities from the base job; check the Soul's actual unlocked actions.
