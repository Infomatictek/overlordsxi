# Bandit (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Opportunistic physical DPS. The reward comes from spotting existing openings, not building a proprietary resource meter. Inspired by the Thief's exploitative melee style.

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapon:** Dagger preferred; Sword supports the opening mechanic.
- **Priority:** Accuracy, Attack, Dexterity, critical rate; ordinary Lv10 equipment is a valid starting point.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. Fight where a tank can hold the boss's attention and you can remain in melee safely.
2. Watch for the boss to gain Defense Down from allies or other effects.
3. Execute a Dagger/Sword weaponskill during that opening; gain a small TP return once every 12s.
4. Do not delay every weapon skill waiting for a perfect Defense Down window. The class maintains ordinary DPS without the opportunistic proc.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Exploit Weakness:** One conditional TP reward, not a stack-building class.
- Strong at coordinated single-target pressure; less effective during enforced disengagement.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't expect Bandit itself to apply Defense Down.
- Don't confuse its small TP rebate with Black Belt's consistent fast WS rhythm.
- Don't assume its positional survivability equals a tank Soul.
