# Bandit (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Bandit is a Tier 1 melee damage Soul whose specialty is turning a well-timed weapon skill into renewed TP. **Find the Gap** marks a personal opening on one enemy, while an existing **Defense Down** effect is another way to prepare a qualifying weapon skill. When that weapon skill lands for positive damage, **Opportunism** refunds TP. **Back Alley Strike** adds a positional critical-hit setup. Your aim is to maintain a steady sequence of correctly prepared weapon skills without wasting the short opening or risking an unsafe rear approach.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Select Bandit at Lv. 10 with the Soul Crystal or `!spec set bandit`.
- **Weapons:** Equip a **Dagger or Sword** as your main weapon. The Bandit opening and TP refund require a compatible weapon when your weapon skill begins and resolves.
- **Gear priorities:** Accuracy first for reliable hits, then DEX, Attack, critical-hit rate and TP generation. Bandit already gains bonuses in these areas as it levels.
- **Party coordination:** If an ally reliably applies Defense Down, use that opening instead of spending Find the Gap unnecessarily. Unlike the ally's Defense Down, Bandit's personal gap does not change the enemy's defense for other players.
- **Positioning:** Rear access matters for Back Alley Strike. Do not compromise tank positioning or survival just to set up a critical attack.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose your quarry:** Engage one enemy with your Dagger or Sword and build enough TP for a weapon skill.
2. **Check for an opening:** If the target already has Defense Down, it qualifies for Opportunism. Otherwise approach within 5 yalms and activate **Find the Gap** to mark the target for 15 seconds.
3. **Commit a damaging weapon skill:** Begin an eligible Dagger or Sword weapon skill while the target is exposed. The opening is checked when the weapon skill starts, then the successful positive-damage hit is checked again at resolution.
4. **Collect Opportunism:** A qualifying landed weapon skill refunds TP (75 at Lv. 10, increasing with level) and starts a separate 12-second internal lockout. Use that refunded TP to maintain offensive momentum.
5. **Set up Back Alley Strike when safe:** Activate **Back Alley Strike** to prepare a critical attack from behind. The preparation lasts up to 20 seconds and the ability has a 45-second recast.
6. **Do not waste overlapping windows:** Find the Gap has a 24-second recast. Time its short 15-second opening around your TP readiness; if an ally's Defense Down already provides an opening, preserve your own setup for another opportunity.
7. **Follow target changes carefully:** Your personal gap is bound to one enemy. If the party switches targets, reassess the new enemy's Defense Down and your gap availability.
8. **Use the Soul HUD:** Monitor the target's Defense Down, personal gap duration, Find the Gap recast, Back Alley Strike recast, and Opportunism's separate 12-second lockout.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Opportunism at Lv. 10–29:** Refunds **75 TP** on a qualified damaging weapon skill.
- **Lv. 30–49:** Opportunism refund increases to **100 TP**.
- **Lv. 50–69:** Opportunism refund increases to **125 TP**.
- **Lv. 70–75:** Opportunism refund increases to **150 TP**.
- **Eligibility:** The target must have your personal gap or Defense Down when the weapon skill starts. You must use Dagger or Sword and deal positive weapon-skill damage to that same target for the refund.
- **Find the Gap:** Single-target personal setup; 5-yalm range, 15-second duration and 24-second recast. It marks an opening for you, not a party-wide defense reduction.
- **Successful consumption:** A qualifying weapon skill that refunds TP clears your personal gap on that target. An interrupted or unsuccessful weapon skill does not consume Opportunism's 12-second lockout.
- **Back Alley Strike:** Prepares a rear-position critical hit for up to 20 seconds with a 45-second recast.
- **Level-scaled passives:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, Bandit gains Dagger skill +12/22/34/48, Accuracy +10/20/30/42, Attack +12/24/38/55, DEX +6/12/18/24, critical-hit rate +3/5/7/9, and Store TP +3/5/7/10.
- **Soul HUD:** The displayed gap is personal and target-specific; the Defense Down indicator instead reflects the status currently on your target.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Casting Find the Gap before TP is ready:** Its 15-second opening can expire before you are prepared to weapon skill.
- **Assuming a failed weapon skill earns TP:** Opportunism needs positive damage to its prepared target; missed or interrupted attempts do not award the refund.
- **Confusing your personal gap with a real Defense Down debuff:** The personal opening benefits Bandit, not the party's damage.
- **Ignoring Opportunism's separate cooldown:** Find the Gap may be ready while the TP refund is still locked for 12 seconds.
- **Using the wrong weapon:** The mechanic checks main-weapon compatibility.
- **Taking unnecessary risks for Back Alley Strike:** A rear critical is useful only if getting there is safe and does not disrupt the party.
