# Bandit (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Bandit is a Tier-1 damage Soul that looks for a weakness before spending TP. Time your Dagger or Sword weapon skill to reclaim part of its cost.

## Setup & Gear

- Select at Lv.10 with `!spec set bandit` or Crystal Socket. Equip a Dagger or Sword.
- Prioritize melee accuracy, DEX and TP flow. A basic weapon skill and ordinary gear are enough to use Opportunism at Lv.10.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Build TP until you can use a weapon skill.
2. If your enemy already has Defense Down, use that opening. Otherwise use **Find the Gap** near the target for a personal opening lasting 15 seconds.
3. Land a damaging weapon skill against the exposed target. **Opportunism** refunds 75 TP at Lv.10, rising to 100/125/150 at Lv.30/50/70.
4. When you can reach the enemy's rear, prepare a critical hit with **Back Alley Strike**.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- Opportunism has a 12-second internal cooldown. Find the Gap has a 24-second recast. Back Alley Strike has a 45-second recast.
- The HUD shows whether an opening is present, whether the refund is ready, and the relevant timer.
- Only Bandit can exploit its own personal gap. The enemy keeps its ordinary Defense for everyone else.

## Common Mistakes

- Use Find the Gap when you can spend the TP soon. The mark applies to one target and expires.
- A missed or interrupted weapon skill leaves the TP refund available. The enemy must already be exposed when the weapon skill starts.
- Back Alley Strike rewards the rear position, so save it for a safe chance to get there.
