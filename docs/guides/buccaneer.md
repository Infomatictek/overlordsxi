# Buccaneer (COR) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Buccaneer turns Corsair into a **swashbuckling 1H Sword + Gun Stoneskin tank** (`Tank` tier: -60% damage dealt, 0.12x WS scaling, +60–120 baseline Enmity). You maintain **Galleon's Roll**, stack **Grapeshot (max 3)** via `Grapeshot Broadside` and **1H Sword Weaponskills (`Point-Blank Pistol`)**, and cash those stacks in with **Quick Draw (`Salt-Spray`)** to generate up to **18% Max HP Stoneskin shields** plus Stuns, Dispels, or Cripple debuffs!

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapons:** **1H Sword in Main Hand + Gun (`Marksmanship`) in Ranged Slot.** Both are required for `Point-Blank Pistol` Sword WS procs, `Grapeshot Broadside`, `Salt-Spray`, and Sword/Marksmanship skill bonuses. (Starter Gun & Bullets auto-equip if empty, with 100% Recycle.)
- **Sub Slot Choice:** Leave **Sub EMPTY** for a built-in **12% virtual Pistol-Guard Block** (35% physical damage reduction), or equip a Buckler shield if preferred.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Stance Up & Open:** Press `Phantom Roll` (20s CD, **self-only** -> **Galleon's Roll**: -12% DT, +20% DEF, +25 Enmity for 60s), then pull with `Grapeshot Broadside` (25s CD, 12y ST: massive threat, Blind, Slow 12s, and **+2 `Grapeshot`**).
2. **Build `Grapeshot` (`1H Sword WSs`):** Land **1H Sword Weaponskills** (`Point-Blank Pistol`) — every Sword WS deals single-target threat, heals you **8–14% Max HP**, and adds **+1 `Grapeshot`** (max 3).
3. **Spend `Grapeshot` with `Quick Draw` (`Salt-Spray`):** Every **Quick Draw** (12s CD) generates high threat and consumes all `Grapeshot` (1–3) for a **6% / 12% / 18% Max HP `Salt-Spray Stoneskin`** (12s) plus elemental utility:
   - **Earth / Water Shot:** Inflicts `Cripple` (`-15% ATK / -15% MATK / -20 ACC`).
   - **Ice Shot:** **2s Stun**.
   - **Light / Dark Shot:** **Dispel**.
4. **Defensive Cooldown:** Pop `Iron Hull` (45s CD, 12s) for **-18% DT**, **Knockback Immunity**, a **20% Max HP self-heal**, and self-Erase.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Grapeshot (0–3 stacks, 30s):** Built by `Grapeshot Broadside` (+2) and 1H Sword WSs (+1). Consumed by any `Quick Draw` for 6% Max HP Stoneskin per stack (up to 18%).
- **Empty Sub Advantage:** Keeping your Sub slot empty gives a free 12% Pistol-Guard block rate passively.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't fire Marksmanship WSs expecting self-heals or Grapeshot:** `Point-Blank Pistol` ONLY triggers on **1H Sword Weaponskills**.
- **Don't swap off Sword or Gun:** Losing either weapon shuts down `Point-Blank Pistol`, `Grapeshot Broadside`, `Salt-Spray`, and your skill bonuses.
- **Don't let `Galleon's Roll` drop:** Refresh it via `Phantom Roll` (20s CD) every 60s.
