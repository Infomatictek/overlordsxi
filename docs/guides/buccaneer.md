# Buccaneer (COR) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Buccaneer is a **Sword-and-Gun Corsair tank**. It builds threat and **Grapeshot** stacks through **Grapeshot Broadside** and eligible Sword weapon skills, then spends those stacks with Quick Draw to generate **Salt-Spray** barriers and situational elemental effects. **Galleon's Roll** gives a long defensive self-buff, and **Iron Hull** is the emergency survival window. Buccaneer is not a party-roll support Soul or a high-damage ranged specialist.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Buccaneer Soul Crystal or use `!spec set buccaneer` at Lv. 10.
- **Required loadout:** Equip a **one-handed Sword in Main** and a **Gun in Ranged**, with compatible ammunition. Both are needed for the special Sword weapon-skill and Quick Draw tank interactions.
- **Sub slot choice:** An empty Sub slot can enable a special **virtual Pistol Guard** chance. A shield is an alternative if your actual equipment and survival requirements favor it.
- **Stats:** HP, Defense, Damage Taken reduction, Enmity, Accuracy and reliable TP gain sustain the tank loop.
- **Role:** Establish hate before party burst, then keep a barrier available for the next dangerous hit. Quick Draw charges are not only damage tools; they are a defensive resource.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Activate Galleon's Roll:** Use your specialized self-only roll for **60 seconds of defensive buffs** including Damage Taken reduction, Defense and Enmity. Refresh it before the next dangerous phase.
2. **Claim with Grapeshot Broadside:** Activate **Grapeshot Broadside** on one enemy within 12 yalms. It supplies significant direct threat, short Blind and Slow effects, and **two Grapeshot stacks**.
3. **Build with Sword weapon skills:** Land eligible one-handed Sword weapon skills to trigger **Point-Blank Pistol**, creating additional threat, personal healing and **one Grapeshot stack** per eligible event.
4. **Spend the stacks with Quick Draw:** Use a suitable Quick Draw shot to consume accumulated Grapeshot and create **Salt-Spray** protection. More stacks produce a larger temporary Stoneskin barrier.
5. **Choose the shot for the fight:** Earth/Water shots can apply **Cripple** to reduce offense; Ice can briefly stun; Light/Dark can dispel an enemy enhancement when valid.
6. **Use Iron Hull before major damage:** **Iron Hull** provides a short, stronger defensive window with recovery, knockback protection and personal status cleansing.
7. **Repeat the defensive loop:** Rebuild Grapeshot with Broadside and Sword weapon skills, then spend before its short timer expires or before a burst that would otherwise consume too much HP.
8. **Adjust the Sub slot intentionally:** If leaving Sub empty for virtual Pistol Guard, remember that equipping a shield changes the defensive mechanics. Use whichever option fits the encounter.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Galleon's Roll:** Self-only special roll, **20-second recast**, **60-second duration**; provides approximately **12% Damage Taken reduction, +20% Defense and +25 Enmity**.
- **Grapeshot:** Up to **3 stacks** with roughly **30 seconds** of availability; Broadside adds 2, eligible Sword weapon skills add 1.
- **Grapeshot Broadside:** **25-second recast**, **12-yalm** single-target action; creates enmity and applies short Blind and Slow where allowed.
- **Point-Blank Pistol:** Eligible **one-handed Sword weapon skills**, with Sword and Gun equipped, generate additional threat, self-heal **8/10/12/14% maximum HP** by Lv. 10/30/50/70 and build Grapeshot.
- **Salt-Spray:** Quick Draw consumes 1–3 Grapeshot to create **6/12/18% maximum-HP Stoneskin**, lasting about **12 seconds**.
- **Quick Draw control:** Earth/Water add Cripple, Ice adds a short Stun and Light/Dark can Dispel according to enemy immunities and existing effects.
- **Iron Hull:** **45-second recast**, **12-second** defensive state with roughly **18% Damage Taken reduction**, knockback immunity, a 20% maximum-HP heal and personal cleansing.
- **Empty-Sub Pistol Guard:** With the correct Buccaneer loadout and no Sub item, incoming eligible physical hits can trigger a **12% chance** to reduce that hit's damage by **35%**.
- **Level progression:** Buccaneer tank passives, Sword/Marksmanship bonuses and Point-Blank recovery grow through the Lv. 10/30/50/70 thresholds.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Using Marksmanship weapon skills for Point-Blank Pistol:** The healing and Grapeshot builder triggers on qualifying **Sword** weapon skills, not Gun weapon skills.
- **Equipping the wrong weapon combination:** Sword and Gun must both remain available for the special tank interactions.
- **Ignoring Grapeshot expiry:** Build and spend stacks within the window rather than treating them as permanent.
- **Using Quick Draw with no stacks before a boss spike:** You lose the opportunity to gain the strongest Salt-Spray shield.
- **Assuming the empty-Sub guard always blocks:** It is a chance-based physical reduction, not a guaranteed defense.
- **Forgetting Galleon's Roll:** A large part of your baseline survival depends on the current defensive roll.
