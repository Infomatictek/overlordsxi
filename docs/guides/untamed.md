# Untamed (BST) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Untamed is a Tier 1 single-target damage Soul built around two combatants sharing the same quarry. You and a summoned hare feed each other TP with successful attacks, creating a fast master-and-companion loop. Maintain target alignment, time your Pack Rend and Raking Kick attacks, and keep the hare alive without expecting tank-level survivability.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Untamed Soul Crystal or use `!spec set untamed` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Axe and Great Axe are the eligible weapon-skill families. The core loop begins without weapon-skill talent points and works with any subjob.
- **Companion:** **Call Packmate** summons your hare without a jug. It has its own native threat, health, TP and damage behavior.
- **Supplies:** **Feed Beast** uses pet food from the ammo slot. Carry appropriate food if you expect the hare to take damage.
- **Combat priorities:** Favor reliable accuracy and opportunities to hit the same enemy as your companion rather than swapping targets unnecessarily.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Summon the hare:** Use **Call Packmate** before engaging. Keep the companion healthy enough to survive direct attacks.
2. **Establish a shared target:** Engage your intended enemy and use **Set Quarry** so both you and the hare focus on it.
3. **Build and transfer TP:** Land ordinary single-target hits and use **Pack Rend**. Pack Rend damages your quarry and feeds TP to your hare; landed Axe and Great Axe weapon skills also contribute to the link.
4. **Spend the hare's attack:** Open **Pack Commands** and use **Raking Kick** to spend one Ready charge. When the kick lands on the shared quarry, it feeds TP back to you.
5. **Continue the loop:** Build TP, use eligible master attacks, let the hare build TP, and spend Ready charges when the kick can connect. The two TP-link directions each recover separately over 8 seconds.
6. **Respond to target changes:** Send the hare to the new quarry when switching enemies. Attacking different targets breaks the TP-sharing opportunity.
7. **Manage the companion:** Use **Fall Back** to recall it, **Dismiss Beast** to release it and **Feed Beast** with equipped ammo-slot pet food when healing is necessary.
8. **At Lv. 30+:** **Rally Packmate** provides a second summon timer. As levels rise, the TP transfers and companion's offensive stats improve.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Level 10–29:** Successful link events grant 125 TP to the hare or 75 TP to the master, depending on direction. Core summons, commands, Pack Rend and Raking Kick are available.
- **Level 30–49:** Transfer increases to 175 TP to the hare and 100 TP to the master; Rally Packmate adds a second summon timer.
- **Level 50–69:** Transfer increases to 225 TP to the hare and 125 TP to the master. The companion gains more attack, accuracy and double attack.
- **Level 70–75:** Final link scaling gives 275 TP to the hare and 150 TP to the master.
- **Independent internal cooldowns:** Both transfer directions have their own 8-second recovery. A triggered transfer in one direction does not imply the other direction has refreshed.
- **Valid hits:** Misses, fully absorbed attacks, defeated targets and attacks on a different quarry provide no link TP.
- **Ready charges:** Raking Kick uses native Ready charge management. Do not confuse its availability with the TP-link cooldown.
- **Soul HUD:** Watch your TP, the hare's TP, whether you share the same quarry and both link timers.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Fighting separate targets:** The link requires both combatants to focus on the same enemy.
- **Assuming every hit transfers TP:** The link has independent 8-second cooldowns and only qualifying landed attacks count.
- **Spending Ready charges when the hare cannot connect:** Position and target alignment matter more than pressing Kick as soon as it lights up.
- **Ignoring your hare's survival:** The companion obeys normal pet damage and threat rules and can be defeated.
- **Forgetting pet food:** Feed Beast requires an appropriate item equipped in the ammo slot.
- **Expecting a defensive tank pet:** Untamed is pure damage. Use party protection and good target control to preserve uptime.
