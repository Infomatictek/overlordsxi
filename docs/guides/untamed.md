# Untamed (BST) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Two-predator tandem DPS.** Untamed and its offensive pet deal damage together; neither acts as a guardian tank.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Untamed when you want high coordinated master-and-pet damage, not Huntsman's pet threat or Alpha Warden's damage sharing.

## Setup & Gear
- Axe or Great Axe with an engaged jug pet; Call Beast, Fight, Reward and Ready have Lv10 starter loans.
- Unlocks at Lv10; final specialization cap is Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Start:** Engage the same living target as your pet and land a master weapon skill.
2. **Core:** That landed hit accelerates pet TP; respond with a real offensive Ready attack.
3. **Timing:** The landed offensive Ready attack accelerates the master's next weapon skill opportunity.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Master WS gives 125/175/225/275 pet TP, once per 8s; landed offensive Ready gives 75/100/125/150 master TP once per 8s.
- No synthetic extra hits, Soul-Link damage split or pet-tank enmity transfer.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't treat the pet as Huntsman's boss tank or expect rewards when targets don't match.
- Do not infer additional spells or job abilities from the base job; check the Soul's actual unlocked actions.
