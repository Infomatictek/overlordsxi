# Untamed (BST) - Playstyle Guide

Untamed is a Tier 1 pure damage Soul. You and your hare generate TP for each other by landing single-target attacks on the same enemy.

## Level 10 opening

1. Equip the Soul Crystal or use `!spec set untamed`. Equip an Axe or Great Axe.
2. Use **Call Packmate** to summon a hare without a jug. Engage an enemy and use **Set Quarry** on that same target.
3. Use **Pack Rend** to damage the enemy and feed your hare TP. Landed Axe and Great Axe weapon skills feed the same link.
4. Use **Raking Kick** under **Pack Commands** to spend one Ready charge. A landed kick feeds TP back to you. Let the hare build TP before spending a charge when you can.
5. If you switch targets, send the hare after your new quarry. **Fall Back** recalls it, **Dismiss Beast** releases it, and **Feed Beast** uses pet food equipped in the ammo slot.

You can start this loop with zero weapon-skill talent points and any subjob.

| Level | TP to hare | TP to master | Benefits |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 10–29 | 125 | 75 | Summon, commands, Rend and Kick |
| 30–49 | 175 | 100 | Rally Packmate offers a second summon timer |
| 50–69 | 225 | 125 | Higher companion attack, accuracy and double attack |
| 70–75 | 275 | 150 | Final offensive scaling |

Both links recover separately in eight seconds. The HUD shows your TP, the hare’s TP, whether you share a quarry, and both link timers. A miss, fully absorbed hit, defeated target, or different quarry gives no TP. Ready charges are tracked by the native command menu.

The hare uses native pet threat and damage rules. Keep it focused on the party’s kill target. Your companion can take damage and be defeated.
