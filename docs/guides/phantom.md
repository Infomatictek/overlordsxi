# Phantom (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Phantom is a Tier 6 evasion tank built around taking the first enemy's attention, then holding it by dodging genuine melee swings. **Phantom's Dare** provides immediate enmity and a short, single-hit guard against magic or breath attacks. Your primary job is to keep the quarry focused on you, survive through Evasion and HP, and use Dare at moments when the incoming magical hit is meaningful. It is not a sustained party mitigation aura or a damage-focused rotation.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Select Phantom at Lv. 10 with the Soul Crystal or `!spec set phantom`.
- **Weapons:** Main-hand **Dagger or Katana** to qualify for Phantom's special evasion-threat mechanic. A Wakizashi can be an early Katana choice from Lv. 7; use your available equipment.
- **Priority stats:** Evasion, AGI and HP are central. The Soul provides increasingly strong Evasion, AGI, HP and Enmity bonuses as you level while reducing personal damage.
- **Party role:** Open on the enemy before your healer begins a large cure or allies unleash heavy damage. Face the quarry away from more vulnerable party members when practical.
- **No special talent dependency:** Phantom's initial Dare and evasion engine are available at Lv. 10 with ordinary gear, without weapon-skill talent investment or a required support job.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Claim the quarry:** Get within 15 yalms and activate **Phantom's Dare** on one living enemy. It has an 18-second recast and starts with a direct burst of enmity to help establish initial control.
2. **Hold the enemy in front of you:** When the enemy attempts ordinary melee attacks, genuine evades reinforce your threat. You receive this bonus at most once every 2 seconds, and only with a compatible main weapon equipped.
3. **Prepare for a magical spike:** Dare also opens a 6-second guard window for the next qualifying **magical or breath** damage hit. Its mitigation is applied once; ordinary melee hits do not consume the protection.
4. **Time future Dares:** Reapply Dare when threat needs reinforcement or when a dangerous spell/breath hit is expected. Using it too early may leave the guard unused or expired.
5. **Maintain stable tank positioning:** Stay engaged and give the party a predictable enemy position. Your evasion-based threat relies on the enemy actually attacking you, not on fighting a distant target.
6. **Use eligible weapon skills as available:** Dagger and Katana weapon skills keep their normal TP and talent requirements; they are not the condition for the special evade threat.
7. **Monitor the HUD:** It displays Dare's cooldown, guard time remaining, evade-trigger recovery and whether your main weapon qualifies. Adjust timing rather than pressing buttons on cooldown.
8. **Coordinate magic-heavy encounters:** Once the one-hit guard has been consumed, the remaining incoming spells and breath hits need ordinary healer support and other defensive planning.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Phantom's Dare:** Available from Lv. 10; maximum target range 15 yalms, recast 18 seconds, and a 6-second one-hit guard. The native action sets 180 cumulative and 650 volatile enmity at activation.
- **Evade threat:** A genuine melee evade against a live mob adds additional enmity, at most every 2 seconds. Other avoidance outcomes do not automatically count as the special evasion event.
- **Level 10–29:** Guard mitigates the qualifying magical or breath hit by 8%; special evade-threat values are 30 cumulative / 85 volatile enmity.
- **Level 30–49:** Guard improves to 10%; evade threat becomes 40 cumulative / 115 volatile.
- **Level 50–69:** Guard improves to 12%; evade threat becomes 55 cumulative / 150 volatile.
- **Level 70–75:** Guard improves to 15%; evade threat becomes 70 cumulative / 190 volatile.
- **Passive progression:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, Phantom gains Evasion +20/35/55/75, AGI +7/12/18/25, Max HP +8/12/16/20% and Enmity +20/30/40/50 while taking a significant personal damage-output penalty.
- **Guard consumption:** Only positive damage from a qualifying magic or breath attack against you triggers the guard. It applies to one hit and then ends, even if time remains.
- **Soul HUD:** Track Dare recast, remaining guard seconds, evade-event internal cooldown and eligible weapon status.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Opening after the party's big damage spike:** Use Dare early to establish threat before allies create large enmity.
- **Equipping the wrong main weapon:** Without a Dagger or Katana, genuine evades will not trigger Phantom's special threat engine.
- **Assuming every avoided hit grants threat:** Parry and absorbed-shadow outcomes are different from an actual registered melee evade.
- **Using Dare as a long-duration magic shield:** Guard lasts at most 6 seconds and only protects the first eligible magic or breath hit.
- **Expecting melee damage to consume the guard:** The protection is reserved for magic/breath damage; time it around known enemy behavior.
- **Trying to maximize personal DPS:** Phantom sacrifices damage for survivability and threat. Prioritize holding enemy attention and giving healers a stable target.
