# Phantom (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Evasive threat tank.** Phantom keeps attention through elusiveness and targeted provocation, not Kagemusha clones or Sensei counters.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Phantom for sustained physical-evasion tanking, not shadow-body tanking or permanent mitigation.

## Setup & Gear
- Dagger or Katana and Evasion, AGI, HP and survivability.
- Unlocks at Lv10; final specialization cap is Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Start:** Use Phantom's Dare at range to establish meaningful enemy threat without /WAR.
2. **Core:** Remain in melee threat range and benefit from regular enemy misses to reinforce hate.
3. **Timing:** Use Dare's brief one-hit guard for unavoidable magic or breath rather than assuming evasion blocks everything.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Phantom's Dare is 15y, 18s cooldown and grants 180 CE/650 VE with a 6s one-hit guard.
- Evaded regular melee attacks add CE/VE every 2s while the right weapon is equipped.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't expect the one-hit guard to withstand repeated unavoidable spell/breath hits.
- Do not infer additional spells or job abilities from the base job; check the Soul's actual unlocked actions.
