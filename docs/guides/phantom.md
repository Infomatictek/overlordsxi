# Phantom (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Phantom is a Tier-6 tank that opens with direct threat and follows up by dodging real melee swings. Time Phantom's Dare when a dangerous magical or breath attack is coming.

## Setup & Gear

- Select at Lv.10 with `!spec set phantom` or Crystal Socket. Equip a Dagger or Katana. Wakizashi is a Lv.7 option.
- Prioritize Evasion, AGI and HP. The cold-start opener and evasion loop work with zero talents and any support subjob.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Use **Phantom's Dare** from up to 15 yalms to challenge one enemy. It has an 18-second recast.
2. Keep that enemy facing you. Genuine regular melee evades reinforce threat at most once every two seconds.
3. Use the six-second Dare Guard window to soften the next magical or breath hit. Ordinary melee damage leaves the guard ready.
4. Through Lv.30/50/70, evasion and threat improve and the guard rises from 8% to 10/12/15% mitigation.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- The HUD tracks Dare, the guard window, and whether evasion can produce threat.
- Phantom can use Dagger and Katana weapon skills with their original TP costs and talent rules.

## Common Mistakes

- Open with Dare before allies begin a large cure or damage burst.
- Use a Dagger or Katana to receive evasion threat. Parry, knockback and absorbed shadows do not qualify.
- The guard protects one eligible hit. Ask the party to cover sustained magical damage once it is spent.
