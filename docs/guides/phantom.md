# Phantom (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
An evasive tank whose survivability is a wager on not getting hit. Inspired by Perfect Dodge and the Thief's defensive mobility. It does not summon clones or counterattack like Sensei.

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapon:** Dagger or Katana for the evade-based enmity engine.
- **Priority:** Evasion, Agility, HP, damage reduction, and reliable enmity.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. Establish threat with normal attacks and native Thief tools.
2. Maintain facing/position and draw enemy attacks where your evasion can matter.
3. Missed mob attacks reinforce enmity every 2s; the mechanic never deals retaliatory damage.
4. Prepare external protection for unavoidables, spell bursts, or bosses with excessive accuracy.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Elusive Attention:** Evasion sustains threat, rather than counter-damage or shadow-clone resources.
- The tank profile remains weak against unavoidable mechanics even with its modest passive damage reduction.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't rely on evasion against unavoidable boss attacks.
- Don't expect self-healing or automatic counters.
- Don't remove your defensive gear for a marginal offensive upgrade.
