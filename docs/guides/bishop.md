# Bishop (WHM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Bishop is a **barrier-focused White Mage healer** whose cures create **Solace Ward** protection, and whose light attacks generate **Sacred Grace**. It alternates proactive single-target shielding with short offensive **Banish/Holy** or Club/Staff weapon-skill actions that recover MP, then spends Sacred Grace for additional party regeneration. **Divine Veil**, **Sanctuary Pulse**, and modified emergency healing actions provide the large group response. Bishop's damage is secondary to protection.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip Bishop's Soul Crystal or use `!spec set bishop` at Lv. 10.
- **Preferred equipment:** **Club and Shield** for extra block chance, or Staff where suitable. Prioritize Max HP/MP, MND, Cure Potency, Fast Cast, Shield skill and survival.
- **Stance:** Keep **Afflatus Solace** active for its enhanced Cure barrier behavior; the Soul makes the stance available at low level.
- **Team planning:** Establish who will receive regular Solace Ward Cures and communicate the timing of Divine Veil or Benediction before high-damage phases.
- **Offensive opportunities:** Only weave Banish/Holy or weapon skills when doing so will not delay essential healing.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Activate Afflatus Solace:** Begin with the defensive healer stance. It supports strong spell interruption resistance and the Soul's specialized Cure ward.
2. **Heal and protect the tank:** Cast a single-target **Cure** to restore HP and apply **Solace Ward**, giving short damage reduction and a Stoneskin barrier based on the effective healing.
3. **Build Sacred Grace:** When the party is stable, use eligible **Banish**, **Holy**, or Club/Staff weapon skills. They recover MP and build Sacred Grace stacks. **Sanctuary Pulse** also provides direct group recovery and a stack.
4. **Spend Sacred Grace at three stacks:** Use an appropriate **Curaga**, **Esuna**, or **Auspice** action to consume accumulated Grace and pulse an additional party-wide hybrid Regen.
5. **Use Divine Veil for a planned group action:** During its short window, your next appropriate Cure can spread healing and ward support; a cleansing spell can instead provide broad debuff removal. Choose the spell that matches the incoming mechanic.
6. **Use Sanctuary Pulse against AoE damage:** The ability quickly heals nearby allies and clears multiple debuffs. Coordinate it with other healer cooldowns.
7. **Maintain MP and emergency responses:** **Martyr** and **Devotion** can recover target HP/MP without the typical Bishop self-HP sacrifice; **Divine Seal** empowers a subsequent Cure.
8. **Save Benediction for the worst moments:** Its specialized version provides massive party recovery, cleansing, MP and additional ward support. Continue the normal healing/Grace loop after the emergency passes.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Solace Ward:** Under Afflatus Solace, qualifying single-target Cure creates about **8% Damage Taken reduction for 8 seconds** plus Stoneskin based on **35–50% of the Cure**, capped at **30% target maximum HP**.
- **Sacred Grace:** Up to **3 stacks** lasting about **30 seconds**; built by eligible Banish, Holy, Club/Staff weapon skills and Sanctuary Pulse. Consumed by qualifying Curaga, Esuna or Auspice to grant additional party Regen.
- **MP return:** Appropriate offensive light casts or qualifying weapon skills can restore **6% maximum MP** through Bishop's Smite-weaving mechanic.
- **Sanctuary Pulse:** **20-second recast**, roughly **25% party HP healing** and three-debuff cleansing, plus Sacred Grace support.
- **Divine Veil:** **25-second recast**; prepares a group-focused barrier, healing or cleansing action depending on the next eligible cast.
- **Martyr and Devotion:** Modified to **15-second recasts** without the normal self-HP cost, restoring an ally's HP or MP.
- **Divine Seal:** Modified **20-second recast**, adding a larger barrier contribution to the next qualifying Cure.
- **Benediction:** Major group rescue with full party healing, extensive debuff cleanse, MP restoration and a defensive ward.
- **Level progression:** Shield block chance improves through **10/14/18/22%** with Club and Shield at Lv. 10/30/50/70; Cure and protective Soul passives improve with level.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Dropping Afflatus Solace accidentally:** It is central to the normal Cure-to-Solace-Ward loop.
- **Using offensive casts while allies are dying:** Sacred Grace and MP returns are valuable only if the group survives long enough to benefit.
- **Spending Divine Veil on the wrong spell:** Decide whether you need a group Cure/ward or a group cleanse before committing.
- **Allowing Sacred Grace to expire unused:** Spend the three-stack opportunity for Regen when the team needs sustained recovery.
- **Forgetting Club/Shield defensive advantages:** A shield contributes meaningful block and casting stability for this front-line-capable healer.
- **Using Benediction for a minor deficit:** Reserve its massive rescue effects for encounters with genuine danger.
