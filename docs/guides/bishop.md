# Bishop (WHM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Bishop evolves White Mage into a **proactive barrier healer and Smite-weaving Cleric** (`Healer` tier: -60% damage dealt, 0.12x WS scaling). Keeping `Afflatus Solace` active turns every single-target `Cure` into a damage-absorbing **Solace Ward barrier (-8% DT + Stoneskin)**, while weaving `Banish`/`Holy` or Club/Staff WSs restores **+6% Max MP** and builds **Sacred Grace** stacks to trigger party-wide AoE Regen.

## Setup & Gear
- **Recommended Weapons:** **1H Club + Shield.** Equipping both grants **+10/14/18/22% Shield Block Rate** passively (Club or Staff WSs both trigger Smite-weaving).
- **Mandatory Stance:** Keep **`Afflatus Solace`** (unlocked @ Lv.10 with **0s CD**) active at all times — Solace (or Misery) grants **100% Spell Interruption Resistance (SIRD)**.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Opener:** Turn on `Afflatus Solace` (0s CD -> 100% SIRD) and equip Club + Shield.
2. **Single-Target Tank Shielding:** Cast `Cure I–VI` on the tank or injured allies to heal AND wrap them in **Solace Ward** (-8% DT for 8s + Stoneskin equal to 35–50% of the heal, capped at 30% Max HP).
3. **Weave Smites for MP & Stacks:** Cast `Banish I–III`, `Holy`, use a Club/Staff WS, or press `Sanctuary Pulse` (20s CD: 25% HP party heal + 3-debuff cleanse) to build **+1 Sacred Grace** (max 3) and recover **+6% Max MP** per Banish/Holy/WS.
4. **Cash in `Sacred Grace` & Cooldowns:** At **3 Sacred Grace**, your next `Curaga`, `Esuna`, or `Auspice` consumes all 3 stacks to blanket the 15y party in a **10s Hybrid Regen**. For burst raid shields or panic cleanses, press `Divine Veil` (25s CD: 22% Max HP party barrier + ward) and immediately cast a single-target `Cure` (splashes full heal + Solace Ward to everyone in 15y) or `Erase`/`Esuna` (cleanses 2 debuffs party-wide).

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Sacred Grace (0–3 stacks, 30s):** Built by `Banish`, `Holy`, Club/Staff WSs, and `Sanctuary Pulse`. Automatically consumed at 3 stacks by `Curaga`, `Esuna`, or `Auspice` for a 10s party Regen.
- **Free Emergency JAs:** `Martyr` (25% target HP heal) and `Devotion` (25% target MP restore) both have a **15s cooldown** and cost **0 self-HP**. `Divine Seal` (20s CD) adds an extra +15% Max HP Stoneskin onto your next `Cure`.
- **`Benediction`:** Full 100% party heal + 10-debuff cleanse + 50% MP restore + party ward & Divine Veil buff for 15s.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't drop `Afflatus Solace`:** Without Afflatus Solace (or Misery) up, you lose your **100% SIRD** and Solace Ward shields.
- **Watch what you cast right after `Divine Veil`:** Because `Erase` and `Esuna` also consume `Divine Veil` (to AoE cleanse), cast `Cure` if you wanted the AoE heal/ward splash — or `Erase`/`Esuna` if you specifically want an AoE cleanse.
- **Don't hoard `Martyr` / `Devotion`:** They cost 0 HP and are only on a 15s cooldown — spam them to keep allies topped off.
