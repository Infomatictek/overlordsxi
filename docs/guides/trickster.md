# Trickster (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Positional threat support.** Trickster positions for targeted threat transfer and briefly suppresses enemy melee pressure.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Trickster when party positioning and offensive suppression are more valuable than personal damage.

## Setup & Gear
- Dagger or Sword, Accuracy and safe positional access.
- Unlocks at Lv10; final specialization cap is Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Start:** Use the Lv10 Trick Attack loan to help manage party threat without needing a particular subjob.
2. **Core:** Land a Dagger or Sword weapon skill to add short Attack Down if the enemy isn't already affected.
3. **Timing:** Coordinate this 16s disruption around a dangerous enemy attack; its trigger has a 20s internal cooldown.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- The landed weapon skill applies 5/7/9/12% Attack Down for 16s at the four level tiers.
- Missed weapon skills do not trigger suppression.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't treat it as Bandit's pure damage role or Phantom's tank.
- Do not infer additional spells or job abilities from the base job; check the Soul's actual unlocked actions.
