# Trickster (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Covert support controlling the enemy's offense and party threat geometry. Inspired by classic Thief Sneak Attack/Trick Attack teamwork, not a builder/spender bar. Trade sustained damage for tactical disruption.

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapon:** Dagger is preferred; Sword works for the tactical WS trigger.
- **Priority:** Accuracy, Dexterity, Subtle Blow, survivability sufficient to stay near allies.
- **Level 10:** The Soul's passive bonuses function immediately; native Trick Attack availability depends on the deployed server's ability unlock table.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. Plan positioning relative to your tank to exploit native enmity-redirection techniques.
2. Use a Dagger/Sword weaponskill to disrupt an enemy's offense (Attack Down, 16s) if Attack Down is not already present.
3. Coordinate the 20s tactical cooldown with a difficult boss attack, rather than using each opening on autopilot.
4. In fights where Attack Down is resisted or already maintained, focus on positioning and reducing your own threat.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Disruption:** One short offense-debuff window every 20s, no personal meter.
- Does not overwrite an existing Attack Down.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't treat Trickster as a pure DPS competitor to Bandit.
- Don't assume the debuff is guaranteed against a resistant boss.
- Don't overwrite another party member's strong Attack Down.
