# Trickster (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Trickster is a Tier-3 support Soul. Decide which ally should receive the threat from your next attack, then use your weapon skill to weaken the enemy's attacks.

## Setup & Gear

- Select at Lv.10 with `!spec set trickster` or Crystal Socket. Equip a Dagger or Sword and prioritize accuracy.
- A basic weapon skill and ordinary gear are enough to apply Suppression at Lv.10, solo or in a party.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. For threat routing, stand so the intended party member is between you and the enemy.
2. Use **Pass the Blame**, then land your next qualifying attack or weapon skill while lined up.
3. Land a Dagger or Sword weapon skill when the enemy has no Attack Down. **Suppression** applies Attack Down for 16 seconds when the enemy accepts it.
4. At Lv.10/30/50/70, Suppression lowers Attack by 5/7/9/12%. Use the HUD to track the 20-second cooldown.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- Pass the Blame lasts up to 20 seconds and has a 35-second recast. It uses normal positional threat rules.
- A miss, existing Attack Down or rejected effect keeps Suppression ready.

## Common Mistakes

- Check the ally's position before triggering Pass the Blame. Without a valid party member in line, threat stays with you.
- Spend TP when you can land the hit. Suppression depends on actual damage.
