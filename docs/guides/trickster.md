# Trickster (THF) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Trickster is a Tier 3 support Soul that controls where its own attack threat goes while weakening a quarry's physical damage. **Pass the Blame** lets you set up a positional threat transfer, and **Suppression** automatically applies Attack Down after an eligible damaging weapon skill. You are not the primary tank or a dedicated healer: your contribution is deliberate threat management and enemy attack reduction while supporting the team's kill target.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Select Trickster at Lv. 10 with the Soul Crystal or `!spec set trickster`.
- **Weapons:** Use a **Dagger or Sword** as your main weapon. Trickster's special attack reduction is triggered by damaging weapon skills with one of these equipped; Pass the Blame also checks for a compatible weapon.
- **Stats:** Prioritize enough Accuracy to land your weapon skills, then DEX and melee throughput. The Soul grants increasing Dagger skill, Accuracy, DEX and Subtle Blow with level, plus reduced personal enmity.
- **Party position:** Before committing Pass the Blame, identify the ally who should receive your next qualifying hit's enmity. You need the ally between yourself and the enemy when the hit lands.
- **Solo use:** Suppression can still weaken an enemy while solo. Without a correctly positioned party member, Pass the Blame will not transfer your threat.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose the tank or threat recipient:** Stand behind the intended party member relative to the quarry. The position at your qualifying attack matters; merely activating the ability is not enough.
2. **Arm Pass the Blame:** Use **Pass the Blame** before a qualifying attack or weapon skill. The setup lasts up to 20 seconds and the ability has a 35-second recast.
3. **Follow through on the position:** Land an eligible attack while your intended recipient is still between you and the target. If the position fails, your threat remains yours.
4. **Track the target's Attack Down:** A damaging Dagger or Sword weapon skill triggers **Suppression** if the target is alive, has no existing Attack Down and accepts the new effect.
5. **Maintain Suppression:** The effect lasts 16 seconds. After a successful application, Suppression has its own 20-second internal cooldown. Coordinate the next damaging weapon skill for when the debuff can be refreshed.
6. **Spend TP intelligently:** Build TP with ordinary attacks, then use a weapon skill when you can both land damage and reapply Suppression. A missed or zero-damage weapon skill does not give the support effect.
7. **Watch your HUD:** It tracks the remaining Pass the Blame and Suppression cooldowns, whether the threat-transfer setup is armed, and whether the current target already has Attack Down.
8. **Reposition as the fight changes:** If the tank moves or the boss turns, redo your positioning before arming and consuming the next Pass the Blame window.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Pass the Blame (Lv. 10+):** Up to 20 seconds to deliver a qualifying hit, with a 35-second ability recast. Its threat routing follows positional party-member rules.
- **Suppression at Lv. 10–29:** Successful qualifying weapon skill applies **5% Attack Down** for 16 seconds.
- **Suppression at Lv. 30–49:** Attack Down improves to **7%** for 16 seconds.
- **Suppression at Lv. 50–69:** Attack Down improves to **9%** for 16 seconds.
- **Suppression at Lv. 70–75:** Attack Down improves to **12%** for 16 seconds.
- **Suppression internal cooldown:** A successful application starts a separate 20-second cooldown. A miss, zero-damage hit, target already having Attack Down, or failed debuff application does not trigger it.
- **Compatible weapons:** Dagger and Sword in the main slot qualify; weapon choice and a successful damaging weapon skill are part of the mechanic.
- **Personal passive scaling:** Trickster gains Dagger skill (+12/20/30/42), Accuracy (+10/16/24/32), DEX (+6/10/15/20), Subtle Blow (+5/8/12/16) and lowered Enmity (-8/-12/-16/-20) at Lv. 10/30/50/70.
- **Soul HUD:** The target-down indicator reports whether the enemy has Attack Down, not necessarily who originally applied it.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Using Pass the Blame without an ally in line:** It does not magically choose the tank; threat transfer depends on the actual attack position.
- **Equipping an unrelated main weapon:** The special support checks require Dagger or Sword.
- **Using a weapon skill while Attack Down is already active:** Suppression does not replace an existing Attack Down, so time your reapplication.
- **Counting a missed weapon skill as suppression:** The effect requires a positive-damage hit and a successful status application.
- **Mistaking the 20-second Suppression lock for the 35-second Pass the Blame recast:** These are independent timers; consult the HUD.
- **Expecting threat transfer while solo:** Suppression remains valuable, but the positioning-based transfer requires a valid teammate.
