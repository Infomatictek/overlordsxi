# Sensei (MNK) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Sensei is a **Staff-using avoidance tank** that protects the party through Evasion, Parry, Counter and immediate retaliation. Enemy swings that are parried or evaded can trigger a **Staff Riposte**: counter-damage, enmity, TP and personal healing. The Soul shifts Monk away from pure Hand-to-Hand damage into holding the enemy's attention, surviving repeated hits and spending its defensive cooldowns deliberately.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Sensei Soul Crystal or use `!spec set sensei` from Lv. 10.
- **Weapon:** A **Staff in the main-hand slot** is essential for the signature avoidance and riposte mechanics. Do not swap to Hand-to-Hand just because the base job is Monk.
- **Stats:** Prioritize HP, Evasion, AGI, Parrying, Counter, Counter Damage and Enmity. The Soul intentionally sacrifices personal weapon-skill damage to perform as a tank.
- **Party role:** Establish threat early with Chi Blast and keep the enemy facing you. Give allies a stable position so they can attack safely.
- **Cooldown preparation:** Know which boss hits can be avoided and which require actual damage reduction or healing support.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare your defensive stance:** Activate **Iron Counterstance** for stronger Counter performance and mitigation without the base severe Defense penalty. Use **Dodge** to improve Evasion and Parry, and **Focus** for Counter/Parry benefits.
2. **Claim the target:** Use **Chi Blast** to build enmity and impose a short Accuracy Down on the quarry. Get into melee range and turn the enemy away from vulnerable allies.
3. **Hold through genuine avoidance:** Parried Staff attacks and enemy misses that count as evasions can generate **Staff Riposte** damage, personal healing, TP and enmity. Keep the valid Staff equipped throughout.
4. **Build and spend TP:** Use **Boost** for a quick TP gain and extra enmity, then eligible Staff weapon skills when they provide useful damage or combat pressure.
5. **Prepare for bursts:** Use **Perfect Counter** when an important physical exchange is imminent. It improves your ability to counter and may provide a short self-heal and threat.
6. **Recover with Chakra:** Use **Chakra** after sustained punishment for personal healing, regeneration and a limited cleanse rather than waiting until an unrecoverable health deficit.
7. **Cover dangerous hits:** Maintain **Mantra** as appropriate for party resilience and use **Inner Strength** when the enemy enters an especially dangerous phase.
8. **Adapt to encounter composition:** Avoidance is strong against eligible melee swings but cannot substitute for healers or support when an enemy relies on unavoidably magical or scripted damage.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Weapon dependence:** Staff is required for Sensei's unique Parry, evade and riposte interactions. Invalid weapon choices interrupt that combat engine.
- **Iron Counterstance:** Its customized version adds **30% Counter, +20 Counter Damage, 10% damage reduction and +20 Enmity** without halving Defense.
- **Dodge and Focus:** Sensei's versions favor Evasion, Parry, Counter and improved riposte effects rather than the purely offensive Black Belt enhancements.
- **Parry benefits:** A qualifying Staff parry can heal **2% maximum HP**, add **75 TP**, generate extra threat and inflict a retaliatory blunt attack.
- **Evasion benefits:** A qualifying dodge can heal **1.5% maximum HP**, grant **50 TP**, generate threat and trigger an evasive counter. Active Dodge enhances the counter's damage.
- **Chi Blast:** Provides an early enmity tool and **Accuracy Down** on the enemy for 30 seconds.
- **Boost:** Offers **50 TP** and enmity reinforcement for Sensei with a Staff.
- **Emergency support:** Chakra and Inner Strength provide situational self-survival, while Mantra supports the party during dangerous periods.
- **Level progression:** Sensei's tank scaling strengthens through Lv. 10/30/50/70. Review current equipment and avoid choosing weapons purely for their damage if they disable riposte behavior.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Equipping Hand-to-Hand because of the parent job:** Sensei is a Staff tank and loses its defining avoidance engine without that weapon.
- **Expecting every incoming hit to trigger riposte:** The special response depends on genuine qualifying Parry or Evasion, not any damage taken.
- **Assuming Counterstance still has its ordinary Defense penalty:** Sensei's specialization changes that tradeoff.
- **Using only avoidance against a magic-heavy boss:** Some attacks will still need direct mitigation, healer throughput or support.
- **Ignoring threat while focusing on counter damage:** Pull with Chi Blast and reinforce enmity; your party needs consistent tank control.
- **Saving Chakra until after danger has passed:** Use healing and cleanse tools while they can preserve frontline stability.
