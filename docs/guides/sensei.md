# Sensei (MNK) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Sensei reimagines Monk as a **Staff-wielding Evasion, Parry, and Counter Tank** (`Tank` tier: -60% damage dealt, 0.12x WS scaling). Instead of soaking damage with heavy armor, every hit you evade or parry with a Staff triggers a **Riposte** that heals you, generates TP and Enmity, and strikes back.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon:** **Staff in Main Hand.** Almost every signature Sensei trigger (Staff Parry bonus, Staff Riposte on Parry, Evasive Counter on Miss, Boost +50 TP) requires a Staff equipped.
- **Priority Stats:** AGI, Evasion, Parrying, Counter, HP, and Enmity.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Stance Up & Pull:** Activate `Counterstance` (unlocked at Lv.10 on a 120s CD with **ZERO Defense penalty**: +30% Counter, +20 Counter DMG, -10% DT, +20 Enmity), `Dodge` (+25 EVA, +15% EVA%, +20% total Parry, +15 Enmity), and `Focus` (+15% Counter, +15 Parry, +10 Counter DMG). Pull with `Chi Blast` (heavy CE/VE + -20 Accuracy Down on target for 30s).
2. **Hold Threat Through Avoidance:** Stand in melee with your Staff. Every **Parry** heals 2% Max HP, grants +75 TP, builds enmity, and deals blunt Staff Riposte damage; every **Evade** heals 1.5% Max HP, grants +50 TP, builds enmity, and counters (+25% damage under `Dodge`).
3. **Weave `Boost` & Staff WSs:** Press `Boost` whenever ready for a quick **+50 TP** and enmity snap, then fire Staff Weaponskills.
4. **Recover & Anchor:** Use `Perfect Counter` (45s CD) for guaranteed counters + 5% heal + Enmity, `Chakra` for a large self-heal + 30s Hybrid Regen + Slow/Silence cleanse + enmity, and `Mantra` (party -5% DT) / `Inner Strength` (-25% DT, +20% EVA%, +50 Enmity) on boss spikes.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **No Counterstance Defense Penalty:** `Iron Counterstance` gives pure mitigation (-10% DT) and +30% Counter without halving your Defense.
- **Avoidance Engine:** Your TP bar fills rapidly from parries and evades rather than slow Staff swings alone.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't equip Hand-to-Hand or unequip your Staff:** Bare-handed or H2H weapons turn off Staff Parry, Staff Riposte, Evasive Counter, and Boost's TP/enmity procs.
- **Don't forget `Dodge` & `Chi Blast`:** `Chi Blast` lowers the mob's Accuracy by -20 while `Dodge` spikes your Evasion% and boosts your Evasive Counter damage by +25%.
