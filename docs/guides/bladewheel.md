# Bladewheel (DNC) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Bladewheel transforms Dancer into a **20-yalm ranged Chakram skirmisher and Throwing WS specialist** (`Damage` tier: 0% damage penalty, 1.00x WS scaling). Using **Throwing Stance**, **20y ranged Steps/Flourishes/Sambas**, and **12 custom Throwing Weaponskills**, you keep Sambas and Dazes rolling from full ranged safety while building Finishing Moves into massive **Flamenco Finale** bursts.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon:** **Chakram (`Throwing` weapon) equipped in your Ranged slot** (with Ammo left empty — all Chakrams are DNC-only with competitive damage/delay and no ammo consumption).
- **Recommended Melee:** Dual Daggers (used only if `Throwing Stance` is OFF and you stand inside melee range).

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Set Your Range & Stance:** Turn on `Throwing Stance` (5s CD toggle) if you want to throw Chakrams **at all ranges (0–20y)**, even in melee. Keep it OFF if you want to melee up close and auto-throw when standing back.
2. **Maintain Ranged Sambas & Steps (`Up to 20y`):** Apply **Haste Samba, Drain Samba, or Aspir Samba** (they proc on ranged Chakram hits!) and weave **Steps & Flourishes** (`Quickstep`, `Box Step`, `Stutter Step`, `Feather Step`, `Violent Flourish` using R.Acc/AGI stun) from up to **20 yalms away**.
3. **Build Finishing Moves & Debuffs with Custom Throwing WSs:** Open with **Pirouette Toss** (+1/+2 Finishing Moves), mix utility Throwing WSs (**Whirling Waltz** Light AoE heal, **Crimson Samba** Dark drain, **Grand Jete** DEF Down, **Aurora Fandango** Haste Samba), or use **Terpsichore's Halo** for a 2x Samba Aftermath buff.
4. **Detonate Full Finishing Moves:** Spend stacked Finishing Moves on **Flamenco Finale** (`+0.40 fTP` per Finishing Move) for huge ranged burst!

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **`Throwing Stance` Toggle (5s CD):** Controls whether close-range auto-attacks slash with Daggers or throw your equipped Chakram.
- **20y Dances & Ranged Samba Procs:** Steps, Flourishes, and Sambas all trigger seamlessly from Chakram throws without closing to melee range.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Make sure your Chakram is in `RANGED`, not `AMMO`.**
- **If you want pure ranged gameplay even near bosses, keep `Throwing Stance` toggled ON** (otherwise stepping into melee range switches back to Dagger swings).
