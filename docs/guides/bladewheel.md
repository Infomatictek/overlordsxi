# Bladewheel (DNC) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Bladewheel is a **ranged Chakram damage Dancer** that trades close-range Dagger pressure for mobile **Throwing** combat. Its **Throwing Stance** determines whether it prefers Chakram auto-attacks even at point-blank distance, and its Steps, Flourishes and Sambas can function at extended range. A set of custom Throwing weapon skills provides builders, status utility and finishing attacks such as **Flamenco Finale**.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Bladewheel Soul Crystal or use `!spec set bladewheel` from Lv. 10.
- **Essential equipment:** Put a compatible **Chakram in the Ranged slot**, not the Ammo slot. Keep the expected ammunition configuration clear for the Chakram system.
- **Melee backup:** Dagger equipment can support melee combat if Throwing Stance is off and you enter close range.
- **Stats:** Ranged Accuracy, Ranged Attack, AGI, CHR, Snapshot and TP sustain are important for reliable Chakram pressure.
- **Role:** Focus on single-target ranged DPS and useful Dancer status actions without unnecessarily closing into dangerous boss melee range.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Equip the Chakram correctly:** Confirm it occupies the Ranged slot. Without a valid Chakram, the defining ranged auto-attack loop will not function as intended.
2. **Choose Throwing Stance:** Toggle **Throwing Stance** on if you want Chakram auto-attacks at all distances, including melee. Leave it off to fight with Daggers in close range and auto-throw farther away.
3. **Stay within the ranged lane:** Bladewheel's special auto-ranged attacks and ranged dances work up to approximately **20 yalms**. Maintain a valid line of fire while staying clear of unwanted boss mechanics.
4. **Keep a Samba active:** Use **Haste Samba**, **Drain Samba** or **Aspir Samba** according to the fight. Their special effects can trigger on qualifying Chakram hits.
5. **Use Steps and Flourishes at range:** Build Finishing Moves and maintain the relevant Daze or stun-related utility without needing to stand inside melee distance.
6. **Build with Throwing weapon skills:** Use **Pirouette Toss** or other available custom Throwing weapon skills to gain Finishing Moves and apply situational effects.
7. **Spend on a strong finishing strike:** When you have sufficient Finishing Moves, use **Flamenco Finale** for a larger ranged burst; its damage scales with the number of moves committed.
8. **Adapt your weapon-skill choices:** Other Throwing skills provide options including a healing attack, an HP-draining strike, Defense Down, Samba support and a Samba-oriented aftermath. Choose based on what the current boss and party need.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Throwing Stance:** A **5-second-recast toggle** that controls whether Chakram throws override melee auto-attacks at all ranges. With it off, close-range attacks can return to ordinary Daggers.
- **Extended Dancer actions:** The Soul permits eligible Steps, Flourishes and Samba-triggering actions at **up to 20 yalms** when the ranged requirements are met.
- **Custom Throwing weapon skills:** The kit grants **12** specialized Throwing weapon skills across its progression, subject to their own level and TP requirements.
- **Finishing Moves:** Build with Steps and certain Throwing weapon skills. **Flamenco Finale** adds **0.40 fTP for each Finishing Move** used, making stack timing part of the ranged rotation.
- **Utility options:** Named Throwing attacks include **Whirling Waltz** for light healing, **Crimson Samba** for dark drain, **Grand Jete** for Defense Down and **Aurora Fandango** for Samba support.
- **Samba aftermath:** **Terpsichore's Halo** provides a Samba-themed aftermath buff on successful use.
- **Lv. 10/30/50/70 progression:** Ranged Accuracy scales at **+10/+22/+36/+48**; other ranged attack, Snapshot, AGI and CHR passives increase as levels rise.
- **Range constraints:** Being inside 20 yalms is not enough if the ranged slot or targeting conditions are invalid. Confirm active stance and weapon placement after equipment changes.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Putting the Chakram in Ammo:** The custom attack system expects a ranged weapon.
- **Leaving Throwing Stance off while fighting in melee:** Close-range swings revert to Daggers if the stance is not forcing Chakram throws.
- **Assuming every Throwing weapon skill is available at Lv. 10:** Their normal progression and unlock requirements still apply.
- **Spending Finishing Moves prematurely:** A full Flamenco Finale can be substantially stronger than an unprepared one.
- **Forgetting Samba and Step upkeep:** Ranged pressure does not eliminate the value of the Dancer utility toolkit.
- **Ignoring line of sight:** Even with ranged dances enabled, valid range and targeting still matter.
