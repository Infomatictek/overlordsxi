# Vampire (DNC) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Vampire turns Dancer into a **0-MP crimson lifesteal Combat Healer** (`CombatHealer` tier: -50% personal damage dealt). Every melee/throwing hit heals allies within 15y based on damage dealt (converting overheal into **Crimson Veil Stoneskin** barriers), Steps & `Nocturne Feast` build **Finishing Moves**, and **Dagger/Throwing WSs** detonate those Finishing Moves for party-wide healing and debuff cleanses!

## Setup & Gear
- **Recommended Weapons:** **Dual Daggers or Throwing Chakram** (required for Dagger/Throwing Weaponskills to detonate Finishing Moves into AoE heals/cleanses).
- **Zero MP Needed:** Runs completely off TP, Steps, and Finishing Moves.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Stay Engaging (`Passive Lifesteal Aura`):** Every `Attack` automatically heals party members within 15y for **18–36% of damage dealt** (`2x` during `Trance`), with overhealing wrapping allies in a **Crimson Veil Stoneskin** (up to 20% Max HP).
2. **Build Finishing Moves (`Steps` & `Nocturne Feast`):** Weave **Steps** (`+2 FMs` + 10% party heal) and `Nocturne Feast` (25s CD: drains up to 15% foe HP to heal party + `+250 TP` + **5 Finishing Moves**).
3. **Detonate FMs with Dagger / Throwing WSs:** Fire a **Dagger or Throwing WS** to consume Finishing Moves (`6%` party heal per FM + 1 debuff cleanse per ally — in `Trance`, counts as 5 FMs without consuming them!).
4. **Defensive & Burst HoTs:** Use `Sanguine Waltz` (18s CD, 200 TP, 0 TP in `Trance`: **30% party HP heal + 2-debuff cleanse + 15s Hybrid Regen**), and press `Contradance` (15s CD) or any Waltz to apply `-15% DT` party ward for 10s.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Crimson Veil Overheal Barrier:** Continuous auto-attack lifesteal keeps shields topped off even between burst heals.
- **`Trance` Spike:** Doubles lifesteal to party and lets every Dagger/Throwing WS trigger 5-FM AoE heals & cleanses without spending Finishing Moves.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't fire non-Dagger/non-Throwing WSs:** Only Dagger and Throwing Weaponskills trigger the Finishing Move AoE heal & cleanse detonation.
