# Vampire (DNC) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Vampire is a Tier 4 **combat healer** powered by melee damage, TP and Finishing Moves instead of MP. Your physical hits trigger short-range party healing; excess healing can become **Crimson Veil** protection. **Steps** and **Nocturne Feast** build Finishing Moves, while Dagger and Throwing weapon skills turn them into group healing and cleansing. Your damage is deliberately reduced compared with a pure DPS Soul: staying alive and providing consistent healing matter more than maximizing personal damage.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Vampire Soul Crystal or use `!spec set vampire` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Favor **Dagger** or **Throwing** weapon skills to trigger Finishing Move healing. Daggers support sustained melee contact; carry a usable throwing option if your build relies on that weapon-skill family.
- **Resources:** You do not need MP for the Soul's distinctive healing loop. Manage TP, Steps, Finishing Moves, available weapon skills and healer cooldowns.
- **Stats:** DEX, AGI, CHR, Waltz Potency, Store TP, Accuracy and HP can improve your healing uptime and survivability. The Soul also gains Subtle Blow, Regain and reduced enmity as it levels.
- **Position:** Most party healing from this kit reaches allies within **15 yalms** of you. Coordinate positioning with your frontline and healer group before triggering a major heal.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Stay in combat:** Land ordinary physical hits on the enemy. Your attacks trigger **Crimson Drain Samba**, healing allies within 15 yalms based on damage. Excess healing becomes a short **Crimson Veil** barrier rather than simply vanishing.
2. **Build Finishing Moves with Steps:** Use eligible **Steps** to gain two Finishing Moves and trigger an immediate 10% party heal. Track your current Finishing Moves as you approach a weapon-skill opportunity.
3. **Spend Finishing Moves for group recovery:** Use a **Dagger or Throwing weapon skill** to convert your available Finishing Moves into party healing at 6% per move, plus one debuff cleanse per nearby ally. Outside Trance, this spends the moves.
4. **Use Nocturne Feast for an emergency swing:** **Nocturne Feast** damages the target and heals nearby party members, adds **250 TP** to those members, and sets your Finishing Moves to five. Use it when its immediate party benefit and follow-up weapon skill will matter.
5. **Recover with Sanguine Waltz:** **Sanguine Waltz** costs **200 TP** (free during Trance). It heals the party for 30%, removes up to two debuffs and applies 15 seconds of regeneration within 15 yalms.
6. **Apply a party ward through Contradance:** **Contradance** has a shortened 15-second recast; Waltzes used while Contradance is active can grant nearby members **10 seconds of damage reduction**. Plan this around sustained incoming damage.
7. **Exploit Trance:** During **Trance**, Crimson Drain Samba healing is doubled. Eligible Dagger/Throwing weapon skills perform the equivalent of five Finishing Moves' group healing without consuming your accumulated moves, and Waltz TP costs are waived.
8. **Adjust your priorities:** If party HP is stable, continue attacking and building resources. If allies are threatened, spend a qualifying weapon skill or emergency healing action instead of saving every charge for damage.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Lv. 10–29:** Crimson Drain Samba heals for **18%** of your qualifying attack damage. Available Steps give two Finishing Moves and an immediate 10% group heal.
- **Lv. 30–49:** Crimson Drain Samba heals **24%** of qualifying damage; offensive and healing-related passive stats increase.
- **Lv. 50–69:** Crimson Drain Samba heals **30%** of qualifying damage.
- **Lv. 70–75:** Crimson Drain Samba heals **36%** of qualifying damage.
- **Crimson Veil:** Overhealing from the attack-based healing loop can become a barrier capped at **20% of the recipient's maximum HP**, with a short duration.
- **Finishing Move conversion:** Eligible Dagger and Throwing weapon skills heal allies for **6% per Finishing Move** and remove one qualifying debuff from each recipient. Trance treats the conversion as five moves without consumption.
- **Sanguine Waltz:** Party heal 30%, cleanse up to two debuffs, 15-second regeneration; ordinarily costs 200 TP, waived during Trance. Its ability recast is 18 seconds.
- **Nocturne Feast:** Has a 25-second recast; its target drain feeds party healing and 250 TP to nearby allies, and resets your Finishing Moves to five. The drain is bounded by target current HP and your own maximum HP, subject to the ability's implementation.
- **Contradance ward:** When Contradance is active, a Waltz can apply an approximately **15% damage-reduction ward for 10 seconds** to nearby allies.
- **Passive scaling:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, Vampire gains Max HP +14/18/22/28%, Waltz Potency +20/30/40/50%, Store TP +10/15/20/25, Regain +12/18/24/30, and increasingly reduced enmity.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Playing too far away to connect:** Without qualifying attacks you lose an important source of frequent party healing.
- **Using unrelated weapon skills to spend Finishing Moves:** The healing conversion is tied to Dagger and Throwing weapon skills.
- **Forgetting the 15-yalm group range:** Your emergency recovery is strongest when allies are within the Soul's support radius.
- **Spending every Finishing Move before a healing spike:** Keep enough resources or a suitable recovery action ready for the next group threat.
- **Expecting Trance to work like ordinary Finishing Move spending:** It uses the five-move healing payoff without consuming your stored moves.
- **Overvaluing personal DPS:** Vampire's damage role is secondary. Its purpose is keeping the group alive through steady hits, timed group recovery and cleanses.
