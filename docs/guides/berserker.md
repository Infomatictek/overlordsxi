# Berserker (WAR) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Berserker is a Tier 1 **all-out physical burst damage** Soul. It enhances Warrior's offensive buffs, uses **Bloodlust Surge** for a short high-output window, and spends accumulated TP on frequent weapon skills. **Savage Cleave** adds an extra damaging action at the cost of your own HP. This Soul wins through aggression and burst uptime, but the same buffs that boost its output expose it to serious incoming damage.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip Berserker's Soul Crystal or use `!spec set berserker` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Berserker does not impose a single weapon lock. Great Axe, Axe, paired Axes and Sword-based setups can be considered according to your eligible weapon-skill talents and gear.
- **Stats:** Prioritize Accuracy, Attack, Double Attack, Weapon Skill Damage, Store TP and sustainable haste. Use survivability gear when the boss has dangerous incidental or unavoidable hits.
- **Party role:** You are a DPS specialist. Coordinate with a tank and healer before stacking defensive penalties or using HP-recoil attacks.
- **Buff stacking:** Berserker can maintain multiple offensive Warrior buffs simultaneously, unlike stance-exclusive specializations. Make sure the important buffs are active before committing high TP.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare offensive buffs:** Activate **Berserk** for increased Attack and an immediate **300 TP** injection. **Aggressor** removes its usual Evasion penalty for Berserker and adds critical chance. Use **Warcry** for an additional Attack boost.
2. **Build weaponskill TP:** Maintain melee uptime with your chosen weapon. Double Attack and Store TP bonuses help you reach the next weapon-skill opportunity.
3. **Begin the burst window:** Use **Bloodlust Surge** just before an important damage sequence. It lasts **10 seconds**, has a **25-second recast**, and increases Attack and Double Attack damage while lowering Defense.
4. **Combine Blood Rage appropriately:** Blood Rage's enhanced Critical Hit bonus and longer duration can overlap with your offensive window when the encounter permits.
5. **Spend TP during the burst:** Use your chosen eligible weapon skills when they can actually land. Berserker's additional all-hit weapon-skill damage bonus strengthens the payoff.
6. **Weave Savage Cleave:** **Savage Cleave** is a close-range physical strike on a **20-second recast**. It grants no TP and causes nonlethal HP recoil, so use it when the extra damage is worth the health cost.
7. **Recover between windows:** Continue ordinary attacks and rebuild TP. Reuse Berserk's instant TP benefit when its native cooldown permits rather than reserving it only for the first pull.
8. **Adjust for boss mechanics:** Avoid using HP recoil and Defense-reducing buffs right before unavoidable AoE damage; maintaining survival is worth more than a theoretical final hit.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Berserk enhancement:** Adds **10 percentage points of Attack potency** over the baseline effect, extends its duration by 60 seconds, and immediately grants **300 TP** when activated.
- **Aggressor enhancement:** Removes the typical Evasion penalty and gives **+5% critical-hit rate** for Berserker.
- **Blood Rage / Warcry:** Blood Rage gains an additional critical-hit chance and increased duration; Warcry gains further Attack potency.
- **Bloodlust Surge at Lv. 10–29:** Ten-second window with **+10% Attack**, **+15% Double Attack damage** and **-10% Defense**.
- **Lv. 30–49:** Bloodlust Surge increases to **+14% Attack** and **+22% Double Attack damage**, retaining the Defense penalty.
- **Lv. 50–69:** Bloodlust Surge grows to **+18% Attack** and **+30% Double Attack damage**.
- **Lv. 70–75:** Bloodlust Surge peaks at **+24% Attack** and **+40% Double Attack damage**.
- **Savage Cleave:** Deals a scaled physical strike at up to 4 yalms with no TP generated. Self-recoil is **6/8/10/12% max HP** at Lv. 10/30/50/70; recoil cannot directly reduce you below 1 HP.
- **Passive progression:** At Lv. 10/30/50+, Berserker receives Attack +8/12/16%, Double Attack +6/10/14%, all-hit weapon-skill damage +5/8/12%, and Store TP +5/10/15. Check the displayed Soul effects for active buffs and native cooldowns.
- **Defensive tradeoff:** Berserk and Bloodlust Surge reduce safety while empowering damage. Neither replaces a tank's mitigation kit.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Using Savage Cleave at dangerously low HP:** The recoil is nonlethal by itself, but a subsequent boss hit can finish you.
- **Stacking every offensive buff before unavoidable damage:** High damage is useless if your Defense penalties cause an immediate death.
- **Treating Savage Cleave like a TP builder:** It grants **zero TP**; weave it for direct damage when safe.
- **Saving Berserk exclusively for opening pulls:** The immediate 300 TP can create useful mid-fight weapon-skill opportunities.
- **Ignoring weapon-skill eligibility:** Having a wide weapon selection does not mean every weapon skill is available under your current progression and talents.
- **Expecting the strongest window to be permanent:** Bloodlust Surge lasts ten seconds. Commit your burst when you can actually attack.
