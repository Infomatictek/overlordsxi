# Berserker (WAR) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Berserker is Warrior's pure **all-out physical burst DPS** crystal (`Damage` tier: 0% damage penalty, 1.00x WS scaling). Every signature Warrior buff is amplified for Double Attack, Attack%, Critical Hits, and instant TP injection, letting you chain relentless Weaponskills.

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapon / Armor:** Any Warrior weapon (Great Axe, Axe/Axe, Sword, etc.). No weapon locks.
- **All Stances Allowed:** Unlike Commander, you can stack `Berserk`, `Aggressor`, `Warcry`, and `Blood Rage` together at the same time.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Stack Offensive Buffs:** Pop `Berserk` (grants up to +45% Attack% + mods AND an instant **+300 TP**), `Aggressor` (zero Evasion penalty +5% Crit Rate), and `Warcry` (+8% extra Attack% potency).
2. **Enter Burst Window:** Trigger `Bloodlust Surge` (25s CD, 10s: +10–24% Attack% & +15–40% Double Attack Damage) alongside `Blood Rage` (30%+ Crit Rate, 45s) right as you reach WS TP.
3. **WS & Double Attack Spree:** Auto-attack with boosted Double Attack & Store TP and unload high-tier Weaponskills repeatedly.
4. **Filler Burst:** Weave `Savage Cleave` (20s CD) right after a Weaponskill when abilities are on cooldown for a free physical cleave.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Instant TP Berserk:** Activating `Berserk` immediately injects `+300 TP`, making it an active DPS opener rather than just a passive buff.
- **No Aggressor Penalty:** `Aggressor` adds Evasion instead of subtracting it while also giving `+5%` Critical Hit Rate.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Watch your HP with `Savage Cleave` & `Bloodlust Surge`:** `Bloodlust Surge` cuts your Defense by -10% (on top of Berserk's -25% DEF), and `Savage Cleave` inflicts 6–12% Max HP self-recoil (non-lethal, stops at 1 HP) while granting **0 TP**. Don't cleave yourself low right before a boss AoE.
- **Don't hold `Berserk` only for pulls:** Its +300 TP burst lets you push straight to your next WS mid-fight too.
