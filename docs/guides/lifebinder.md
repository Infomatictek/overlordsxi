# Lifebinder (RUN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Lifebinder turns Rune Fencer into a **Sacred-Rune frontline healer whose `Vivacious Pulse` changes effects based on which 3 Runes you hold** (`Healer` tier: -60% damage dealt, 0.12x WS scaling). Landing melee swings and WSs while holding Runes heals injured allies automatically (**Runic Lifetap**), your **Regen I–IV** spells turn into **10y AoEs with +50% potency & +60s duration**, and **Vivacious Pulse** triggers a **15s CD party heal** layered with Regen (`Lux`), Cleanses (`Unda`), MP/Refresh (`Tenebrae`), or Stoneskin (`Elemental`)!

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapon / Armor:** Great Sword (or any melee weapon to swing for Runic Lifetap) + MND/Cure Potency/HP/MP/Fast Cast/Regen gear. **Hold 3 Runes at all times (unlocked @ Lv.10+)**!
- **`Regen IV` Backported:** Lifebinder learns `Regen IV` at Lv.75, and all `Regen I–IV` casts become 10y radial party AoEs.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Stack 3 Runes for the Fight (`Vivacious Pulse` Loadout):** Always keep **3 Runes** up — what you harbor decides `Vivacious Pulse`'s bonus effect every 15 seconds:
   - **3x Lux:** Maximum heal (`+75%` heal potency) + strong party **Hybrid Regen**.
   - **3x Unda:** Cleanses up to **3 debuffs** off every party member!
   - **3x Tenebrae:** Restores massive **party MP + Refresh**.
   - **Elemental Runes (Ignis/Gelus/Flabra/Tellus/Sulpor):** Wraps the party in a **60s Stoneskin** (25% of heal per rune). *(Or mix e.g. 2 Lux + 1 Unda / 1 Tenebrae!)*
2. **Maintain Supercharged Party `Regen` & Swing (`Runic Lifetap`):** Cast **AoE `Regen I–IV`** on the party, then stay in melee — every landed hit (`2.5% HP + MND/Rune heal` + `1% self MP`) and WS (`5% HP heal` + `3% self MP`) automatically heals the most injured ally within 12y!
3. **Spam `Sacred Pulse` (`Vivacious Pulse`, 15s CD):** Fire `Vivacious Pulse` every 15s to heal the 12y party and trigger your active Runes' Regens, Cleanses, MP/Refresh, and Stoneskins.
4. **Emergency Spot-Heal & Raid Ward:**
   - `Font of Life` (20s CD, 15y Ally): huge `22–48%` target heal + 2-debuff cleanse + `-10–22%` DT & `+10–25%` Cure Received (12s).
   - `Sanctified Ward` (35s CD, 12y Party): `10–22%` instant heal + 15s Hybrid Regen + `-15% DT / +35 MEVA` party ward.
   - `One for All` (60s CD) also heals the party 18% of your Max HP, and `Valiance` (60s CD) gives the party `+15% Cure Rcvd` & `+15% Fast Cast`.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Dynamic Rune Tuning:** Changing your 3 Runes lets you adapt `Vivacious Pulse` mid-battle from pure healing (`Lux`) to AoE Esuna (`Unda`), MP battery (`Tenebrae`), or shields (`Elemental`).
- **`Regen` AoE Scaling:** Your `Regen I–IV` spells gain automatic 10y AoE, `+50%` potency, and `+60s` duration passively.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Never fight with 0 Runes:** With 0 Runes active, `Runic Lifetap` stops healing on melee/WS hits and `Vivacious Pulse` loses all bonus Regen/Cleanse/Refresh/Stoneskin boons!
- Note: `Cure` spells require Accession to go AoE, whereas **`Regen I–IV`** go 10y AoE automatically every cast.
