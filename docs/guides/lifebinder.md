# Lifebinder (RUN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Lifebinder is a **frontline Rune Fencer healer** whose support changes with its currently held Runes. **Runic Lifetap** turns melee hits and weapon skills into automatic healing for injured allies, **Regen** spells provide expanded party coverage, and **Vivacious Pulse** translates Rune combinations into healing, cleanses, MP/Refresh or barriers. The challenge is selecting an appropriate three-Rune setup for the next boss phase while maintaining enough melee uptime to support the party.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Lifebinder Soul Crystal or use `!spec set lifebinder` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** **Great Sword** is a practical Rune Fencer option; qualifying melee hits and weapon skills support Lifetap. The current healing loop is not intended to depend on a single weapon-skill talent.
- **Stats:** Max HP/MP, MND, Cure Potency, Fast Cast, Enhancing Magic and Regen gear. Accuracy helps maintain Lifetap without wasted melee rounds.
- **Runes:** Keep **three Runes** active. Runes of the same type maximize their corresponding pulse bonus; mixed Runes trade peak output for flexibility.
- **Position:** Stay close enough to the intended group for healing—Lifetap reaches roughly 12 yalms and the customized Regen covers a smaller radial area.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Select the Rune loadout:** Use **Lux** for maximum healing and Regen, **Unda** for cleansing, **Tenebrae** for MP/Refresh, or elemental Runes for shielding. Mix types when an encounter requires more than one benefit.
2. **Cast party Regen:** Maintain eligible **Regen I–IV** spells, which the Soul converts into enhanced **10-yalm radial** group regeneration.
3. **Engage for Runic Lifetap:** Successful melee hits automatically heal the lowest-health ally in range and restore some MP to you. Eligible weapon skills produce stronger Lifetap recovery.
4. **Use Vivacious Pulse regularly:** It performs the base party healing and applies bonus effects from the held Runes. Its specialized recast is **15 seconds**.
5. **Change Runes for boss phases:** Shift from Lux sustain to Unda if several allies need cleansing, Tenebrae in an MP-starved endurance fight, or elemental shielding before a dangerous hit.
6. **Spot-heal with Font of Life:** Use **Font of Life** on a badly injured ally to deliver a major heal, limited cleanse and short damage-reduction/Cure Received support.
7. **Use Sanctified Ward for group damage:** The party-wide **Sanctified Ward** combines immediate recovery, Regen and a brief defensive ward against heavy sustained damage.
8. **Layer Rune Fencer support:** **One for All** and **Valiance** have additional healing or Cure support in this specialization. Coordinate them with the primary healer rather than stacking cooldowns blindly.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Vivacious Pulse:** A **15-second** group healing tool. Its additional boon changes with the active Runes.
- **Lux:** Increases the healing potency of Vivacious Pulse and contributes group Regen. Three Lux Runes emphasize maximum sustained healing.
- **Unda:** Adds group debuff cleansing; three Unda Runes can remove up to three qualifying debuffs per member.
- **Tenebrae:** Adds group MP restoration and Refresh; three Tenebrae Runes emphasize resource recovery.
- **Elemental Runes:** Contribute a party Stoneskin component; mixed combinations can balance heal, cleanse, MP and barrier support.
- **Runic Lifetap:** Qualifying melee hits heal an injured ally within **12 yalms**, with some MP returned to the caster; eligible weapon skills provide a larger heal and MP restoration.
- **Regen I–IV:** Eligible Regen casts gain approximately **10-yalm area coverage**, **50% stronger potency** and **60 seconds** extra duration. Regen IV becomes accessible by Lv. 75.
- **Font of Life:** **20-second recast**, targeted ally within 15 yalms, grants a **22–48%** level-scaled heal and additional cleanse/defensive support.
- **Sanctified Ward:** **35-second recast**, group recovery, hybrid Regen and damage/elemental protection over a short window.
- **Level progression:** Rune-based pulse potency, Lifetap healing and emergency support improve through Lv. 10/30/50/70/75; Rune selection remains the central choice throughout.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Entering a fight without Runes:** Lifetap and Vivacious Pulse lose important bonuses with an empty Rune setup.
- **Only using Lux in every situation:** Cleanse, MP support and shielding can outweigh additional raw healing in the right phase.
- **Standing too far away:** Lifetap and group Regen use limited radii, not full-party global effects.
- **Assuming Cure spells become unconditional AoE:** The broad automatic conversion described in this kit applies to eligible **Regen**; Cure still requires its applicable area effect.
- **Ignoring melee uptime:** Lifetap is a large part of sustained healing when a safe enemy is available.
- **Spending Font of Life on minor deficits:** Save its additional ward/cleanse for serious spot-healing needs.
