# Defender (PLD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Silver Aegis Interception Tank.** Defender protects allies through Club + Shield blocking and Divine Intercept, turning absorbed pressure into Zeal and Holy Retribution.

**Why play Defender?** It specializes in directly intercepting damage that would otherwise hit nearby teammates. It is not Templar's frontline healing or Spartan's personal shield-wall defense.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required synergy:** Club and Shield for Divine Intercept, block mastery and the full Holy Retribution engine.
- **Priority:** HP, Vitality, Shield and Club skill, enmity, block chance and survivability.
- **Unlock:** Lv10. Keep the party within the protection area while ensuring Defender stays above its health threshold.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Establish tank threat:** Engage the boss and use appropriate native enmity tools while maintaining Club + Shield.
2. **Intercept damage:** Stand near allies so Divine Intercept transfers eligible party damage to you while your HP remains safely above 35%.
3. **Build Zeal naturally:** Blocking and interception produce Zeal, up to five stacks.
4. **Convert Zeal:** At five stacks, use Flash, Shield Bash, or a Club weaponskill to trigger Holy Nova, extra enmity and a temporary defensive party benefit.
5. **Respond to boss pressure:** Use the Soul's enhanced tank abilities, and heal or reposition before the interception threshold fails.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Divine Intercept:** Redirects 25% of qualifying party damage from allies within 10y (40% during Consecration), with per-hit and area-window caps to prevent lethal uncontrolled transfers.
- **Zeal (0–5):** Earned on block/intercept; Holy Retribution spends five Zeal to create Holy Nova, 8% maximum MP return, -10% party damage taken for 8s and substantial enmity.
- **Defensive identity:** Increasing HP, DEF, damage reduction, block rate and enmity through level tiers 10/30/50/70.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Do not play as a general-purpose sword damage Paladin; Club + Shield unlocks the defining engine.
- Do not intercept damage without monitoring your own HP or the ally positioning radius.
- Do not confuse this tank with Templar's party-healing specialization.
