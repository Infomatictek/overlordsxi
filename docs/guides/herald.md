# Herald (SMN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Avatar elemental team conductor.** Herald's avatar invites the party into a brief elemental teamwork opportunity, rather than providing Celestial's healing sanctuary.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Herald when multiple players can coordinate elemental hits; Celestial is for avatar healing, Primalist for personal Rage burst.

## Setup & Gear
- An available avatar of the desired element, MP and nearby allies within the accord snapshot.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Summon an avatar with an element your party can follow.
2. **Act:** Start Astral Accord shortly before teammates land matching elemental attacks.
3. **Adapt:** Coordinate each party member's first matching hit within the window; avoid swapping avatars mid-accord.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Astral Accord has a 30s cooldown, costs 8% MP and lasts 8–10s for nearby allies (15y snapshot).
- Each member's first matching ordinary elemental hit receives 8/10/10/12% more damage. Master and pet share one claim; periodic ticks and extra Soul burst are excluded.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't repeatedly refresh the same accord expecting everyone to regain the consumed bonus.
- Confirm available commands and casting rules in the active Soul's menu before building a rotation.
