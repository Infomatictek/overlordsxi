# Herald (SMN) - Playstyle Guide

Herald is a Tier 3 support Soul. Choose an avatar’s element, then give each nearby party member a chance to strengthen one matching hit.

## Level 10 opening

1. Equip the crystal or use `!spec set herald`. Staff and Club are the eligible weapon-skill families.
2. Cast **Invite Carbuncle** for light or **Invite Ifrit** for fire. Both are available without a permanent avatar unlock.
3. Use **Astral Accord** while your party is within 15 yalms. It costs 8% of your maximum MP, rounded up, and has a 30-second recast.
4. Each covered party member can claim once with a matching direct spell, elemental weapon skill, or damaging pet ability. Select **Light Response** or **Flame Response** under **Envoy Responses** for your own follow-through, including when solo.
5. Use **Send Envoy** and **Recall Envoy** to position your avatar. **Dismiss Envoy** ends the current Accord before you invite a different avatar.

The opener works with any subjob and zero weapon-skill talents. Summons cost 5 MP. The HUD shows the active avatar, open claims and the Accord timer.

| Level | Available loans | Damage bonus | Accord duration |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 10–29 | Carbuncle and Ifrit | 8% | 8s |
| 30–49 | Adds Shiva and Garuda | 10% | 8s |
| 50–69 | Adds Titan, Leviathan and Ramuh | 10% | 8s |
| 70–75 | All seven | 12% | 10s |

Master and pet share one claim. A stronger active Accord takes priority. A same-element refresh keeps spent claims spent. Members must stay in the party and battle area, but can move away from the Herald after the initial snapshot. Periodic ticks, skillchains, and separate Soul burst bonuses do not claim or grow the Accord bonus. Losing or replacing the active envoy ends its Accord.
