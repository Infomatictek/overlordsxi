# Herald (SMN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Herald is a Tier 3 elemental support Soul that invites temporary avatar envoys and uses **Astral Accord** to empower one matching elemental hit per eligible party member. Your main decisions are choosing the element your party can exploit, keeping the envoy available, and timing Accord when allies are ready to attack. This is a coordinated burst window rather than a permanent party-wide damage aura.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Herald Soul Crystal or use `!spec set herald` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Staff and Club are the eligible weapon-skill families. The opening support loop requires no weapon-skill talent investment and works with any subjob.
- **Avatar selection:** At Lv. 10, **Invite Carbuncle** offers light and **Invite Ifrit** offers fire without requiring permanent avatar unlocks. Additional elements become available with level progression.
- **MP planning:** Inviting an envoy costs 5 MP. Astral Accord costs 8% of maximum MP, rounded up; avoid triggering it when the group cannot make use of its short window.
- **Team planning:** Ask which matching spell, elemental weapon skill or damaging pet ability each ally can land during Accord.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Select an element:** Choose an envoy whose element matches the party's upcoming damage. Start with **Invite Carbuncle** for light or **Invite Ifrit** for fire.
2. **Place the envoy:** Use **Send Envoy** and **Recall Envoy** to position your avatar so it remains useful and available.
3. **Gather the party:** Have intended recipients within 15 yalms when you activate **Astral Accord**. It has a 30-second recast.
4. **Call the burst:** Cast Astral Accord immediately before coordinated elemental attacks. Each eligible covered party member gets one claim for a matching direct spell, elemental weapon skill or damaging pet ability.
5. **Contribute yourself:** Use **Envoy Responses** to select **Light Response** or **Flame Response** as appropriate for your own follow-through, including when fighting alone.
6. **Watch the timer:** The window lasts 8 seconds through Lv. 69 and 10 seconds from Lv. 70 onward. Use the HUD to see which claims remain open.
7. **Change elements between windows:** **Dismiss Envoy** ends the current Accord; invite a different envoy only when the elemental change is worth sacrificing the current opportunity.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Level 10–29:** Invite Carbuncle and Ifrit; 8% matching damage bonus for 8 seconds.
- **Level 30–49:** Add Shiva and Garuda; 10% matching damage bonus for 8 seconds.
- **Level 50–69:** Add Titan, Leviathan and Ramuh; 10% matching damage bonus for 8 seconds.
- **Level 70–75:** All seven envoys remain available; 12% matching damage bonus for 10 seconds.
- **Who receives Accord:** Recipients must be within 15 yalms at the initial snapshot and remain in the party and appropriate battle area. They may move away from the Herald after the snapshot.
- **Claim limits:** Each eligible member can claim one matching hit; master and pet share a single claim.
- **What does not count:** Periodic damage ticks and skillchains do not spend a claim. Separate Soul burst effects do not amplify Accord's bonus.
- **Refreshing and overlap:** A same-element refresh does not restore already spent claims. The stronger active Accord takes precedence. Losing or replacing the envoy ends the current Accord.
- **Soul HUD:** Track the active envoy element, open claims and remaining Accord time.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Choosing an element nobody can use:** Select the envoy around the party's actual attacks rather than always casting the same one.
- **Firing Accord before allies are ready:** The window is short and each member only receives one eligible claim.
- **Expecting all damage to match:** Only direct qualifying elemental hits can claim Accord; ordinary physical swings, periodic ticks and skillchains cannot.
- **Assuming the pet grants an extra claim:** Master and pet share one claim, not two.
- **Changing the envoy during an active Accord:** Dismissing or replacing the envoy ends the current window.
- **Trying to refresh spent claims:** A same-element refresh does not grant extra charges to a member who already used theirs.
