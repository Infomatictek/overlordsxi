# Templar (PLD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Templar transforms Paladin into a **frontline Holy Light & Perpetual Majesty AoE Cure Crusader** (`CombatHealer` tier: -50% damage dealt, 0.20x WS scaling). Every single-target `Cure` splashes party-wide (15y) at 100% potency and converts overhealing into a **Light's Grace** HoT, while Club melee hits and Weaponskills pulse **Beacon of Light** heals to your lowest-HP ally and restore MP.

## Setup & Gear
- **Recommended Weapons:** **1H Club + Shield.** Equipping a Shield grants **100% Spell Interruption Resistance (SIRD)** and **+10/14/18/22% Shield Block Rate**, and **Club + Shield** is required to activate `Holy Radiance`.
- **Activation:** Socket via `!spec set templar` at Lv.10+. `Perpetual Majesty` is active passively at all times with zero lockout.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Stay Engaged on the Frontline:** Equip **Club + Shield** and engage the boss so every melee swing and Club Weaponskill triggers **Beacon of Light**, healing the lowest-HP ally within 12y for **8/11/14/18% of your Max HP** and restoring **+5% Max MP**.
2. **Splash Cures & Stack Light's Grace:** Cast single-target `Cure` spells on the tank or any party member — `Perpetual Majesty` splashes the full heal to everyone within 15y, and any overhealing wraps targets in **Light's Grace** (`15 + 2.2% Max HP/tick` HoT for 10s).
3. **Rotate Party Mitigation & Cleanses:** Weave `Holy Radiance` (20s CD: 25% HP party heal + 2-debuff cleanse + 10% MP) and `Crusader's Aegis` (25s CD: 10s -15% DT + Knockback Immunity + 2x Beacon power) around heavy boss AoEs.
4. **Sustain MP & Emergency Recovery:** Use `Chivalry` (15s CD: 40% MP restore + 15% AoE heal), `Divine Seal` (20s CD: 2-debuff party cleanse), `Rampart` (-15% DT party aura for 15s), and `Invincible` (replaced by **Sanctuary Benediction**: 100% party HP heal, 10-debuff cleanse, and 15s -20% DT).

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Perpetual Majesty:** Always active at Lv.10+ (0s lockout). Single-target `Cure` spells splash to all party members within 15y at 100% potency; overhealing applies **Light's Grace** (10s HoT).
- **Beacon of Light:** Melee hits and Club Weaponskills automatically heal the lowest-HP ally within 12y for 8/11/14/18% PLD Max HP and restore +5% Max MP. Doubled while `Crusader's Aegis` is active.
- **Sanctuary Benediction:** Replaces `Invincible` with a full 100% 15y party heal, 10-debuff cleanse, and 15s -20% DT shield.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't unequip your Club or Shield:** Without a Shield you lose 100% SIRD, and without **Club + Shield** you cannot use `Holy Radiance`.
- **Don't stand in the backline doing nothing between Cures:** Auto-attacking and using Club Weaponskills fuels your **Beacon of Light** heals and +5% MP returns.
- **Don't hold `Chivalry` or `Divine Seal` for emergencies only:** Both have short cooldowns (15s / 20s) and add free party healing/cleanses to your rotation.
