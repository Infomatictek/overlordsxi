# Templar (PLD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Templar is a **frontline Paladin combat healer**. Its always-on **Perpetual Majesty** spreads qualifying Cures across nearby party members and converts overhealing into **Light's Grace** regeneration. **Club melee hits and weapon skills** can produce **Beacon of Light** recovery for the lowest-HP ally while restoring MP. **Holy Radiance**, **Crusader's Aegis** and a transformed emergency rescue kit turn a tank-shaped Paladin into a reliable group healer.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Templar Soul Crystal or use `!spec set templar` at Lv. 10.
- **Preferred weapon:** **One-handed Club and Shield**. The shield grants strong spell interruption resistance and extra block chance; the pair is required for Holy Radiance.
- **Stats:** MND, HP, Cure Potency, Accuracy for Club hits, Shield block, Fast Cast and MP recovery.
- **Position:** Remain within melee range when it is safe, but stay close enough to reach party members inside the healing radius (about 15 yalms).
- **Party role:** Templar is an active healer, not the primary tank by default. Coordinate with the assigned tank before moving into the boss's dangerous frontal attacks.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Equip Club and Shield:** Confirm both are present before engaging. The healing kit rewards standing close enough to strike enemies while retaining casting stability.
2. **Use Perpetual Majesty Cures:** Target an injured ally with an eligible **Cure**. Perpetual Majesty spreads the healing to the nearby party and applies **Light's Grace** after overhealing.
3. **Weave Club attacks:** Successful Club melee hits or weapon skills can trigger **Beacon of Light**, healing the lowest-HP ally within range and restoring MP for continued Cures.
4. **Use Holy Radiance when the team is hurt:** It provides a large group heal and cleanses up to two debuffs; Club and Shield are required for the specialized action.
5. **Prepare Crusader's Aegis before major damage:** The short group ward reduces incoming damage, prevents knockback and improves Beacon of Light during its active duration.
6. **Use Chivalry to restore resources:** Its modified short cooldown helps recover MP and supplies extra nearby party healing.
7. **Coordinate cleanses:** Use the modified **Divine Seal** or your broader healing skills when the encounter applies dangerous multiple debuffs.
8. **Deploy Sanctuary Benediction for emergencies:** The Soul's major rescue action heals the party fully, clears many debuffs and supplies a temporary protective ward. Resume Club/Cure gameplay afterward.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Perpetual Majesty:** A passive effect from Lv. 10, with no manual activation or lockout. Eligible single-target Cures spread at full potency to party members within about **15 yalms**.
- **Light's Grace:** Overhealing can create a **10-second** regeneration effect around **15 flat HP plus 2.2% of target maximum HP per tick**.
- **Beacon of Light:** Qualifying Club melee hits or Club weapon skills heal the lowest-HP nearby ally for **8/11/14/18% of Templar maximum HP** at Lv. 10/30/50/70, and restore about **5% maximum MP**.
- **Crusader's Aegis:** **25-second recast**, **10-second** group protection with roughly **15% Damage Taken reduction** and knockback immunity; doubles Beacon healing while active.
- **Holy Radiance:** **20-second recast**, **25% party HP heal**, two-debuff cleanse and **10% MP recovery**, requiring Club and Shield.
- **Chivalry:** Modified **15-second recast** with about **40% personal MP restoration** and an additional **15% nearby-party heal**.
- **Sanctuary Benediction:** The major emergency action replaces Templar's normal defensive ultimate with full party healing, extensive cleansing and roughly **20% Damage Taken protection** for 15 seconds.
- **Shield benefits:** Strong spell interruption resistance and increasing block-rate bonuses (**10/14/18/22%** through Lv. 10/30/50/70).
- **Role tradeoff:** Templar's damage is reduced as a combat healer; the Club attack loop is valuable mainly because it feeds recovery.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Removing the shield to gain small personal damage:** You lose important casting stability, block chance and Holy Radiance eligibility.
- **Standing permanently out of combat reach:** You forfeit Beacon of Light triggers and passive MP return from Club attacks.
- **Assuming Cure splash has unlimited range:** Party members must stay within the relevant 15-yalm support radius.
- **Ignoring Light's Grace overhealing:** Carefully timed Cures can leave protective regeneration for subsequent damage.
- **Using Crusader's Aegis after the spike:** Apply its ward before a known dangerous boss action when possible.
- **Trying to tank and heal every mechanic alone:** Coordinate with the actual tank and healer team to preserve safe casting and melee uptime.
