# Templar (PLD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Frontline Holy Combat Healer.** Templar wields Club and Shield to turn cures and melee pressure into party healing. Single-target cures spread to nearby allies and overhealing applies Light's Grace; Club attacks also trigger Beacon of Light.

**Why play Templar?** It is a combat healer that stays near the fight and sustains several allies while fighting, unlike Defender's enmity/interception tank or Bishop's dedicated healing barriers.

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapons:** Club and Shield for the full combat-healing kit.
- **Priority:** Cure Potency, MND, MP, Club Accuracy and enough durability to remain near teammates.
- **Unlock:** Lv10; combat healing works before Lv75 endgame equipment.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Keep the group together:** Position so allies benefit from the 15y Perpetual Majesty cure splash.
2. **Heal through single-target Cures:** Each qualifying Cure also heals nearby party members at full potency; overheal applies Light's Grace regenerative healing.
3. **Maintain melee:** Club hits or Club weapon skills trigger Beacon of Light on the lowest-health nearby ally and recover some MP.
4. **Respond to danger:** Use Holy Radiance for substantial area healing and cleansing, or Crusader's Aegis before threatening attacks.
5. **Emergency utility:** Divine Seal, Chivalry, Rampart, and Sanctuary Benediction have Soul-specific support behavior.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Perpetual Majesty:** 15y party splash for single-target Cures at 100% potency; Light's Grace gives 10s healing over time following overheal.
- **Beacon of Light:** Club attacks/WSs heal the lowest-HP ally within 12y for 8/11/14/18% of Templar's maximum HP and refund 5% maximum MP.
- **Holy Radiance:** 20s cooldown, 25% HP area heal, two-debuff cleanse and 10% MP restoration.
- **Crusader's Aegis:** 25s cooldown, 10s protection granting -15% damage taken, knockback immunity and stronger Beacon throughput.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Do not mistake Templar for Defender: this Soul is a **healer**, not the party's primary tank.
- Do not step too far away for your Cure splashes or Beacon to reach the group.
- Do not assume melee replaces casting when the party is under heavy damage.
