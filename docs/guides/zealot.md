# Zealot (WHM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Staff Holy battlemage.** Staff blows restore MP, which powers damaging Banish and Holy magic. Zealot is a damage Soul, not Bishop's primary healer.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Zealot over ranged magical DPS when a fight supports Staff uptime and continuous self-funded Holy damage.

## Setup & Gear
- Use a main-hand Staff. Prioritize landing melee hits and maintaining enough MP to cast.
- Unlocks at Lv10; final specialization cap is Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Start:** Engage in Staff melee and land regular main-hand hits to trigger Staff Communion.
2. **Core:** Spend the recovered MP on single-target Banish or Holy, especially when a safe damage window opens.
3. **Timing:** Use Divine Seal before a damaging divine spell you want to empower; it waits through a canceled cast within its window.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- First landed main-hand staff hit per round can restore 3.5/4/4/4.5% max MP, with a 2.5s lock and 2 MP minimum.
- Divine Seal grants +20% to the next completed damaging divine spell within 20s. Divine Benediction of Light becomes an option at Lv70.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Do not stand in dangerous enemy melee range just to recover MP; a denied melee window is an intentional weakness.
- Do not infer additional spells or job abilities from the base job; check the Soul's actual unlocked actions.
