# Zealot (WHM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Zealot is a Tier 1 single-target light-magic damage Soul that **must fight in melee to sustain its casting**. Successful damaging staff attack rounds trigger **Staff Communion**, restoring MP for the next spell. Use **Consecration** to empower an important cast, then return to staff swings to rebuild resources. Zealot is not the party's primary healer; its role is to deal focused damage while allies cover defensive responsibilities.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip Zealot's Soul Crystal or use `!spec set zealot` from Lv. 10.
- **Required weapon:** Equip a **Staff** as your main weapon. The custom damaging spells, Consecration and Staff Communion check that a Staff is equipped.
- **Priority stats:** Melee Accuracy helps trigger reliable Communion events. MND, Magic Accuracy and Magic Attack Bonus support the light-magic half of the loop. A larger MP pool increases spell costs in absolute terms because costs are percentage-based, but also increases the potential Communion refund.
- **Fight position:** Stand close enough to land staff hits, but plan your casts around boss mechanics that could interrupt or force repositioning.
- **Talent dependency:** The basic Soul rotation works at Lv. 10 without weapon-skill talent points. Staff weapon skills retain normal TP and unlock rules, but are not Staff Communion triggers.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Engage and hit with Staff:** Begin attacking a single target. A successful damaging staff attack round can restore MP through **Staff Communion**, at most once every 3 seconds.
2. **Cast Dawnfire (Lv. 10+):** Use **Dawnfire** as the early reliable light spell. It costs 8% maximum MP, casts in 2 seconds and has a 6-second recast.
3. **Time Consecration:** Activate **Consecration** before a damaging Zealot spell that you can safely land. It lasts up to 20 seconds, increases the next eligible spell's damage by 20%, and has a 90-second recast.
4. **Return to melee between spells:** Staff Communion is your unique MP sustain. Repeated spells without staff contact will quickly consume your available MP.
5. **Add Sunbrand at Lv. 30:** It costs 12% of maximum MP, has a 2.5-second cast and an 8-second recast. Use it when you can afford a larger commit.
6. **Add Pillar of Day and Last Light at Lv. 50:** Pillar of Day costs 16% maximum MP (3-second cast; 12-second recast). **Last Light** costs 20% maximum MP (3.5-second cast; 14-second recast) and is your heavier single-target commitment.
7. **At Lv. 70+:** Last Light gains additional power but its cost rises to 22% of maximum MP. Save Consecration for a reliable damage window rather than using your most expensive spell into an interruption.
8. **Use your HUD:** Watch staff qualification, the Communion lockout, remaining Consecration window, and Consecration recast so you can align attacks and casts.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Staff Communion at Lv. 10–29:** Restore 3.5% maximum MP on a qualifying staff hit, with a minimum of 2 MP, no more often than once every 3 seconds.
- **Lv. 30–49:** Communion improves to 4% maximum MP; Sunbrand unlocks.
- **Lv. 50–69:** Communion remains at 4%; Pillar of Day and Last Light unlock.
- **Lv. 70–75:** Communion improves to 4.5%; Last Light's base damage multiplier and MP cost increase.
- **Consecration:** A 20-second window for 20% more eligible spell damage, with a 90-second recast. A positive-damage Zealot spell consumes it; a failed or non-damaging attempt does not count as a landed damaging spell.
- **Single-target damage kit:** Dawnfire (Lv. 10), Sunbrand (Lv. 30), Pillar of Day and Last Light (Lv. 50). Their respective recasts are 6, 8, 12 and 14 seconds.
- **Staff Communion vs. weapon skills:** Communion listens to qualifying staff melee hits, not weapon-skill events. Weapon skills still contribute ordinary damage using their normal unlock and TP rules.
- **Level-scaled passives:** Zealot gains Staff skill +12/22/34/46; Accuracy +10/18/28/40; MND +6/12/18/25; and both Magic Attack Bonus and Magic Accuracy +8/14/22/30 at Lv. 10/30/50/70.
- **Soul HUD:** Staff eligibility, remaining Communion cooldown, Consecration window and Consecration recast are visible.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Standing far away for the whole fight:** Zealot loses its core MP recovery without Staff melee hits.
- **Equipping a different main weapon:** The special spells, Consecration and Communion all expect a Staff.
- **Treating weapon skills as Communion triggers:** Only the qualifying melee hit event restores MP.
- **Using Consecration before an unsafe cast:** It expires after 20 seconds. Wait for a cast window rather than wasting the empowerment.
- **Ignoring percentage MP costs:** A larger maximum MP value increases the absolute MP spent per spell, so keep Communion uptime high.
- **Trying to replace a dedicated healer:** Zealot is a pure DPS Soul. Build around light damage and rely on party healers for sustained recovery.
