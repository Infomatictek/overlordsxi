# Zealot (WHM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Zealot is a Tier-1 damage Soul. Land staff swings to refill MP, then spend it on single-target light magic. Staying at range costs you your main source of MP. Leave party healing and emergency recovery to your teammates.

## Setup & Gear

- Equip a two-handed staff and select Zealot at Lv.10 with `!spec set zealot` or Crystal Socket.
- Use melee accuracy for reliable Communion. MND, magic accuracy and magic attack support your spell damage.
- Staff weapon skills use their normal unlocks and TP. The kit works with zero weapon-skill talent points.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. **Lv.10:** Engage one enemy. Cast Dawnfire when you have MP and enough time to finish your cast. Return to staff swings for Communion.
2. **Lv.10:** Use Consecration before a spell you can safely finish. It empowers your next damaging Zealot spell for up to 20 seconds.
3. **Lv.30:** Use Sunbrand when you can afford the longer cast and larger MP cost.
4. **Lv.50:** Use Pillar of Day for heavier regular pressure or commit to Last Light during a longer opening.
5. **Lv.70–75:** Save more MP for an intensified Last Light and use Consecration when the boss gives you a clean cast window.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- Staff Communion restores 3.5%, 4%, 4% and 4.5% of max MP at Lv.10, 30, 50 and 70 respectively, with a minimum of 2 MP. It triggers at most once per three seconds from a damaging staff attack round.
- The HUD shows staff readiness, the Communion lock and Consecration's remaining time or recast.
- Trust summons remain available. The combat spell and ability menus carry the Zealot kit.

## Common Mistakes

- Avoid casting through a boss mechanic that will force you to move. Wait for a window if you've armed Consecration.
- Watch the MP cost before chaining expensive spells. A missed swing gives you no Communion.
- Keep your staff equipped through a cast. Weapon skills do not trigger Communion.
