# Shinobi (NIN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Offensive Shadow Clone Manager.** Shinobi is about creating, maintaining and directing fragile offensive shadow copies while dealing its own Katana damage. Its active Soul key remains `kurogane` for existing saves and wiki mappings, but the player-facing identity is **Shinobi**.

**Why play Shinobi?** Managing multiple real attacking shadows creates a very different damage rhythm from Black Belt's direct melee, Pugilist's automaton pairing and Kagemusha's decoy/shadow tank. The shadows are attackers, not miniature tanks.

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapon:** Katana; maintain your personal Accuracy, Attack and Katana weapon-skill damage.
- **Squad:** Up to five offensive shadows, with short lifespans and vulnerability to boss area damage.
- **Unlock:** Lv10; the full clone command interface and exact JA availability must be checked against the currently deployed client/server pair.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Build the squad:** Use the available Utsusemi/Soul action to create a few offensive shadows on your engaged target.
2. **Fight alongside them:** Keep attacking with Katana while the shadows independently contribute melee pressure.
3. **Watch clone uptime:** Shadows expire after roughly 25–30s and dangerous boss AoE can kill them earlier.
4. **Rebuild intelligently:** Preserve the opportunity to summon replacements after predicted AoE attacks instead of refreshing blindly at maximum shadows.
5. **Use command windows:** Where available in the deployed build, Shadow Legion coordinates surviving shadows into a single-target burst and Phantom Assault briefly accelerates their attacks.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Shadow count:** Maximum five attacking shadows; their damage and availability are the Soul's primary tactical engine, not a generic builder/spender meter.
- **Damage and defense:** The Ninja remains an active DPS; shadows are fragile and neither intercept enemy attacks nor function as tanks.
- **Planned production actions:** Utsusemi: Ichi creates two, Utsusemi: Ni creates three; Shadow Legion and Phantom Assault require existing shadows. These require client/server availability verification before describing them as live.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Do not summon all five just before an unavoidable boss area attack.
- Do not expect the clones to absorb damage like Kagemusha's defensive mechanics.
- Do not stop attacking while managing shadows; your own Katana output matters.
- Do not expose the old internal name Kurogane to players; it is a compatibility key only.
