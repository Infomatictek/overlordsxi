# Musician (BRD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Musician is a **magical song damage and burst-support** Bard. Its offensive Threnody, Requiem and Elegy songs inflict magical damage while building **Musician's Resonance**. At three stacks, **Grand Finale** turns accumulated Resonance and the target's active song debuffs into a stronger magical burst. Musician retains the Bard's important party buff songs rather than replacing them with its offensive rotation.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Musician Soul Crystal or use `!spec set musician` at Lv. 10.
- **Equipment:** Use an appropriate instrument and gear emphasizing **CHR, Singing and instrument skill, Magic Attack Bonus and Magic Accuracy**. You are not locked into a specific melee weapon.
- **Party responsibilities:** Keep useful group songs active. Offensive debuff-song usage is designed not to overwrite your protected party buffs.
- **Casting conditions:** The Soul grants extensive resistance to spell interruption and progressively faster song casting. Stand where your songs reach their intended target without exposing yourself unnecessarily.
- **Burst preparation:** Coordinate with teammates' skillchains so your offensive songs and Grand Finale can land as Magic Bursts.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Maintain the party's important buff songs:** Use your normal song toolkit to support the group before committing to magical offense.
2. **Start building Resonance:** Sing eligible offensive **Threnodies, Requiems or Elegies** on the selected enemy. These songs can deal CHR- and skill-based magical damage; successful eligible casts build Resonance.
3. **Reach three stacks:** Track the stack indicator and remaining duration. Resonance caps at **3 stacks**; additional buildup after the cap does not create a fourth stack.
4. **Prepare target song debuffs:** Grand Finale gains additional base power when the enemy has active **Threnody, Requiem and Elegy** debuffs. Coordinate these applications around the target's resistance and the burst window.
5. **Activate Lyrical Overdrive:** Use **Lyrical Overdrive** shortly before your concentrated damage sequence. Its 15-second empowerment raises Magic Attack Bonus, Magic Accuracy, Fast Cast and special song damage.
6. **Detonate with Grand Finale:** Cast **Grand Finale** on the enemy during the prepared window, ideally aligned to a compatible skillchain for Magic Burst damage. Finale consumes your available Resonance stacks for a larger light-magic strike.
7. **Rebuild and repeat:** Restore useful party buffs as needed, continue offensive songs to rebuild Resonance, then plan another Finale. The action is not a replacement for all of your ordinary Bard support.
8. **Adapt to encounter resistance:** A resisted offensive song may yield less value. Adjust your choice of song and targets rather than forcing the same elemental damage every time.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Musician's Resonance:** Up to **3 stacks** with a **30-second** duration. Eligible offensive songs add stacks, each associated with an additional **+5 Magic Attack Bonus**.
- **Grand Finale:** A **20-second-recast** single-target light-magic attack that consumes Resonance. Its base-power calculation includes **95 + 45 per Resonance stack + 35 per active qualifying song debuff**, then receives an additional **1.25×** ability damage modifier.
- **Lyrical Overdrive:** A **45-second-recast**, **15-second** self-buff granting **+35 Magic Attack Bonus, +25 Magic Accuracy and +20 Fast Cast**, alongside **1.30×** damage for qualifying magical songs and a **1.25×** Magic Burst enhancement while active.
- **Magic Burst:** Offensive magical songs and Grand Finale can benefit from a valid elemental Magic Burst opportunity, subject to resistance and the server's magic-burst calculation.
- **Protected Bard buffs:** Offensive debuff songs are intended not to displace the party's important maintained buff songs.
- **Level 10–29:** The Soul provides CHR +6, Magic Attack Bonus +18, Magic Accuracy +15, Singing and instrument skill +10, song cast-time reduction and +1 Refresh.
- **Level 30–49:** CHR +12, Magic Attack Bonus +30, Magic Accuracy +25, Singing/instrument skill +18 and +2 Refresh.
- **Level 50–69:** CHR +18, Magic Attack Bonus +42, Magic Accuracy +35, Singing/instrument skill +26 and +3 Refresh.
- **Level 70–75:** CHR +25, Magic Attack Bonus +55, Magic Accuracy +45, Singing/instrument skill +35, additional Fast Cast and +4 Refresh.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Using Grand Finale without building Resonance:** It still has a damage calculation, but loses the additional power from accumulated stacks.
- **Overlooking the target's debuffs:** Threnody, Requiem and Elegy on the intended quarry can strengthen Finale's base power.
- **Burning Overdrive before the damage window:** Its 15-second duration should overlap your damage songs and Finale, not the group's preparation downtime.
- **Assuming every Bard song becomes an offensive nuke:** The damage conversion focuses on specific offensive song families; normal buff songs retain their support purpose.
- **Ignoring skillchain coordination:** A well-timed Magic Burst can improve your song damage compared with casting into an unrelated window.
- **Sacrificing party buffs for personal damage:** Maintaining the group's needed support remains part of Musician's job.
