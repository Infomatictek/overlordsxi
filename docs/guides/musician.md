# Musician (BRD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Musician turns Bard into a **magical Song Nuker & Magic Burster** (`HybridSupport` tier: -25% personal damage dealt, 0.50x WS scaling). Your party buff songs stay protected so you can freely cast **Threnodies, Requiems, and Elegies** to deal CHR + MAB magic damage (with full Magic Burst capability), stack **Musician's Resonance (max 3)**, and detonate them with `Grand Finale`.

## Setup & Gear
- **Weapon / Instruments:** Any Instrument + caster/CHR/MAB gear. No weapon lock.
- **Built-In QoL:** `105%` SIRD, `-35–50%` Song Cast Time, `-2–4s` Song Recast, and **offensive songs never overwrite your party buff songs** (`Ballad`, `Scherzo`, `Madrigal`, etc.).

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Maintain Party Buff Songs:** Sing your party's core buff songs as normal — debuff songs won't wipe them out.
2. **Nuke & Stack `Resonance` (Max 3):** Rotate **Threnody, Requiem, and Elegy** on your target. Each cast deals CHR + Singing/Instrument Skill + MAB magical damage (**can Magic Burst** off Skillchains!) and adds **+1 Musician's Resonance** (max 3 stacks, +5 MAB per stack, 30s).
3. **Detonate with `Grand Finale`:** Once you have **3 Resonance stacks** AND multiple active song debuffs (`Threnody`, `Requiem`, `Elegy`) on the mob, cast `Grand Finale` (20s CD, 20y) to consume your Resonance for a huge **1.25x amplified magic nuke** (also Magic Burst-capable!).
4. **Burst Window (`Lyrical Overdrive`):** Pop `Lyrical Overdrive` (45s CD, 15s: `+35 MAB`, `+25 MACC`, `+20% Fast Cast`, `1.30x` song damage, and `1.25x` Magic Burst bonus) before firing a 3-stack `Grand Finale` + Threnody bursts.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Protected Buff Songs:** Casting offensive debuff songs won't overwrite your existing party buffs.
- **Musician's Resonance (0–3 stacks, 30s):** Built by Threnody/Requiem/Elegy (+5 MAB/stack); consumed by `Grand Finale` (`95 + 45*stacks + 35*activeSongDebuffs` base power × 1.25).

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't waste `Grand Finale` at 0 Resonance stacks:** Always land 3 Threnody/Requiem/Elegy casts first so `Grand Finale` hits at full potency.
- **Don't expect damage from Lullabies or Maiden's Virelai:** Only Threnodies, Requiems, Elegies, and `Grand Finale` deal magic nuke damage.
