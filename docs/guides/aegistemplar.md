# Aegis Templar (PLD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Aegis Templar turns Paladin into a **Club + Shield guardian tank that intercepts 25–40% of party damage** (`Tank` tier: -60% damage dealt, 0.12x WS scaling, +60–120 Enmity). Blocking attacks and intercepting hits for allies builds **Zeal (max 5 stacks)**, which you detonate on `Flash`, `Shield Bash`, a Club WS, or `Judgment Gavel` for a **Holy Nova** AoE blast + **-10% DT party aura**.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon & Shield:** **1H Club in Main Hand + Shield in Sub.** Without **both** Club + Shield, you lose Divine Intercept, Zeal building, Club WS party healing & debuffs, +10% bonus Block Rate, `Aegis Consecration`, and `Judgment Gavel`. (Keep 100% SIRD from your Shield.)
- **Priority Stats:** VIT, MND, HP, Shield Skill/Block, DT-, Enmity, Cure Potency Received.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Pull & Stack Early Zeal:** Open with `Flash` + `Judgment Gavel` (20s CD, 4y: blunt hit scaled on VIT+MND, 4s Stun, -20% ATK/MATK Down for 12s, and **+3 Zeal**).
2. **Anchor & Protect Allies (`Divine Intercept`):** Stay within **10 yalms of your party**. As long as your HP is above 35%, you automatically intercept **25%** of damage allies take (or **40%** inside `Aegis Consecration`) at 80% reduced value, gaining **+1 Zeal** and enmity each time. Drop `Aegis Consecration` (25s CD, 10s: +20% Block Rate, 2s AoE enmity pulses, heals 25% of blocked damage on every block).
3. **Detonate `Holy Nova` at 5 Zeal:** Blocking attacks and intercepting hits quickly caps **5 Zeal**. As soon as you hit 5 Zeal, press `Flash`, `Shield Bash`, `Judgment Gavel`, or any **Club WS** to trigger **Holy Nova** (heavy Light AoE damage + huge AoE enmity + **+8% Max MP** + 8s **Holy Retribution** `-10% DT` to party).
4. **Maintain Club Debuffs & Sustain:** Use **Skullbreaker** or **Brainshaker** to apply -15% ATK/MATK Down (60s), or **Starlight / Moonlight** when your party needs extra HP/MP restoration. Cycle `Sentinel` (20s CD, +30% Block), `Rampart` (15% party Stoneskin + 20 MDEF), `Chivalry` (15s CD + erases 2 debuffs), and `Invincible` (party -25% DT for 15s).

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Zeal (0–5 stacks, 30s):** Built by Shield Blocks (+1), Divine Intercepts (+1), and `Judgment Gavel` (+3). Consumed all at once at **5 stacks** by `Flash`, `Shield Bash`, `Judgment Gavel`, or a Club WS to trigger Holy Nova.
- **Self-Sustain Loop:** `Shield Block` heals 25% of blocked damage during `Aegis Consecration`, `Holy Nova` restores 8% MP, and every Club WS scales off `VIT + MND` while healing allies within 10y.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't equip a Sword:** Swapping off a Club breaks Divine Intercept, Zeal, and both custom JAs.
- **Don't fall below 35% HP or drift >10y away:** `Divine Intercept` turns off whenever your HP hits 35% or lower, and only protects allies within 10 yalms.
- **Don't trigger 5-Zeal `Holy Nova` out of range of adds:** Save your 5-stack `Flash` / `Club WS` when engaged mobs are within 10y so the Light AoE blast and enmity hit everything.
