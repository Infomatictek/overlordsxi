# Aegis Templar (PLD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Aegis Templar is a **Club-and-Shield guardian tank** that protects nearby allies by intercepting part of their incoming damage. Each successful Shield Block or **Divine Intercept** builds **Zeal**. At five Zeal, an eligible action releases **Holy Nova**, creating substantial threat and granting **Holy Retribution** protection to the party. The core decision is when to spend Zeal while keeping enough HP to intercept and enough defenses to survive taking damage for others.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Aegis Templar Soul Crystal or use `!spec set aegistemplar` from Lv. 10.
- **Required equipment:** One-handed **Club in Main** and **Shield in Sub**. The specialized intercept, Zeal, weapon-skill sustain and custom actions rely on both; Shield also provides spell interruption resistance.
- **Stats:** HP, VIT, MND, Defense, Shield Block/Skill, Enmity, Damage Taken reduction and Cure Potency Received.
- **Position:** Remain within **10 yalms** of allies who need intercept protection. You must stay above **35% of your maximum HP** to perform the passive interception.
- **Party role:** Inform healers that you may take redirected damage from allies, so your personal HP requires active support even when the boss attacks someone else.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Establish threat:** Use **Flash** and **Judgment Gavel** against the enemy. Judgment Gavel offers a close-range strike, control effects, and **three Zeal** to begin your defensive cycle.
2. **Hold near allies:** Maintain Club-and-Shield equipment and keep vulnerable party members within 10 yalms. **Divine Intercept** can redirect part of their qualifying incoming damage to you while your HP remains above 35%.
3. **Build Zeal through protection:** Blocking hits or intercepting allied damage adds Zeal. The resource lasts around 30 seconds and caps at five stacks.
4. **Activate Aegis Consecration:** Use it before sustained incoming attacks for extra Block, enhanced intercept share and healing based on blocked damage, along with additional enmity.
5. **Release Holy Nova at full Zeal:** Once you have five stacks, use a qualifying **Flash**, **Shield Bash**, **Judgment Gavel** or **Club weapon skill** to spend the stacks for Holy Nova damage/enmity and a short **Holy Retribution** party damage-reduction aura.
6. **Use Club weapon skills for support:** **Skullbreaker** and **Brainshaker** can impair the enemy's offense. **Starlight** and **Moonlight** provide more direct HP/MP support. Do not treat weapon skills as personal-DPS buttons alone.
7. **Rotate major defense:** **Sentinel**, **Rampart**, **Chivalry** and **Invincible** gain specialist effects useful for mitigation, resource recovery or party protection.
8. **Watch your HP before every intercept:** If it drops to or below 35%, stop relying on Divine Intercept until recovered. Re-establish Zeal and threat after stabilizing.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Divine Intercept:** With Club and Shield, while above **35% HP**, redirects approximately **25% of qualifying nearby allied damage** onto you, or **40% during Aegis Consecration**; redirected damage is mitigated by the special intercept rules.
- **Zeal:** Up to **five stacks** for roughly **30 seconds**. Blocking and intercepting grant one; Judgment Gavel adds **three**.
- **Holy Nova:** At five Zeal, a valid action consumes stacks for an area light blast, a large threat pulse and approximately **8% maximum MP** return. Its defensive follow-up grants **10% party Damage Taken reduction** for eight seconds.
- **Judgment Gavel:** **20-second recast**, target within **4 yalms**. Deals VIT/MND-scaled blunt damage, briefly stuns and weakens the enemy's offense.
- **Aegis Consecration:** **25-second recast**, **10-second** window. Provides approximately **20% additional Block**, higher intercept share, repeated group enmity and healing from blocked damage.
- **Club-and-Shield sustain:** Eligible Club weapon skills can provide nearby ally healing and enemy debuffs. The specialized shield setup increases Shield Block rate and casting stability.
- **Emergency Paladin tools:** Sentinel reinforces blocks, Rampart provides party protection, Chivalry supports recovery/cleansing, and the specialized major defensive ability protects the party.
- **Level progression:** HP, VIT/MND, Defense, Damage Taken reduction, Shield skill, Enmity and Cure Potency Received improve through Lv. 10/30/50/70.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Equipping a Sword instead of Club:** Doing so disables the specialized Divine Intercept/Zeal and Club weapon-skill interactions.
- **Dropping below 35% HP:** Divine Intercept stops at this threshold; coordinate healing before you lose your guardian role.
- **Standing beyond 10 yalms from allies:** You cannot intercept teammates you are not close enough to protect.
- **Firing Holy Nova before the party needs it:** Its threat and defensive aura are valuable; time the five-stack release around a meaningful combat moment.
- **Using Consecration only after damage:** Precast it so intercept and Block benefits cover the danger window.
- **Forgetting diverted damage affects you:** The party may be safer while your own health becomes more vulnerable.
