# Skyborn (DRG) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Skyborn is a Tier 6 **Polearm tank** focused on holding one enemy's attention and timing a brief guard against a dangerous direct hit. **Grounding Cry** establishes threat, while **Skyward Guard** mitigates one impact during a 1.5-second window. A successful intercept unlocks a short follow-up ward from Lv. 30. Unlike jump-and-disengage styles, Skyborn stays targetable and keeps its position on the enmity list.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Skyborn Soul Crystal or use `!spec set skyborn` at Lv. 10.
- **Required weapon:** Use a Polearm in the main slot. Grounding Cry, Skyward Guard and Skyborn's defensive window require it; Polearm is also its eligible weapon-skill family.
- **Priorities:** HP, Enmity and survival-oriented gear complement the Soul's passive HP and threat bonuses. Personal damage is penalized to reinforce its tank role.
- **No pet dependency:** Skyborn's guard and enmity mechanics do not require a wyvern; its Soul setup despawns an existing pet.
- **Team role:** Establish a stable enemy position, turn dangerous attacks away from the group where possible, and communicate that Guard only protects one correctly timed hit.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Open with Grounding Cry:** Target a living enemy within 15 yalms and use **Grounding Cry** to gain an immediate 200 cumulative and 700 volatile enmity. The ability has a 16-second recast.
2. **Hold position and engage:** Keep the target attacking you, build ordinary weapon-skill TP as available, and watch for a direct enemy hit worth intercepting.
3. **Brace with Skyward Guard:** Activate **Skyward Guard** immediately before a dangerous hit. Its 1.5-second window reduces the first qualifying direct impact and has a 20-second recast.
4. **Benefit from the guard's landing:** At the end of the window, the original living enemy can receive an additional 100 cumulative and 300 volatile enmity. This occurs even if the guard was mistimed, as long as the target remains valid.
5. **Use the ward after Lv. 30:** If Guard actually intercepts a qualifying attack, you gain a 5-second ward that absorbs later direct damage. At higher levels, the ward's HP allowance grows.
6. **React rather than spam:** If the incoming hit is too early or too late for the 1.5-second window, Guard does not reduce it. Align the defensive window with boss tells or predictable attack cycles.
7. **Reestablish threat:** Reuse Grounding Cry as needed while the fight continues, without relying on your defensive guard to do all the tanking.
8. **Watch the HUD:** Monitor the millisecond guard window, available ward HP and duration, and cooldowns for both key actions.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Level 10–29:** Skyward Guard reduces one qualifying direct hit by **35%**. The post-intercept ward is not yet available.
- **Level 30–49:** Guard reduction **40%**; a successful intercept grants a 5-second ward equal to **5% maximum HP**.
- **Level 50–69:** Guard reduction **45%**; ward capacity rises to **8% maximum HP**.
- **Level 70–75:** Guard reduction **50%**; ward capacity rises to **10% maximum HP**.
- **Guard eligibility:** Direct physical/ranged enemy hits qualify. Magical and breath hits qualify when flagged as direct scripted monster actions. Periodic damage and scripted HP changes do not use this damage interception.
- **Guard timing:** Skyward Guard is a 1.5-second, one-hit defense with a 20-second recast. An expired or interrupted intercept does not produce the Lv. 30+ ward.
- **Threat toolkit:** Grounding Cry grants 200 cumulative / 700 volatile enmity at a 15-yalm range every 16 seconds. A valid Guard landing adds 100 cumulative / 300 volatile enmity to the tracked target.
- **Passive scaling:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, Skyborn gains Polearm skill +10/20/30/42, HP +10/14/18/22% and Enmity +20/30/40/50, while its personal damage output is heavily reduced.
- **Soul HUD:** Includes Guard time in milliseconds, ward time and remaining capacity, Grounding Cry recast and Skyward Guard recast.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Using Guard as a long-duration damage-reduction buff:** It lasts only 1.5 seconds and affects one qualifying impact.
- **Equipping something other than a Polearm:** Skyborn's unique actions and defensive interception require the correct main weapon.
- **Expecting a ward from a missed intercept:** The Lv. 30+ ward requires an actual qualifying hit during Guard.
- **Assuming all damage types pass through the guard:** Periodic damage and direct HP adjustments may bypass it entirely.
- **Abandoning your original tank target:** The landing enmity is tied to the tracked living enemy in the same valid battle.
- **Relying on personal damage to hold hate:** Skyborn's job is threat and timed protection. Use Grounding Cry and maintain encounter control.
