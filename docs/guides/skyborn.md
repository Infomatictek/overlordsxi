# Skyborn (DRG) - Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Skyborn is a Tier 6 polearm tank. Open with **Grounding Cry**, then time **Skyward Guard** just before a heavy hit. Guard keeps you in place, targetable and on the enmity list.

## Setup & Gear

- Equip the crystal or use `!spec set skyborn` at Lv. 10. Bring an ordinary polearm. Only Polearm weapon skills are eligible.
- Grounding Cry and Skyward Guard work at Lv. 10 with any subjob and zero weapon skill talents. Skyborn's guard is independent of a wyvern.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Use **Grounding Cry** on one enemy within 15 yalms to open with 200 cumulative and 700 volatile enmity.
2. Engage and use **Skyward Guard** just before a dangerous hit. The 1.5-second window reduces the first direct impact.
3. Keep fighting the enemy you were targeting when you used Guard. The landing adds 100 cumulative and 300 volatile enmity to that foe.
4. From Lv. 30, a successful interception also grants a five-second landing ward that absorbs subsequent direct damage.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

| Level | First-hit reduction | Landing ward |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | 35% | — |
| 30 | 40% | 5% max HP |
| 50 | 45% | 8% max HP |
| 70–75 | 50% | 10% max HP |

- Grounding Cry and Skyward Guard have 16- and 20-second recasts. The HUD displays Guard timing, ward HP and Cry recast.
- Guard covers one hit. The ward can cover later hits up to its remaining HP.

## Common Mistakes

- Time Guard for the impact. A mistimed Guard can still grant its landing enmity but spends the recast.
- Keep your polearm equipped. Guard and its ward require the tank's weapon.
- Scripted HP changes and periodic damage bypass Guard and the ward.
