# Skyborn (DRG) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Timed aerial guard tank.** Skyborn is a polearm tank with an active short defensive guard and built-in targeted threat; it never removes itself from the boss's hate list.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Skyborn to tank bosses whose dangerous attacks are telegraphed and can be timed, rather than Phantom's ongoing physical evasion.

## Setup & Gear
- Polearm, HP, defensive equipment and enough awareness to read incoming hits.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Use Grounding Cry to establish single-target enemy threat without relying on /WAR.
2. **Act:** Time Skyward Guard just before a dangerous qualifying attack rather than keeping it up permanently.
3. **Adapt:** Remain targetable throughout; use the short defensive window to survive and retain boss attention.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Grounding Cry is 15y/16s cooldown and adds 200 CE/700 VE to one enemy.
- Skyward Guard has a 20s cooldown, 1.5s window and reduces the first qualifying hit by 35/40/45/50%. Lv30+ creates a short 5/8/10% HP ward.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't expect Super Jump immunity or protection from scripted fatal HP-set effects.
- Confirm available commands and casting rules in the active Soul's menu before building a rotation.
