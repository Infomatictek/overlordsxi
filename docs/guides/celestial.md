# Celestial (SMN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Celestial is an **Avatar-anchored Summoner healer**. While an Avatar is present, **Celestial Sanctuary** supplies nearby allies with regeneration, MP recovery and mitigation. **Blood Pact: Ward** and eligible Staff/Club weapon skills trigger **Starlight Mend** for burst party healing, while **Celestial Convergence** lets that same loop restore MP during a defensive window. Your job is to keep the Avatar alive, sustain the sanctuary and answer sudden party damage with short-cooldown healing tools.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Celestial Soul Crystal or use `!spec set celestial` from Lv. 10.
- **Avatar:** Keep a suitable Avatar summoned. The Soul provides greatly reduced Avatar perpetuation cost; without the pet, sanctuary and many triggered heals stop.
- **Weapons:** Use **Staff or Club** if you intend to trigger Starlight Mend through weapon skills. Appropriate Summoning, Healing and Cure Potency equipment remains useful.
- **Stats:** MND, CHR, Max HP/MP, Cure Potency, Fast Cast and Summoning skill support the specialized healer role.
- **Position:** The principal party pulses reach roughly **15 yalms**; plan positioning with healers and the frontline so allies remain covered.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Summon an Avatar:** Use Carbuncle or another Avatar offering useful wards for the current fight. Verify the pet is active before pulling.
2. **Maintain Celestial Sanctuary:** Your active Avatar provides the ongoing party-area regeneration, Refresh and damage-reduction effect. Avoid dropping or dismissing it unnecessarily.
3. **Cast Blood Pact: Ward frequently:** Celestial's Ward recast is sharply shortened. Each eligible Ward triggers **Starlight Mend**, healing your Avatar and nearby party members.
4. **Use a Staff or Club weapon skill when safe:** Eligible weapon skills can trigger an additional Starlight Mend pulse, so melee/TP opportunities become potential healing tools rather than only personal damage.
5. **Activate Celestial Convergence for MP support:** During its short window, Starlight Mend additionally returns **10% maximum MP** to eligible nearby party members, alongside defensive protection.
6. **Use Astral Benediction for emergencies:** **Astral Benediction** is a stronger immediate party heal and cleanse with Reraise support for party PCs; reserve it for dangerous group-wide events.
7. **Recover MP with Elemental Siphon:** The enhanced **Elemental Siphon** restores substantial personal MP and supplies a party heal without needing to dismiss your current Avatar.
8. **Use Astral Flow or Apogee deliberately:** These states further shorten Blood Pact: Ward recasts; plan healing pulses around the boss's most dangerous sequence.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Celestial Sanctuary:** Requires an active Avatar and reaches about **15 yalms**. Its level-based regeneration improves from **12 to 24 flat HP plus 1.5% maximum HP per tick**, Refresh from **4 to 10 MP per tick**, alongside **10% Damage Taken reduction**.
- **Starlight Mend:** Triggered by eligible Blood Pact: Ward and Staff/Club weapon-skill use. Heals the Avatar by **50% maximum HP** and nearby party members by **15% maximum HP**.
- **Blood Pact: Ward recast:** Reduced to **12 seconds** normally, about **6 seconds** with Apogee or **5 seconds** during Astral Flow. Blood Pact: Rage is not included in this shortened Ward schedule.
- **Celestial Convergence:** **25-second recast**, **12-second** protective state, increasing damage mitigation and Magic Evasion; while active, Starlight Mend also restores **10% maximum MP** to nearby allies.
- **Astral Benediction:** **22-second recast**, party recovery equal to about **32% HP**, cleansing of up to three debuffs and Reraise support for qualifying PCs.
- **Elemental Siphon:** Modified to a **20-second recast**; returns **45% maximum MP** and triggers roughly **20% party HP recovery** without forcing Avatar dismissal.
- **Astral Flow protection:** Shortened Ward recast and an additional party Stoneskin effect during its enhanced window.
- **Passive progression:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, Max HP/MP +14/18/22/28%, MND/CHR +12/18/24/32, Cure Potency +18/26/34/42 and improved Refresh and Fast Cast.
- **Role tradeoff:** Celestial's personal and Avatar damage are reduced to emphasize healing and utility rather than offensive summon bursts.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Fighting without an Avatar:** You lose the sanctuary pulse and the most distinctive Ward-triggered healing opportunities.
- **Expecting Blood Pact: Rage to use the Ward recast:** The cooldown transformation applies to **Ward** actions.
- **Forgetting the Staff/Club requirement for weapon-skill healing:** Other weapon families do not trigger the special Starlight Mend interaction.
- **Saving all Wards despite short recasts:** Frequent appropriate Ward use is part of the healing loop, not only an emergency button.
- **Allowing Convergence to expire before healing pulses:** Schedule your Wards or weapon skills during its active duration for MP recovery.
- **Assuming personal damage is the goal:** Prioritize group survival, Avatar uptime and positioning.
