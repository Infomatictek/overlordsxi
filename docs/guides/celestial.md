# Celestial (SMN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Celestial converts Summoner into a **0-perpetuation Avatar Sanctuary healer** (`Healer` tier: -60% damage dealt for both SMN and Avatar). Simply having any Avatar summoned pulses **Celestial Sanctuary** (AoE Regen, Refresh, and -10% DT) across your party, while **12s-cooldown Blood Pact: Wards**, Staff/Club WSs, and `Elemental Siphon` continuously trigger **Starlight Mend** (50% Avatar heal + 15% party heal — plus 10% party MP inside `Celestial Convergence`).

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Companion:** Keep **any Avatar summoned** at all times (`-100 Avatar Perpetuation` makes summons MP-free).
- **Required Weapon:** **Staff or Club in Main Hand.** Non-Staff/Club weapons disable `Starlight Mend` triggers on Weaponskills.
- **Priority Stats:** MND, Cure Potency, Max HP, Max MP, Fast Cast, Summoning/Healing Skill.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Keep an Avatar Out (`Celestial Sanctuary`):** Summon Carbuncle or any Avatar matching needed Wards. Every combat tick automatically applies **Celestial Sanctuary** (15y party: Hybrid Regen `12–24 + 1.5% HP/tick`, Refresh `+4–10 MP/tick`, `-10% DT`).
2. **Spam 12s `Blood Pact: Ward` & Staff/Club WSs (`Starlight Mend`):** Under Celestial, **Blood Pact: Ward** is locked to a flat **12s recast** (`6s` under `Apogee`, `5s` under `Astral Flow`). Every Ward (and every Staff/Club WS) triggers **Starlight Mend**, healing your Avatar for **50% Max HP** and your 15y party for **15% Max HP**!
3. **Turn Heals into Party MP (`Celestial Convergence`):** Activate `Celestial Convergence` (25s CD, 12s: -15% DT & +30 MEVA ward). While Convergence is active on you, every **Starlight Mend** (Wards & Staff/Club WSs) ALSO restores **+10% Max MP** to the party!
4. **Emergency Heals & MP Siphon:**
   - `Astral Benediction` (22s CD): **32% party HP heal + 3-debuff cleanse + Reraise III** on all party PCs!
   - `Elemental Siphon` (20s CD): Restores **+45% of your Max MP WITHOUT dismissing your Avatar** AND triggers a **20% party HP heal**!

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Free Perpetual Wards:** Blood Pact: Ward cooldown drops from 45s to **12s** (6s in Apogee / 5s in Astral Flow). Under `Astral Flow`, Wards also grant a **25% Max HP party Stoneskin**.
- **Safe `Elemental Siphon`:** Keep using `Elemental Siphon` every 20s for free party heals + self MP — your Avatar stays on the field!

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't run around without an Avatar out:** No Avatar = no Celestial Sanctuary Regen/Refresh/DT aura and no Blood Pact: Ward triggers.
- **Note that Blood Pact: Rage does NOT get the 12s CD reduction:** Only **Blood Pact: Ward** abilities drop to 12s/6s/5s recast.
- **Don't forget Staff/Club WSs trigger `Starlight Mend`:** Building TP with Staff/Club lets you weave extra party heals and 10% MP bursts during `Celestial Convergence`.
