# Crimson Duelist (RDM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Crimson Duelist transforms Red Mage into an **empty-offhand fencing Parry/Stoneskin tank** (`Tank` tier: -60% damage dealt, 0.12x WS scaling, +60–120 baseline Enmity). Every enfeeble on the boss both **reduces incoming damage (-5% per enfeeble, up to -25%)** and boosts your **Parry Rate (+3% per enfeeble, up to +15%)**, while Sword Weaponskills make your next `Stoneskin`, `Phalanx`, or Enfeeble **instant-cast and 0 MP**.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapons:** **1H Sword in Main Hand + STRICTLY EMPTY Sub Slot.** Do **NOT** equip a shield, grip, or offhand weapon — equipping anything in Sub (or anything other than a 1H Sword in Main) turns off your Sword/Enfeebling/Enhancing skill bonuses, Parry bonuses, Hex-Mitigation, Crimson Riposte, Crimson Flow, and `Hex Volley`.
- **Priority Stats:** Sword/Enfeebling/Enhancing skill, VIT, INT, MND, DT-, Parry, and Enmity.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Buff Up & Pull:** Keep `Composure` (+30 ACC/MACC, +50% Enfeebling duration), `Phalanx` (30s CD), and `Stoneskin` (15s CD, 25% Max HP barrier) active. Engage with `Hex Volley` (25s CD, 8y: instantly lands **Dia II, Slow II, and Paralysis II** for 120s + heavy threat), instantly giving you 3 enfeebles (-15% damage taken, +9% Parry).
2. **Stack Up to 5 Enfeebles & Parry:** Add 2 more enfeebles (such as `Blind`, `Poison`, `Addle`, `Gravity`, etc.) to cap **Hex-Mitigation** at **-25% enemy damage dealt** and **+15% Parry Rate**. Every parry triggers **Crimson Riposte** (Light/Ice counter-damage, heavy enmity, and **+4% Max MP restore**).
3. **WS-Weave Instant Free Barriers (`Crimson Flow`):** Build 1000 TP and fire **Flat Blade, Vorpal Blade, Swift Blade, Savage Blade, or Sanguine Blade** to trigger **Crimson Flow** (6s window). Spend those 6 seconds casting an **instant-cast, 0-MP `Stoneskin`, `Phalanx`, or Enfeeble** with bonus Volatile Enmity!
4. **Defensive & Panic Buttons:** Pop `Crimson Fleche` (20s CD, 6s: +35% Parry + Knockback Immunity) right before one of those 5 Sword WSs (or an Enfeeble) to instantly trigger a free 25% `Stoneskin` + heavy enmity. Use `Convert` (**30s CD**, never drops below 50% HP and auto-casts full `Stoneskin`) or `Chainspell` (-25% DT + high-enmity spell pulses for 30s) on heavy pulls.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **5-Enfeeble Cap (`Hex-Mitigation`):** Counts distinct enfeebles on the attacker up to 5 (-5% damage taken & +3% Parry per enfeeble).
- **Safe `Convert` (30s CD):** Unlike vanilla Red Mage, `Convert` won't drop your HP below 50% and refreshes your 25% Stoneskin automatically.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **NEVER equip a shield or offhand weapon:** Even a buckler or dagger in your Sub slot silently disables almost the entire Crimson Duelist kit! Keep `Sub` empty.
- **Only specific Sword WSs trigger `Crimson Flow`:** Only **Flat Blade, Vorpal Blade, Swift Blade, Savage Blade, and Sanguine Blade** trigger Crimson Flow and consume `Crimson Fleche` (Fast Blade, Circle Blade, Burning Blade, Red Lotus Blade, Spirits Within, and Requiescat do not).
