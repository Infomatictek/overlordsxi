# Crimson Duelist (RDM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Crimson Duelist is an **empty-offhand Sword tank** whose enfeebles directly improve personal mitigation and Parry. Successful Sword weapon skills create **Crimson Flow**, allowing an instant, free defensive or enfeebling spell. **Crimson Riposte** rewards Parry with elemental retaliation, enmity and MP. Its gameplay alternates between controlling the quarry through several distinct debuffs, maintaining barriers and using Sword attacks to refresh defensive casting opportunities.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Crimson Duelist Soul Crystal or use `!spec set crimsonduelist` from Lv. 10.
- **Strict weapon setup:** Main-hand a **one-handed Sword** and leave **Sub completely empty**. A shield, grip, Dagger or any other Sub item can disable the core tank engine.
- **Stats:** HP, VIT, INT, MND, Sword, Enfeebling and Enhancing skill, Enmity, Parry and Damage Taken reduction.
- **Spell preparation:** Have **Stoneskin**, **Phalanx**, and several different offensive enfeebles ready. Distinct enfeeble categories matter more than repeated applications of the same one.
- **Party role:** Take enemy attention, stand close enough to Parry, and preserve sufficient MP for emergency defensive spells between Flow windows.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare your defenses:** Activate **Composure**, **Stoneskin** and **Phalanx** before sustained tanking. Composure improves your accuracy and enfeebling durations.
2. **Open with Hex Volley:** Target one enemy within 8 yalms and use **Hex Volley** to apply several distinct enfeebles and immediate threat. This quickly starts your Hex-Mitigation.
3. **Complete the Hex setup:** Add different qualifying enfeebles until the enemy holds up to **five distinct effects** for the maximum personal mitigation and Parry boost.
4. **Maintain Sword contact:** Build TP, use eligible Sword weapon skills and watch for **Crimson Flow**. Its short window makes the next permitted Stoneskin, Phalanx or enfeeble instant and free.
5. **Choose the Flow spend wisely:** Refresh a barrier when a strong hit is coming, or replace an expiring enfeeble if the current defenses are healthy.
6. **Use Crimson Fleche reactively:** Activate it ahead of a dangerous strike for a temporary Parry increase, knockback protection, threat and a free barrier tied to an eligible follow-up.
7. **Return resources through Parry:** Genuine Parry can trigger **Crimson Riposte** for elemental counter-damage, enmity and a percentage MP return.
8. **Use emergency RDM tools:** The specialized **Convert** avoids dropping below half HP and renews Stoneskin. **Chainspell** provides additional defensive/offensive pressure during major enemy phases.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Hex-Mitigation:** Each distinct qualifying enfeeble on the current attacker reduces incoming damage by about **5%**, up to **25%** from five, and improves Parry by **3%** each, up to **15%**.
- **Hex Volley:** **25-second recast**, range about **8 yalms**, applies multiple enfeebles together and adds considerable enmity.
- **Crimson Flow:** Eligible Sword weapon skills open an approximately **6-second** opportunity for an instant, zero-MP Stoneskin, Phalanx or qualifying enfeeble.
- **Eligible Sword skills:** The special Flow interaction recognizes **Flat Blade, Vorpal Blade, Swift Blade, Savage Blade and Sanguine Blade**. Other Sword skills do not necessarily create this opportunity.
- **Crimson Riposte:** A qualifying Parry with correct equipment triggers light/ice counter-damage, extra enmity and approximately **4% maximum MP** return.
- **Crimson Fleche:** **20-second recast**, short Parry/knockback window; qualifying actions can create a free protective Stoneskin and more threat.
- **Convert:** Specialized **30-second recast**, with health safety and automatic Stoneskin renewal rather than the normal dangerous HP/MP trade.
- **Level progression:** Core Sword skill, defensive enfeebling, enhancing potency and tank stats increase through Lv. 10/30/50/70. The five-debuff cap remains central.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Equipping any Sub item:** Even a defensive shield can disable the very mechanics the build relies on.
- **Counting multiple casts of the same enfeeble toward five:** The system counts distinct qualifying effects on the attacker.
- **Using an ineligible Sword weapon skill for Flow:** Not every Sword weapon skill opens the instant/free spell window.
- **Spending Flow on a low-value spell:** Choose the barrier or debuff the encounter actually needs before its six-second timer expires.
- **Assuming Parry is automatic:** Build for genuine avoidance; Crimson Riposte depends on a qualifying Parry event.
- **Using Convert without checking the current encounter:** The Soul's Convert is safer, but it remains a cooldown to plan rather than a substitute for regular mitigation.
