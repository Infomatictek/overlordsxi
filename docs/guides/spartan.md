# Spartan (WAR) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Spartan is a **Polearm-and-Buckler frontline tank** that collects **Grit** while receiving and blocking damage, then converts it into **Iron Resolve** barriers with Polearm weapon skills. **Defender** prepares a lighter Brace, while **Phalanx Ward** prepares a stronger response to an imminent boss hit. Successful play means anticipating which hit will consume your Brace and keeping enough Grit for the next barrier, not simply pressing every defensive action on cooldown.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Spartan Soul Crystal or use `!spec set spartan` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Equip a **Polearm** in Main. In Sub use an eligible size-one Buckler, or leave the slot empty for the built-in virtual shield.
- **Shield decision:** A real size-one Buckler improves block chance and allows a higher Iron Resolve cap. Larger shield sizes are not supported by the special loadout.
- **Stats:** HP, VIT, Defense, Block/Shield skill, Enmity and Damage Taken reduction. Polearm accuracy helps land the weapon skills needed to spend Grit defensively.
- **Party role:** Stay close to the boss and maintain threat. Tell healers about your available Brace window and whether Iron Resolve is active.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Enter Defender:** Activate **Defender** for tank-focused Defense, physical damage reduction and Block bonuses without Spartan's usual offensive penalty. It also arms a **Light Brace**.
2. **Establish threat:** Use **Provoke** where available and **Impaling Thrust** for a threatening single-target opener. Impaling Thrust can stun or silence a qualifying enemy action, apply Maim and generate Grit.
3. **Build Grit under attack:** Blocks, qualifying hits and certain abilities generate **Grit** up to five stacks. Watch its indicator rather than overcapping.
4. **Spend with Polearm weapon skills:** When your current **Iron Resolve** barrier is weak or absent, use an eligible Polearm weapon skill to consume Grit and create a 30-second Stoneskin barrier with further enmity and Maim.
5. **Use the Light Brace for an expected hit:** Defender's prepared Brace lasts up to 10 seconds and reduces the **next qualifying hit**; it is not a blanket reduction to everything that follows.
6. **Prepare Heavy Brace with Phalanx Ward:** Spend the required Grit just before a dangerous monster action. The stronger Brace can greatly reduce its next hit, reinforce Grit and provide control or knockback protection.
7. **React to boss tells:** The special sensing message indicates a dangerous action may be building. Time Brace close to the actual hit so a harmless ordinary swing does not consume it.
8. **Recover from spikes:** Use the Soul's modified **Adrenaline Surge**, Provoke, Warcry and defensive buffs to regain health or threat when the fight becomes unstable.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Grit:** Maximum **five stacks**. Builds from qualifying hits or blocks, **Impaling Thrust** (+2 or +4 with the proper stance), Light Brace interaction and special burst preparation.
- **Iron Resolve:** Polearm weapon skills spend accumulated Grit for approximately **4–7% maximum HP** Stoneskin per stack, lasting **30 seconds**; cap is about **35% maximum HP** with virtual shield or **40%** with eligible Buckler.
- **Defender:** Specialized **15-second recast**, stronger Defense, physical protection and Block without the normal Spartan Attack penalty; arms a 10-second Light Brace.
- **Light Brace:** A prepared next-hit response reducing qualifying damage by roughly **25%**, earning a Grit stack and applying the relevant drawback or opening.
- **Phalanx Ward / Heavy Brace:** **35-second recast**, consumes **two Grit**, reduces the next qualifying hit by about **50%**, can maximize Grit on a successful intercept, and adds brief Stun/Silence or knockback response under eligible conditions.
- **Impaling Thrust:** **20-second recast**, single-target control and threat; may interrupt a readied action and apply Maim, providing a faster Grit builder.
- **Adrenaline Surge:** The specialized Provoke/Warcry recovery can heal **20% maximum HP** if you are below 50% HP, with its own **30-second** recovery lock.
- **Level progression:** The Grit-to-Stoneskin percentage and tank bonuses grow across Lv. 10/30/50/70, with Buckler choice modifying maximum barrier capacity.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Equipping a non-Polearm weapon or oversized shield:** This may disable the special Grit, Brace and Iron Resolve toolkit.
- **Using Heavy Brace too early:** The next eligible hit consumes it, even if a more dangerous boss action is just seconds away.
- **Spending Grit over a stronger existing barrier:** Iron Resolve will not necessarily replace a stronger Stoneskin.
- **Hoarding five Grit indefinitely:** Convert it into timely mitigation before the tank-buster, then rebuild.
- **Ignoring the sensed boss tell:** The warning helps time Brace responses, but it does not stop normal hits from consuming them first.
- **Expecting the emergency heal at full health:** Adrenaline Surge has a low-HP condition and a separate recovery timer.
