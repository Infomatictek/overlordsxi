# Spartan (WAR) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Spartan transforms Warrior into a **Polearm + Buckler shield-wall tank** (`Tank` tier: -60% damage dealt, 0.12x WS scaling, +60–120 baseline Enmity). You build **Grit** by taking/blocking hits and using `Impaling Thrust`, spend Grit with Polearm Weaponskills to refresh your **Iron Resolve** damage barrier, and time **Brace** parries/blocks right when a boss winds up a TP move or spell.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon & Shield:** **Polearm in Main + a Size-1 Buckler in Sub** (or keep Sub empty for a built-in virtual Aspis Buckler; equipping a Size-1 Buckler gives +10% Block Rate and raises your Iron Resolve Stoneskin cap from 35% to 40% Max HP). Size 2+ shields are blocked.
- **Subjob / Role:** Focus on VIT, HP, DT-, Block/Shield skill, and Enmity.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Pull & Load Grit:** Open with `Defender` (15s CD w/ Polearm: no Attack penalty, +30% DEF, -10% PDT, +15% Block, arms **Light Brace**) -> `Provoke` / `Impaling Thrust` (20s CD: Stuns 2s or Silences 5s mid-cast, applies Maim -15% ATK/-20 ACC, and grants +2 Grit — or **+4 Grit** if `Defender` or `Mighty Strikes` is active).
2. **Tank & Build Grit:** Blocking or taking hits automatically builds **+1 Grit** (max 5). Watch your chat log for `[Sense] <mob> is coiling for a heavy attack!` (boss >=750 TP or casting).
3. **Spend Grit on Barrier:** Whenever your **Iron Resolve** barrier drops, fire **any Polearm Weaponskill** to consume all Grit (1–5) for a 30s Stoneskin (`4–7%` Max HP per Grit, up to 35%/40% Max HP) + heavy enmity + Maim.
4. **Time Braces for Tank Busters:** Press `Defender` (15s CD) for **Light Brace** (-25% next hit, +1 Grit, Off-Balance) or spend 2 Grit on `Phalanx Ward` (35s CD) for **Heavy Brace** (-50% next hit, maxes Grit to 5, 2s Stun, +4s Silence vs spells, Knockback Immunity, Exposed Guard).

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Grit (0–5):** Resource built via blocking/taking hits (+1), `Defender` Light Brace trigger (+1), `Impaling Thrust` (+2 / +4 in Defender), and `Mighty Strikes` (instant 5). Consumed by Polearm WSs to create Iron Resolve Stoneskin.
- **Brace Window (10s):** Both Light Brace (`Defender`) and Heavy Brace (`Phalanx Ward`) sit ready for 10 seconds and absorb the **very next hit** you take.
- **Adrenaline Surge:** `Provoke` or `Warcry` automatically heals you for 20% Max HP if you're below 50% HP (30s cooldown).

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't swap off Polearm or equip a non-Size-1 shield:** Dropping Polearm disables Defender's 15s CD/bonuses, Grit building, Braces, `Impaling Thrust`, and `Phalanx Ward`.
- **Don't pop `Phalanx Ward` randomly against fast autos when a boss TP move is coming:** Because Brace triggers on the very next hit taken, time `Phalanx Ward` right when the `[Sense]` warning appears or during cast bars.
- **Don't burn Grit on a WS while a stronger Iron Resolve barrier is still up:** Iron Resolve will not overwrite a stronger Stoneskin, so save your WS until your shield cracks.
