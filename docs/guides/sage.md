# Sage (SCH) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Sage turns Scholar into a **Titania-assisted Dark Arts elemental nuker and Helix master** (`Damage` tier: 0% damage penalty, 1.00x WS scaling, +30 MAB, +10% MEVA penetration). `Addendum: Black` lasts **2 hours** and keeps **Ebullience permanently active**, your **Helix DoTs deal +50% damage and last 2 hours**, and your Dark Pixie companion **Titania** enfeebles targets, Drains/Aspirs, and nukes their weakest element alongside you!

## Setup & Gear
- **Recommended Companion Item:** Equip **Orphic Egg (#18256)** in your **Ammo** slot to grant Titania level-scaling MP Refresh.
- **Weapon / Armor:** Standard Elemental Staff/Club + INT/MAB/MACC/Elemental Skill/Magic Burst gear.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Summon Titania & Activate Black Arts:** Cast `Call Titania` (Ability #289, 300s CD -> summons Dark Pixie #129) and turn on `Addendum: Black` (**2-hour duration** + automatically enables **permanent 2-hour Ebullience**!).
2. **Apply a 2-Hour Supercharged Helix:** Cast a matching elemental **Helix** on the boss — it deals **+50% merit-boosted damage** and lasts **2 hours (7200s)** unless overwritten, while targeting the mob updates Titania's lock-on target (`OX_TITANIA_TARGET`).
3. **Nuke & Magic Burst with Permanent Ebullience:** Every Black Magic nuke automatically benefits from permanent Ebullience, `+30 MAB`, and `10% MEVA bypass` — spend Stratagems on `Alacrity`, `Parsimony`, or `Manifestation` instead!
4. **Coordinate with Titania:** Keep within **20 yalms** of your target. Titania automatically casts **Dispel, Bio II, Slow, Paralyze, Blind, Poison II, Addle**, sustains herself via **Aspir/Drain**, and nukes with **whichever Tier I–V element the enemy is weakest against** (`!eos heal` / `!eos dismiss`).

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Auto-Lock Target Tracking:** Casting any spell or targeting a mob focuses Titania onto that enemy even if you never swing your staff in melee.
- **Permanent Ebullience + 2hr Helix:** Both `Addendum: Black` and every Black Magic cast keep Ebullience active automatically for 2 hours.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't manually burn Stratagem charges pressing `Ebullience`:** It is already permanently active (re-pressing just refreshes it or reports already active). Save Stratagems for `Alacrity`, `Parsimony`, `Focalization`, or `Equanimity`.
- **Keep Orphic Egg equipped** so Titania doesn't run dry while chaining Tier IV/V nukes.
