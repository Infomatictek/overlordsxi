# Sage (SCH) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Sage is a **Scholar elemental damage Soul** supported by the dark faerie **Titania**. Long-lasting **Dark Arts**, **Addendum: Black** and **Ebullience** enable sustained elemental spell pressure, while strong **Helix** damage rewards keeping a suitable effect on the current enemy. Titania contributes enfeebles, elemental attacks and resource sustain when it has an appropriate target. You remain responsible for your own spell selection, positioning and MP management.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Sage Soul Crystal or use `!spec set sage` at Lv. 10.
- **Equipment:** Staff or Club, elemental skill, INT, Magic Attack Bonus, Magic Accuracy, Magic Burst gear and enough MP for repeated nukes. **Orphic Egg** in the ammo slot can support faerie MP where available.
- **Companion:** Summon **Titania** through its faerie ability. A pet cannot be summoned where pet use is prohibited or another pet is already active.
- **Role:** Pure offensive spellcasting with a companion. Let the tank provide stable enemy positioning and maintain a clear casting lane.
- **Targeting:** The Sage spell hooks can update Titania's preferred quarry when you target an enemy with magic. Avoid rapidly switching targets during a planned burst.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare Dark Arts and Titania:** Summon Titania when available and enable **Addendum: Black** before an important encounter.
2. **Keep Ebullience active:** Sage extends Addendum: Black and automatically maintains a long-lasting **Ebullience** effect. You do not need to spend repeated Stratagem charges on the ordinary Ebullience action.
3. **Choose the enemy's element:** Identify an elemental weakness using experience, resistance results or relevant party information. Select direct magic and Helix spells accordingly.
4. **Apply a matching Helix:** Sage strengthens eligible **Helix** damage and extends the duration of its own eligible Helix effect, allowing an extended source of single-target pressure.
5. **Cast and Magic Burst:** Use direct black magic during safe cast windows. Sage receives additional magic attack support and partial elemental Magic Evasion bypass against qualifying enemies.
6. **Coordinate Titania:** Keep the companion able to reach the target. Titania can perform enfeebling and elemental actions autonomously, but its results still depend on range, MP and enemy resistance.
7. **Spend other Stratagems intelligently:** With automatic Ebullience handled, reserve available charges for casting-time, MP-efficiency or targeting adjustments you actually need.
8. **Maintain a reliable phase plan:** If the enemy's resistances or target change, reapply your matching spell setup and verify Titania is acting against the intended quarry.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Addendum: Black:** Sage extends the effect's duration to **7,200 seconds**, giving long-lasting access to the relevant spell setup.
- **Ebullience:** The Sage-specific hook automatically keeps it active; qualifying black magic receives an additional multiplier that includes Ebullience's normal potency contribution.
- **Helix scaling:** Eligible Helix spells receive a **1.5×** modifier to their Helix merit damage calculation. The special duration handling can hold eligible Helix effects for up to **7,200 seconds**.
- **Extra elemental damage support:** Sage adds **30 Magic Attack Bonus** through its magic-damage calculation and translates **10% of the target's relevant Magic Evasion** into a Magic Accuracy bypass bonus for qualifying elemental magic.
- **Titania:** A real faerie companion with independent ability, spell, HP and MP behavior. It can coordinate around the enemy Sage is targeting.
- **Companion resource needs:** Keep Titania supplied and safe, particularly during extended caster-heavy fights. Companion support equipment can improve long-term reliability.
- **Level progression:** Your accessible elemental spell ranks, Helix choices, passives and available gear change through Lv. 10/30/50/75. The core long-duration Sage spell overrides do not depend on weapon-skill talent points.
- **Magic Burst coordination:** Time appropriate direct elemental spells to the party's skillchain windows. Long Helix duration does not eliminate the benefit of well-placed active nukes.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Spending repeated charges on Ebullience:** Sage automatically maintains the enhanced state; use available Stratagems for actions it does not duplicate.
- **Casting mismatched elements into strong resistance:** The spell damage bonus does not guarantee a hit against poor elemental matchups.
- **Ignoring Titania's positioning and MP:** The faerie is an autonomous companion, not an infinite-range or infinite-resource damage aura.
- **Assuming extended Helix duration prevents overwriting:** An existing effect can still be replaced or removed by encounter interactions.
- **Forgetting Addendum: Black:** Keep the underlying caster stance active to access the intended magic toolkit.
- **Using only the pet:** Sage remains the primary magic damage dealer. Titania helps, but your own casting decisions determine much of the result.
