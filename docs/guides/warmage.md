# Warmage (BLU) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Warmage is a Tier 1 **single-target Blue Magic damage** Soul that uses **Assess Prey** to identify the damage categories a particular quarry resists least. Your monster-spell kit covers blunt, slashing and piercing physical damage from Lv. 10, then gains elemental options later. Choosing a correctly matched spell during the eight-second assessment window grants extra damage after resistance checks; the goal is adaptable offense, not a fixed one-button rotation.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Warmage Soul Crystal or use `!spec set warmage` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Sword and Club are its eligible weapon-skill families. Ordinary equipment, any subjob and zero weapon-skill talents are sufficient for the starting magic loop.
- **Spell availability:** Warmage automatically supplies its own Soul spell kit as you level. Your previous learned and set Blue Magic lists are preserved when you leave the Soul.
- **Stats:** Accuracy and Magic Accuracy both support the split physical/magical identity; INT and Magic Attack Bonus become more important as elemental spells unlock.
- **Combat distance:** Assess Prey requires a living engaged enemy within 15 yalms. Some later elemental techniques have their own reach limits, so check positioning before spending MP.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Engage the intended quarry:** Target one living enemy and approach within 15 yalms.
2. **Assess its defenses:** Use **Assess Prey**. The HUD shows which of the available damage categories—blunt, slashing, piercing or eventually elemental—currently have the best relative damage result. Tied best categories all qualify.
3. **Choose the matching starter spell:** At Lv. 10 use **Briar Hammer** for blunt damage, **Razor Heel** for slashing, or **Needle Lunge** for piercing.
4. **Cast during the eight-second window:** Direct matching Blue Magic gains the assessment damage bonus, determined by level. Use more than one appropriate spell if their recasts and your MP allow.
5. **Reassess deliberately:** Assess Prey has a **12-second recast** and its effect is bound to the examined target. If you change enemies or the boss changes defenses, reassess before selecting your next technique.
6. **Expand physical options at Lv. 30:** **Skullbreaker Volley** gives three blunt hits, while **Iron Cleave** provides a heavier slashing attack.
7. **Add elemental play at Lv. 50:** **Gale Knuckle** and **Serrated Bite** add stronger physical techniques; **Ember Charge** introduces a single-target elemental fire option.
8. **Use the finishers at Lv. 70:** **Guillotine Shears** provides heavy slashing damage, while **Furnace Breath** (fire) and **Storm Gaze** (lightning) offer additional elemental answers.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Lv. 10–29:** Match bonus **12%**; Briar Hammer (blunt, 6 MP / 7s), Razor Heel (slashing, 5 MP / 7s), Needle Lunge (piercing, 5 MP / 7s).
- **Lv. 30–49:** Match bonus **15%**; Skullbreaker Volley (three blunt hits, 16 MP / 12s) and Iron Cleave (slashing, 24 MP / 13s) unlock.
- **Lv. 50–69:** Match bonus **18%**; Gale Knuckle (three blunt hits, 28 MP / 16s), Serrated Bite (slashing, 25 MP / 17s), Ember Charge (elemental fire, 26 MP / 16s) unlock.
- **Lv. 70–75:** Match bonus **20%**; Guillotine Shears (slashing, 32 MP / 20s), Furnace Breath (elemental fire, 36 MP / 20s), Storm Gaze (elemental lightning, 30 MP / 18s) unlock.
- **Assess Prey:** Eight-second target-bound analysis window, 12-second ability recast and a maximum assessment distance of 15 yalms.
- **Categories and ties:** At early levels, only the three physical damage families are compared. From Lv. 50, elemental fire joins; Lv. 70 also considers lightning. Ties can yield more than one valid choice.
- **Damage rule:** The matching bonus applies to eligible direct Blue Magic damage **after normal resistance**. Regular weapon attacks and weapon skills are not granted this assessment bonus merely by matching a physical type.
- **Automatic Soul kit:** The Soul-supplied monster techniques do not require manually rebuilding your saved Blue Magic set; leaving Warmage restores the preserved set.
- **Passive progression:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, Warmage gains Accuracy +10/18/28/38, Magic Accuracy +10/18/28/38, Magic Attack Bonus +8/14/22/30 and INT +6/12/18/24.
- **Soul HUD:** Shows which damage-category matches are active for the current quarry, the eight-second window and Assess Prey's recast.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Casting your habitual spell without checking the matchup:** Assess Prey rewards the least-resisted category, not whichever monster spell has the highest listed power.
- **Assuming the buff transfers between enemies:** The eight-second assessment applies only to the examined quarry.
- **Trying elemental matching before Lv. 50:** Warmage begins with blunt, slashing and piercing categories; elemental choices arrive later.
- **Mistaking a tied result for an error:** Multiple best categories can all qualify.
- **Expecting ordinary Sword or Club attacks to receive the bonus:** Only matching offensive Blue Magic can use it.
- **Ignoring MP efficiency and reach:** A cheaper matching spell can outperform an expensive choice you cannot sustain or land.
