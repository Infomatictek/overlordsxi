# Warmage (BLU) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Adaptive monster-magic damage.** Warmage reads the opponent's physical and elemental vulnerabilities and casts the right Blue Magic category.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Warmage for offense that adapts to resistance profiles, instead of Spellblade's rune-driven sword magic.

## Setup & Gear
- Available Blue Magic types matter; starter direct spells include Sprout Smack (blunt) and Power Attack (piercing).
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Use Assess Prey to inspect the target's least-resistant attack categories.
2. **Act:** Choose a direct offensive Blue spell whose type matches one of the identified openings.
3. **Adapt:** Repeat the assessment as the target or encounter changes; avoid indiscriminate area pulls.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Assess Prey has a 12s cooldown and an 8s window; matching direct offensive Blue Magic gains 12/15/18/20% post-resist damage.
- Tied lowest-resistance categories are valid. Some formerly AoE monster spells, such as Bomb Toss, are converted into single-target actions for this Soul.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't assume every monster spell is a perfect match or that Weapon Skills gain Azure Bastion's free-cast engine.
- Base-job access does not guarantee every spell or ability is approved for this Soul.
