# Warmage (BLU) - Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Warmage is a Tier 1 Blue Magic damage Soul. Use **Assess Prey** to read your engaged enemy, then choose a monster spell that matches its least-resistant damage category.

## Setup & Gear

- Equip the crystal or use `!spec set warmage` at Lv. 10. Sword and Club are the eligible weapon-skill families.
- Warmage automatically supplies its Soul spells as you level. Your regular Blue Magic learned and set lists are preserved. You can use the starting rotation with ordinary gear, any subjob, and zero weapon skill talents.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Engage your target and use **Assess Prey** within 15 yalms. The HUD shows all tied matches from the categories you can cast.
2. At Lv. 10, choose **Briar Hammer** for blunt, **Razor Heel** for slashing, or **Needle Lunge** for piercing.
3. Cast matching spells during the eight-second window. Assess Prey has a 12-second recast. Reassess after a defense change or target swap.
4. Add stronger techniques as they unlock. **Ember Charge** opens the elemental choice at Lv. 50. **Furnace Breath** and **Storm Gaze** expand it at Lv. 70.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

| Level | New Soul spells | Matching bonus |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 10 | Briar Hammer, Razor Heel, Needle Lunge | 12% |
| 30 | Skullbreaker Volley, Iron Cleave | 15% |
| 50 | Gale Knuckle, Serrated Bite, Ember Charge | 18% |
| 70–75 | Guillotine Shears, Furnace Breath, Storm Gaze | 20% |

- The bonus applies to matching offensive Blue Magic after resistance. Spells use MP and target one enemy.
- When multiple categories tie, you can use any of the matches. An assessed enemy holds its own eight-second window, shown while you target it.

## Common Mistakes

- Use the spell's listed damage category. Sword and club attacks do not receive the assessment bonus.
- Reassess after targeting a different enemy. The benefit belongs to the enemy you examined.
- Consider MP and range alongside the HUD. A cheaper matching spell is useful when conserving MP.
