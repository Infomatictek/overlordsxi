# Scarlet Weaver (RDM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Scarlet Weaver is a Tier 1 **single-target damage** Soul that chooses between two offensive forms: **Yin** for elemental spell damage and **Yang** for ordinary main-hand Sword hits. You start in Yang and switch with **Spellweave** or **Bladeweave** when the fight rewards a different kind of offense. Switching during combat creates a short recovery where neither form receives its special damage bonus, so good timing matters more than changing constantly.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Scarlet Weaver Soul Crystal or use `!spec set scarletweaver` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapon:** Equip a **Sword** to capitalize on Yang's main-hand attack bonus. Sword weapon skills keep their ordinary unlock, TP and talent rules; they do not gain the form bonus simply for being weapon skills.
- **Stats:** Use melee Accuracy and DEX for Yang, or INT, Magic Accuracy and Magic Attack Bonus for Yin. A hybrid setup can trade peak output for flexibility between modes.
- **Form commitment:** Choose the likely dominant form before the pull. Out-of-combat switching is unrestricted by the in-combat 12-second shared timer.
- **Role:** Scarlet Weaver is a damage specialist, not a replacement for the party's dedicated healer or tank.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Start in Yang:** Scarlet Weaver initializes in **Yang** if no form has been chosen. Stay in range and deal sustained damage through regular main-hand Sword hits when you can safely connect.
2. **Move to Yin for casting:** Use **Spellweave** to enter **Yin** before a suitable spell window. At Lv. 10 your choices are **Searing Needle**, **Rime Stitch**, **Gale Needle**, **Copper Stitch**, **Storm Needle** and **Tide Stitch**.
3. **Allow the switch recovery:** Changing forms in combat imposes **2 seconds** with no Yin/Yang damage bonus and starts the **12-second shared form-switch recast**. Do not expect immediate bonus damage on the switch frame.
4. **Cast the appropriate element:** Yin boosts eligible single-target elemental spells. Each starter Needle/Stitch spell has a 1.5-second cast and a 3-second recast, giving you choices by element.
5. **Return to Yang only for a real melee opening:** Use **Bladeweave** when you can remain in Sword range. Switching repeatedly without time to use the new form wastes both recovery and cooldown.
6. **Add Scarlet Spindle at Lv. 30:** This stronger single-target flame weave has a 2-second cast and 6-second recast.
7. **Add Violet Shuttle at Lv. 50:** This stronger lightning weave has a 2.4-second cast and 7-second recast. Yin/Yang potency also improves as levels rise.
8. **Add Tempest Loom at Lv. 70:** This more committed wind weave has a 3-second cast and 12-second recast. Save it for a reliable cast window and use the Soul HUD to verify the form bonus is active.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Yin:** Increases damage from Scarlet Weaver's eligible elemental spell kit when the 2-second post-switch recovery has ended.
- **Yang:** Increases ordinary **main-hand Sword attack** damage. It is not a generic multiplier on every attack source.
- **Level 10–29:** Form bonus **15%**. Six starter elemental spells are available.
- **Level 30–49:** Form bonus **18%**. Scarlet Spindle unlocks.
- **Level 50–69:** Form bonus **22%**. Violet Shuttle unlocks.
- **Level 70–75:** Form bonus **25%**. Tempest Loom unlocks.
- **Combat switching:** Both form abilities share a **12-second recast** in combat and impose **2 seconds of offensive recovery**. When safely out of combat, form changes do not apply that recovery.
- **Spell progression:** Starter elemental spells (Lv. 10) each cast in 1.5 seconds, recast in 3 seconds. Scarlet Spindle (Lv. 30) casts in 2 seconds / 6-second recast; Violet Shuttle (Lv. 50) in 2.4 / 7; Tempest Loom (Lv. 70) in 3 / 12.
- **Passive scaling:** Sword skill +10/18/28/40, Accuracy +10/18/28/38, DEX +6/10/15/20, INT +6/12/18/25 and Magic Attack Bonus +8/14/22/30 at Lv. 10/30/50/70.
- **Soul HUD:** Current form, damage-bonus percentage, shared switch recast and remaining 2-second recovery window.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Switching forms after every action:** The 12-second shared cooldown and 2-second recovery penalize reactive over-switching.
- **Assuming both bonuses stack:** Only the currently selected form applies, and only to the qualifying damage type.
- **Expecting Sword weapon skills or off-hand hits to receive Yang's main-hand bonus:** Keep those attacks on their normal damage rules.
- **Casting immediately after switching into Yin:** The form bonus is suppressed during the 2-second recovery.
- **Standing in dangerous melee just to maintain Yang:** Switch to Yin when range and safety make casting more reliable.
- **Treating Scarlet Weaver as party utility:** Its special form kit is dedicated to offense. Let teammates handle tanking and sustained healing.
