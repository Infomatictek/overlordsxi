# Scarlet Weaver (RDM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Yin/Yang sword-and-spell DPS.** Scarlet Weaver chooses a sword-centered Yang mode or an elemental-casting Yin mode; switching carries real combat downtime.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Scarlet Weaver for conscious stance choice between direct melee and low-tier magic, unlike RUN Spellblade's rune conduit.

## Setup & Gear
- Use a Sword and the approved Tier I–III elemental spells; optimize around your current mode.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Begin in Yang, which favors main-hand Sword attacks, or switch to Yin out of combat for spell damage.
2. **Act:** Time your damage around the active stance rather than trying to make every hit and every spell simultaneously optimal.
3. **Adapt:** Change stance when the enemy or encounter demands it; expect a shared recast and recovery if switching mid-fight.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Yin grants 15/18/22/25% damage to selected Tier I–III elemental spells; Yang grants that bonus to main-hand Sword melee.
- Switching outside combat is free, while in combat it incurs 12s shared recast and 2s recovery. Enspells, WS and skillchains do not gain either multiplier.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Do not expect a stance to buff all attacks; that would erase the trade-off.
- Base-job access does not guarantee every spell or ability is approved for this Soul.
