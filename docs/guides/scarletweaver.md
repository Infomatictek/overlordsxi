# Scarlet Weaver (RDM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Scarlet Weaver is a Tier-1 damage Soul. Choose one offensive form at a time. Yin rewards safe single-target casting windows. Yang rewards sustained contact with a Sword.

## Setup & Gear

- Select at Lv.10 with `!spec set scarletweaver` or Crystal Socket. Equip a Sword and prioritize accuracy for Yang or INT and magic accuracy for Yin.
- The Sword WS tree uses its normal progression. Both forms work at Lv.10 with zero talent points.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Start in **Yang** and deal damage through regular main-hand Sword swings.
2. Use **Spellweave** to enter **Yin** when magic has the better opening. Start with Searing Needle, Rime Stitch, Gale Needle, Copper Stitch, Storm Needle, or Tide Stitch.
3. Use **Bladeweave** to return to Yang when your Sword can connect reliably. In combat, each change starts a shared 12-second recast and a two-second period without a form bonus.
4. At Lv.30, 50 and 70, the bonus rises to 18%, 22% and 25%. Scarlet Spindle, Violet Shuttle and Tempest Loom unlock at those levels.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- Yin increases eligible elemental spell damage. Yang increases normal main-hand Sword hits. The forms do not stack.
- Your HUD shows the current form, its bonus, recovery and shared recast.

## Common Mistakes

- Change form for an encounter opening that will last long enough to use it. Repeated changes create recovery downtime.
- Weapon skills, skillchains, extra attacks and off-hand swings use their ordinary rules.
- Leave healing and survival windows to teammates. Scarlet Weaver's kit uses both forms for offense.
