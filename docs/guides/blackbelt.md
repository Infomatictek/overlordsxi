# Black Belt (MNK) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Black Belt is Monk's pure **Hand-to-Hand martial arts DPS** spec (`Damage` tier: 0% damage penalty, 1.00x WS scaling). It amps up Kick Attacks, Double Attack, Critical Hits, and Attack Speed, and refunds **+75 TP** after every Hand-to-Hand Weaponskill so you can rapidly chain WSs.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon:** **Hand-to-Hand weapons (or bare fists) only.** Putting any non-H2H weapon (Staff, Club, etc.) in Main or Sub causes the spec to **automatically unequip it**.
- **Priority Stats:** STR, DEX, Attack, Haste, Double Attack, Kick Attacks, Crit Rate/Damage.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Activate Combat Stances:** Keep `Focus` (+10% Crit Rate, +15% Crit DMG, +25 ACC), `Dodge` (+10% Ability Haste, +15 Subtle Blow), and `Footwork` (+15 Kick DMG, +10% Kick Rate, +10% Attack%) rolling.
2. **Debuff & Open:** Use `Chi Blast` for +35% Breath damage, **-15% Defense Down** (20s), and an instant **+100 TP**, then tap `Boost` (**+100 TP**, +8% Crit Rate, +15% Weaponskill Damage on your next WS).
3. **WS Chaining:** Auto-attack at high speed and drop H2H Weaponskills — every WS immediately refunds **+75 TP** (`Combo Momentum`). Always weave `Boost` right before a WS whenever its cooldown is up.
4. **Burst & Cleanse:** Pop `Hundred Fists` (+25% Attack%, +15% Crit, +20% DA, +20 Store TP) for burst phases, and use `Chakra` for an emergency heal + **+150 TP** + Paralysis/Blindness/Weight cleanse.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Offensive `Dodge` & `Chakra`:** Unlike other specs, `Dodge` gives +10% Ability Haste and `Chakra` injects +150 TP while removing accuracy/speed debuffs.
- **Guaranteed Post-WS Refund:** Every landed H2H/unarmed WS starts you back at 75+ TP minimum.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't try to equip a Staff or Shield:** The spec's weapon gate will force-unequip any non-H2H weapon every combat tick.
- **Don't waste `Boost` on auto-attacks:** Keep `Boost`'s +15% WSD & +8% Crit charge for right before your Weaponskill.
