# Black Belt (MNK) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Black Belt is a Tier 1 **Hand-to-Hand burst DPS** Soul. It boosts Kick Attacks, critical damage, attack speed and weapon-skill throughput while providing **Combo Momentum**, a TP refund after eligible Hand-to-Hand weapon skills. Your core rhythm is to maintain offensive Monk buffs, use **Boost** before a decisive weapon skill, and turn sustained punches and kicks into more frequent weapon-skill opportunities.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Black Belt Soul Crystal or use `!spec set blackbelt` from Lv. 10.
- **Strict weapon restriction:** Use **Hand-to-Hand weapons or bare fists only**. Non-Hand-to-Hand weapons placed in Main or Sub are automatically unequipped. A Staff, Club or unrelated off-hand setup will not remain equipped.
- **Stats:** Prioritize Accuracy, STR, DEX, Attack, Haste, Double Attack, Kick Attack Rate/Damage, Critical Hit and Weapon Skill Damage.
- **Role:** This is a pure offensive melee specialization. Work with your tank and healer to stay in melee while avoiding lethal boss spikes.
- **Weapon-skill choice:** Eligible Hand-to-Hand weapon skills keep their normal unlock and TP rules. The +75 TP refund supports sustained damage but does not replace landing accurate attacks.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Enter melee with Hand-to-Hand:** Equip your valid weapon and attack the party's single-target quarry.
2. **Keep offensive stances active:** **Focus** enhances accuracy and critical performance; **Dodge** adds ability haste and Subtle Blow for this specialization; **Footwork** increases Kick Attack rate, damage and Attack.
3. **Build TP efficiently:** High Kick Attack rate, Double Attack and Store TP help generate frequent weapon-skill windows during normal combat.
4. **Use Boost before a weapon skill:** **Boost** adds immediate TP and enhances critical chance and damage for the next weapon skill. Plan to spend the benefit on a landed attack instead of an ordinary swing.
5. **Use Chi Blast to open pressure:** **Chi Blast** provides an offensive hit, a Defense Down effect and immediate TP. It can help prepare the enemy for a follow-up weapon skill.
6. **Spend on Hand-to-Hand weapon skills:** **Combo Momentum** returns **75 TP** after a qualifying landed Hand-to-Hand weapon skill, accelerating the next cycle. Continue attacking rather than waiting for a large single burst.
7. **Use Hundred Fists for major windows:** This adds further Attack, critical chance, Double Attack and Store TP; align it with a stable boss phase where uninterrupted melee is possible.
8. **Recover with Chakra when necessary:** Black Belt's **Chakra** adds emergency healing, TP and a limited cleanse against Paralysis, Blindness and Weight. Use it to maintain damage uptime rather than imagining it replaces a healer.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Combo Momentum:** Eligible landed Hand-to-Hand weapon skills refund **75 TP**. A missed or ineligible action should not be treated as successful momentum.
- **Focus enhancement:** Adds **+10% critical hit rate**, **+15% critical damage** and **+25 Accuracy**.
- **Dodge enhancement:** Grants **+10% ability haste** and **+15 Subtle Blow**.
- **Footwork enhancement:** Adds **+15 Kick Damage**, **+10 Kick Attack Rate** and **+10% Attack**.
- **Boost enhancement:** Adds **100 TP**, **+8% Critical Hit Rate** and **+15% weapon-skill damage** to the prepared weapon-skill opportunity.
- **Chi Blast enhancement:** Offers additional offensive damage, Defense Down for a short window and **+100 TP**.
- **Chakra enhancement:** Improves recovery, adds **150 TP** and removes qualifying movement/accuracy debuffs.
- **Hundred Fists enhancement:** Adds **+25% Attack**, **+15% critical chance**, **+20 Double Attack** and **+20 Store TP** during the burst.
- **Level progression:** Black Belt's passives increase through Lv. 10/30/50/70. Hand-to-Hand skill, Accuracy, Attack, Kick Attack Rate/Damage, Critical Rate, Store TP, Subtle Blow and all-hit weapon-skill damage all scale upward with level.
- **Weapon enforcement:** The Soul's combat setup automatically removes non-Hand-to-Hand main/sub weapons. Recheck equipment after a forced unequip or job/Soul change.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Equipping a Staff, Club or Shield:** Black Belt is restricted to Hand-to-Hand or bare fists; invalid equipment is removed.
- **Pressing Boost without a weapon-skill plan:** Save its bonus for a qualifying weapon skill rather than wasting the timing.
- **Expecting Combo Momentum from every TP action:** The mechanic requires an eligible landed Hand-to-Hand weapon skill.
- **Treating Dodge like an ordinary purely defensive stance:** Black Belt's version also increases ability haste and TP control.
- **Using Hundred Fists into a movement or invulnerability phase:** Its best value comes from sustained contact and successful attacks.
- **Confusing emergency Chakra with a healing specialization:** It helps maintain personal uptime; party healing remains the dedicated healer's responsibility.
