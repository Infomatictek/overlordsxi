# Alpha Warden (BST) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Alpha Warden is a **Beastmaster tank who fights in tandem with a Guardian Beast**. Your beast intercepts part of the damage you receive through **Soul-Link**, while the pet's actions feed enmity back to you. One-handed Axe weapon skills heal the beast and reinforce threat. The loop is not about sending your pet to tank by itself: **you** hold the target while the Guardian shares punishment, provides control and helps you survive.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Alpha Warden Soul Crystal or use `!spec set alphawarden` at Lv. 10.
- **Strict loadout:** Equip a **one-handed Axe in Main and nothing in Sub**—no shield, grip or offhand weapon. Your Guardian mechanics are disabled by other combinations.
- **Guardian selection:** **Bestial Loyalty** cycles your pet choice out of combat, with four zero-jug Guardians: **CourierCarrie** (crab), **PanzerGalahad** (beetle), **CrudeRaphie** (tortoise), or **SaberSiravarde** (tiger).
- **Items:** Avoid unapproved jug items in Ammo. The Guardian summons use the Soul's special zero-jug rules.
- **Stats:** HP, VIT, Defense, Enmity, Guard/Parry and Damage Taken reduction; keep sufficient Axe accuracy to land the weapon skills that help heal your beast.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose the right Guardian:** Use **Bestial Loyalty** out of combat to cycle beasts. Favor crab or tortoise for mitigation, beetle for Guard/Parry and control, or tiger for offensive control and TP.
2. **Summon before engaging:** Use **Call Beast** to bring your chosen Guardian, which scales with your level. Keep it active for the damage-sharing engine.
3. **Establish enmity:** Use **Feral Roar** to generate threat and build a Ready charge. **Snarl** can reinforce the master's enmity when enemy attention shifts.
4. **Maintain Soul-Link:** As the tank, accept direct damage knowing the Guardian receives a share. Watch **both HP bars**; a low-health beast can no longer help intercept reliably.
5. **Use Ready for the encounter:** Spend Ready charges on the Guardian's defense, threat or debuff actions. Its completed commands can grant you TP and threat.
6. **Heal the Guardian with Axe weapon skills:** Land eligible one-handed Axe weapon skills to restore a percentage of pet maximum HP and apply additional enemy pressure.
7. **Use Reward and Soul Bond proactively:** **Reward** heals and cleanses master and pet; **Soul Bond** equalizes their HP percentages and grants short protection. Do not wait until the Guardian is beyond recovery.
8. **Plan Familiar for emergencies:** The enhanced **Familiar** supplies pet survival and Ready charges. Its value is highest when danger is expected and both combatants can exploit the defensive window.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Soul-Link:** Up to **50% of eligible incoming master damage** is redirected to the active Guardian. The link pauses if the pet falls below roughly **15% HP**.
- **Enmity routing:** Special pet melee and Ready threat is transferred toward the master to maintain your role as tank.
- **Guardian protection:** Guardians have specialized damage reduction and defenses against qualifying AoE, conal, magic and breath effects. This does not make the master immune.
- **Guardian access:** Four beasts can be selected using **Bestial Loyalty**, whose choice cycle is designed for out-of-combat use.
- **Feral Roar:** An approximately **25-second recast** threat/control action that can supply a Ready charge.
- **Axe healing:** Eligible one-handed Axe weapon skills restore approximately **15–25% pet maximum HP** according to level.
- **Cure mirroring:** Qualifying Cure, Waltz and Regen healing on the master can mirror part of effective and overheal recovery to the active Guardian.
- **Reward / Soul Bond:** Short-cooldown team maintenance; Reward offers dual healing, cleanse and Regen, while Soul Bond balances HP and gives Stoneskin.
- **Level progression:** Guardian HP, master enmity, tank mitigation and Axe weapon-skill recovery scale through Lv. 10/30/50/70.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Putting anything in Sub:** Even a seemingly helpful shield disables the most important Alpha Warden mechanics.
- **Using an unrelated jug:** The special Guardian roster expects the correct zero-jug summon setup.
- **Letting Guardian HP collapse:** The link pauses at low pet HP; keep both combatants healthy.
- **Treating the Guardian as an independent tank:** The core threat routing supports the **master** as the frontline tank.
- **Using pet commands without understanding their role:** Pick the Guardian's defensive or control action that matches the present boss threat.
- **Forgetting your Axe weapon skills:** They are a recurring means of pet healing and threat, not only personal damage.
