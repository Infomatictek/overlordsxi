# Alpha Warden (BST) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Alpha Warden turns Beastmaster into a **0-jug, single-Axe symbiotic pet tank** (`Tank` tier: -60% damage dealt for both Master and Pet, +60–120 Enmity, 0.25x pet Ready damage). An always-on **50% Soul-Link** splits half the damage you take onto your AoE/Magic/Breath-immune Guardian Beast, while 100% of your pet's melee & Ready enmity funnels back onto you.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapons:** **1H Axe in Main Hand + STRICTLY EMPTY Sub Slot.** Do **NOT** equip a shield or offhand weapon — anything in Sub disables Guard, Parry/Guard mods, Axe WS pet healing, and `Feral Roar`.
- **0 Jugs Needed:** Keep your Ammo slot empty or clear of non-Guardian jugs. Use `Bestial Loyalty` (5s CD, out of combat) to cycle between 4 zero-cost Tank Guardian Beasts summoned at 100% of your level:
  - **CourierCarrie (Crab):** Physical/Magic defense & Stoneskin + AoE Cripple.
  - **PanzerGalahad (Beetle):** Parry/Guard/MEVA + Stun + AoE Slow & Expose.
  - **CrudeRaphie (Tortoise):** -15% DT & Knockback Immunity + Stun & Breach.
  - **SaberSiravarde (Tiger):** AoE Paralyze/Cripple + AoE Threat/+150 Master TP + Heal & Breach.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Pick & Call Your Beast:** Cycle your beast out of combat with `Bestial Loyalty` (5s CD), then summon it with `Call Beast` (15s CD, usable even in combat; auto-summons on socket if idle).
2. **Pull & Establish Aggro:** Open with `Feral Roar` (25s CD, 8y AoE: massive CE/VE, 2s Stun, 20% Slow, **+1 Ready charge**) or `Snarl` (15s CD: dumps massive CE/VE onto Master).
3. **Weave `Ready` Skills & 1H Axe WSs:** Use `Ready` (15s CD) for pet defense/debuffs — every completed Ready grants **+100 TP and huge Master enmity**. Spend TP on **any 1H Axe WS** (empty Sub) to heal your pet for `15–25%` Max HP, generate Master threat, and inflict `Cripple`.
4. **Sync HP & Mitigate (`Soul Bond` / `Reward` / `Familiar`):** Your pet intercepts 50% of damage you take (at `-30%` Pet DT and **100% immunity to AoE/Conal/Magic/Breath**). Press `Reward` (25s CD, free) for a 20% dual-heal + 2-debuff cleanse + 12s Hybrid Regen, `Soul Bond` (35s CD) to equalize Master & Pet HP% + apply a 15% Stoneskin, and `Familiar` (30s) for `-25%` DT, `+25%` Max HP, and +3 Ready charges.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Cure Mirroring:** Any `Cure`, `Curaga`, `Waltz`, or Master `Regen` tick healing you automatically mirrors **60% of the heal + 100% of overhealing** to your pet.
- **Guardian Beast Rotation:** Pick Crab/Beetle/Tortoise for heavy physical/magic mitigation or Tiger when you want AoE Paralyze + extra Master TP.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **NEVER equip a shield or offhand weapon:** Having anything in your `Sub` slot disables Guard, Parry/Guard mods, Axe WS pet heals, and `Feral Roar`.
- **Don't put an unrelated Jug item in your Ammo slot:** A non-whitelisted jug blocks `Call Beast`.
- **Don't let your pet drop below 15% HP:** Soul-Link pauses below 15% Pet HP until you heal it back up with `Reward`, `Soul Bond`, a Cure, or a 1H Axe WS.
