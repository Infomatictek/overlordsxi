# Warlord (SAM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Warlord is a **Great Katana offensive support captain** whose Tachi weapon skills and skillchain closures build **Ashura's Edge**, a nearby-party buff that combines weapon-skill and accuracy support with TP regeneration. At three stacks, the Warlord's melee swings can restore nearby allies' HP and MP. **Hagakure** adds an Iaijutsu draw-slash opportunity, while **Onimaru Blood-Quench** rewards correctly closing skillchains with party recovery. Warlord has real damage tools but exists primarily to make the entire group stronger.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Warlord Soul Crystal or use `!spec set warlord` at Lv. 10.
- **Required weapon:** Main-hand a **Great Katana**. Its special passive modifiers, Hasso aura, Noto action and Ashura's Edge support depend on that weapon.
- **Stats:** Accuracy, Store TP, Great Katana skill, Attack, Skillchain Damage and enough survival gear for frontline positioning.
- **Position:** Keep the party within roughly **15 yalms** of you for group auras, healing pulses and TP returns.
- **Party planning:** Discuss the skillchain sequence and its closing player before beginning a boss burst, because Warlord provides a significant group reward for successful closures.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Start Hasso:** Activate the specialized **Hasso** to share portions of its offensive benefits with nearby allies.
2. **Open with Onimaru Tithe:** Use **Onimaru Tithe** for immediate Ashura's Edge stacks, party TP and an offensive support window. This can make the first skillchain sequence much easier to begin.
3. **Maintain Ashura's Edge:** Use eligible **Tachi weapon skills** and skillchain closures to build or refresh up to three stacks. The party receives increasing Subtle Blow, weapon-skill damage, accuracy and TP support.
4. **Attack at three stacks:** At full Ashura's Edge, ordinary eligible melee swings can pulse small HP/MP restoration to nearby party members.
5. **Prepare a Noto draw-slash:** Use **Hagakure** before a Tachi weapon skill. The next eligible strike gains the special Iaijutsu payoff, with additional offensive effects and **Onimaru Sundering** on the quarry.
6. **Close skillchains deliberately:** Correctly completed skillchains trigger **Onimaru Blood-Quench**, improving skillchain damage and providing an immediate party HP, MP and TP pulse.
7. **Use Samurai support actions:** **Meditate**, **Sekkanoki**, **Blade Bash** and **Sengikori** provide additional timing, TP or control opportunities in the Warlord kit.
8. **Plan Meikyo Shisui:** During the special high-output window, Ashura's Edge reaches full strength, Hagakure becomes much easier to repeat and Blood-Quench rewards become stronger.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Ashura's Edge:** Up to **3 stacks**, approximately **20-second** duration; nearby party aura improves Subtle Blow, weapon-skill damage, Accuracy/Magic Accuracy and Regain.
- **Ashura's Edge progression:** At lower/mid/full stacks, the native table provides approximately **+10/16/24 Subtle Blow**, **+6/10/15% weapon-skill damage**, **+8/14/20 Accuracy/Magic Accuracy** and **+8/12/16 Regain**.
- **Three-stack healing:** Qualifying Great Katana melee hits at full stacks can restore about **15 HP and 5 MP** to allies within 15 yalms.
- **Onimaru Tithe:** **25-second recast**; starts a strong support window with **three Ashura's Edge stacks**, substantial party TP and an offensive buff.
- **Hagakure / Noto:** A **20-second** recast outside the major burst; prepares the next Tachi strike for draw-slash bonuses, additional TP Bonus and a short target debuff.
- **Onimaru Sundering:** The Iaijutsu follow-up can lower the quarry's Defense, Magic Defense and Evasion for the subsequent team attack.
- **Onimaru Blood-Quench:** An eligible skillchain closer triggers group recovery near the Warlord: about **10% HP, 8% MP and 250 TP**; Meikyo Shisui amplifies the reward.
- **Meikyo Shisui:** Special stance maximizes Ashura's Edge, accelerates Hagakure use and strengthens the skillchain recovery loop.
- **Level progression:** Passive accuracy, damage and party effects grow through Lv. 10/30/50/70. Great Katana remains the central equipment condition throughout.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Equipping the wrong main weapon:** Great Katana is required for the Soul's characteristic aura and weapon-skill interactions.
- **Fighting far from the party:** Many benefits reach only allies inside 15 yalms.
- **Ignoring skillchain planning:** A large share of Warlord support comes from successfully closing skillchains.
- **Letting Ashura's Edge expire:** Refresh stacks with appropriate Tachi actions before losing the full-aura benefit.
- **Spamming Hagakure without a follow-up:** Its value comes from the next eligible Tachi weapon skill.
- **Using Onimaru Tithe just for personal damage:** Its instant party support is often best timed around a team burst.
