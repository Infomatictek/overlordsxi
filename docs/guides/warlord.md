# Warlord (SAM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Warlord turns Samurai into a **Great Katana offensive aura captain & Skillchain battery** (`HybridSupport` tier: -25% damage dealt, 0.50x WS scaling, -15–30 Enmity). Your `Hasso`, Tachi WSs, `Hagakure` **Noto Iaijutsu Draw-Slashes**, and `Onimaru Tithe` stack **Ashura's Edge (1–3)** as a 15y party aura (`WSD`, `ACC/MACC`, `Subtle Blow`, `Regain`, and melee HP/MP heals), while every Skillchain you close triggers **Onimaru Blood-Quench** to surge HP, MP, and +250 TP to your whole party.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon:** **Great Katana in Main Hand.** Almost every Warlord mechanic (passives, shared `Hasso` aura, `Ashura's Edge`, `Hagakure` Noto Draw-Slash, `Onimaru Tithe`, and `Onimaru Blood-Quench`) requires a Great Katana equipped.
- **Stay Near Party (15y):** Your buffs, healing pulses, and TP surges radiate 15 yalms around you.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Share `Hasso` & Kickstart `Ashura's Edge`:** Turn on `Hasso` (shares 50% Hasso STR +5 ACC +5% Haste to allies in 15y) and press `Onimaru Tithe` (25s CD) to immediately set **3 Ashura's Edge stacks** and grant your 15y party `+350 TP`, `+12% WSD`, and `+15% Lifesteal` (12s).
2. **Maintain 3 `Ashura's Edge` Stacks:** Every Tachi WS or Skillchain closer adds/refreshes **Ashura's Edge (up to 3 stacks, 20s)** (`+10/16/24 Subtle Blow`, `+6/10/15% WSD`, `+8/14/20 ACC/MACC`, `+8/12/16 TP/tick Regain`). At **3 stacks**, your melee swings automatically restore `+15 HP / +5 MP` to nearby party members!
3. **Sheathe into `Hagakure` (`Noto`) Iaijutsu:** Press `Hagakure` (Lv.10+, 20s CD — **0s CD inside `Meikyo Shisui`**) for 15s of `Noto` (`+200–350 Save TP`). Your next Tachi WS triggers an **Iaijutsu Draw-Slash** (`+35–100%` Crit Rate, `+250–600` TP Bonus, `Chainbound`) and inflicts **Onimaru Sundering** (`-15% DEF`, `-10% MDEF`, `-25 Evasion` for 15s)!
4. **Close Skillchains & Surge (`Blood-Quench`):** Closing a Skillchain triggers **Onimaru Blood-Quench** (`+20–35%` SC damage and heals party **+10% HP / +8% MP / +250 TP**; **doubled** during `Meikyo Shisui`). Weave `Meditate` (shares +400 TP to party), `Sekkanoki` (party +15% Crit), `Blade Bash` (DEF Down), and `Sengikori`.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Ashura's Edge (1–3 stacks, 20s):** Shared 15y party offensive aura built by Tachi WSs, Skillchains, `Onimaru Tithe`, and `Meikyo Shisui`. Reach 3 stacks quickly so every swing restores party HP/MP.
- **`Meikyo Shisui` Overdrive:** Maxes Ashura's Edge to 3, removes `Hagakure`'s cooldown (`0s` Noto spam!), and **doubles** Onimaru Blood-Quench party HP/MP/TP surges.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't unequip Great Katana or stand >15y from your party.**
- **Don't ignore Skillchains:** Closing Skillchains with Warlord is massive party support (`+10% HP / +8% MP / +250 TP` per SC, doubled in Meikyo).
- Note: `Onimaru Tithe` melee lifesteal does not heal off Undead targets.
