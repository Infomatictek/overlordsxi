# Nightblade (NIN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Reactive ninjutsu sabotage.** Nightblade watches active enemy action telegraphs and answers with an appropriate seal rather than using clones.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Nightblade when a boss's telegraphed spell/TP actions are dangerous and someone can reliably counter them.

## Setup & Gear
- Ninjutsu proficiency and line of sight to the boss. Watch for casts versus physical TP actions.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** When an enemy is casting, respond with Severing Seal to interrupt or soften an uninterruptible spell.
2. **Act:** When a physical TP attack is being prepared, use Blunting Seal instead.
3. **Adapt:** Avoid guessing: a mismatched seal has no effect, though it offers a faster retry.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Severing Seal interrupts a valid spell or reduces uninterruptible spell damage by 8/10/10/12%; Blunting Seal reduces physical TP damage by 10/13/13/16%.
- The seals share a 20/18s cooldown, take 0.5s to cast and cost no tools. Wrong-match retries take 3s; scripted executions are excluded.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't spam seals into untargeted enemies or expect Shinobi's offensive clones.
- Confirm available commands and casting rules in the active Soul's menu before building a rotation.
