# Nightblade (NIN) - Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Nightblade is a Tier 3 support Soul. Read the enemy's current action and put the matching seal on that exact telegraph.

## Setup & Gear

- Equip its crystal or use `!spec set nightblade` from Lv. 10. Katana is the eligible weapon skill family. Your seals work with zero WS talents and any subjob.
- Signal Flare and both seals use no ninja tools. Bring Magic Accuracy for more full Severing Seal landings.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Watch the enemy's casting or readying message and the target's tell in the HUD.
2. Use **Severing Seal** within 15 yalms during a hostile spell cast. A full landing can interrupt a susceptible spell. Direct damage is reduced if the sealed spell still resolves.
3. Use **Blunting Seal** when the enemy readies a physical TP move. It weakens that move's physical damage for everyone hit.
4. Use **Signal Flare** for single-target fire damage between tells. From Lv. 30, use **Quick Read** before a short tell to cut the next seal's base cast from 0.5 to 0.25 seconds.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- Seals share a 20-second recast, reduced to 18 at Lv. 50. A wrong or ended tell gives a three-second retry. Physical damage typing is confirmed at resolution. A nonphysical move shortens Blunting Seal's cooldown to the three-second retry.
- Each enemy action can hold one seal. The seal lasts up to eight seconds and ends with that action.
- Severing's fallback reduces matching spell damage by 8/10/10/12%. Blunting reduces matching physical damage by 10/13/13/16%, at Lv. 10/30/50/70+.
- Quick Read lasts eight seconds, with a 45-second recast. The HUD shows current tell, seal status, seal recast and Quick Read.

## Common Mistakes

- Aim at the active enemy and cast before the tell ends. A seal does not carry over to the next cast.
- Coordinate with other Nightblades. A second seal cannot stack on the same action.
- Some scripted and instant actions offer no seal window. Scripted HP changes are outside the damage reduction.
