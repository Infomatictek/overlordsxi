# Nightblade (NIN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Nightblade is a Tier 3 **reactive support** Soul that watches hostile spell and technique telegraphs, then answers the **exact enemy action** with the correct seal. **Severing Seal** can interrupt susceptible spell casts or reduce the direct damage of spells that finish; **Blunting Seal** reduces a physical enemy technique's damage. Instead of applying a long-term general debuff, Nightblade protects the party by reading and responding within real enemy action windows.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Nightblade Soul Crystal or use `!spec set nightblade` from Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Katana is the eligible weapon-skill family. The reactive seal kit works with ordinary equipment, any subjob and zero weapon-skill talent points.
- **Spell preparation:** **Severing Seal**, **Blunting Seal** and **Signal Flare** all use no ninja tools. Magic Accuracy improves the chance that Severing Seal fully lands against susceptible magic.
- **Position:** Stay within 15 yalms of the enemy you expect to seal. You must respond while its current cast or readying sequence is active.
- **Party communication:** Tell teammates that your protection responds to a single telegraphed action, not every attack the enemy makes.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Watch the enemy's tell:** Track the target's spell-casting or technique-ready message. The Soul HUD identifies the current tell and whether the action is already sealed.
2. **Answer a hostile spell:** Cast **Severing Seal** during an active spell telegraph within 15 yalms. On a full successful landing against an interruptible spell, it may stop the spell; otherwise the seal can reduce matching direct magical damage if the spell completes.
3. **Answer a physical technique:** Cast **Blunting Seal** when the target readies an enemy technique. Its physical-damage reduction applies to the corresponding resolved physical action, including other party members struck by it.
4. **Use Signal Flare as filler:** **Signal Flare** provides single-target fire damage with no ninja tools and a 10-second recast. Do not let filler casting prevent reacting to a dangerous tell.
5. **At Lv. 30, prepare Quick Read:** **Quick Read** lasts 8 seconds and shortens the next valid seal cast from 0.5 seconds to 0.25 seconds. It has a 45-second recast; reserve it for tight response windows.
6. **Track seal cooldowns:** The two seals share a 20-second recast, reduced to 18 seconds at Lv. 50. A mismatched or already-ended tell normally produces a 3-second retry rather than the full successful seal recast.
7. **Avoid wasted overlaps:** Each enemy action can hold only one seal. If another Nightblade has sealed that action, save yours for a later tell.
8. **Stay reactive:** After a successful seal, return to normal damage and observation until the next actionable cast or technique appears.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Lv. 10–29:** Severing reduces a matching completed hostile spell's direct damage by **8%** if it is not interrupted. Blunting reduces matching physical technique damage by **10%**.
- **Lv. 30–49:** Severing reduction rises to **10%**; Blunting rises to **13%**. Quick Read unlocks.
- **Lv. 50–69:** The respective reductions remain **10%** and **13%**, while successful seals' shared recast falls from 20 to **18 seconds**.
- **Lv. 70–75:** Severing's fallback reduction improves to **12%** and Blunting's to **16%**.
- **One exact action:** Seal ownership is tied to the target and its current action sequence. It cannot be banked for the next spell, technique or different enemy.
- **Timing and retry:** Valid seal casts are normally 0.5 seconds; Quick Read can shorten the next one to 0.25 seconds. An invalid or mismatched tell gives a short 3-second recovery. A nonphysical technique can similarly restore Blunting's shorter retry.
- **Severing interrupt:** The enemy spell must actually be interruptible and the seal must fully land; otherwise the ability's damage-reduction path is the fallback.
- **Scope of prevention:** Only the matching action's eligible direct damage is reduced. Instant/non-telegraphed effects and scripted HP adjustments may not provide a usable window.
- **Soul HUD:** Shows the current action tell, whether it is sealed, shared seal recast, Quick Read's remaining empowerment and its own cooldown.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Casting a seal after the telegraph ends:** The seal is bound to one live action sequence; waiting too long cannot protect against that finished action.
- **Using the wrong seal type:** Severing answers hostile spells; Blunting is for physical techniques. A mismatched tell does not provide full protection.
- **Assuming every Severing cast interrupts:** Some spells cannot be interrupted or are resisted; a correctly applied seal may instead reduce direct spell damage.
- **Doubling a seal already on an action:** A second Nightblade's seal cannot stack on the same enemy sequence.
- **Expecting persistent global damage reduction:** The debuff does not carry to future actions or periodic damage.
- **Spending Quick Read on an easy tell:** Save its 45-second cooldown when the short seal cast time will actually make the difference.
