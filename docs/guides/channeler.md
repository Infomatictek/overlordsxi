# Channeler (BLM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Single-cast conduit support.** Channeler lends its own action window to accelerate and reduce the MP cost of one ally's next eligible spell.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Channeler when a single allied cast's timing and efficiency matters more than broad party buffs.

## Setup & Gear
- MP and defensive positioning, within 15y initially and 20y at later tiers.
- Unlocks at Lv10; final specialization cap is Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Start:** Identify the ally about to cast an important ordinary spell and activate Conduit Link.
2. **Core:** Maintain the link through that ally's completed cast; its cast time and MP cost are reduced.
3. **Timing:** At Lv30+, completed linked casts relay a portion of actual ally MP expenditure back to you.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Conduit Link has a 24s cooldown and an 8s duration, with 20–30% faster eligible casts and 15–25% MP cost reduction.
- It cannot accelerate Ancient Magic, instant casts, or item casts, and cannot link another active Channeler.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't use Conduit Link on an ineligible spell or assume canceled spells return MP.
- Do not infer additional spells or job abilities from the base job; check the Soul's actual unlocked actions.
