# Channeler (BLM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Channeler is a Tier 2 hybrid-support Soul that gives **one chosen ally** a faster, cheaper spell by committing to a short channeling window. **Conduit Link** is strongest when a healer or caster has a specific spell ready, rather than being used as a passive permanent aura. Self-linking is possible when solo, but it discounts MP only and does not speed your own casting. Channeler remains a caster with **Conduit Bolt** for single-target damage and **Aether Tap** to recover MP from suitable enemies.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Channeler Soul Crystal or use `!spec set channeler` from Lv. 10.
- **Weapon:** Staff is the eligible weapon-skill family and complements your magic use. The core Conduit Link interaction requires no weapon-skill talent points or mandatory support subjob.
- **Stats and resources:** Maintain a useful MP pool, Magic Accuracy and survivability so you can reach and support the planned caster. A link to another party member costs **8% maximum MP**, rounded up; self-linking has no activation MP cost.
- **Party coordination:** Choose the next high-value eligible spell beforehand. Link a living party member inside 15 yalms until Lv. 50, then up to 20 yalms.
- **Avoid invalid recipients:** You cannot place an allied Conduit Link on another Channeler. Only one active eligible link can apply to the recipient.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose the beneficiary:** Identify the healer or offensive caster preparing an important spell. Ask them to be ready to begin casting immediately after your setup.
2. **Activate Conduit Link:** Select that ally and use **Conduit Link** shortly before the planned spell. They have **8 seconds** to begin an eligible cast. The ability has a **24-second recast**.
3. **Hold your channel:** Remain within the permitted link range during the unspent window. While maintaining an allied link, your own spell and job-ability use is restricted, so do not attempt a competing rotation.
4. **Let the ally finish:** A successful eligible cast receives the level-dependent casting-time and MP discounts. At Lv. 30+, a completed allied cast also returns a small amount of its actual MP expenditure to you.
5. **Return to ordinary action:** Once the link is spent or ends, resume using **Conduit Bolt**, a Lv. 10 single-target spell costing 9 MP, with a 2-second cast and 4-second recast.
6. **Solo option:** Target yourself with Conduit Link. This costs no MP and applies the next-spell MP discount, but **does not** grant casting-time reduction or an allied MP relay.
7. **Add Aether Tap at Lv. 30:** Use **Aether Tap** on an enemy with MP to siphon resources when appropriate. It costs 10 MP, has a 3-second cast and a 60-second recast.
8. **Monitor and reapply carefully:** The HUD shows the remaining link window, whether the beneficiary has started a cast and when your Link recast ends. Time the next link around an actual high-value spell.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Lv. 10–29:** Allied link gives **20% cast-time savings** and **15% MP savings**; 15-yalm range. A self-link gives only the 15% MP savings.
- **Lv. 30–49:** Allied cast-time saving improves to **25%**; MP saving remains 15%. An eligible completed ally cast relays **5% of the actual MP expenditure** to Channeler. Aether Tap unlocks.
- **Lv. 50–69:** Link range increases to **20 yalms** and MP saving increases to **25%**. Allied cast-time saving remains 25%.
- **Lv. 70–75:** Allied cast-time saving rises to **30%**; MP saving remains 25% and range remains 20 yalms.
- **Link timing and cost:** Conduit Link lasts 8 seconds before an eligible cast starts, has a 24-second recast and costs 8% max MP (minimum 1 MP) when linked to another player. Self-link activation costs no MP.
- **Eligibility:** Ancient Magic, instant, item and zero-cost casts are excluded from the intended linked-spell benefit. Another Channeler cannot be the target of an allied link.
- **Replacement:** An ally holds at most one active link; a stronger unspent eligible link may replace a weaker one. A reserved/started cast cannot be displaced by a fresh link.
- **Solo behavior:** Self-link gives only MP savings, not cast acceleration or MP relay.
- **Level-scaled passives:** Channeler gains Max MP +8/12/16/20%, Magic Accuracy +8/14/22/30 and Max HP +4/6/8/10% at Lv. 10/30/50/70.
- **Soul HUD:** Displays link time remaining, whether the target has reserved a cast, and Conduit Link's remaining recast.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Linking too far ahead of a cast:** The 8-second unspent window is brief; an unused link expires without a useful completion.
- **Trying to cast through an allied link:** You are committing your own casting window to speed your teammate, so prioritize their action.
- **Using it on an excluded spell:** Confirm the recipient's cast is actually eligible before spending the link.
- **Expecting self-link to speed casting:** It only discounts MP when linked to yourself.
- **Overwriting a better link:** Stronger existing links and reserved spells have priority; coordinate multiple Channelers.
- **Counting on an MP relay from an interrupted cast:** The relay is linked to successful completion of an eligible allied cast, not merely starting it.
