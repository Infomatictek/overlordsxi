# Channeler (BLM) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Channeler is a Tier-2 hybrid support Soul. Time Conduit Link for one party member who is about to cast an important spell. You commit your own casting window so theirs is shorter and cheaper.

## Setup & Gear

- Select at Lv.10 with `!spec set channeler` or Crystal Socket. A staff supports your magic and is your only WS family.
- The Lv.10 loop uses Conduit Link and Conduit Bolt, with regular MP and cooldowns. It needs zero weapon-skill talent points.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Choose a healer or caster within 15 yalms. Use **Conduit Link** shortly before their next eligible spell. A party link costs 8% max MP and gives them eight seconds to start.
2. Stay in range. Your own spells and job abilities are unavailable while holding an allied link.
3. In solo play, link yourself. The setup costs no MP and discounts the next eligible spell without speeding it up. Use **Conduit Bolt**.
4. At Lv.30, **Aether Tap** can recover MP from enemies with MP. A finished allied cast relays 5% of its actual MP expenditure to you.
5. At Lv.50, range increases to 20 yalms and the linked MP discount reaches 25%. At Lv.70, the allied cast-time reduction reaches 30%.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- Each ally can hold one link. A stronger unspent link can replace a weaker one. A started cast has priority.
- The HUD shows the link window, whether an ally has started casting, and the 24-second recast.

## Common Mistakes

- Link immediately before the planned heal or nuke. An unused link expires in eight seconds.
- Keep Ancient, instant, item and zero-cost casts out of the plan. Another Channeler cannot receive your party link.
- If the ally stops the spell, the link ends and you receive no completion relay.
