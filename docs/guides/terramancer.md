# Terramancer (GEO) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Predictive single-target faults.** Terramancer places an impending geological strike at a fixed enemy position and must predict whether the target will stay there.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Terramancer for predictive boss-window burst rather than Verdant's healing Luopan aura.

## Setup & Gear
- Magic Accuracy, INT and a reliable view of the target's movement.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Use Shallow Fault to plant a delayed, target-locked eruption at the enemy's current position.
2. **Act:** For a steadier target at Lv30+, choose the larger but slower Deep Fault instead.
3. **Adapt:** Plan around where the target will be when the fault resolves; reposition or wait rather than spamming placement.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Shallow Fault detonates in 2s starting at Lv10; Deep Fault detonates in 4s from Lv30. They share an 8s recast.
- Only the originally marked target can take damage, if still close to the fixed point. One outstanding fault per caster; no accidental spectator damage.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't cast a four-second fault at a boss that will obviously move away.
- Only the actions actually granted to this Soul should be treated as part of its kit.
