# Terramancer (GEO) - Playstyle Guide

Terramancer is a Tier 1 damage Soul. Predict where a claimed enemy will stand when your elemental fault erupts.

## Level 10 opening

1. Equip the crystal or use `!spec set terramancer`. Club and Staff are the eligible weapon-skill families.
2. Claim the enemy with **Stone Chip**, or follow an ally’s existing claim.
3. Cast **Shallow Fault** within 18 yalms to mark the target’s current position. It erupts after two seconds.
4. The eruption hits only that enemy if it is still claimed and remains within three horizontal and three vertical yalms of the mark when the fuse ends.
5. Use **Stone Chip** between faults. Use **Cancel Fault** to prevent a pending eruption if the fight changes. Cancellation keeps the MP cost and recast.

The opener works with any subjob and zero weapon-skill talent points. Deep Fault opens at Lv. 30 and spends more MP for a stronger hit after four seconds.

| Level | Shallow multiplier | Deep multiplier |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 10–29 | 1.8× | — |
| 30–49 | 1.8× | 2.8× |
| 50–69 | 2.0× | 3.2× |
| 70–75 | 2.2× | 3.6× |

Multipliers apply to the base earth damage, before normal mitigation. Shallow costs 7% of maximum MP and Deep costs 12%, rounded up. The two faults share an eight-second recast and use one slot. The HUD shows the pending fault, fuse, quarry position and shared recast.

Faults disappear on death, zoning, Soul changes or claim loss. A different enemy or a respawn cannot trigger the eruption, even on the same mark.
