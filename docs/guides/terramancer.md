# Terramancer (GEO) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Terramancer is a Tier 1 single-target damage Soul built around predicting where a claimed quarry will stand. Place a delayed earthen fault under your target, keep it inside the marked area until the fuse expires, and weave Stone Chip between eruptions. Positioning and claim control determine whether your MP becomes damage: a fault that misses still costs MP and incurs its recast. Terramancer is not an area-damage specialist; each fault can hit only its original quarry.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** At Lv. 10, equip the Terramancer Soul Crystal or use `!spec set terramancer`.
- **Weapons:** Club and Staff are eligible weapon-skill and talent families. Use whichever fits your available gear; the fault rotation works without weapon-skill talent investment.
- **Priority stats:** INT, Magic Attack Bonus and Magic Accuracy support the Soul's earth damage. Carry enough maximum MP to fund repeated percentage-cost faults; more maximum MP also increases their absolute MP cost.
- **Positioning:** You must be within 18 yalms to place a fault on a claimed target. Mark a target that is likely to remain near the same position until detonation.
- **Party coordination:** Let a tank or other teammate establish a stable enemy position where possible. You can also claim an unclaimed target yourself with Stone Chip.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Establish your quarry (Lv. 10+):** Use Stone Chip to engage an enemy or follow an ally's claim. Stone Chip costs 3 MP and has a 4-second recast.
2. **Plant Shallow Fault (Lv. 10+):** At 18 yalms or closer, cast Shallow Fault on your claimed enemy. It records the quarry's location and erupts after 2 seconds. Casting costs 7% of your maximum MP, rounded up.
3. **Keep the quarry on its mark:** At eruption, the original enemy must still be alive, claimed and within a 3-yalm horizontal radius and 3 yalms vertically of the marked position. Movement beyond either limit causes the fault to miss. No other enemy standing at the mark can take its damage.
4. **Weave and repeat:** Cast Stone Chip between eruptions while watching the shared fault recast. Shallow and Deep Fault use the same 8-second recast; only one pending fault can be armed at a time.
5. **Upgrade the decision at Lv. 30:** Deep Fault becomes available. It costs 12% of maximum MP and detonates after 4 seconds, making it stronger against enemies you expect to remain stationary but riskier against moving targets. Choose Shallow Fault when a quicker hit is more reliable.
6. **Cancel a failed setup:** If the battle changes while a fault is pending, use Cancel Fault to collapse it. This prevents the later eruption but does not refund the MP or reset the shared fault recast.
7. **During movement-heavy fights:** Favor Stone Chip and shorter-fuse Shallow Fault. Do not repeatedly spend MP on Deep Fault when the quarry is unlikely to remain in position.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **One quarry, one fault:** The pending fault binds to the original enemy and spawn; it cannot be transferred to a new target, a respawn, or another mob inside the marked area.
- **Shallow Fault scaling:** 1.8× base earth damage at Lv. 10–49, 2.0× at Lv. 50–69, and 2.2× at Lv. 70–75, before normal mitigation.
- **Deep Fault scaling:** Unavailable before Lv. 30; 2.8× base earth damage at Lv. 30–49, 3.2× at Lv. 50–69, and 3.6× at Lv. 70–75, before normal mitigation.
- **Shared recast and MP:** Placing either fault starts the 8-second shared recast. Shallow costs 7% and Deep 12% of maximum MP, rounded up, whether or not the eruption lands.
- **Soul HUD:** Track the fault type, remaining fuse, original quarry, whether the quarry is inside the mark, and the shared cooldown.
- **Invalidation:** A pending fault is discarded on death, zoning, Soul change or loss of claim. It cannot trigger for the wrong enemy or spawn.
- **Tier 1 identity:** This is a damage-focused positioning specialist. The verified fault kit does not provide a dedicated defensive or healing mechanic; depend on the party to stabilize the fight.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Casting Deep Fault on a mobile enemy:** Its 4-second fuse gives the target more time to escape. Prefer Shallow Fault when movement is uncertain.
- **Mistaking the marker for an AoE:** Only the original quarry can be hit, even if other enemies cross the marked ground.
- **Ignoring the claim:** The enemy must still qualify as your claimed quarry when the delayed hit resolves.
- **Casting outside placement range or during shared recast:** The placement check will reject the action. Check your range and HUD before retrying.
- **Assuming Cancel Fault refunds resources:** It only cancels the pending event. The consumed MP and recast remain spent.
- **Overinvesting in maximum MP without considering costs:** Percentage-based fault costs rise with maximum MP; balance your MP pool against sustainable recovery.
