# Adventurer (BRD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Adventurer is a **Dagger/Sword melee damage Bard** that retains useful party songs while converting melee uptime into **Cadence**. Successful attacks and **Lunge & Flourish** build up to five Cadence stacks, then eligible weapon skills spend them for bonus light sonic damage and an **Encore** self-buff. You gain Dual Wield and increasing offensive stats, but your core loop depends on actually fighting with the right main-hand weapon.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip Adventurer's Soul Crystal or use `!spec set adventurer` from Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Main-hand a **Dagger or Sword**; off-hand a compatible weapon when appropriate. The Soul grants Dual Wield, but a wrong main weapon prevents special Cadence interactions.
- **Equipment priorities:** Accuracy, Attack, DEX, CHR, Critical Hit, Dual Wield and Haste. Keep a usable instrument for maintaining party songs.
- **Buffs:** Maintain the party's appropriate songs. Lunge & Flourish and the sonic follow-up scale with the number of currently active qualifying buff songs, up to four.
- **Role:** This is a melee DPS Soul with Bard song utility, not a dedicated tank or healer.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare your music:** Maintain up to **four appropriate buff songs**, subject to your available song slots. Damage abilities gain value from having useful buffs active; choose songs that help the actual party.
2. **Engage with Dagger or Sword:** Successful eligible melee hits accumulate **Cadence**. The stack buff adds critical-hit chance while it remains active.
3. **Use Lunge & Flourish:** Strike a nearby enemy with **Lunge & Flourish** for piercing damage, up to **10% Defense Down** when the target lacks it, **two Cadence stacks** and **200 TP**.
4. **Spend Cadence on weapon skills:** Use an eligible **Dagger or Sword weapon skill** to consume existing Cadence for a light sonic follow-up strike and activate a brief **Encore** offensive buff.
5. **Exploit Encore:** Continue attacking while the Encore haste, critical chance and weapon-skill damage bonuses remain active. Build new Cadence as the next weapon-skill window approaches.
6. **Trigger Troubadour's Encore for a planned burst:** **Troubadour's Encore** immediately supplies five Cadence and a stronger 15-second Encore. Align it with a safe target and a ready damaging weapon skill.
7. **Keep party songs stable:** Weave song renewals around melee pressure, rather than dropping party utility to chase one extra damage hit.
8. **Adjust for boss mechanics:** When forced out of melee, use the time to prepare songs and positioning. Resume Cadence-building only when valid Dagger or Sword attacks can land.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Cadence:** Up to **5 stacks**, normally lasting about **20 seconds**; each stack provides **+2% critical-hit rate**. Eligible melee hits and Lunge & Flourish build it; eligible weapon skills spend it.
- **Lunge & Flourish:** Approximately **20-second recast** and close-range strike. Bonus damage increases with up to four active songs; it also grants 2 Cadence and 200 TP.
- **Encore after weapon skill:** Cadence consumption grants a **12-second** self-buff with approximately **15% ability haste, +10% critical chance and +12% weapon-skill damage**.
- **Troubadour's Encore:** About **45-second recast**, immediately grants five Cadence for 25 seconds and a **15-second** stronger Encore: **20% ability haste, +15% critical chance and +15% weapon-skill damage**.
- **Progression:** At Lv. 10/30/50/70, the Soul gains DEX +5/10/16/22, CHR +4/8/14/18, Attack +12/24/42/60, Accuracy +10/20/32/45, Critical Rate +3/5/8/10 and increasing Dual Wield.
- **Protected Bard identity:** Party songs are still part of the class; offensive melee buffs and Cadence are layered on top rather than replacing the entire song toolkit.
- **Weapon eligibility:** Dagger/Sword weapon skills and eligible melee attacks are essential for special Cadence/Encore interactions. Other weapon skills use their regular mechanics.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Using an unrelated main-hand weapon:** It may disable the special Cadence attack triggers even when the weapon itself deals damage.
- **Ignoring song upkeep:** Fighting with few useful songs loses both party benefit and some of the Soul's song-dependent attack payoff.
- **Spending a weapon skill with no Cadence:** You lose the special follow-up opportunity; build stacks first when practical.
- **Overcapping at five stacks:** Spend before losing the temporary stack buff, but do not waste a weapon skill into invulnerability.
- **Treating Troubadour's Encore as only a defensive buff:** Its instant Cadence is intended to enable a strong offensive sequence.
- **Abandoning party coordination:** Adventurer is a damage Soul, but still benefits enormously from well-chosen Bard buffs.
