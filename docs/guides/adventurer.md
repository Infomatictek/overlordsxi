# Adventurer (BRD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Adventurer converts Bard into an **agile Dual-Wield Dagger/Sword melee DPS** (`Damage` tier: 0% damage penalty, 1.00x WS scaling). You maintain up to 4 passive Buff Songs, land Dagger/Sword swings and `Lunge & Flourish` to stack **Cadence (max 5)**, and cash those stacks in with Weaponskills to unleash Light sonic follow-up damage + **Encore** (`+15% Haste`, `+10% Crit`, `+12% WSD`).

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon:** **Dagger or Sword in Main Hand** (Dual Wield is granted automatically at Lv.10+ with delay reduction mods). Using non-Dagger/Sword weapons disables `Cadence` stacking and `Encore` WS bursts.
- **Priority Stats:** DEX, CHR, Attack, Accuracy, Crit Rate, Haste, Dual Wield. (105% SIRD & reduced Song Cast Time are built in.)

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Sing Up to 4 Buff Songs:** Keep up to **4 party buff songs** active (Ballads, Minuets, Madrigals, Marches, etc.) — your `Lunge & Flourish` damage and WS follow-ups scale up with active buff songs (capped at 4).
2. **Build `Cadence` (Max 5):** Engage with Dagger/Sword auto-attacks (`+1 Cadence` per swing, +2% Crit Rate per stack) and weave `Lunge & Flourish` (20s CD, 4y: piercing strike + `-10% DEF Down` + **+2 Cadence** + **+200 TP**).
3. **Spend `Cadence` on Weaponskills:** Fire any Dagger or Sword WS while holding 1–5 `Cadence` stacks to consume them for bonus **Light sonic follow-up damage** and activate **Encore** (12s: +15% Haste, +10% Crit Rate, +12% Weaponskill Damage).
4. **Reset & Burst:** Press `Troubadour's Encore` (45s CD) to immediately jump to **5 Cadence** (25s) and a supercharged 15s **Encore** (`+20% Haste`, `+15% Crit`, `+15% WSD`).

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Cadence (0–5 stacks, 20s):** Grants `+2%` Crit Rate per stack passively; consumed on Dagger/Sword WS to deal scaled Light sonic damage and trigger Encore.
- **Encore (12s–15s buff):** Haste + Crit + WSD steroid triggered by spending Cadence on a WS or pressing `Troubadour's Encore`.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't main-hand a Staff or Club:** Swings and WSs will not build or spend `Cadence`.
- **Don't neglect your Song slots:** Fighting with 0–1 buff songs weakens `Lunge & Flourish` and your WS sonic damage; keep 4 songs up (offensive songs won't overwrite your buffs cleanly if needed, or stick to buffs).
