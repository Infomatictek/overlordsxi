# Wyvernheart (DRG) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Wyvernheart transforms Dragoon into a **Polearm Combat Healer** (`CombatHealer` tier: -50% damage dealt for both Dragoon and Wyvern, 0.20x WS scaling). You keep your AoE/Magic-immune Wyvern alive, build **Wyrmscale stacks (0–3)** through Jumps, `Spirit Link`, and **Polearm Weaponskills** (each WS also heals the most injured ally for 18–28% DRG Max HP with overheal converting to Stoneskin), and cash those stacks in with `Restoring Breath` for huge party healing + 15% Stoneskin.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapon:** **Polearm in Main Hand.** Non-Polearm weapons disable `Crimson Triage` healing and `+1 Wyrmscale` stacking on WSs.
- **Wyvern Required:** Keep your Wyvern summoned (`Call Wyvern` is unlocked at Lv.10+ on a 15s CD and usable in combat; your Wyvern gains +40–75% HP, -40% DT, and 100% Breath/Magic immunity).
- **Priority Stats:** Max HP (your heals scale directly off **DRG Max HP**!), Cure Potency, MND, VIT, Haste, Store TP.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Summon & Anchor:** Call your Wyvern (`15s CD`). For raid-wide damage phases, pop `Dragonscale Sanctuary` (75s CD, 15s: 15% Max HP party Stoneskin, Knockback Immunity, and `-12% DMG / +30 MEVA` party ward).
2. **Build `Wyrmscale` (Max 3) & Triage Heal:**
   - Fire **Polearm WSs** for **+1 Wyrmscale** + **Crimson Triage** (`18–28% DRG Max HP` heal on lowest ally in 15y; overheal becomes up to 12% Stoneskin).
   - Weave `Jump` (20s CD / 10s in Spirit Surge or Fly High: +150 TP, +1/+2 Wyrmscale, 12s Hybrid Regen + Erase on 2 lowest allies) and `High Jump` (35s CD / 18s: +1/+2 Wyrmscale, 2-debuff party cleanse + 10% Stoneskin).
   - Press `Spirit Link` (Lv.10+, 15s CD, **0 self-HP cost**: +40% Wyvern HP, **+1 Wyrmscale**, 22–32% DRG Max HP heal + 2-debuff cleanse on lowest ally).
3. **Spend 3 Stacks on `Restoring Breath`:** Press `Restoring Breath` (Lv.10+, 20s CD) to consume all `Wyrmscale` (0–3) for a **`14 / 20 / 26 / 32% DRG Max HP` party heal** (multiplied by Cure Potency). At **3 stacks**, it also wraps your entire party in **15% Max HP Stoneskin** for 12s!
4. **Reset Jumps:** Once `Jump` and `High Jump` are both on cooldown, press `Wyrmblood Pulse` (45s CD) for an instant `24% DRG Max HP` party heal + 2-debuff cleanse + **full reset of Jump & High Jump**. (`Super Jump` wipes hate and grants an instant 3 Wyrmscale.)

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Wyrmscale (0–3 stacks, 30s):** Built by Polearm WSs (+1), `Jump` (+1/+2), `High Jump` (+1/+2), `Spirit Link` (+1), and `Super Jump` (3). Consumed by `Restoring Breath` (at 3 stacks = 32% DRG HP base party heal + 15% Stoneskin).
- **HP Scaling:** Stack Max HP + Cure Potency — almost every heal scales directly off your own Max HP!

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't fire `Restoring Breath` without a living Wyvern (or at 0–1 stacks unless urgent):** At 3 stacks you more than double the heal AND add a 15% party Stoneskin.
- **Don't press `Wyrmblood Pulse` while `Jump` / `High Jump` are ready:** Use both Jumps first so Pulse resets both cooldowns immediately.
- **Don't equip non-Polearm weapons:** Polearm WSs are required for Crimson Triage heals and Wyrmscale procs.
