# Wyvernheart (DRG) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Wyvernheart is a **Polearm combat healer** whose healing draws directly on the Dragoon's maximum HP and an active Wyvern. **Jump**, **High Jump**, **Spirit Link** and qualifying Polearm weapon skills build **Wyrmscale**. **Restoring Breath** spends it for substantial party healing, with a protective Stoneskin at three stacks. The rotation alternates front-line damage, reactive triage, and deliberate cooldown resets rather than treating the Wyvern as a pure damage pet.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip Wyvernheart's Soul Crystal or use `!spec set wyvernheart` at Lv. 10.
- **Required weapon:** Main-hand **Polearm** to trigger **Crimson Triage** and Wyrmscale on eligible weapon skills.
- **Wyvern:** Keep your Wyvern summoned via **Call Wyvern**, which has a shortened recast and can be used in combat.
- **Stats:** Max HP, Cure Potency, MND, VIT, Accuracy, Haste and Store TP. Many heals scale from **your maximum HP**, not from the Wyvern's stats.
- **Position:** Most emergency recovery reaches allies within roughly **15 yalms**; remain close enough to your party without taking avoidable boss cleaves.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Summon your Wyvern:** Activate **Call Wyvern** early. This enables the pet-centered healing engine and provides a resilient companion.
2. **Build Wyrmscale with Polearm weapon skills:** Each eligible weapon skill grants **one Wyrmscale** and triggers **Crimson Triage**, healing the most injured ally and converting qualifying overheal into Stoneskin.
3. **Weave Jump and High Jump:** These abilities build additional Wyrmscale while providing TP, Regen, cleansing or group shielding according to the action used.
4. **Use Spirit Link:** Its modified version restores Wyvern health without the ordinary self-HP cost, gains another Wyrmscale and heals/cleanses an injured party member.
5. **Spend at three Wyrmscale:** Use **Restoring Breath** for the strongest party-wide recovery and the bonus Stoneskin. Earlier spending can be appropriate when an ally is in immediate danger.
6. **Reset the Jump cycle:** When Jump and High Jump are both unavailable, use **Wyrmblood Pulse** to deliver additional healing and cleansing and refresh both cooldowns.
7. **Prepare Dragonscale Sanctuary:** Use it before predictable heavy party damage for Stoneskin, knockback protection and additional mitigation.
8. **Use emergency Dragoon abilities deliberately:** **Super Jump** can control enmity and prepare maximum Wyrmscale; **Spirit Surge** or **Fly High** improve the Jump-driven healing cycle.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Wyrmscale:** Up to **3 stacks** lasting around **30 seconds**. Eligible Polearm weapon skills and Spirit Link grant one; Jumps may grant one or two, and Super Jump can grant three.
- **Crimson Triage:** Eligible Polearm weapon skills heal the lowest-HP ally within about **15 yalms**, scaling from **18–28% of Dragoon maximum HP**, with overheal barrier potential.
- **Restoring Breath:** **20-second recast**, consumes available Wyrmscale for a group heal scaling with the caster's maximum HP. A full three-stack spend also grants **15% maximum-HP Stoneskin** for 12 seconds.
- **Call Wyvern:** Specialized **15-second recast** with strong pet HP and defense scaling. The Wyvern has special protection against qualifying magical/breath events.
- **Jump and High Jump:** Modified to generate Wyrmscale and direct healing/support alongside their ordinary combat role. Their recasts can shorten during major Dragoon burst states.
- **Spirit Link:** Specialized **15-second recast**, no normal self-HP penalty, extra Wyvern healing plus party triage and cleansing.
- **Wyrmblood Pulse:** **45-second recast**, immediate group recovery and two-debuff cleanse, resetting Jump and High Jump recasts.
- **Dragonscale Sanctuary:** **75-second recast**, approximately **15-second** Stoneskin and mitigation window for nearby party members.
- **Level progression:** The three-stack Restoring Breath base recovery improves through **14/20/26/32%** of Dragoon maximum HP at Lv. 10/30/50/70, alongside Triage and passive survival scaling.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Equipping a non-Polearm main weapon:** You lose Crimson Triage and weapon-skill Wyrmscale generation.
- **Fighting without a Wyvern:** Several important healing actions depend on a living companion.
- **Always spending Restoring Breath at one stack:** The three-stack payoff adds a valuable team barrier; spend early only when necessary.
- **Using Wyrmblood Pulse while Jumps are already ready:** Its reset effect is wasted if both abilities are available.
- **Stacking only offensive stats:** The healer's maximum HP and Cure Potency are central to healing throughput.
- **Ignoring party spacing:** Most group recovery has a limited radius, not unlimited encounter coverage.
