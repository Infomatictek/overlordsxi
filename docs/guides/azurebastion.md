# Azure Bastion (BLU) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Azure Bastion turns Blue Mage into a **Dual-Wielding Monster-Core Tank** (`Tank` tier: -60% damage dealt, +60–120 baseline Enmity, 100% SIRD, Lv.10+ Dual Wield). Casting tagged physical Blue spells builds **Chitin Carapace stacks (1–5)** for up to **+20% DEF / -15% DT** (and unlocks +35% potency & +6% MP refunds on Blue defense/healing spells at 3+ stacks), while **Sword/Club Weaponskills grant `Azure Surge`**, making your next Blue spell **instant-cast, 0 MP, and an 8y AoE taunt**.

## Setup & Gear
- **Required Weapons:** **Sword or Club in Main Hand** (Dual Wield is granted automatically at Lv.10+, no shield needed) to trigger `Azure Surge` on Weaponskills.
- **Required Blue Spell Set:** Always equip several of the **6 Chitin Carapace spells**: `Head Butt`, `Sudden Lunge`, `Bludgeon`, `Smite of Rage`, `Heavy Strike`, `Goblin Rush`, plus defensive/healing spells (`Cocoon`, `Metallic Body`, `Diamondhide`, `Saline Coat`, `Reactor Cool`, `Magic Fruit`).

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Pull & Instant 5-Stack:** Engage with `Behemoth Roar` (25s CD, 8y: 4s Stun, -20% ATK & -20% MATK Down 12s, heavy AoE threat, and an **instant 5 stacks of Chitin Carapace**).
2. **Maintain `Chitin Carapace` (Up to 5 Stacks):** Cast **Head Butt, Sudden Lunge, Bludgeon, Smite of Rage, Heavy Strike, or Goblin Rush** (-30% MP cost, **3x Volatile Enmity**, **+1 Chitin Carapace** up to +20% DEF & -15% DT for 15s).
3. **Cast Buffs/Heals at `3+ Carapace` Stacks:** At 3+ stacks, casting `Cocoon`, `Metallic Body`, `Diamondhide`, `Saline Coat`, `Reactor Cool`, or `Magic Fruit` gains **+35% potency AND restores +6% Max MP** (without spending stacks!).
4. **Weave Sword/Club WSs -> Instant AoE Blue Spell (`Azure Surge`):** Every Sword or Club WS grants `Azure Surge` (6s). Cast **any Blue Magic spell** inside that 6s window for **0s cast time, 0 MP cost, and an 8y AoE Volatile Enmity pulse**!
5. **Defensive & Affinity Cooldowns:** Use `Diamond Shell` (30s CD, 10s: -20% DT, 25% Stoneskin, Knockback Resist, 20% damage taken reflected as VE), `Chain Affinity` + physical spell (3s Stun + 20% Stoneskin), and `Burst Affinity` + breath/nuke spell (heavy threat + -15% MATK Down).

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Chitin Carapace (1–5 stacks, 15s):** Only built by `Head Butt`, `Sudden Lunge`, `Bludgeon`, `Smite of Rage`, `Heavy Strike`, `Goblin Rush`, and `Behemoth Roar`. Never consumed when triggering the 3+ stack MP refund!
- **Azure Surge (6s after Sword/Club WS):** Turns slow spells like `Diamondhide` or `Magic Fruit` into instant 0-MP AoE taunts.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Don't expect random physical Blue spells to build Carapace:** Only those 6 tagged physical spells (plus `Behemoth Roar`) give 3x VE and `Chitin Carapace`.
- **Don't cast `Diamondhide`/`Cocoon`/`Magic Fruit` at 0–2 stacks if low on MP:** Hit 3+ Carapace stacks first for the +35% potency and +6% Max MP refund.
- **Note on Offense:** Actual combat code puts Azure Bastion in `Tank` tier (-60% damage dealt) — focus on threat, Stoneskins, and debuffs rather than personal nuke/melee DPS.
