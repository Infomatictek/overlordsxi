# Azure Bastion (BLU) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Azure Bastion is a **Blue Mage monster-core tank**. Specific physical Blue Magic spells accumulate **Chitin Carapace**, which increases Defense and reduces incoming damage. At three or more stacks, defensive Blue Magic becomes more effective and restores MP. Eligible Sword or Club weapon skills create **Azure Surge**, making the next Blue spell instant, free and threatening to nearby enemies. This Soul uses spell sequencing to maintain protection, enmity and personal survival—not high personal damage.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Azure Bastion Soul Crystal or use `!spec set azurebastion` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Equip **Sword or Club** in Main to trigger Azure Surge on weapon skills. The specialization grants Dual Wield; a Shield is not required for the Blue tank loop.
- **Core physical spells:** Prepare **Head Butt, Sudden Lunge, Bludgeon, Smite of Rage, Heavy Strike** and **Goblin Rush**, the spells designated to build Chitin Carapace.
- **Defensive Blue Magic:** Keep **Cocoon, Metallic Body, Diamondhide, Saline Coat, Reactor Cool** and **Magic Fruit** available as appropriate.
- **Stats:** HP, Defense, VIT, Magic Accuracy, Enmity, Spell Interruption Resistance and sufficient Accuracy for Sword/Club weapon-skill procs.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Open with Behemoth Roar:** Use the high-threat opener to stun and weaken nearby enemies, and instantly establish **five Chitin Carapace stacks**.
2. **Maintain Carapace:** Cast your tagged physical Blue spells when the stack timer approaches expiry; they build one stack and add special Volatile Enmity.
3. **Use defensive spells at three or more stacks:** Cast **Cocoon**, **Metallic Body**, **Diamondhide** or a needed healing spell when the bonus potency and MP return are active.
4. **Attack to build TP:** Use Sword/Club attacks and eligible weapon skills. A qualifying weapon skill grants **Azure Surge** for a six-second prepared cast.
5. **Spend Azure Surge wisely:** Cast the defensive or control Blue spell needed next. It becomes **instant, zero MP** and produces a nearby enmity pulse.
6. **Use Diamond Shell for heavy damage:** Activate it before a known tank-buster or sustained boss phase for reduction, Stoneskin and knockback protection.
7. **Use the affinity cooldowns for control:** **Chain Affinity** can reinforce a physical Blue spell with short Stun and Stoneskin; **Burst Affinity** can create a high-threat magic response.
8. **Keep the boss controlled:** Watch how the Blue Magic effects and Surge threat interact with your group's position; you are a tank first, not an AoE damage specialist.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Chitin Carapace:** Up to **five stacks** for **15 seconds**. Tagged physical Blue spells build one; Behemoth Roar can immediately set five.
- **Carapace protection:** At full stacks, approximately **20% Defense improvement and 15% Damage Taken reduction**.
- **Three-stack threshold:** At three or more stacks, qualifying protective/healing Blue spells receive approximately **35% extra potency** and restore **6% maximum MP**, without consuming stacks.
- **Azure Surge:** Eligible Sword or Club weapon skills provide a **six-second** opportunity for an instant, zero-MP Blue spell and **8-yalm** volatile enmity pulse.
- **Behemoth Roar:** **25-second recast**, close-range high-threat opener with stun, enemy Attack/Magic Attack reduction and maximum Carapace setup.
- **Diamond Shell:** **30-second recast**, **10-second** tank defensive window; reduces incoming damage, supplies a barrier, resists knockback and can turn damage taken into additional enmity.
- **Affinity interaction:** Chain Affinity and Burst Affinity have specialized defensive/threat results when followed by eligible Blue spells.
- **Level progression:** Max HP, Defense, tank enmity and suitable Blue spell availability improve through Lv. 10/30/50/70. The Chitin builder list remains specific.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Assuming every physical Blue spell builds Carapace:** The system uses the named spell list, not the entire damage family.
- **Casting an expensive defense spell at zero stacks without a need:** The three-stack potency and MP benefits are worth establishing when time allows.
- **Failing to spend Azure Surge:** The free instant-cast opportunity expires after six seconds.
- **Using ineligible weapon skills:** The special Surge trigger checks Sword or Club.
- **Trying to compete in DPS:** Damage penalties exist because the kit is built around threat and tank survivability.
- **Pulling additional enemies accidentally:** Its close-range enmity tools can affect nearby mobs; coordinate positioning in hard encounters.
