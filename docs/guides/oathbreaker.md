# Oathbreaker (PLD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Committed Great Sword vows.** Oathbreaker sacrifices a normal Paladin's flexible defense to commit to an offensive or reactive vow with a Great Sword.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Oathbreaker for boss-pattern commitment, instead of Reaper's enemy recovery exploitation or Defender's tanking.

## Setup & Gear
- Great Sword; choose Condemn or Absolve when safe and out of combat.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Select Condemn for focused Great Sword pressure or Absolve to retain reactive defense.
2. **Act:** Use Reckoning for a deliberate single-target hit, with stronger punishment after suffering a qualifying enemy strike.
3. **Adapt:** Reconsider your vow between engagements; switching mid-fight is prohibited.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Condemn grants +10/13/16/18% Great Sword melee damage but disables Flash; Absolve provides 8/10/12% DT and retaliation.
- Reckoning is a 4y, 18s action with 1.5–2.0x normal damage, doubling once within 5–7s after a qualifying hit of at least 8% maximum HP.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Do not expect to switch oaths while engaged or copy Defender's party interception.
- Base-job access does not guarantee every spell or ability is approved for this Soul.
