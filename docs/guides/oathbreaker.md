# Oathbreaker (PLD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Oathbreaker is a Tier 1 **Great Sword damage** Soul that chooses a combat vow before engaging. **Vow of Condemnation** favors steady Great Sword autoattacks but makes incoming enemy damage more dangerous. **Vow of Absolution** instead prepares you to retaliate after taking a substantial direct hit. Your primary offensive strike, **Reckoning**, becomes especially strong when aimed at the enemy that opened a retaliation window. This is a committed damage specialization, not a conventional Paladin tank.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Oathbreaker Soul Crystal or use `!spec set oathbreaker` at Lv. 10.
- **Weapon:** Equip a **Great Sword**; Reckoning and the retaliation system require it. Great Sword is also the eligible weapon-skill family.
- **Stats:** Favor enough Accuracy to land weapon swings and Reckoning, then STR and Attack. The Soul provides additional Great Sword skill and offensive stats with level.
- **Choose before the pull:** Vows can only be changed while **not engaged and not on an enemy's hate list**. The default vow on activation is Condemnation if no vow has been selected.
- **Party role:** Coordinate with tank and healer. Condemnation explicitly increases damage taken, while Absolution provides retaliation rather than reducing the hit itself.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose a vow before combat:** Use **Vow of Condemnation** for stronger regular Great Sword swings, or **Vow of Absolution** when you expect to be struck hard enough to enable retaliation.
2. **Condemnation path:** Stay in melee and keep attacking. Regular Great Sword damage is boosted by 10/13/16/18% as you progress, but enemy damage against you increases by 10%. Keep your healer aware of the risk.
3. **Absolution path:** Allow a single enemy hit to remove at least **8% of your maximum HP**. This opens a short retaliation window against that specific enemy; the vow itself does not reduce the damage taken.
4. **Spend with Reckoning:** Use **Reckoning** within 4 yalms with a Great Sword equipped. Against the enemy that triggered an active Absolution retaliation, its damage multiplier is doubled.
5. **Use Ashen Judgment at range:** **Ashen Judgment** is a single-target light spell available from Lv. 10. It costs 15 MP, has a 2-second cast and a 15-second recast; use it when approaching or unable to swing.
6. **React to the outcome:** A successfully damaging empowered Reckoning consumes the retaliation opportunity. A failed/missed strike still spends its ordinary ability recast, so do not gamble carelessly.
7. **Respect vow lockout:** Both vows share a 5-second recast and cannot be swapped during combat or while you retain enemy enmity, even if you momentarily disengage.
8. **Monitor the Soul HUD:** Check the active vow, vow recast, Reckoning recast and remaining retaliation window for your current target.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Condemnation at Lv. 10–29:** +10% regular Great Sword damage, but **10% more incoming enemy damage**.
- **Level 30–49:** Condemnation's regular-swing bonus rises to **13%**; Reckoning's base multiplier becomes 1.7×.
- **Level 50–69:** Condemnation bonus rises to **16%**. Absolution retaliation lasts **7 seconds**, up from 5 seconds, and Reckoning's empowered hit retains its doubled multiplier.
- **Level 70–75:** Condemnation regular-swing bonus reaches **18%**; Reckoning's base multiplier rises to 2.0×.
- **Reckoning damage:** Base normalized damage multiplier is 1.5× at Lv. 10, 1.7× at Lv. 30 and 50, and 2.0× at Lv. 70. A valid Absolution retaliation doubles that multiplier against the attacker that opened it. The action has a 4-yalm range and 18-second recast.
- **Absolution trigger:** The recorded direct HP loss from a single hit must be at least 8% of your maximum HP, dealt by a living enemy, with Great Sword equipped. The opportunity lasts 5 seconds before Lv. 50 or 7 seconds afterward.
- **Vow swapping:** The two vows share a 5-second cooldown and can only change outside both engagement and the hate list. A change clears any prepared retaliation.
- **Level-scaled passives:** Great Sword skill +12/22/34/46, STR +6/12/18/25, Accuracy +10/18/28/38 and Attack +12/22/36/50 at Lv. 10/30/50/70.
- **Soul HUD:** The retaliation timer is target-specific. Selecting a different enemy may hide a valid retaliation opportunity on the original attacker.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Trying to switch vows mid-fight:** Disengaging is not enough if an enemy still holds you on its hate list.
- **Treating Condemnation like free damage:** It increases incoming enemy damage by 10%; your healer and tank must be able to cover the extra risk.
- **Expecting Absolution to provide mitigation:** It rewards taking a substantial hit with a retaliatory opening; the hit still hurts.
- **Using Reckoning on the wrong target:** Only the enemy that triggered retaliation qualifies for the doubled strike.
- **Ignoring the retaliation timer:** Five seconds at lower levels is short; be ready before the hit occurs.
- **Equipping a different weapon:** Reckoning and the retaliation trigger are tied to Great Sword combat.
