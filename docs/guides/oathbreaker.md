# Oathbreaker (PLD) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance

Oathbreaker is a Tier-1 Great Sword damage Soul. Choose steady swings at the cost of taking more damage, or commit to punishing the enemy after a serious hit. Make the decision before the fight.

## Setup & Gear

- Select at Lv.10 with `!spec set oathbreaker` or Crystal Socket. Equip a Great Sword and prioritize accuracy and STR.
- Both vows and Reckoning work at Lv.10 with ordinary gear and zero weapon-skill talents.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)

1. Choose **Vow of Condemnation** when you can stay in melee. Your regular Great Sword swings hit harder and you take 10% more enemy damage.
2. Choose **Vow of Absolution** when you expect a single hit to cost you at least 8% max HP. That hit opens a brief retaliation against its source.
3. Use **Reckoning** within four yalms. Retaliation doubles the strike against the matching enemy. Reach for **Ashen Judgment** when the enemy is out of sword range.
4. At Lv.30/50/70, Condemnation and Reckoning scale. Lv.50 extends the retaliation window from five to seven seconds.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks

- Vows share a five-second recast and can change only while disengaged and off enemy hate lists.
- Reckoning has an 18-second recast. The HUD shows your vow, recast, and any retaliation window on your target.

## Common Mistakes

- Make the vow decision before pulling. Losing your engagement mark while retaining enemy hate does not allow a switch.
- Pair retaliation with the enemy that dealt the qualifying damage. A different enemy does not receive the doubled strike.
- Let the tank and healer cover survivability. Both vows commit their payoff to damage.
