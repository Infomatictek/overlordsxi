# Primalist (SMN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
**Elemental avatar challenger.** Primalist's damage comes from choosing an offensive avatar and committing to a Blood Pact: Rage against the enemy's least-resistant element.

**Why play this Soul?** Choose Primalist for phase-specific avatar elemental damage rather than Sage's damage-over-time or Celestial's protective sanctuary.

## Setup & Gear
- An available offensive avatar and its corresponding Rage pact; Ifrit and Carbuncle are loaned at Lv10.
- Soul unlock Lv10; character cap Lv75.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare:** Summon the elemental avatar best suited to the boss's current resistances.
2. **Act:** Activate Challenge Aspect before an offensive elemental Blood Pact: Rage from that avatar.
3. **Adapt:** Keep the same avatar for the committed attack; switching avatar cancels your prepared challenge.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- Challenge Aspect recurs every 20s; its 12–15s window adds 20/25/30/35% to the next resistance-matched elemental Rage.
- Ifrit and Carbuncle are accessible at Lv10, two selected prime avatars join at Lv30, and six prime avatars are accessible at Lv50; physical-only Rage and the wrong enemy do not qualify.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- Don't swap avatars after committing or expect Celestial-style healing/ward resets.
- Only the actions actually granted to this Soul should be treated as part of its kit.
