# Primalist (SMN) - Playstyle Guide

Primalist is a Tier 1 damage Soul. Commit one elemental avatar to an enemy, then land its Rage during the opening.

## Level 10 opening

1. Equip the Soul Crystal or use `!spec set primalist`. Staff and Club are the eligible weapon-skill families.
2. Use **Call Carbuncle** for light or **Call Ifrit** for fire. Both summons are available as Soul loans without a permanent avatar unlock.
3. Use **Challenge Aspect** on your enemy. The avatar engages, and the HUD shows whether its element currently matches the weakest resistance rank.
4. Open **Elemental Pacts** and use **Astral Comet** or **Inferno Brand** on the same enemy. Challenge is spent on resolution. A resistant element still deals ordinary pact damage.
5. Use **Recall Avatar** to bring it back or **End Manifestation** to dismiss it before calling another avatar. Either command closes an active Challenge.

The opening works with any subjob and zero weapon-skill talents. Summons cost 5 MP. Pacts share an 18-second base recast, and Challenge has a separate 20-second recast.

| Level | Avatar loans | Challenge bonus | Window | Pact MP |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10–29 | Carbuncle and Ifrit | 20% | 12s | 8 |
| 30–49 | Two additional primes of your choice | 25% | 12s | 14 |
| 50–69 | All seven | 30% | 12s | 20 |
| 70–75 | All seven | 35% | 15s | 26 |

Use **Attune First Prime** and **Attune Second Prime** at Lv. 30–49 to choose two distinct loans from Shiva, Garuda, Titan, Leviathan and Ramuh. The initial choices are Shiva and Garuda. A changed slot may dismiss its old avatar. The selection is saved for later level sync.

The challenge compares all eight elemental resistance ranks at the moment the pact lands. Tied lowest ranks qualify. The selected avatar, target and target spawn must match. Use the alternate element when an enemy changes resistance.
