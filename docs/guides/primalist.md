# Primalist (SMN) — Playstyle Guide

## At a Glance
Primalist is a Tier 1 single-target elemental damage Soul. Rather than keeping every avatar available immediately, you build a focused set of elemental loans, challenge one quarry and land a matching avatar Rage pact during a short window. The challenge rewards choosing an element the enemy resists least; a poorly matched element still deals ordinary pact damage without the bonus.

## Setup & Gear
- **Unlock:** Equip the Primalist Soul Crystal or use `!spec set primalist` from Lv. 10.
- **Weapons:** Staff and Club are the eligible weapon-skill families. The starting avatar-and-pact loop works with any subjob and zero weapon-skill talent points.
- **Starter avatars:** **Call Carbuncle** provides light and **Call Ifrit** provides fire, without permanent avatar unlock requirements. Each summon costs 5 MP.
- **MP planning:** Elemental pacts have increasing MP costs as you level: 8, 14, 20 and 26 MP at the respective progression brackets. Do not spend a pact just because it is off cooldown if your challenge or element is wrong.
- **Target priority:** Stay on a single quarry through the challenge and pact resolution. Position and pet control should support reliable single-target contact.

## How to Play (Step-by-Step)
1. **Choose a useful element:** Call an avatar suitable for the current target. Start with **Call Carbuncle** (light) or **Call Ifrit** (fire).
2. **Challenge your target:** Use **Challenge Aspect** on the enemy you intend to damage. The summoned avatar engages, and the Soul HUD indicates whether its element matches the enemy's weakest resistance rank.
3. **Land the pact:** Open **Elemental Pacts** and use the available matching Rage pact, such as **Astral Comet** or **Inferno Brand**, against that same enemy. The challenge is consumed when the pact resolves.
4. **Decide whether to swap:** If the active element is poorly matched, the pact still deals ordinary damage, but you lose the Challenge bonus. Use another available element when the target's resistances favor it.
5. **Manage the avatar:** **Recall Avatar** brings it back. **End Manifestation** dismisses it so you can summon a different avatar. Both commands close an active Challenge, so avoid using them mid-window without a reason.
6. **Repeat when ready:** Pacts share an 18-second base recast. Challenge Aspect uses its own 20-second recast; plan to align them rather than repeatedly opening unused Challenge windows.
7. **At Lv. 30–49:** Use **Attune First Prime** and **Attune Second Prime** to select two distinct additional avatar loans from Shiva, Garuda, Titan, Leviathan and Ramuh. The initial two choices are Shiva and Garuda.
8. **At Lv. 50+:** All seven avatar loans become available. Choose the element for the actual quarry rather than locking into a habitual summon.

## Key Mechanics & Stacks
- **Level 10–29:** Carbuncle and Ifrit; Challenge grants a 20% matching-element bonus for a 12-second window; pact MP cost is 8.
- **Level 30–49:** Two additional selectable Prime loans; Challenge bonus 25%, 12-second window and pact MP cost 14.
- **Level 50–69:** All seven avatars available; Challenge bonus 30%, 12-second window and pact MP cost 20.
- **Level 70–75:** All seven avatars available; Challenge bonus 35%, 15-second window and pact MP cost 26.
- **Element check:** At pact resolution, the target's eight elemental resistance ranks are compared. A tie for the lowest rank qualifies for the matching bonus.
- **Target and avatar binding:** The challenged target, target spawn and selected avatar must still match when the pact lands.
- **Attunement persistence:** Lv. 30–49 Prime choices are saved for level sync. Changing a slot may dismiss its old avatar.
- **Soul HUD:** Shows the avatar's element and whether it currently matches the quarry's lowest resistance tier, helping decide whether to use or change your pact.

## Common Mistakes (Don't Do This)
- **Challenging one enemy and attacking another:** The challenge is bound to its original quarry and spawn.
- **Dismissing the avatar mid-window:** Recall and dismissal commands end an active Challenge; time them between attack cycles.
- **Treating every element as equally strong:** The bonus requires a weakest-resistance match, though an unmatched pact can still deal base damage.
- **Forgetting that pacts share a recast:** An 18-second shared pact timer affects what you can cast next; Challenge has its own 20-second timer.
- **Using the wrong Prime loans after level sync:** Check your saved attunement at intermediate levels.
- **Overspending MP on unprepared pacts:** Match your Challenge timing, target and element before committing expensive Rage damage.
